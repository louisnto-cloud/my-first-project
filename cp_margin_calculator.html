Why it matters

A margin computed on invoice cost alone overstates reality. Freight, duty, packaging and inbound handling typically add 8–25% to a CPG unit cost. Duty is applied on the invoice value; everything else is entered per unit. The total here is the CP the rest of this workbench should run on.

Channel price ladder

Every tier takes its margin on its own selling price. Run it forward from your cost — or backward from a target shelf price.

Channel

Reading the ladder

Forward: tier price = buy price ÷ (1 − tier margin), compounding at each step — three 30% tiers turn a $1.00 cost into a $2.92 shelf price. Reverse strips each tier off a target shelf price to reveal the maximum cost you can afford; if your actual cost is higher, the status pill goes red and shows the gap.

Margin vs Markup — what's the difference?
Margin % = (Price − Cost) ÷ Price × 100 — profit as a share of selling price.
Markup % = (Price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100 — profit as a share of cost.
A 50% markup equals a 33.3% margin. A 100% margin is impossible; a 100% markup doubles the cost.

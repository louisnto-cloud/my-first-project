# Research log 2026-10-11, prefix fw-grandpa (Field Wisdom lessons 11 to 20, "Grandpa's rules")

Scope: 100 traditional or widely repeated hunting sayings, 10 per lesson, each with meaning, evidence, limits and origin.
Honesty rules followed: no invented grandpa, anecdote or quote. Every saying was found in a hunting educator, agency, conservation group, hunting magazine, or in an earlier app lesson that cites its own source. Labels: Tradition (long passed down, widely taught) or Opinion (a named writer, guide, or the app). Certainty only on the factual part.

## Read directly (full text)
- US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, MMWR, "Hunting Associated Injuries and Wearing Hunter Orange Clothing, New York, 1989 to 1995": https://cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/00044112.htm (90%). 356 two party injuries; 125 mistaken for game; 117 (94%) no orange, 6 (5%) orange, 2 unknown; turkey hunting 78% of mistaken for game.
- National Deer Association, "The Hunter's Guide to Deer Vision", NDA Staff, 2012-03-15: https://deerassociation.com/hunters-guide-deer-vision/ (85%). Dichromats; blue and green; cannot tell orange from green or red; blue clothing a disadvantage; no UV filter; "scent and movement are far more important than the color of your clothing".
- National Deer Association, "You just shot a deer. Here's how to find it", 2023-10-25: https://deerassociation.com/watch-you-just-shot-a-deer-heres-how-to-find-it/ (85%). Firearm heart or lung: a few minutes; archery 15 to 30 min; liver 8 to 10 h; guts 10 to 12 h; leg or bone: track at once, follow up shot; flagging, landmarks; stomach hit deer bed quickly.
- Penn State Deer Forest blog, "Wandering in the moonlight", Jessica Hepner, 2017-08-09: https://deer.psu.edu/wandering-in-the-moonlight (85%). GPS does, October 2015 and 2016, Susquehannock, Bald Eagle, Rothrock State Forests; "Moon phase has an insignificant effect on deer movement"; bucks excluded.
- Penn State Deer Forest blog, "Wind's surprising effects on deer movement", Leah Giralico, 2015-04-20: https://www.deer.psu.edu/winds-surprising-effects-on-deer-movement/ (80%). More on windy days, less on windy nights, even at light air.
- Penn State News, 2015-03-16, research focus on moon and weather: https://www.psu.edu/news/research/story/research-focus-how-deer-respond-changes-moon-phase-weather (80%). Prior research contradictory.
- Penn State Extension, "Proper Care and Handling of Venison from Field to Table", Catherine Cutter, updated 2020-11-18: https://extension.psu.edu/proper-care-and-handling-of-venison-from-field-to-table (90%). Cool to 35 to 40 F (3 to 4 C) quickly; ice in sealed bags in cavity; hold at 40 F or below 2 to 3 days at most; aging not necessary for sausage; if washed, dry quickly.
- Ohio Department of Natural Resources, "Field dressing deer" PDF (pdftotext): https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/ohiodnr.gov/documents/wildlife/hunting-trapping/field%20dressing%20deer%20pub111.pdf (90%). Two misconceptions: cutting the throat (heart and lung removal bleeds better) and tarsal gland removal (unnecessary, contamination risk); rinse cavity with clean cold water; remove hide ASAP to cool.
- Ducks Unlimited, "Shotgunning: How Far is Too Far?", Phil Bourjaily, 2025-11-01: https://www.ducks.org/hunting/shooting-tips/shotgunning-how-far-is-too-far (85%). Maximum competent range; 5 yard misjudgement; many self taught hunters struggle with 30 yard crossers; pattern board.
- Delta Waterfowl, "Hunt Ducks, Lose the Guilt", Christy Sweigart, winter 2025: https://deltawaterfowl.org/whats-new/hunting/hunt-ducks-lose-the-guilt/ (75%). Todd Arnold: drake harvest no impact, maybe positive; about 3% of adult female pintails harvested; hunting 5 to 10% of hen mortality versus about 25% to breeding season predation; compensatory versus additive debate ongoing.

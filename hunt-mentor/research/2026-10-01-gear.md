## Rules
- Migratory birds: shotgun max 3 shells (magazine + chamber); plug is one piece, removable only by disassembly; detachable magazine max 2. Migratory Birds Regulations, 2022 (SOR/2022-105) https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105/page-3.html (85%, search preview)
- Non toxic shot required for migratory game birds (steel, tungsten, tin, bismuth). Same source (85%)
- Lead legal for BC upland birds; banned in National Wildlife Areas and for waterfowl (70%, VERIFY). bcfirearmsacademy.ca/toxic-lead-shot/
- No rifles for game birds except turkey, grouse, ptarmigan: .22 LR legal for grouse; quail need a shotgun (70%, VERIFY). BC Reg 190/84

## Shotgun
- Pump: cheapest, reliable, most recoil, short stroke risk (70%)
- Gas semi auto: softest recoil, best for beginner with 3 in steel, more cleaning (70%) Field & Stream
- Inertia semi auto: clean, reliable in cold, sharper recoil, light loads may not cycle (70%) Impact Guns
- Over/under: 2 shots, many not steel rated; weak as one gun for ducks + upland (65%)
- 12 gauge for one gun ducks + upland (70%, Ducks Unlimited). 3 in chamber enough (75%). 28 in semi auto good compromise; 26 in for grouse timber (65%).
- Chokes with steel: IC (Improved Cylinder) and M (Modified). Never Full or tighter unless marked steel safe. Benelli Crio Plus (80%), Stoeger M3000 IC/M ok, XFT not for steel (75%), Mossberg not with X Full/XX Full (80%), Browning Invector Plus stainless (75%), Beretta nothing tighter than M (75%).
- Fit: length of pull ~14 to 14.75 in (65%); winter jacket makes stock feel longer; A300 Ultima 13.8 in + 2 spacers (70%).
- Used: bore both ends, chokes not seized, cycle action, gas piston/O ring or recoil spring, plug present (65%).

## Scope for Tikka T3x Arctic
- 20 in stainless, 1:11 twist, removable rail (80% Sako). Weight 4.1 kg (Sako) vs 8.13 lb (Athlon): VERIFY.
- Rail ~4.75 to 5 in, shortened for the rear aperture sight (70% Recoil).
- Rear rotary drum peep sight 100 to 600 m (75% all4shooters). Scope must clear it. Owners: high rings needed (Vortex Pro high barely cleared a Razor LHT); cantilever clears but too high; some remove irons (60 to 65%, Canadian Gun Nutz threads).
- Short rail limits ring spacing: dry fit at the store (tip 60%). Removing rear sight without gunsmith: VERIFY.
- Ring height: with rear sight, expect HIGH rings for 40 to 44 mm objective (60%). Irons off: low/medium (60%).
- Magnification 3 to 9x40 or 3.5 to 10x40 covers BC big game to ~300 m; low end 2.5 to 3x matters in timber (65%). Objective 40 to 44 mm (65%). SFP (second focal plane) for beginners (70%). Duplex simplest; BDC/hash only after confirming drops (70%). Capped turrets for beginners (65%). Eye relief ~3.5 in+ (65%). Fixed parallax 100 yd fine to ~300 m (65%).

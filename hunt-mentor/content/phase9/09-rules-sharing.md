## Rule 81: Never sell or trade game meat

```diagram fw-rules-09-meat
```

- **Rule:** no selling, trading or bartering wildlife meat for anything of value, and no offering to (99%).
- **Slip:** venison swapped for a case of beer or a hand with the roof.
- **Fix:** give it, nothing back. A true gift is not trafficking (90%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 7, page 11
> "To traffic in live wildlife, wildlife meat or offer to do so, except as authorized by permit."

> [!law] Definition, page 4
> "TRAFFIC OR TRAFFICKING - means to buy, sell, trade or distribute for gain or consideration or to offer to do so."

- A favour or goods in return is "consideration" (90%, my reading). Ask a conservation officer if unsure (VERIFY).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** trading meat feels like neighbourly sharing.
- **How common:** officers investigated moose and elk meat sales in Quesnel and Williams Lake (60%, CBC News, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 7; [definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 4 (99%).
```

## Rule 82: Never buy or sell ducks or geese

```diagram re-mb-label
```

- **Rule:** no buying or selling migratory birds, their eggs or nests (99%).
- **Slip:** selling a few plucked geese to a co worker.
- **Fix:** give them away, labelled (rule 67), or keep them.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 6, page 11
> "To buy or sell migratory birds (or their eggs or nest)."

- **Why it trips people (Tip):** "it is only a couple of birds at cost."
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 6 (99%).
```

## Rule 83: Carrying someone else's meat needs a Record of Receipt

```diagram rb-record-receipt
```

- **Rule:** to possess or transport meat or parts of an animal someone else killed, carry a Record of Receipt (99%).
- **Slip:** driving your brother's deer home with no paper.
- **Fix:** write the receipt the moment the meat changes hands; photograph it (Tip).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Page 15: carry your species licence for your own kill, or a [[Record of Receipt]] showing date and place, both names and addresses, the hunter's FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID), the species licence number, and species and sex (99%).
- The form is printed on page 14 (99%).
- Ducks and grouse included: the rule says wildlife meat (95%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** family meat feels like your own.
- **How common:** road checks list unlawful possession of dead wildlife or parts among common tickets (70%, Q101 News, read directly; 60%, KelownaNow, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: bag limits](#/s/rb-bag-limits) and [after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill), pages 14 and 15 (99%).
```

## Rule 84: Packing a partner's quarter: 3 conditions

```diagram rb-transport-papers
```

- **Rule:** your piece of a partner's animal may skip the tail and evidence of sex only if: going to their home, a meat cutter, cold storage or an inspector; with a Record of Receipt; and they keep a piece with the parts attached (99%).
- **Slip:** all four quarters split up, no piece keeps the tail.
- **Fix:** the hunter carries the piece with tail and evidence.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Page 15 exception, sections 1 to 3: caribou, elk, moose, deer, bison, goat and sheep. Cougar is not in it (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** every pack feels like "part of the same deer".
- **How common:** see rule 72, carcass without parts attached.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill), page 15 (99%).
```

## Rule 85: Gifted ducks count in the receiver's freezer

```diagram re-mb-preserved
```

- **Rule:** once your friend accepts your ducks, they count toward his possession limit (99%).
- **Slip:** giving 10 unpreserved ducks to someone who already holds 20.
- **Fix:** ask what they already hold; label every gift (rule 67).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Federal s. 46(5): a gift counts in the receiver's possession once accepted (99%, as carried in the Rule Book).
- The possession limit for ducks is 24, home included, until preserved (99%, page 12).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the gift feels like it leaves your count, not joins theirs.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: ducks and geese](#/s/rb-migratory-birds), s. 46 (99%).
```

## Rule 86: Keep the paper until the meat is eaten

```diagram rb-transport-papers
```

- **Rule:** keep every document an animal was taken under until the animal has been eaten (99%).
- **Slip:** throwing out the cancelled tag in January with half the deer still in the freezer.
- **Fix:** tape the cancelled licence inside the freezer lid (Tip).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 7
> "Hunters must keep all documents under which an animal was taken until the animal has been consumed."

- A mount or tanned hide: keep the licence and any inspection report in a safe place for good (99%, page 7).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the licence expires 31 March, so it feels like trash.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 7 (99%).
```

## Rule 87: Bring the papers to the butcher and taxidermist

```diagram rb-transport-papers
```

- **Rule:** at a meat cutter or taxidermist, have your hunting licence and cancelled species licence, plus any LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) authorization or inspection sheet that applies (99%).
- **Slip:** dropping the deer off and leaving the tag at camp.
- **Fix:** the licence goes into the shop with the meat (Tip).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 16
> "When a hunter takes the parts of an animal to the business concerned, the person must have the following documentation with them"

- The meat cutter records species, sex and MU (Management Unit): know your MU (99% rule, 95% my reading).
- Page 15: you "should obtain from the butcher a receipt" showing your FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID), the licence number, species and sex (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the shop feels like the end of the trail.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill), pages 15 and 16 (99%).
```

## Rule 88: Never keep live wildlife

```photo g-md-fawn A fawn alone: look, then leave it be.
```

- **Rule:** no capturing, possessing or keeping live wildlife without a permit (99%).
- **Slip:** taking a wounded duck home alive, or "rescuing" a fawn.
- **Fix:** finish a wounded bird at once (95%). Leave fawns where they are (Tip).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 4, page 11
> "To capture, possess or keep in captivity any live wildlife without a permit."

- Federal s. 42: a wounded migratory bird must be killed and retrieved as soon as the circumstances permit (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** kindness, or a dog that brings back a live bird.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 4; [ducks and geese](#/s/rb-migratory-birds), s. 42 (99%).
```

## Rule 89: Out of province deer: meat and clean parts only

```diagram parts-home-bc
```

- **Rule:** from a deer, elk, moose or caribou killed outside BC, bring in only the meat of quarters, loins, neck and ribs, and a hide, antlers and skull plate cleaned of all tissue (99%).
- **Slip:** a whole Alberta deer, head on, in the truck bed.
- **Fix:** bone out and clean the skull plate before the BC border.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 50, page 12
> "To possess any part of a Cervidae (caribou, deer, elk, moose), that was killed outside of British Columbia, other than (1) the edible meat of the four quarters, loins, neck, and ribs, or (2) the hide, antlers, and skull plate if, before being brought into British Columbia, the part is cleaned in a manner that removes all tissue."

- Item 51: no deer, elk, moose or caribou scents or lures from outside BC (99%).
- These rules help keep out CWD (Chronic Wasting Disease) (90%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a successful trip, and the whole animal looks better.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), items 50 and 51 (99%).
```

## Rule 90: Bear parts: no galls, no selling paws

```photo g-bb-sow-cubs Black bear: the gall bladder stays at the kill.
```

- **Rule:** no possessing or trafficking bear galls; leave the gall bladder at the kill, or dispose of the gut pile with it within 48 hours. Never sell or trade bear paws (99%).
- **Slip:** bagging the whole gut pile to sort at home.
- **Fix:** gall bladder out and left at the kill site.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Item 48 bans possessing or trafficking bear galls, any part or derivative of the gall bladder or bile, or male genitalia separated from the hide and carcass (99%, page 12).
- Item 49: you may keep your legal bear's paws for personal and ceremonial use, never traffic them (99%).
- Black bear meat must come home like deer meat (99%, item 44).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** few hunters know galls are regulated at all.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), items 44, 48 and 49 (99%).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram fw-rules-09-meat
```

- **Rhyme:** "Give it, never sell it; paper with the meat."
- **Number trick:** 3 conditions to carry a partner's quarter; 48 hours for a bear gut pile.
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "If anything comes back, it is a sale."

```more mistakes and field drill
> [!rule]
> Old hand's rule (wisdom, not law): every cooler of game meat gets a paper inside the lid.

> [!mistake]
> - Trading venison for work or goods.
> - Hauling a brother's deer with no Record of Receipt.
> - Tossing the cancelled licence while meat is still in the freezer.

> [!field]
> When meat changes hands: write the Record of Receipt, both sign, photograph it, tuck it in the cooler.
```

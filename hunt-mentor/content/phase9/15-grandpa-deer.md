## 1. You will never fool the nose

```anim mmd-scent-cone Your scent spreads downwind in a cone.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "You can fool the eyes, you can fool the ears, you will never fool the nose."

- **Means:** play the wind every time. No spray or soap beats a deer's nose.
- **Label:** tradition (app). Scent matters more than colour (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** the National Deer Association ranks scent and movement far above clothing colour (85%, read directly).
- App: [Mule deer masterclass 3](#/s/mc-mule-deer-03), [Deer senses and mind](#/s/deer-mind).
- **When it is wrong:** never. You can only keep your scent away from the deer.
- **Where it comes from:** deer hunters everywhere.
```

## 2. Count one side twice

```diagram mmd-buck-ages
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Count one side twice, then decide."

- **Means:** for a points rule, count the tines on one antler, then count again.
- **Label:** tradition (app). The legal count uses one antler (99%, Rule Book).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- **Why it works:** a [[4 point buck]] has at least 4 tines on one antler, not counting the [[Brow tine]] (99%, synopsis page 4, via [Region 3 rules](#/s/rb-region-3)).
- App: [Mule deer masterclass 1](#/s/mc-mule-deer-01).
- **When it is wrong:** never wrong to count twice. If you cannot see one side clearly, no shot.
- **Where it comes from:** BC hunters under points rules.
```

## 3. Read the body before the bone

```gallery
g-md-young-bucks | Young bucks: thin necks, small racks.
g-md-buck-4pt | Mature: deep chest, thick neck.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "Read the body before the bone."

- **Means:** age a buck by neck, chest and belly, not by counting points.
- **Label:** opinion. Points are a poor age guide (90%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- BC's mule deer account: "The number of antler points is not a reliable way to determine the age" (90%, BC Ministry account 2000, via [Mule deer masterclass 1](#/s/mc-mule-deer-01)).
- Body clues for older bucks (deep chest, thick neck, sagging belly) are hunter consensus (Tip).
- **When it is wrong:** for legality you still need the points count (rule 2).
- **Where it comes from:** deer managers and old hands.
```

## 4. In the rut, hunt the does

```diagram sg-deer-rut-phases
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "In the rut, hunt the does. The buck will find them before you find him."

- **Means:** in November, find the doe groups. Bucks come to them.
- **Label:** tradition (app). The rut is timed by day length (70%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** bucks seek does in the rut (Tip). Fetal aging studies show peak breeding dates stay steady year to year (70%, secondary).
- App: Grandpa's rule in [The rut](#/s/the-rut) and [Mule deer masterclass 5](#/s/mc-mule-deer-05).
- **When it is wrong:** before and after the rut, bucks keep to themselves (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** deer hunters' tradition.
```

## 5. A flag is a goodbye

```anim mwt-escape-run Flag up, gallop to cover.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "A flag in the air means goodbye. Let it go."

- **Means:** do not shoot at a white tail bounding away with its tail up.
- **Label:** tradition (app). Running shots are poor shots (Tip).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** hunter education teaches shots at a still animal, broadside or quartering away (85%, Texas Parks and Wildlife vital areas page). A running deer offers neither.
- App: Grandpa's rule in [White tailed deer](#/s/white-tailed-deer); "A flag is a goodbye, not a target" ([masterclass 1](#/s/mc-white-tailed-deer-01)).
- **When it is wrong:** the deer often stops just inside cover. Stay ready, but only shoot a still, clear animal (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** white tail hunters.
```

## 6. A mulie may look back

```anim mmd-stot-lookback Bounce, bounce, stop and look back.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "When a mulie bounces off, get ready and count."

- **Means:** a spooked mule deer often stops at a safe distance to look back.
- **Label:** tradition. Widely reported; older bucks may not (60%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **For:** after reaching relative safety, a mule deer "will often stop and take a look" (60%, MeatEater mule deer guide, search preview).
- **Against:** the same guide says old bucks often head straight for cover without looking back (60%). App: "Big bucks don't look back" ([Mule deer masterclass 4](#/s/mc-mule-deer-04)).
- **When it is wrong:** with old bucks, and at ranges past your limit.
- **Where it comes from:** western mule deer hunters.
```

## 7. Big bucks reach the field after dark

```diagram fw-grandpa-daylight-visits
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Hunt the trail, not the field. Big bucks reach the field after dark."

- **Means:** under pressure, sit back on the trail into cover, not on the open field.
- **Label:** tradition (app). One collar study: daylight visits fell from 1 in 3 to 1 in 20 (60%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Evidence:** GPS (Global Positioning System) collared bucks near stands: daylight visits fell from about 1 in 3 in weeks 1 to 3 to about 1 in 20 by week 13 (60%, secondary report of an Auburn University study).
- App: Grandpa's rule in [White tailed deer](#/s/white-tailed-deer); "An old buck didn't get old by being careless" ([masterclass 4](#/s/mc-white-tailed-deer-04)).
- **When it is wrong:** in the rut, bucks show up in daylight more (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** white tail hunters.
```

## 8. Shoot the lungs, eat the shoulders

```diagram vitals-deer
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Shoot the lungs, eat the shoulders."

- **Means:** aim behind the front leg, a third of the way up: the lungs. Leave the meat intact.
- **Label:** tradition (app). Heart and lungs are the hunter education target (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** hunter education teaches the heart and lung area as the vital zone (85%, Texas Parks and Wildlife).
- App: [Mule deer masterclass 2](#/s/mc-mule-deer-02), [Shot placement](#/s/shot-placement).
- **When it is wrong:** steep angles; follow the bullet's path to the far shoulder (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** hunters and meat cutters.
```

## 9. Bedded deer watch below, smell behind

```anim rs-bd-wind A bedded deer: eyes downhill, nose to the wind behind.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "A bedded buck watches below and smells behind."

- **Means:** mule deer often bed with a view out front and the wind at their back.
- **Label:** opinion. No study tested the wind part (Tip).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **For:** Field and Stream's mule deer guidance lists a good view out front and wind at the back as bed site features (60%, search preview).
- **What science measured:** in 236 mule deer day beds, temperature and canopy cover mattered most; wind was not measured (85%, Germaine and others 2004).
- App: [Beds](#/s/rs-beds).
- **When it is wrong:** hot days, when shade beats view (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** western hunting writers.
```

## 10. Wear orange, skip blue

```diagram mc-skills-colour-see
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "Orange for the hunters, not blue for the deer."

- **Means:** deer see blue well and orange poorly. Wear orange; leave the blue jeans home.
- **Label:** opinion. Deer are red green colourblind (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- National Deer Association: deer have two cone types, see blue and green, cannot tell orange from green, and blue clothing is a disadvantage (85%, read directly).
- **When it is wrong:** movement and scent still give you away in any colour (85%).
- Whether orange is required for your hunt: [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods).
- **Where it comes from:** University of Georgia deer vision research, popularized by the National Deer Association.
```

## Myth check

```diagram fw-grandpa-myth-meter
```

| Saying | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Cut off the tarsal glands or the meat is ruined" | Myth (90%) |
| "UV brighteners make you glow to deer" | Mixed (70%) |
| "Count the points to age the buck" | Myth (90%) |

```more the evidence for each
- **Tarsal glands:** removal is unnecessary; if you do, glands on your knife or hands can contaminate the meat (90%, Ohio Department of Natural Resources field dressing guide, read directly; Montana State University Extension agrees). See [[Tarsal gland]].
- **UV brighteners:** deer lack a UV filter and see short wavelengths well (85%, National Deer Association). The Georgia researchers called the finding not entirely conclusive, and doubt deer see a "glow" (65%, secondary). Scent and movement matter more (85%).
- **Points and age:** "not a reliable way to determine the age" (90%, BC account).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mmd-buck-ages
```

- **Rhyme:** "Eyes and ears, you can fool. The nose is the rule."
- **Number trick:** 1 side, 2 counts: count one antler twice (99% legal count).
- **Number trick:** 1 in 3 to 1 in 20: daylight buck visits as pressure builds (60%).

```more more hooks
- **Body before bone** for age.
- **Flag up, gun down.**
- **Orange yes, blue no.**

> [!mistake]
> - Counting both antlers together for a points rule.
> - Shooting at a flagging, running white tail.
> - Cutting the tarsal glands, then handling the meat with the same knife.

> [!field]
> At the next deer you see: say out loud "wind, side, points, body". Four checks, every time.
```

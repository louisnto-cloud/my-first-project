## Rule 51: Mule deer 4 point: the brow tine does not count

```diagram rb-4point
```

- **Rule:** a 4 point mule deer buck has at least 4 tines on one antler, not counting the brow tine (99%).
- **Slip:** 3 points plus a brow tine, called a 4 point.
- **Fix:** count one side, skip the brow tine, then count again.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Definition, page 4
> "MULE (BLACK-TAILED) DEER - FOUR POINTS OR GREATER BUCK - means any buck having at least four tines, excluding the brow tine, on one antler."

- For elk and moose the brow tine does count (99%, page 3).
- A [[4 point buck]] is counted on one antler, not both added (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** eastern counts add both sides, and the brow tine counts on elk.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found. The Rule Book lists it as a top mistake.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), pages 3 and 4 (99%).
```

## Rule 52: A bump is not a point

```diagram rb-tine
```

- **Rule:** a tine is at least 2.5 cm (1 in) long and longer than it is wide (99%).
- **Slip:** counting a short knob to make the fourth point.
- **Fix:** not sure it passes 2.5 cm? It is not a point. No shot.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Definition, page 4
> "TINE OR "POINT" - means a branch of an antler which is longer than its breadth and is at least 2.5 cm (1 inch) in length"

- Width is measured at least 2.5 cm down from the tip (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** at 150 m a knob looks like a point.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 4 (99%).
```

## Rule 53: Region 3: from 1 November, 4 points or nothing

```anim re-r3-mule-week From 1 November it is 4 points or nothing.
```

- **Rule:** MU (Management Unit) 3-27 and 3-28 mule deer: any buck 1 to 31 October; 4 point bucks only 1 November to 10 December (99%).
- **Slip:** a forked buck on 2 November.
- **Fix:** read the row for the date, not just the season's end.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Page 34: 4 point bucks 10 to 30 September, bucks 1 to 31 October, 4 point bucks 1 November to 10 December, in 3-12 to 3-20 and 3-26 to 3-44 (99%).
- Region 8 mule deer close 10 November, a month sooner. MUs 8-12 to 8-15 have no any buck row (99%, page 68).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** "deer season runs to 10 December" is only half the row.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found. The Rule Book lists it as a top Region 3 mistake.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 34; [Region 8](#/s/rb-region-8), page 68 (99%).
```

## Rule 54: Antler only season: antlers stay on the skull

```diagram rb-evidence-deer
```

- **Rule:** after an antler or horn restricted season kill, keep the antlers on part of the upper skull, with the licence, until home and butchered, at the meat cutter, or inspected. No altering (99%).
- **Slip:** cutting the antlers off to pack them out.
- **Fix:** take the skull plate with antlers attached, licence beside it.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 15
> "A person must, while returning from hunting, keep the antlers or horns, naturally attached to a portion of the upper skull, of wildlife killed during an antler/horn restricted season (e.g., 6 pt, full curl, spike-fork, etc.) and the species licence under which the wildlife was killed, together and available for inspection by an officer."

- Item 54 bans altering or tampering with those antlers (99%, page 12).
- Region 3 footnote on 4 point rows: "The antlers must accompany the species licence." (99%, page 34). A 4 point season is antler restricted (95%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** heads are heavy, antlers are not.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill), page 15; [it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 54 (99%).
```

## Rule 55: One mule deer buck for Regions 3 to 8

```diagram fw-rules-06-one-buck
```

- **Rule:** only 1 mule deer buck a licence year from Regions 3, 4, 5, 6, 7A, 7B and 8 combined, and 3 deer at most in all of BC (99%).
- **Slip:** a Heffley Creek buck in October, then an Okanagan buck in November.
- **Fix:** one mule buck between your two interior areas. Mission black tails sit outside it (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 14
> "Hunters may not harvest more than 3 deer in one licence year, only 1 may be a mule (black-tailed) deer buck from regions 3, 4, 5, 6, 7A, 7B, and 8 combined."

- Region 2 bucks are outside that 1 (95%, my reading of page 14).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** each region's table looks like its own limit.
- **How common:** a Lower Mainland hunter was fined $3,000 after an investigation found he was over his limit and had not cancelled his licence (60%, Prince George Citizen, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: bag limits](#/s/rb-bag-limits), page 14 (99%).
```

## Rule 56: A button buck is antlerless

```diagram re-r3-deer-bag
```

- **Rule:** a male fawn with skin covered bumps is antlerless. Antlerless mule deer in Regions 3 and 8 are LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) draw only (99%).
- **Slip:** "it is a little buck" in a bucks only season.
- **Fix:** no visible bony antler, no shot, unless you hold the right draw.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Definition, page 3
> "The small skin or hair covered protuberances of male fawns and calves do not constitute antlers."

> [!law] Page 33
> "Antlerless mule (black-tailed) deer are under LEH only."

- [[Button buck]] fawns are [[Antlerless]] (99%). Region 8 prints the same LEH line (99%, page 67).
- Wrong animal down? Self report: rule 79.
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** it is male, so it feels like a buck.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 3; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 33 (99%).
```

## Rule 57: Spike fork moose: count one antler

```photo g-moose-young-bull Young bull: count the tines on one antler.
```

- **Rule:** a spike fork bull has no more than 2 tines on one antler, brow palm tines included. A calf never counts (99%).
- **Slip:** a 3 point side read as a fork in poor light.
- **Fix:** see both antlers clearly; 2 or fewer on at least one side (90%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Definition, page 3
> "MOOSE - SPIKE-FORK BULL - means a bull moose having no more than two tines (points) on one antler. (Includes tines on main antler and brow palms.) Does not include a calf."

- Only one side needs to qualify (90%, my reading). Ask a conservation officer if unsure (VERIFY).
- MUs 3-27 and 3-28: spike fork bulls 1 to 15 November (99%, page 34). File the Mandatory Hunter Report: rule 77.
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** moose antlers hide points in brush.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 3; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 34 (99%).
```

## Rule 58: Never a bear cub, or any bear with one

```photo g-bb-sow-cubs A sow with cubs: never legal, neither the cubs nor the sow.
```

- **Rule:** no hunting a black bear under 2 years old, or any bear in its company. White and blue (Glacier) colour phases are never legal (99%).
- **Slip:** a lone looking sow, cubs out of sight in the brush.
- **Fix:** watch a while before the shot, look for small bears.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Items 14 and 15, page 11
> "To hunt a black bear less than 2 years old or any bear in its company." "To hunt the white or blue (Glacier) colour phases of the black bear."

- A [[Cub]] or [[Yearling]] is off limits (99%).
- Same idea for cats: no cougar kitten or any cougar with it, no lynx or bobcat with another (99%, items 16 and 17).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** cubs trail far behind in thick cover.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), items 14 to 17 (99%).
```

## Rule 59: No shooting big game while it swims

```diagram rb-boat-rules
```

- **Rule:** no hunting, wounding or killing big game while it swims, unless you already wounded it (99%).
- **Slip:** a deer swimming the lake beside the boat.
- **Fix:** let it reach land and leave. No chase with the boat (rule 29).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 26, page 11
> "To hunt, take, wound or kill big game while it is swimming unless it has been previously wounded."

- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a swimming deer looks like an easy chance.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 26 (99%).
```

## Rule 60: Dogs on a leash for deer and moose

```diagram rb-dog-leash
```

- **Rule:** dogs used to hunt deer, elk, moose, sheep, goat and caribou must be on a leash (99%).
- **Slip:** letting the dog run loose on a wounded deer's trail.
- **Fix:** tracking dog on a long leash. Off leash only for birds and small game (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Dogs, page 12
> "dogs must be on a leash when used to hunt deer, elk, moose, mountain sheep, mountain goat and caribou."

- A dog tracking your wounded deer is used to hunt it (90%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the same dog runs free for grouse.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), page 12 (99%).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram rb-4point
```

- **Rhyme:** "Brow tine out, count one side, then count again."
- **Number trick:** 2.5 cm makes a point; 2 points or fewer makes a spike fork moose.
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "If you are not sure, you are not shooting."

```more mistakes and field drill
> [!rule]
> Old hand's rule (wisdom, not law): look at the bear for a full minute. Cubs show up late.

> [!mistake]
> - A second mule deer buck in Region 8 after one in Region 3.
> - A forked buck in Region 3 after 31 October.
> - Cutting antlers off the skull in an antler restricted season.

> [!field]
> Before any buck shot, say out loud: "species, date row, points on one side, no brow tine."
```

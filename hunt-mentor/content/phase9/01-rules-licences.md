## Rule 1: Carry every species licence, used or not

```diagram rb-carry
```

- **Rule:** every [[Species licence]] bought this licence year rides with you while you hunt, cancelled or not (99%).
- **Slip:** the used deer tag stays home on the fridge.
- **Fix:** one wallet pocket holds every tag until 31 March.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "You must carry all your species licences while hunting, both cancelled and uncancelled."

- Item 52 makes hunting without them an offence (99%, page 12).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the hunting licence went electronic, so people think all the paper went with it. Species licences did not (99%, page 5).
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No enforcement count found for this one. The Rule Book lists it as a top mistake.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees) page 5, and [it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts) item 52, page 12, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 2: Know your FWID, carry photo ID

```diagram rb-licence-path
```

- **Rule:** you must know or produce your FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID) number and photo ID when an officer asks (99%).
- **Slip:** "It is all on their app", so no ID in the pocket.
- **Fix:** photo ID in the wallet, FWID number memorized.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "Please note that you are legally required to know or produce your FWID number and photo ID upon request of an officer."

- Conservation officers can open your [[FWID]] profile in the field (99%, page 5). That does not remove your duty to know or produce it (95%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** no paper hunting licence, so the wallet feels optional.
- **How common:** a Prince George area blitz reported charges for failing to produce hunting licences and LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) documents (60%, Prince George Citizen, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 5, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 3: The species licence must be in hand

```anim rb-mail-vs-counter Online: it comes by mail. In person: right away.
```

- **Rule:** a species licence bought online is mailed; in person you get it at once (99%). You must carry it to hunt (99%).
- **Slip:** buying the deer tag online the night before opening day.
- **Fix:** buy in person, or online weeks ahead.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Page 5: a species licence bought online "will be mailed" and is "subject to Canada Post delivery times"; bought in person, you get it immediately (99%).
- Not in the mail yet? You may qualify for a free replacement if you report it to FrontCounter BC or Service BC between 15 and 30 business days after purchase (99%, page 8).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** everything else in the system is instant online.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), pages 5 and 8, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 4: Some licences wait 2 days

```diagram rb-two-day-wait
```

- **Rule:** cougar, goat, sheep, caribou, bobcat, lynx and wolverine licences are not valid until 2 days after issue (99%).
- **Slip:** buying a cougar or bobcat licence on the day you hunt.
- **Fix:** buy it at least 2 days early. Deer, moose, elk and bear have no wait (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- The page 8 fee table marks those 7 species with a footnote: the licence is not valid until 2 days after the date of issue (99%).
- Owner note: Region 3 has cougar, bobcat and lynx seasons (99%, page 34). Region 8 cougar too (99%, page 68).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** deer, moose and bear licences work the day you buy them, so people expect the same of every licence.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 8, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 5: An LEH draw is not a licence

```diagram rb-licence-path
```

- **Rule:** an LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) authorization is not a licence. You still need the hunting licence and the species licence (99%).
- **Slip:** winning the draw and thinking that is all.
- **Fix:** draw, hunting licence, species licence: all three.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "A resident hunting licence and species licences are required in addition to an LEH authorization."

- In Regions 3 and 8, antlerless mule deer are LEH only (99%, pages 33 and 67).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the draw feels like the hard part, so the licences feel automatic.
- **How common:** a Vernon moose case included charges for hunting without an LEH authorization (60%, Global News, search preview). Tip: that case was about no draw at all, not missing licences.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 5, 2 October 2026 edition (99%). The Rule Book lists this as a common mistake.
```

## Rule 6: Never use or lend a licence

```diagram rb-unlawful-1
```

- **Rule:** never use another person's licence, and never lend or transfer yours, for any reason (99%).
- **Slip:** your brother tags the deer you shot because he still has a tag.
- **Fix:** the hunter who kills it cancels their own licence.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 2, page 11
> "To use another person's licence or to loan or transfer any licence under any circumstance."

> [!law] Page 8
> "All licences issued under the Wildlife Act are non-transferable and non-refundable."

- **Why it trips people (Tip):** partners think of the tags as the group's tags. The law treats each one as personal.
- **How common:** one BC case had four people facing 43 charges, including using another person's licence (60%, Castanet, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts) item 2, page 11; [licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 8, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 7: Ducks need the federal permit too

```diagram re-mb-permit
```

- **Rule:** for ducks, geese, coots, snipe, doves and band tailed pigeons, carry the federal MGBHP (Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit) with its stamp, plus your BC licence (99%).
- **Slip:** "I have my BC licence."
- **Fix:** buy the federal permit before the first duck hunt.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 7
> "When hunting migratory game birds (see Definitions section), you must carry with you a valid Canadian federal Migratory Game Bird Hunting (MGBH) Permit in addition to any required provincial hunting licences."

- The [[MGBHP]] needs the habitat conservation stamp on it (99%, Migratory Birds Regulations, 2022, s. 31). It expires on 30 June after the date of issue (99%, s. 33).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** two governments, two permits. Grouse need only the BC licence, ducks need both.
- **How common:** a waterfowl warden report for the Mississippi Flyway (14 US states and 3 Canadian provinces) lists hunting without the federal stamp at 242 citations a year on average (70%, Wisconsin Waterfowl Association, read directly). US data, not BC.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 7; [ducks and geese](#/s/rb-migratory-birds), s. 31 and 33 (99%).
```

## Rule 8: Licences die on 31 March

```diagram rb-inclusive
```

- **Rule:** all hunting and species licences expire 31 March. The licence year runs 1 April to 31 March (99%).
- **Slip:** hunting spring bear in April on last fall's licence.
- **Fix:** buy the new year's licence before any April hunt.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 8
> "All hunting and species licences expire March 31 of each year"

- Region 3 spring black bear runs 1 April to 30 June (99%, page 34): a new licence year.
- The federal duck permit runs to 30 June instead (99%, s. 33). Two different years (Tip).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** it feels like one season from fall to spring.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), pages 3 and 8, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 9: Mission needs a special licence

```diagram re-r2-fvsla
```

- **Rule:** all of MU (Management Unit) 2-4, and 2-8 inside Mission, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows and Coquitlam, need the Fraser Valley Special Area licence plus $1,000,000 liability insurance (99%).
- **Slip:** hunting Mission ducks on only the regular licence.
- **Fix:** buy the $10 special licence and carry the insurance.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Map B10, page 29
> "All persons hunting within MU 2-4 and those portions of MU 2-8 within the corporate limits of ... the district of Mission ... are required to purchase a Fraser Valley Special Area Hunting Licence in addition to other Provincial licences ... $1,000,000.00 Public Liability and Property Damage insurance is required."

- Fee $10.00 (99%, page 8). Electronic, on your FWID profile (99%, page 5).
- What proof of insurance to carry is not printed. Ask a conservation officer (VERIFY).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** it is printed on a map page, not with the other licences.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: Region 2](#/s/rb-region-2), pages 27 and 29; [licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 8 (99%).
```

## Rule 10: Lost a tag? Replace it before you hunt on

```diagram rb-carry
```

- **Rule:** a lost, stolen or destroyed species licence, cancelled or not, must be replaced if you keep hunting this licence year (99%).
- **Slip:** "I lost last month's used deer tag, it does not matter now."
- **Fix:** FrontCounter BC or Service BC, $10, declare the harvest if it was cancelled (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 8
> "You must replace your lost, stolen or destroyed species licence, both cancelled and uncancelled, if you plan to continue hunting during the current licence year."

- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a used tag feels finished. The law wants it with you all year (rule 1).
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 8, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram rb-carry
```

- **Pocket rhyme:** "ID, number, every tag, duck permit in the bag."
- **Number trick:** 2 days for cats, goats and sheep; 31 March for everything.
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "The tag you used is still a tag you carry."

```more more hooks, mistakes and field drill
> [!rule]
> Old hand's rule (wisdom, not law): buy tags at the counter, never the night before.

> [!mistake]
> - The used deer tag left on the fridge.
> - Hunting Mission ducks without the Fraser Valley Special Area licence.
> - Tagging the deer your partner shot.

> [!field]
> At the truck: photo ID, every species licence, the federal permit if birds are on the menu, your FWID number said out loud.
```

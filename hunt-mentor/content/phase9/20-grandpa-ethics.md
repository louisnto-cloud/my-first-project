## 1. Fair chase

```diagram fw-grandpa-fair-chase
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Give the animal a fair chance to get away."

- **Means:** hunt wild, free animals in an ethical, sporting and lawful way.
- **Label:** tradition. The Boone and Crockett Club's fair chase definition (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Boone and Crockett Club: fair chase is the "ethical, sportsmanlike, and lawful pursuit and taking" of free ranging wild game; an unfair advantage is when the game has no reasonable chance to escape; success is measured by the quality of the chase, not the quantity of game (85%, search preview).
- App: [Ethics and fair chase](#/s/ethics-fair-chase).
- **When it is wrong:** fair chase is an ethic, not the law. The law is the [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods).
- **Where it comes from:** the Boone and Crockett Club.
```

## 2. The shot you pass on

```diagram fw-grandpa-sure
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "The shot you pass on is the one you never have to explain."

- **Means:** a bad angle, too far, unsure: let it walk. Pride is not worth a wounded animal.
- **Label:** tradition (app). Hunter education: be certain of your target (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- This is the app's Grandpa's rule in [Ethics and fair chase](#/s/ethics-fair-chase); "The shot you pass is never the one you regret" ([Skills masterclass 7](#/s/mc-skills-07)).
- Hunter education's four rules include being certain of the target (85%, [lesson 1](#/s/fw-grandpa-01)).
- **When it is wrong:** never.
- **Where it comes from:** hunters' tradition.
```

## 3. Leave the gate as you found it

```diagram fw-grandpa-gates
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Leave every gate the way you found it."

- **Means:** closed stays closed, open stays open, unless the owner says otherwise.
- **Label:** tradition. Idaho Fish and Game lists it for hunters on private land (80%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Idaho Fish and Game: leave gates the way they are found, park where the landowner wants, stay away from livestock and buildings, know the boundaries (80%, search preview).
- GoHunt's ethics list: leave gates and fences as you found them unless the landowner says otherwise (70%).
- App: "Every fence has an owner and every gate has a reason" ([Land status maps](#/s/maps-land-status)).
- **When it is wrong:** if the owner asks you to do something else.
- **Where it comes from:** ranch country everywhere.
```

## 4. Ask first, get it in writing

| Ask the owner | Why |
|---|---|
| Which days? | Last year's yes may not stand |
| Which species? | Deer may be fine, birds not |
| Who may come? | Your brother needs a yes too |
| Where to park? | Gates, crops, livestock |

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Never assume last year's permission still stands."

- **Means:** ask each season, get it in writing, and confirm the details.
- **Label:** tradition. Idaho Fish and Game advises written permission (80%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Idaho Fish and Game: get written permission, preferably before the season; confirm days, species and who may come; never assume last year's permission still applies; leave a note with your name, phone and vehicle (80%, search preview).
- Thank the owner whether the answer is yes or no (80%).
- BC rules on private land and posted land: [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt).
- **Where it comes from:** landowner relations programs.
```

## 5. Pack it in, pack it out, leave it better

| Bring home | Leave behind |
|---|---|
| Wrappers, cans, brass | Nothing you brought |
| Flagging tape you hung (Tip) | Gates as you found them |
| Trash you find (Tip) | A cleaner spot |

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Leave it a little better than you found it."

- **Means:** carry out everything you carried in, plus a bit of someone else's trash.
- **Label:** tradition. Leave No Trace Canada principle 3 (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Leave No Trace Canada, principle 3, dispose of waste properly: bring back what you carried in; burning trash in a campfire is not acceptable; check campsites for micro waste (85%, search preview).
- Idaho Fish and Game: pick up shell casings; leaving them counts as littering under Idaho law (80%). BC littering rules: [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts).
- "Leave this world a little better than you found it" is from Robert Baden Powell's last message to Scouts, found after his death in 1941 (80%, Scouting sources, search preview).
- **When it is wrong:** never.
- **Where it comes from:** Scouting and Leave No Trace.
```

## 6. Waste nothing

```diagram lc-ml-apprentice
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "If you shoot it, you eat it."

- **Means:** take all the edible meat home and use it. Share it if you cannot use it all.
- **Label:** tradition. Hunter education calls it a legal and ethical duty (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- South Dakota hunter education: hunters are "legally and ethically obligated to harvest as much of the animal as reasonably possible" (85%, search preview).
- Idaho instructor code: never take more game than you can use (75%). Montana suggests donating meat you cannot use (70%).
- BC rules on wasting edible game: [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts).
- App: [Meat care](#/s/meat-care), [Cooking wild game](#/s/cooking).
- **Where it comes from:** hunters' tradition, written into law in many places.
```

## 7. Notch first, knife second

```anim sc-notch-order Notch, confirm, then the knife.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Notch first, knife second."

- **Means:** cancel your licence on the spot, before you touch the knife.
- **Label:** tradition (app). The tagging rule itself is law (see the Rule Book).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- This is the app's Grandpa's rule in [After the shot](#/s/after-the-shot) and [White tailed deer masterclass 10](#/s/mc-white-tailed-deer-10).
- The exact tagging and reporting rules: [Rule Book: after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill).
- **When it is wrong:** never.
- **Where it comes from:** BC hunters' habit, built on the licence rules.
```

## 8. Report it, do not confront it

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Note date, time, place | Confront the person |
| Note plates, gear, actions | Follow them |
| Call RAPP (Report All Poachers and Polluters) | Post it online first |

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "A poacher steals from every hunter. Report him, do not fight him."

- **Means:** write down the details and call the [[RAPP]] line.
- **Label:** opinion. BC's line: 1 877 952 7277 (95%, Rule Book).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- RAPP (Report All Poachers and Polluters) line: 1 877 952 7277, 24 hours (95%, synopsis page 2, via the app's Rule Book and [Predator sign](#/s/rs-predator-sign)).
- BC government: never confront or try to stop a suspect; record date, time, location, actions, gear, vehicle and witnesses (85%, gov.bc.ca page, search preview).
- If someone is in danger, call 911 first (85%).
- "Post it online first" in the table is the app's advice, not the government's (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** the BC Conservation Officer Service.
```

## 9. Take a new hunter out

| BC Wildlife Federation | What it is |
|---|---|
| Learn to Hunt orientation | Monthly, in person |
| Learn to Hunt Academy | 2 day course |
| Volunteer instructors | 5 years hunting, PAL |

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Somebody took you. Take somebody."

- **Means:** pass it on: mentor a new hunter, when you are ready.
- **Label:** tradition. Mentoring is the core of hunter recruitment programs (70%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- BC Wildlife Federation runs a monthly Learn to Hunt and Fish orientation and a 2 day Learn to Hunt Academy; volunteer instructors need at least 5 years of hunting and a valid PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) (80%, search preview).
- Recruitment programs (often called R3: recruit, retain, reactivate) find keeping new hunters is the hard part; peer mentors help (70%, NC State researcher, search preview).
- App: [Season planning](#/s/season-planning). Wait until you have a few seasons yourself (Tip).
- **When it is wrong:** take a new hunter only where you know the rules and the ground well (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** hunting families and clubs.
```

## 10. Your licence buys habitat

```diagram fw-grandpa-licence
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Hunters paid for the wild."

- **Means:** part of what you pay for licences goes straight to fish and wildlife habitat.
- **Label:** tradition. BC surcharges go to the Habitat Conservation Trust Foundation (80%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Surcharges on BC hunting, angling, trapping and guiding licences are dedicated to the Habitat Conservation Trust Foundation (HCTF); the fund began in 1981 after hunters and anglers asked for it (80%, Union of BC Municipalities and news reports, search preview).
- The HCTF says the surcharge funds over 100 fish, wildlife and habitat projects each year (75%).
- Exact surcharge amounts: [Rule Book: licences and fees](#/s/rb-licences-fees).
- **When it is wrong:** base licence fees go elsewhere; only the surcharge is dedicated (80%).
- **Where it comes from:** the North American conservation tradition.
```

## Myth check

```diagram fw-grandpa-myth-meter
```

| Saying | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Fair chase is the law" | Myth (85%) |
| "Last year's permission still counts" | Myth (80%) |
| "Licence money all goes to government" | Myth (80%) |

```more the evidence for each
- **Fair chase:** an ethic defined by the Boone and Crockett Club; the law is the synopsis and the Wildlife Act (85%). Ethics often ask more than the law.
- **Permission:** Idaho Fish and Game: never assume last year's permission still applies (80%).
- **Licence money:** BC conservation surcharges are dedicated to the Habitat Conservation Trust Foundation (80%).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram fw-grandpa-gates
```

- **Rhyme:** "Found it closed, leave it closed. Found it open, leave it open."
- **Rhyme:** "Notch first, knife second."
- **Number trick:** 1981: the year hunters and anglers got the conservation fund started (80%).

```more more hooks
- **Ethical, sporting, lawful:** the three legs of fair chase (85%).
- **RAPP** (Report All Poachers and Polluters): 1 877 952 7277 (95%).
- **Somebody took you. Take somebody.**

> [!mistake]
> - Assuming last year's yes still stands.
> - Leaving brass and flagging tape in the bush.
> - Confronting a poacher instead of reporting.

> [!field]
> Save the RAPP number in your phone today. Write a thank you note to a landowner after your next hunt.
```

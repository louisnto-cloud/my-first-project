## Rule 41: Deer and grouse: 1 hour each side of the sun

```anim rb-sunrise-hours Deer and grouse: 1 hour before sunrise. Ducks: 30 minutes.
```

- **Rule:** game other than migratory birds: from 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (99%).
- **Slip:** "It is still light enough" 75 minutes after sunset.
- **Fix:** look up sunrise and sunset for your spot; set a phone alarm.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 38 (first), page 11
> "The responsibility remains with the hunter to make the determination of sunrise and sunset in the field based on existing terrain and circumstances."

- Times: the [National Research Council sunrise and sunset calculator](https://nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/eng/services/sunrise/index.html) (99%), or the app's Field Mode legal light screen.
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** deer move most in the last light, and the clock is in the pocket.
- **How common:** see rule 42.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 38; [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods) (99%).
```

## Rule 42: Ducks and geese: only 30 minutes each side

```diagram rb-hours-bar
```

- **Rule:** migratory game birds: from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset (99%).
- **Slip:** shooting ducks at "deer time", 45 minutes before sunrise.
- **Fix:** on a mixed trip, use the duck clock for everything with feathers on water.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 37, page 11
> "To hunt migratory game birds from 1/2 hour after sunset to 1/2 hour before sunrise."

- Federal rule says the same (99%, Migratory Birds Regulations, 2022, s. 28(3)(b)).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** two clocks on one trip.
- **How common:** hunting before or after legal hours is the top waterfowl citation in a Mississippi Flyway warden tally (14 US states and 3 Canadian provinces): 416 a year on average (70%, Wisconsin Waterfowl Association, read directly). US flyway data.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 37; [ducks and geese](#/s/rb-migratory-birds), s. 28 (99%).
```

## Rule 43: No row in the table means no season

```diagram rb-inclusive
```

- **Rule:** open seasons are only those in the synopsis. No row for an animal in your MU (Management Unit) means no season (99%).
- **Slip:** a quail or elk shot in Region 3, where neither has a row (99%).
- **Fix:** find the row: species, MU, class, dates. No row, no shot.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 5
> "Open Seasons are only those defined in this synopsis. It is unlawful to hunt at any time during the year except within the open season, or by authority of a permit issued under the Wildlife Act."

- No row means no season (95%, my reading of page 5). Season dates are inclusive (99%).
- Region 3: no row for elk, quail, turkey (99%, page 34). Region 2: no white tailed deer row (99%, page 28).
- Each duck and goose species has its own season: an open duck day can be a closed goose day (99%, s. 28(1)).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** "grouse are open" turns into "birds are open".
- **How common:** hunting waterfowl in the closed season averages 230 citations a year in the Mississippi Flyway tally (70%, Wisconsin Waterfowl Association, read directly). US flyway data.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: licences](#/s/rb-licences-fees), page 5; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 34 (99%).
```

## Rule 44: No light to hunt by

```diagram fw-rules-05-leave-home
```

- **Rule:** no hunting with the use or aid of any light or illuminating device (99%).
- **Slip:** a truck spotlight swept over a field "just to look".
- **Fix:** lights for walking only, never to find, hold or shoot game (90%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 38 (second), page 11
> "To hunt wildlife by the use of, or with the aid of a light or illuminating device."

- Hunting includes searching for wildlife with a weapon (99%, page 3). Sweeping a light from a truck with a rifle aboard is searching (90%, my reading).
- A headlamp to walk in before light is not hunting with a light (90%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** spotting at night feels like scouting.
- **How common:** a Cariboo conservation officer described a high number of night hunting cases (60%, CBC News, search preview). A Williams Lake case alleged hunting with a light after sunset (60%, CTV News, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 38 (99%).
```

## Rule 45: Thermal optics stay home

```diagram rb-unlawful-2
```

- **Rule:** no using or possessing infrared optics, thermal scopes and monoculars, on a hunting trip (99%).
- **Slip:** the thermal monocular rides in the pack "only for tracking".
- **Fix:** leave it at home, not in the truck (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 39, page 11
> "To use or possess infrared optics on a hunting expedition."

- Infrared optics: any optic that can detect infrared radiation (99%, page 3).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** shops sell them as hunting gear.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 39; [definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 3 (99%).
```

## Rule 46: No drone on the trip

```diagram rb-unlawful-2
```

- **Rule:** no operating or possessing a drone on a hunting trip, and no using drone information from less than 6 hours before the trip starts (99%).
- **Slip:** flying the drone the evening before to find the bucks.
- **Fix:** leave the drone home on hunting days.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 36, page 11
> "To operate or posses a drone while on a hunting or trapping expedition, or to use information that a drone obtains during, or less than 6 hours before the start of, a hunting or trapping expedition."

- The synopsis prints "posses" for possess (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** "I only flew it, I did not hunt with it."
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). News reports describe the ban and its fines, not how often it is broken (60%, Times Colonist, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 36 (99%).
```

## Rule 47: Cell trail cameras: not 1 August to 10 December

```diagram fw-rules-05-leave-home
```

- **Rule:** no using a wireless camera, one that sends pictures to your phone, for hunting from 1 August to 10 December (99%).
- **Slip:** the cell camera on the deer trail in October.
- **Fix:** in season, use a camera that only stores to its card (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 30, page 11
> "To use a wireless camera for the purposes of hunting from Aug. 1 to Dec. 10, except in region 1 (see page 20 for further details)."

- [[Wireless camera]]: obtains an image by remote or infrared sensor and can send it remotely (99%, page 5).
- Region 1 has its own stricter rule (95%, my reading of page 20).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the camera is legal to own and legal in spring.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 30 (99%).
```

## Rule 48: No electronic calls for deer, ducks or grouse

```diagram rb-unlawful-2
```

- **Rule:** no recorded or electronic calls except for wolf, coyote, cougar, lynx, bobcat and Schedule C birds. No live decoys or recorded calls for game birds (99%).
- **Slip:** a phone app call for geese.
- **Fix:** mouth and hand calls only (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Items 41 and 42, page 12
> "To use recorded or electronic calls to hunt wildlife except wolf, coyote, cougar, lynx, or bobcat or any bird listed in Schedule C (see "What is Wildlife?" Section)." "To use live birds as decoys or recorded bird calls to hunt game birds."

- BC has no federal special measures table, so no electronic calls for snow geese either (95%, my reading, Rule Book duck session).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** other provinces and states allow them for some birds.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), items 41 and 42 (99%).
```

## Rule 49: Never bait or feed bears

```diagram rb-unlawful-1
```

- **Rule:** no hunting bears over bait or over a dead animal, and no feeding bears, cougars, coyotes or wolves (99%).
- **Slip:** sitting over last week's gut pile for a bear.
- **Fix:** hunt bears by glassing and stalking, never over food.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 21, page 11
> "To hunt bears by placing bait or by using a dead animal or using part of it as bait."

- Item 20: no feeding dangerous wildlife, except where baiting is authorized for cougar, coyote and wolf, never bear (99%).
- Sitting over your own deer carcass counts as using a dead animal as bait (95%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** baiting bears is legal in some other provinces.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), items 20 and 21 (99%).
```

## Rule 50: No feeding deer near homes in Regions 3 and 8

```diagram re-r3-special
```

- **Rule:** in Regions 3 and 8, no intentional baiting or feeding of ungulates within 200 m (219 yd) of a dwelling, school yard or playground (99%).
- **Slip:** apples out for deer behind the house at Heffley Creek.
- **Fix:** no food out for deer, elk or moose near any home. New this edition (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 33
> "To intentionally bait or feed ungulates within 200m of a dwelling, school yard, or playground in region 3."

- Region 8 prints the same rule (99%, page 67). Region 2 does not (99%).
- [[Bait]] includes grain, crops and fake foods; a decoy is not bait (99%, page 3).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** feeding deer feels kind, and it was legal last edition.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). New rule, no count yet.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 33; [Region 8](#/s/rb-region-8), page 67 (99%).
```

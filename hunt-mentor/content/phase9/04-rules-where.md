## Rule 31: 100 m from houses, even with permission

```diagram rb-100m-ring
```

- **Rule:** no hunting or shooting within 100 m (109 yd) of a dwelling, an occupied farm or ranch building, a church, a school, a playground or a regional district park (99%).
- **Slip:** "The farmer said go ahead," 60 m from his house.
- **Fix:** pace 100 m from any occupied building before you load.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 10
> "It is unlawful to hunt or discharge a firearm within 100 m of a church, school building, school yard, playground, regional district park, dwelling house, or farm or ranch building that is occupied by persons or domestic animals."

- The only exemption is the owner, occupier or employee slaughtering livestock (99%). Permission to hunt is not an exemption (95%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the owner's yes feels like it covers everything.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 10 (99%).
```

## Rule 32: Crop fields and grazing leases need a yes

```diagram fw-rules-04-ask
```

- **Rule:** no hunting cultivated land, or a Crown grazing lease with livestock on it, without the owner's, lessee's or occupant's consent (99%).
- **Slip:** an empty lease pasture, so "no cows, no problem".
- **Fix:** ask first. Leased land is under the Trespass Act even with no cattle (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "Note: leased land is subject to the Trespass Act, regardless of whether livestock are present."

- Same rule as item 53 on page 12 (99%).
- Okanagan orchards and vineyards are [[Cultivated land]]: get consent (90%, my reading).
- Region 3 asks you to leave gates and fences as you find them (99%, page 33).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the old rule only mentioned livestock; the leased land note is new this edition (99%).
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9; [it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 53 (99%).
```

## Rule 33: Fenced or signed land is closed

```diagram rb-enclosed
```

- **Rule:** private or leased land is enclosed if it has signs at each normal way in, or a lawful fence all round, or a natural boundary all round (99%).
- **Slip:** "There was only one sign, at the far gate."
- **Fix:** any one of the three means ask the owner first (90%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Trespass Act, page 9
> "Private or leased property is considered enclosed if any one of the following conditions are met: there are clearly visible signs prohibiting trespassing posted at each ordinary access point; or the property is surrounded by a lawful fence; or the property is surrounded by a natural boundary such as a riverbank or a 4 1/2 foot hedge."

- [[Enclosed land]] is closed to you without permission (90%, my reading).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a fence with a gap, or a missing sign, looks like an invitation.
- **How common:** a Midway RCMP report reminded hunters that hunting private land without permission breaks the Wildlife Act, and crossing a marked fence breaks the Trespass Act (60%, Boundary Creek Times, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9 (99%).
```

## Rule 34: Reserves are private land

```diagram fw-rules-04-ask
```

- **Rule:** Indian Reserves are private land. Get permission from the band office to hunt on them or even to cross them (99%).
- **Slip:** cutting across reserve land to reach Crown land.
- **Fix:** call the band office first, or go around.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "Indian Reserves are private land. Permission must be obtained from the local Indian business office in order to hunt on or across these lands."

- Kamloops Map C7 marks I.R. 1 (Indian Reserve 1), and Map J10 marks I.R. 1 near Kaleden (99%, Region 3 and Region 8 sessions).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** reserve edges are not always fenced or signed.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9 (99%).
```

## Rule 35: A trespass kill is an illegal kill

```diagram rb-can-i-shoot-here
```

- **Rule:** killing wildlife while breaking any other law, trespass included, is its own offence (99%).
- **Slip:** a legal buck, legal season, on land you had no right to be on.
- **Fix:** know whose land you stand on before the shot.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 19, page 11
> "To kill wildlife while in the process of committing offences against any statute including, but not limited to, the Wildlife Act. Examples would be wildlife taken while trespassing on private property or on mine property in violation of the Mine Safety Code."

- **Why it trips people (Tip):** they check the season and the tag, not the land.
- **How common:** a Williams Lake case involved alleged trespass on private property along with night hunting (60%, CTV News, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 19 (99%).
```

## Rule 36: BC Parks: check first, gun stays in the truck

```diagram rb-park-road
```

- **Rule:** before hunting a BC Park, confirm its own page allows hunting. If closed, a firearm or bow may only be in a vehicle (99%).
- **Slip:** walking a park trail with the shotgun because grouse are "always open".
- **Fix:** read the park page. No hunting within 400 m (437 yd) of a park road (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "When a BC Park is closed to hunting or there is no open season for any species, both the possession and discharge of a firearm or bow are prohibited except when authorized by a park officer. Possession is only allowed when such weapons are carried within a vehicle or when authorized by a park officer."

- Parks, conservancies, recreation areas and protected areas all count (99%).
- Wells Gray Park, MU 3-45: no hunting, no firearm discharge (99%, page 33).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** some parks allow hunting, so people assume all do.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 33 (99%).
```

## Rule 37: No shooting in a recreation site

```diagram rb-rec-site
```

- **Rule:** no shooting a firearm, bow or crossbow on or into the developed part of a recreation site: 100 m (109 yd) from any structure, campsite or staging area (99%).
- **Slip:** a grouse shot from the campsite picnic table.
- **Fix:** walk 100 m clear first. Never leave guts or scraps there (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "It is unlawful to dispose of game residue in the developed portion of a recreation site."

- On a recreation trail, the shooting ban applies only where posted (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** rec sites sit in the middle of good country.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9 (99%).
```

## Rule 38: No Shooting is not No Hunting

```diagram rb-closed-places
```

- **Rule:** a No Shooting Area bans firearms; bows are allowed unless restricted. A No Hunting Area bans hunting by any method (99%).
- **Slip:** a shotgun on the South Thompson flats inside the Kamloops Map C7 band.
- **Fix:** learn the shaded maps for your MU (Management Unit).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 10
> "No Shooting Areas are open to the use of bows (including crossbows) unless specifically restricted (see regional sections)."

- [[No Shooting Area]] and [[No Hunting Area]] are defined on page 4 (99%).
- Near your bases: Map C7 Kamloops, Map C11 Sun Peaks (400 m), Map C12 McQueen Lake (1 km), all touching MUs 3-27 or 3-28 (99%, page 35). Okanagan: Maps J10, J11, J17 (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the two names sound the same.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 10; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3); [Region 8](#/s/rb-region-8) (99%).
```

## Rule 39: Towns and regional parks have their own rules

```diagram rb-closed-places
```

- **Rule:** in Regions 1 and 2, no hunting or shooting within 100 m of any regional district park. Inside towns, the local bylaw decides (99%).
- **Slip:** hunting the edge of a Mission area park.
- **Fix:** check the park line and the town bylaw before the hunt.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "Hunting and the discharge of firearms is prohibited within 100 m of all Regional District Parks in Regions 1 and 2."

> [!law] Page 9
> "Municipal bylaws are NOT included in this synopsis. Consult municipal clerks for details."

- Inside the City of Kamloops line, no firearms or bows on Lac du Bois or Crown land (99%, page 33).
- Mission's own firearm bylaw is not in the synopsis (99%). It is the [District of Mission Discharge of Firearms Regulation Bylaw 5433-2014](https://www.mission.ca/media/file/discharge-firearms-regulation-bylaw-5433-2014pdf): no firing anywhere in the District except as it allows (s. 4). Hunters with a BC hunting licence may fire, in season, only a shotgun with shot in Area B, a shotgun with shot or slug in Area C, and a rifle or shotgun in Area D of its Schedule A map (s. 11). Never on or across a road, which here includes forestry roads, or within 150 m of a school, park, church, home, farm building, workplace or road (s. 3 and s. 7) (85%, District copy read 2026-10-17; later amendments not checked).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the synopsis cannot print every town's bylaw.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9; [Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), page 33 (99%).
```

## Rule 40: Federal bird lands are closed

```diagram rb-closed-places
```

- **Rule:** National Wildlife Areas and Migratory Bird Sanctuaries are closed to hunting unless a federal permit or posted notice allows it. Blue loon signs mark the line (99%).
- **Slip:** following ducks onto the Vaseux Lake sanctuary.
- **Fix:** learn the blue loon sign; stop at it.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 9
> "Hunting is prohibited in National Wildlife Areas and Migratory Bird Sanctuaries unless a special federal permit is granted or notices to the contrary are posted. Blue signs depicting a loon mark the boundaries."

- Vaseux Lake, between Oliver and Okanagan Falls: no hunting in the sanctuary or the National Wildlife Areas (99% rule, 90% place reading, Region 8 session). Exact line: VERIFY.
- Ecological reserves are closed to hunting and to any firearm or bow too (99%, page 9).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** marsh edges blur, and birds do not read signs.
- **How common:** a US waterfowl writer lists site specific rules on refuges and wildlife areas among the top 5 waterfowl violations (60%, Split Reed, 2020, read directly). US source.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9; [Region 8](#/s/rb-region-8) (99%).
```

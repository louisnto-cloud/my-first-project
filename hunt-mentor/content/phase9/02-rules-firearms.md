## Rule 11: A hunting licence is not a firearms licence

```diagram rb-carry
```

- **Rule:** a BC hunting licence gives no authority to carry a firearm (99%).
- **Slip:** thinking CORE (Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education) plus a hunting licence covers the rifle.
- **Fix:** hold the federal firearms licence too, the PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Federal Firearms Legislation, page 10
> "A BC hunting licence does not give authority to carry a firearm."

- The federal licence is the [[PAL]] (95%, my reading). The synopsis names the Canadian Firearm Centre, 1 800 731 4000 (99%, page 10).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** CORE teaches firearm handling, so it feels like a gun licence. It is not.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 10, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 12: A clipped in magazine means loaded

```diagram rb-loaded-firearm
```

- **Rule:** rounds in the chamber, a fixed magazine, a hinged floor plate, or a detachable magazine clipped in: any one means [[Loaded]] (99%).
- **Slip:** "Chamber is empty, so it is unloaded."
- **Fix:** magazine out, chamber empty, then look again.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Definition, page 3
> "A firearm is loaded even if the firearm is temporarily incapable of being discharged."

- Safety on, bolt up, action open: still loaded if the clipped in magazine holds rounds (99%).
- A loaded magazine in your pocket, out of the gun, is not a loaded firearm (95%, my reading).
- Your Tikka has a detachable magazine: pull it before the truck (Tip).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** everyday talk calls a rifle loaded only with one in the chamber.
- **How common:** see rule 13.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions), page 3, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 13: No loaded firearm in or on a vehicle

```anim rb-loaded-truck Magazine out, chamber empty, then into the truck.
```

- **Rule:** no live round in the chamber or attached magazine in or on a truck, ATV (All Terrain Vehicle), bicycle, sleigh or other conveyance (99%).
- **Slip:** riding the quad with the magazine still clipped in.
- **Fix:** unload fully before you climb on anything with wheels.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 28, page 11
> "To discharge, carry or have in possession a firearm containing live ammunition in its breech or in its magazine attached to the firearm, in or on a railway car, motor vehicle, sleigh, aircraft, bicycle or other conveyance."

- Related: no cocked crossbow in or on a vehicle, and no bow shot from one (99%, item 29).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a deer on the road edge, and the gun is ready "just in case".
- **How common:** at a late October 2023 Boston Bar road check of over 400 hunters and anglers, conservation officers wrote 3 tickets for loaded firearms in vehicles (70%, Q101 News, read directly). A Highway 1 road check listed it among the violations (60%, KelownaNow, search preview).
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), item 28, page 11, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 14: Boats: loaded only when the motor is not pushing

```diagram rb-boat-rules
```

- **Rule:** since 1 October 2026 a loaded firearm in or on a boat is banned. The exemption covers hunting from a boat with no motor, or one its motor is not propelling (99%).
- **Slip:** loading up while idling to the decoys.
- **Fix:** load only with the motor off. Your kayak, with no motor, fits the exemption (95%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Firearm Violence Prevention Act, section 4(1), in force 1 October 2026 (99%). Exemption: Designation and Exemption Regulation, section 18 (99%). Both as read in the Rule Book.
- Holding the boat still on the motor may not qualify for the loaded exemption (85%, my reading). Ask a conservation officer (VERIFY).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** it is new this season, and the old power boat definition with its tilted motor exception is gone (99%, B.C. Reg. 66/2026, as read in the Rule Book).
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). New rule, no count yet.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts), items 23, 24 and 28 (99%).
```

## Rule 15: The plug is not just for ducks

```diagram rb-plug
```

- **Rule:** to hunt with a pump or semi auto shotgun, the magazine needs a plug so it holds no more than 2 shells (99%).
- **Slip:** pulling the plug for grouse or quail.
- **Fix:** plug in all season. Only slugs for big game may skip it (99%).

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 12
> "It is unlawful to hunt or trap with a set gun or with a pump, repeating or auto loading shotgun unless the magazine contains a plug that is incapable of holding more than 2 cartridges."

- Ducks add the federal wording: 3 shells in total, plug removable only by taking the gun apart (99%, s. 37(1)(c)). See rule 62.
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** most hunters learn the plug as a duck rule.
- **How common:** waterfowl wardens in Wisconsin average 44 citations a year for a gun holding more than 3 shells (70%, Wisconsin Waterfowl Association, read directly). US data, ducks only.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), page 12, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 16: No full metal jacket bullets

```diagram rb-deer-gun-flow
```

- **Rule:** no hunting with full metal jacket non expanding bullets, or tracer, incendiary or explosive bullets (99%).
- **Slip:** range ammo in the Tikka on a deer trip.
- **Fix:** hunting bullets only in the hunting pack. Range boxes stay home.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Page 12
> "It is unlawful to hunt or trap with a rifle using a full metal jacketed non-expanding bullet, or a tracer, incendiary, or explosive bullet."

- No tracer or incendiary shotshells either (99%, page 12).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** range ammo is cheaper and the boxes look alike.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), page 12, 2 October 2026 edition (99%).
```

## Rule 17: No handgun, not even to finish

```diagram rb-methods-grid
```

- **Rule:** no hunting with a handgun, and no long gun with a barrel under 305 mm (12 in) (99%).
- **Slip:** a handgun on the belt to finish a wounded animal.
- **Fix:** finish it with your legal rifle or shotgun.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Item 46, page 12
> "To hunt wildlife with a handgun."

- Page 12 also bans a firearm aimed and fired with one hand, or with a barrel under 305 mm (99%).
- Finishing a wounded animal counts as hunting (95%, my reading of the page 3 hunting definition).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** films and other places allow it.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts) item 46; [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), page 12 (99%).
```

## Rule 18: Deer with a shotgun: buckshot or slugs only

```diagram fw-rules-02-guns
```

- **Rule:** for deer, a 20 gauge or larger with No. 1 Buck or larger shot, or slugs (99%).
- **Slip:** a grouse load in the 12 gauge when a deer steps out.
- **Fix:** no buckshot or slug in the pocket, no deer shot.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Note 1, page 13
> "Shotgun must have a bore size of 20 gauge or larger and use shells of shot size No. 1 Buck or larger."

- Page 12 allows single projectiles (slugs) where shotguns are allowed for big game (99%).
- Moose and elk: no shotgun at all (99%, page 13).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** a deer at 20 m with bird shot looks easy. It is illegal and cruel.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), pages 12 and 13 (99%).
```

## Rule 19: The .22 is for grouse, not quail or deer

```diagram rb-methods-grid
```

- **Rule:** a rimfire is legal for grouse, ptarmigan and turkey only among birds, and never for deer. A centrefire is legal for grouse and ptarmigan only (99%).
- **Slip:** the CZ .22 on an Okanagan quail covey.
- **Fix:** quail, chukar, pheasant, partridge: shotgun, air gun or bow.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] Notes 2 and 3, page 13
> "2. Grouse, ptarmigan, or turkey only." "3. Grouse or ptarmigan only."

- Quail, pheasant and partridge: shotgun, air rifle or bow (95%, my reading of notes 2 and 3).
- Ducks and geese: shotgun or a class D bow only (99%).
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** "it is a bird, the .22 works on grouse."
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), page 13 (99%).
```

## Rule 20: National Parks: gun cased, gun in the vehicle

```diagram rb-closed-places
```

- **Rule:** no hunting in any National Park. Passing through, the firearm is dismantled, cased, or wrapped and tied, and it stays in the vehicle (99%).
- **Slip:** an uncased rifle on the back seat through a park.
- **Fix:** case it before the park gate.

```more why, how common, and the source
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law] National Parks, page 9
> "All firearms transported in National Parks must be dismantled, carried in a closed case, or wrapped and tied securely so as not to expose any part of the firearm."

- Out of the vehicle only to move it into or out of your premises, or with a superintendent's permit (99%).
- BC Parks have their own rule: rule 36.
- **Why it trips people (Tip):** the highway runs through, so it feels like any other road.
- **How common:** Tip (opinion). No count found.
- **Source:** [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt), page 9 (99%).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram fw-rules-02-guns
```

- **Rhyme:** "Mag out, chamber clean, then the wheels."
- **Number trick:** 2 in the tube, 1 in the chamber, 3 in all.
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Range ammo lives at the range."

```more mistakes and field drill
> [!rule]
> Old hand's rule (wisdom, not law): one bag per hunt. Deer bag, bird bag, duck bag. Never mix the shells.

> [!mistake]
> - Calling the rifle unloaded with a full magazine clipped in.
> - Pulling the plug for upland birds.
> - The .22 on quail.

> [!field]
> Before you drive: magazine out, chamber checked by eye and finger, plug in, right shells in the right bag.
```

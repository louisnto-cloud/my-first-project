## 1. Stand ten times longer than you walk

```diagram fw-grandpa-ten-to-one
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Still hunting is mostly standing still."

- **Means:** a few steps, then a long stop to look and listen.
- **Label:** tradition. Hunter education says at least 10 times longer still than walking (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** a moving hunter is easy to spot; a still one finds the animal first (Tip).
- Hunter education: "spend at least 10 times longer being still and observing than walking" (85%, Iowa course on the Hunter Ed site, search preview). National Rifle Association Women: "maybe 10 times as much stillness as movement" (70%).
- See [[Still hunting]] and [Moving quietly](#/s/still-hunting).
- **When it is wrong:** when you are crossing dead ground to a glassing point, move steadily and quietly (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** hunter education and old timber hunters.
```

## 2. Slow? Halve it

```anim hf-step-cadence A few steps, a long pause, a few steps.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "If you think you are going slow enough, halve it."

- **Means:** your slow is still too fast.
- **Label:** tradition. Guides range from 3 to 5 m between stops to one step every 30 seconds (70%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- New South Wales hunter education: walk 3 to 5 m, then stop and look into the timber (75%, search preview).
- Buckmasters: go slow, "maybe one step every 30 seconds", and look (65%, search preview).
- This is the app's Grandpa's rule in [Moving quietly](#/s/still-hunting).
- **When it is wrong:** short days and long walks out. Leave time to get back before dark (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** still hunters everywhere.
```

## 3. Head down, you move

```anim mc-skills-freeze Head down: move. Head up: freeze.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "Head down, you move. Head up, you are a stump."

- **Means:** close the distance only while the animal feeds with its head down.
- **Label:** opinion. Movement gives hunters away more than colour (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** deer pick up movement; the National Deer Association says scent and movement matter far more than clothing colour (85%, read directly).
- The app's line in [Black bear masterclass 3](#/s/mc-black-bear-03).
- **When it is wrong:** a deer can lift its head fast with no warning. Move in short bursts, each one ending where you can freeze in a natural pose (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** stalking advice in many hunting books and the app.
```

## 4. Stay off the skyline

```anim fw-grandpa-skyline Walk below the crest. Peek, do not parade.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Never walk the top of the ridge."

- **Means:** travel just below the crest, and creep up to peek over.
- **Label:** tradition. A human outline spooks game (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Hunter education: "keep a low profile; a human silhouette will spook many game species" (85%, New South Wales course, search preview).
- Hunting writers: approach ridges from below, stop short of the crest and glass; even a small rise can skyline you (70%, Outdoor Life, MeatEater, search preview).
- Military writers call the line just below the top the "military crest" (60%).
- **When it is wrong:** in thick fog or deep timber nobody can see your outline (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** mountain and plains hunters.
```

## 5. When it stares, you are a stump

| The deer does | You do |
|---|---|
| Stares at you | Freeze, eyes down |
| Drops its head to feed | Move a few steps |
| Stamps or blows | It has you. Wait, do not rush |

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "When it stares, you are a stump."

- **Means:** a staring animal is not sure. Do not move until it looks away.
- **Label:** opinion, from the app's deer lessons (Tip).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** movement is what deer catch (85%, National Deer Association). Stillness keeps the question unanswered.
- App: [Mule deer masterclass 3](#/s/mc-mule-deer-03) and the Grandpa's rule in [Deer senses and mind](#/s/deer-mind): "When a deer stamps, you've already lost the eyes. Freeze."
- **When it is wrong:** if the wind is carrying your scent to it, freezing will not save you (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** deer hunters' lore, the app.
```

## 6. Let the animal do the walking

```anim rl-pp-hourglass Sit where the trails squeeze together.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "Let the animal do the walking."

- **Means:** sit on a trail, a pinch or an edge, and let the animal come to you.
- **Label:** opinion. Most Penn State hunters sat still in the morning (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Evidence:** 72% of hunters were stationary in the morning in the Penn State public land study (85%, app research notes).
- App: [Skills masterclass 5](#/s/mc-skills-05) and "Don't hunt where the animals are. Hunt where they have to go" ([Pinch points](#/s/pinch-points)).
- **When it is wrong:** in open mule deer country, glassing and stalking often beat waiting (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** stand hunters everywhere.
```

## 7. Do not burn your best stand

```diagram fw-grandpa-stand-days
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (opinion)
> "The best stand is the one the deer do not know you use."

- **Means:** every sit leaves scent and noise. Save your best spot for the best wind and days.
- **Label:** opinion. One study: bucks avoided a stand for about 3 days after one sit (60%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Evidence:** in a South Carolina study, 37 GPS (Global Positioning System) collared bucks stayed out of a stand's zone for about 3 days after a hunter sat there once, shot or not (60%, Auburn University study reported by Outdoor Life, search preview).
- App: [White tailed deer masterclass 9](#/s/mc-white-tailed-deer-09).
- **When it is wrong:** the rut, when bucks travel and may walk past anyway (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** white tail hunters and one collar study.
```

## 8. Sit longer than you think

```anim hf-glass-sweep Sweep the slope in strips. Then sweep it again.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "When you want to move, glass it one more time."

- **Means:** animals appear late. Give the spot more time than feels right.
- **Label:** tradition. The Mule Deer Foundation: look longer, look smarter (80%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Mule Deer Foundation: "look longer, look smarter, and let the deer reveal themselves" (80%, search preview).
- App versions: "Sit longer than you think" ([Glassing](#/s/glassing)), "When you think you have waited long enough, wait 20 minutes more" ([Elk masterclass 9](#/s/mc-elk-09)), and a one hour glassing rule ([Skills masterclass 1](#/s/mc-skills-01)).
- **When it is wrong:** if the wind is wrong for that spot, waiting only spreads scent (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** western glassers.
```

## 9. Know where your partner is

```diagram mgs-partner-zones
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Know where your partner is before you know where the deer is."

- **Means:** agree on zones of fire, routes and meeting points before you split up.
- **Label:** tradition. Zones of fire are taught in hunter education (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- **Why it works:** each hunter shoots only into a zone agreed in advance (85%, hunter education).
- This is the app's Grandpa's rule in [Hunting with a partner](#/s/hunting-partner).
- **When it is wrong:** never.
- **Where it comes from:** hunter education.
```

## 10. Eyes ahead, not on your boots

```anim rs-tf-lookahead Glance at the track, then look far ahead.
```

> [!rule] Grandpa's rule (tradition)
> "Look into the woods, not at your feet."

- **Means:** each pause, scan ahead and to the sides. The animal is out there, not under your boots.
- **Label:** tradition. Hunter education says the same (85%).

```more why, when it is wrong, where it comes from
- Hunter education: take a few steps forward while looking into the forest, not down at the ground (85%, Iowa course on the Hunter Ed site, search preview).
- App: "Look up from the track" ([Mule deer masterclass 8](#/s/mc-mule-deer-08)) and "The tracks are behind the animal. Your eyes should be in front of it" ([Following a track](#/s/rs-track-following)).
- **When it is wrong:** steep or icy ground, where a quick look at your footing keeps you safe (Tip).
- **Where it comes from:** still hunters and trackers.
```

## Myth check

```diagram fw-grandpa-myth-meter
```

| Saying | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Good camo makes you invisible" | Myth (85%) |
| "Cover more ground, see more deer" | Myth for still hunting (85%) |
| "Sitting a stand does not spook deer" | Mixed (60%) |

```more the evidence for each
- **Camo:** scent and movement matter far more than colour (85%, National Deer Association). App: "Camo hides you when you are still" ([Stay unseen](#/s/stay-unseen)).
- **Cover ground:** hunter education says 10 times more still than moving (85%).
- **Stand pressure:** one collar study saw bucks avoid a stand area about 3 days after a sit (60%, secondary report).
```

## Memory hooks

```anim fw-grandpa-skyline Below the crest, then peek.
```

- **Rhyme:** "Head down, I go. Head up, I'm a stump."
- **Number trick:** 1 to 10: walk 1, stand 10 (85%).
- **Number trick:** 3: days bucks avoided a stand after one sit in one study (60%).

```more more hooks
- **Halve it:** if it feels slow enough, halve it.
- **Peek, do not parade** on a ridge.
- **One more sweep** before you stand up.

> [!mistake]
> - Walking at a hiking pace while still hunting.
> - Standing up on the ridge top to look around.
> - Sitting the best stand every day with any wind.

> [!field]
> Next still hunt: count 5 steps, then stand for a slow count of 60. Do it for a full hour.
```

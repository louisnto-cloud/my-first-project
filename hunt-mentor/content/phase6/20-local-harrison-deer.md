## Bucks only: what counts

```diagram mk-hd-buck
```

- A buck is a deer "bearing visible bony antlers" (99%, page 4). These rows say Bucks, not 4 point bucks (99%, page 28).
- In these MUs the antlerless 1 is out of reach: the only rows are bucks (99%, page 28).

```regs deerR2
```

```more the official wording
> [!law] Regional Bag Limits, page 27
> "Deer: Unless otherwise indicated, the bag limit for mule (black-tailed) deer is 2, one of which may be antlerless."

> [!law] Hours, page 11, item 38
> No hunting game (except migratory birds) from 1 hour after sunset to 1 hour before sunrise.

- The page 28 row reads "MULE DEER (black tailed)", MUs "2-2 to 2-4, 2-6 to 2-8, 2-13 to 2-15, 2-17 to 2-19", class "Bucks", "Sept 10 to Dec 15" (99%).
- Province wide, you may take at most 3 deer a licence year (99%, page 14).
- The app works out sunrise and sunset for your spot on the plan and on Home (Tip: check it the night before).
```

## Licence and tag

```diagram mk-hd-tag
```

- Each hunter: a resident hunting licence ($32.00) plus a deer [[Species licence|species licence]] ($15.00) (99%, page 8).
- Kill first, then cancel the licence, then touch the deer (99%, page 11).
- The shooter cancels their own licence, never a partner's (99%).

```more licence detail
- You may hold up to 3 mule deer species licences in a licence year (99%, page 7). A black tailed deer uses the mule deer licence (99%, page 8 fee table: "Deer (mule, black tailed included, and white tailed)").
- Fraser Valley Special Area licence: only inside Mission, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows and Coquitlam limits in 2-8, and all of 2-4 (99%, Map B10, page 29). Harrison, the Chehalis and the east side are outside it (90%, my reading of Map B10).
- Legal firearm for deer: a centrefire rifle (no .22), among others (99%, page 13).
- Compulsory inspection in Region 2 is for mountain goat and cougar, not deer (99%, page 28). No head or inspection trip for a buck.
- Full detail: [Bag limits and tags](#/s/rb-bag-limits/0).
```

## Black tailed deer or mule deer?

```gallery
g-bt-doe | Black tailed doe: the whole top of the tail is dark.
g-md-buck-rear | Mule deer: thin white tail with a black tip.
```

- Same species, same licence: the synopsis lists them as "mule (black-tailed) deer" (99%, page 27).
- Black tailed: wider tail, dark down the whole top (Tip).
- Antlers fork like a mule deer's (99%, page 4).

```more ID detail
- Black tailed deer are the coast deer, the ones around Mission and Harrison (85%, BC Ministry of Environment).
- A white tail has a wide brown tail that flags up white (85%). There is no white tailed deer season in Region 2, and some live near Hope at Herrling Island (99%, page 27). Be sure before you shoot.
- More photos: [Deer gallery](#/s/gallery-deer/0).
```

## Where they are in November

```diagram mk-hd-november
```

- November into early December is the black tailed rut (80%). Bucks move more by day (Tip).
- Cutblock edges, old roads, salal and alder (90%, Region 2 Rule Book strategy).
- Snow over about 30 cm: deer move under old timber crowns, or to low south facing slopes (80 to 85%, Wet forests lesson).

```more where detail
- Coast black tailed deer use logged ground often in spring and summer (80%, BC Ministry of Environment).
- Winter foods: Douglas fir, western redcedar, red huckleberry, salal, deer fern and tree lichens (85%).
- Timber deer: still hunt slowly with short shots, not long glassing (Tip).
- Spots: open the [Hunt Map](#/map), filter deer, and use the Good for 2 and Good solo tags (Tip). Every spot is a candidate: scout it first.
```

## With your brother or solo

```diagram zones-of-fire
```

- Pair: one sits at a crossing or glasses, the other walks the edge slowly toward the stander, wind at the walker's back (Tip).
- Pair: agree zones of fire, a meeting point and a clock time (Tip).
- Solo: pick a short pack out, 1 km or less and 100 m climb or less (Tip).

```more pair and solo detail
- Pushes are on foot only (99%, page 11, item 25).
- BC has no blaze orange law. Wear it anyway with a partner (95% for the law, Tip for the advice).
- Solo: leave a trip plan with someone at home, with your truck, the road and the time you will be out (75%, AdventureSmart).
- Solo: a buck on the ground alone is a lot of work. Quarter it if the pack out is long (Tip).
- Full lessons: [Hunting with a partner](#/s/hunting-partner/0), [Packing out](#/s/packing-out/0).
```

## Logging roads and the closures here

```steps The radio call in 4 parts
fl-fsr-call-1 | Road name, as on the sign.
fl-fsr-call-2 | The kilometre you are at.
fl-fsr-call-3 | Up or down.
fl-fsr-call-4 | Anything else the sign asks.
```

- Sts'ailes (West) [[FSR]] (Forest Service Road), locally Harrison West, is run by the Chilliwack district (95%, BC government).
- Use the radio channel on the road entry sign. The app gives no channel numbers (95%).
- Active hauling: the district page posts no hauling schedule. VERIFY at the road sign or the district office.

```more road notices and no shooting bands
- Chilliwack district notices, page last updated 21 October 2026 (95%, BC government road safety page): Harrison East FSR, rockfall hazard "No Stopping" signs near 13 km, caution at 2, 13, 16.1 and 21.3 to 22.8 km, no access past 55 km through to the Kookipi FSR. Francis Lake FSR narrow at about 1.3 km (12 May 2026). Mystery 5500 FSR no access past 0 km, washout at 2.0 km. Hemlock Valley FSR no access at 0 km, four washed out culverts. Fleetwood FSR no vehicle access past 5.0 km. The district map also flags a post wildfire rockfall area on the Mystery FSR: do not stop. Read the page before you go: [Chilliwack road safety](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/natural-resource-use/resource-roads/local-road-safety-information/chilliwack-natural-resource-district-district-road-safety-information).
- Sts'ailes (West) FSR (Harrison West) and the Chehalis FSR: no notice posted on the district page or map on 23 October 2026 (95%). No notice is not a promise the road is open.
- Unless lawfully hunting or trapping, no firearm discharge within 400 m of select FVRD (Fraser Valley Regional District) roads, the Chehalis area on Map B33, and Crown land along the Francis Lake, Weaver Lake and Sts'ailes (West) FSRs (99%, page 27; Closed Areas Regulation Schedule 5.1). Hunting is allowed; target shooting is not (90%, my reading).
- Map B29 Elbow Lake: No Shooting Area 800 m either side of the Chehalis road for 2.4 km north of Elbow Lake (99%, page 31).
- Map B32 Hemlock Valley: No Shooting Area 50 m, then 150 m, either side of the Hemlock Valley road (99%, page 31).
- Harrison Mills and Nicomen lowland: no rifles, and shot only on much of it (99%, Closed Areas Regulation Schedules 9 and 11; 85% my reading of the boundary). Hunt deer up in the hills, not on the flats.
- Highway 7 and Highway 1: shot only within 150 m (99%, Schedule 9 s. 1). Never shoot on or across a road allowance (99%, page 10).
- No hunting or shooting within 100 m of a house, farm building, school or regional district park (99%, page 10 and 9).
- Sasquatch and Kilby provincial parks: closed to hunting. Neither is on Schedule B of the Park, Conservancy and Recreation Area Regulation, so no firearm outside a vehicle and no shooting there (99%, sections 28 and 29).
- Harrison Hot Springs village: no firearm discharge anywhere inside the village, and no shot that goes into, over or through it (99%, Discharge of Firearms Bylaw No. 1207, 2024, section 3). There is no hunting exception.
- The Hunt Map: turn on No shooting / no hunting, and read "Still check yourself" in Can I hunt here now?
```

```diagram mk-hd-mistakes
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule: on a logging road, the truck always wins. Pull over early and let it by.

```more 3 common mistakes as text
> [!mistake]
> - Shooting a doe because "the limit is 2, one antlerless". There is no antlerless season in 2-8, 2-18 or 2-19.
> - Taking a rifle onto the Harrison Mills flats. That lowland bans rifles.
> - Driving a closed or washed out FSR because a map shows it open.
```

> [!field]
> The night before: read the Chilliwack road notices, check sunrise for your spot, cancel tags in your head (kill, cancel, touch), and tell someone at home where you will be.

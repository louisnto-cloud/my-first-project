> [!why]
> Manitoba sells a Canadian resident deer licence with no draw and no guide, but only in listed areas, and it has the strictest orange rule of the five trips.

Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Can a BC resident hunt here

```diagram ld-trip-status
```

- Canadian resident: deer and birds with no guide (85%).
- Bear and moose guide rule: VERIFY.

```more your status and the guide rule
- Yes. Manitoba calls you a **Canadian resident**: not a Manitoba resident, but a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, or someone who lived in Canada 6 consecutive months of the last 12 (85%).
- The guide rule in Manitoba is written for **non Canadian residents**: they must hunt big game with a guide employed by the outfitter that issued the licence (85%).
- As a Canadian resident you may hold Canadian resident licences for white tailed deer, general moose, conservation moose, black bear, wild turkey and game birds (85%).
- Whether every one of those is sold without an outfitter: deer and game bird yes (85%), bear and moose VERIFY.
- Your brother: same status, his own licences.
```

- High risk parts stay in the area (85%).
- BC: meat and cleaned parts only (99%).

```more Manitoba export, CWD and BC import rules, quoted
### Manitoba's export and CWD rules
- Manitoba lists a $20 wild animal export permit (85%). Whether it applies to your own harvest: VERIFY with the regional office.
- Manitoba's CWD advice: "Do not transport high risk parts (such as the brain, eyes and spine) outside the area where the animal was harvested." (85%)
- Manitoba's own import ban: no deer, elk, moose or caribou from another province or state may enter Manitoba unless the head, hide, hooves, mammary glands, entrails, internal organs and spinal column are removed and left behind. Deboned or processed meat is allowed (85%).
- That ban cuts both ways on a road trip. Do not carry BC deer bones east either.

### BC's import rule (CWD)
- From the BC Synopsis 2026 to 2028, page 12, It's Unlawful item 50 (99%):
- "To possess any part of a Cervidae (caribou, deer, elk, moose), that was killed outside of British Columbia, other than (1) the edible meat of the four quarters, loins, neck, and ribs, or (2) the hide, antlers, and skull plate if, before being brought into British Columbia, the part is cleaned in a manner that removes all tissue." (99%)
- Item 51: no out of province cervid part or derivative may be used for hunting in BC (99%).

- You will cross Saskatchewan and Alberta with that meat. Boned out meat and a cleaned skull plate satisfy all three provinces (80%).
- CFIA (Canadian Food Inspection Agency): no national border, no CFIA step. VERIFY only if shipping commercially.
```

## Blaze orange rules

```diagram ld-mb-orange
```

- Orange hat plus 2,580 cm² (400 in²) above the waist (85%).

```more the regulation wording, quoted
- Manitoba's General Hunting Regulation, M.R. 351/87, for anyone hunting, dressing or retrieving a big game animal or coyote, or guiding or accompanying them (85%):
- A head covering "completely hunter orange in colour (other than on the brim of a hat)", a small crest or logo allowed (85%).
- Plus "2,580 cm² (400 in²) of material hunter orange in colour, affixed above the waist and visible from all sides" (85%).
- "Hunter orange" is defined by wavelength, 595 to 605 nanometres, and brightness (85%).
- Archery only exceptions: VERIFY. Birds: no orange rule found, VERIFY.
- Your brother walking with you, no rifle, still needs the orange. The rule covers anyone accompanying (85%).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> When a province writes its orange rule in square centimetres, it means it. Buy the vest before the trip, not at the gas station.

> [!mistake]
> - Hunting deer in a GHA that is not on the Canadian resident list.
> - Orange vest but a camouflage hat. The hat must be completely orange.
> - Hauling a head home for a mount. Three provinces say no to the brain and spine.

> [!field]
> Aspen parkland deer: still hunt the bush edges along cut grain at first light, orange on, rifle unloaded until you are off the road allowance.

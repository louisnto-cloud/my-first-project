> [!why]
> Yukon is the closest big wild country, but a BC resident cannot hunt its big game alone. Know that before you book fuel.

Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Can a BC resident hunt here

```diagram ld-trip-status
```

- Yukon: birds on your own, big game with a guide (85%).
- Deer and elk: Yukon residents only (85%).

```more your status, the guide rule and the warning
- Yes. In Yukon you are a **non resident Canadian** (85%).
- Small game and game birds: you may hunt **without a guide** on a non resident small game licence (85%).
- Big game: you must be guided by a registered Yukon outfitter, or by a Yukon resident who holds a **special guide licence** (85%).
- Deer and elk: **Yukon residents only**. No non resident may hunt them, even with a guide (85%).
- Your brother is in the same boat. Same licences, same guide rules.

> [!warn]
> A friend in Whitehorse cannot just "take you moose hunting". They need a special guide licence won in a lottery, and they may not be paid (85%).
```

## How to apply

```diagram ld-yk-apply
```

- Small game: buy online. Big game: guide first (85%).

```more the 5 steps in full
1. Small game licence: buy online on yukon.ca, or at an Environment office or vendor (85%).
2. Bring photo ID. Yukon asks for identity documents to issue a licence (85%).
3. Ducks and geese: your federal [[MGBHP]] from BC is valid across Canada (80%).
4. Big game: book a registered outfitter, or have a Yukon resident friend enter the special guide lottery in spring (85%).
5. Read the current Yukon hunting regulations summary before you go. Seasons and subzones change (85%).
```

## Taking firearms there

```diagram ld-no-border-gun
```

- No border. Same federal rules (85%).

```more firearms in Yukon in full
- No border. Yukon is Canada. Your [[PAL]] (Possession and Acquisition Licence) covers you (85%).
- Transport rules are federal and the same as at home: unloaded, and treated as the RCMP (Royal Canadian Mounted Police) transport rules require (85%).
- Yukon is bear country. A loaded firearm in camp is still a storage question. Follow the RCMP storage rules (80%).
```


- Export permit before parts leave Yukon (85%).
- BC: meat and cleaned parts only (99%).

```more Yukon export, BC and Yukon import rules, quoted
### Yukon's export rule
- You need a **wildlife export permit** before you remove or ship wildlife parts from Yukon, including antlers or horns you find (85%).
- Exception: up to 20 kg (44 lb) of processed big game meat needs no permit (85%).
- Apply in person at a Department of Environment office. Free (85%).
- Birds and small game: the permit page talks about wildlife parts generally. Ask at the office: VERIFY.

### BC's import rule (CWD)
- [[CWD]] (Chronic Wasting Disease) drives BC's rule. From the 2026 to 2028 Synopsis, page 12, It's Unlawful item 50 (99%):
- "To possess any part of a Cervidae (caribou, deer, elk, moose), that was killed outside of British Columbia, other than (1) the edible meat of the four quarters, loins, neck, and ribs, or (2) the hide, antlers, and skull plate if, before being brought into British Columbia, the part is cleaned in a manner that removes all tissue." (99%)
- Item 51 bans using any out of province deer, elk, moose or caribou part or derivative, such as scents, for hunting in BC (99%).

### Yukon's own import rule
- Yukon bans bringing in cervid carcasses or parts except a cleaned skull cap with antlers, cleaned teeth, meat detached from head and backbone, finished mounts and tanned hides (85%).
- So do not carry BC deer bones north with you either.

- CFIA (Canadian Food Inspection Agency): no national border is crossed, so no CFIA step. VERIFY if you ever ship meat commercially.
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> The further you drive, the simpler the hunt should be. Birds first, moose later.

> [!mistake]
> - Driving 2,300 km expecting to hunt moose with a Whitehorse friend who has no special guide licence.
> - Buying a deer tag plan. Deer are residents only in Yukon.
> - Leaving the export permit for the morning you drive home. Offices keep office hours.

> [!field]
> Grouse along the Alaska Highway gravel in the first hour of light. Walk the edges, 12 gauge, improved cylinder, 7.5 shot. Watch for traffic and the road allowance rules: VERIFY Yukon's road rules.

> [!why]
> The hunters who fill tags in October did their homework in February. Know the year and nothing sneaks up on you.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law. Region 3 dates below are for the main MU (Management Unit) blocks. Always read your own MU row in the synopsis.

- Licence year: 1 April to 31 March. All hunting and species licences expire 31 March (99%).
- The [[Synopsis]] in this app is in force 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2028 (99%).
- [[LEH]] (Limited Entry Hunting) main draw: applications in by 11:59 pm, Tuesday 23 June 2026 (99%).
- LEH application fee: $6.30 including taxes, not refundable (99%).
- 2027 Special Mountain Sheep draw: deadline Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific Time (99%, LEH page dated 24 September 2026).
- Spring 2027 draw (Skeena sheep, Haida Gwaii black bear): deadline 11:59 pm, Thursday 4 February 2027 (99%).
- The LEH synopsis is posted in April each year (95%). The day the main draw opens: VERIFY on the LEH page each spring.

- Four blocks: paperwork (March to June), practice and scouting (June to August), seasons (September to December), review and sheds (January to April). Tip.
- Dates in this session come from the Region 3 table on page 34 of the synopsis unless another page is named (99%).

## January to March

| Month | Admin and law | Region 3 seasons | Do this (tips) |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | The Hunter Sample Questionnaire is mailed every January to many licence holders (99%). Replies from hunters who did not hunt are "just as valuable" (99%). | Cougar, bobcat, lynx, wolf and coyote seasons run (99%). Snowshoe hare to 30 April (99%). | Read the journal. Fix what broke. |
| February | Spring LEH draw deadline, 4 February 2027 (99%). | Bobcat and lynx close 15 February (99%). | Clean and oil firearms. Check scope mounts. |
| March | Licences expire 31 March (99%). Mandatory Hunter Report due 31 March for anyone who bought an elk, moose or caribou species licence (99%). | Canada and cackling geese 1 to 10 March (99%). Cougar closes 31 March (99%). | Sheds as the low slopes clear. |

- Miss the Mandatory Hunter Report and you cannot buy a licence or LEH application next season until it is in (99%).

## April to June

| Month | Admin and law | Region 3 seasons | Do this (tips) |
|---|---|---|---|
| April | New licence year, 1 April (99%). Resident hunting licence $32 including the conservation surcharge, GST (Goods and Services Tax) extra (99%). LEH synopsis posted (95%). | Black bear 1 April to 30 June, bag limit 2 (99%). Coyote runs (99%). | Walk winter range. Note where deer wintered. |
| May | Apply for LEH in [[WILD]] (Wildlife Information and Licensing Data) or in person (99%). One application per species per draw (99%). | Bear and coyote open (99%). | Hike fall spots while trails are quiet. |
| June | Main draw deadline 23 June 2026, 11:59 pm (99%). New synopsis in force 1 July (99%). | Bear closes 30 June. Wolf closes 15 June in MUs 3-12 to 3-16 and 3-18 to 3-20 (99%). | Sight in the rifle. Pattern the shotgun. |

- You must prove BC residency every three years to keep buying resident licences and LEH (99%).
- LEH applications are not refundable and not transferable (99%).

## July and August

| Month | Admin and law | Region 3 seasons | Do this (tips) |
|---|---|---|---|
| July | Check LEH results in WILD after the draw (99%). Results are not mailed; an email goes only to profiles with an email address (99%). Read the "important messages" for any hunt you drew (99%). | No big game general seasons open in Region 3 in July (95%). | Shoot from field positions, not the bench. |
| August | Buy species licences. Online orders are mailed; buy in person and you get them at once (99%). You must carry a valid federal [[MGBHP]] (Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit) when hunting ducks and geese (99%). | Snowshoe hare opens 1 August (99%). Dogs may be trained on game birds 1 August to 30 April (99%). | Build the pack. Check boots, waders, decoys. |

- Some species licences are not valid until 2 days after issue. Deer is not one of them (90%, fee table page 8).
- 2026 federal bird permit runs from 1 August 2026 (80%, from Phase 1 Session 2; canada.ca blocked here).

## September

- 1 September: youth only and bow only mule deer bucks open in most MUs (99%). Youth grouse 1 to 9 September (99%). Black bear and coyote open (99%). Doves 1 to 30 September (99%).
- 1 to 9 September: 4 point mule deer bucks in MUs 3-15, 3-16, 3-32, 3-33 only (99%).
- 8 September: ducks, coots and snipe open to 23 December (99%). Canada and cackling geese 8 to 20 September (99%).
- 10 September: 4 point mule deer bucks to 30 September (99%). White tailed bucks to 10 December (99%). Grouse to 30 November, 5 a day each, aggregate 10 (99%).
- 20 September: moose spike fork bulls in MUs 3-34 to 3-38, 3-40 to 3-44, 3-46, to 31 October (99%). MU 3-46 deer seasons start 20 September (99%).
- Opening weekend is crowded on the roads. Walk in. Tip.

## October

- 1 October: any mule deer buck, 1 to 31 October, every Region 3 MU listed (99%).
- 1 October: chukar, gray partridge and pheasant (cocks) to 30 November (99%). Canada geese reopen 1 October to 23 December (99%).
- 1 October: Region 8 quail, 1 October to 30 November, 10 a day, 30 in possession (99%, page 68).
- 10 October: antlerless white tailed deer, 10 to 31 October (99%). Region 2 ducks open 10 October 2026 to 24 January 2027 (99%, page 28).
- 15 October: moose spike fork bulls in MUs 3-15 to 3-17, 3-31 to 3-33, to 15 November (99%).
- Any buck October is the busiest month. The first weekend is the worst. Tip.

## November and December

- 1 November: 4 point mule deer bucks again, to 10 December (99%). Youth either sex white tailed deer, 1 to 30 November (99%).
- 1 to 15 November: moose spike fork bulls in MUs 3-12 to 3-14, 3-18 to 3-20, 3-26 to 3-30, 3-39 (99%).
- 1 to 30 November: LEH antlerless mule deer hunts across Region 3, hunt codes 7004 to 7028 (99%, LEH synopsis page 14).
- 15 November: cougar (MUs 3-12 to 3-20, 3-26 to 3-33), bobcat and lynx open (99%).
- 30 November: grouse, partridge, pheasant, black bear and Region 8 quail close (99%).
- 10 December: deer buck seasons close (99%). 11 to 20 December: bow only either sex white tailed deer, new for 2026 (99%).
- 23 December: ducks, geese, coots and snipe close in Region 3 (99%).
- BC asks hunters to submit deer, elk and moose heads for [[CWD]] (Chronic Wasting Disease) testing (99%). It is mandatory only in the Kootenay zone (95%).

## Sheds and scouting

- Cast (shed) antlers: the synopsis allows trafficking in cast antlers, so picking one up is not treated like a wildlife part (90%, my reading of page 11, item 8).
- Winter range in March and April is where deer are weakest. Walk, keep dogs home, do not push them. Tip.
- Region 3: all motor vehicles except snowmobiles are banned above 1,700 m except on existing roads and trails (99%).
- Spring and summer closures exist too. Maps: [gov.bc.ca/thompsonaccess](https://www.gov.bc.ca/thompsonaccess) (99%).
- Summer scouting: bucks sit in bachelor groups and show at dusk. June to August is when you learn a mountain. Tip.
- Log every scouting trip in the Journal. Session 8 shows how. Tip.

## Range, gear and giving back

- "Practice your marksmanship at a licensed firing range" (99%, synopsis ethics, page 77).
- Rifle: confirm [[Zero]] in June and again two weeks before opening day. Tip.
- Shotgun: pattern each choke and load before duck season. Tip.
- January is maintenance month. August is packing month. Tip.
- [[CORE]] (Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education) is run by the BC Wildlife Federation through certified examiners (99%). After a few seasons, help a new hunter get ready. Session 10.

> [!rule]
> Buy the licence the week it goes on sale and apply for the draw the week the synopsis comes out. Nobody ever lost a tag by being early.

> [!mistake]
> - Waiting for a letter about the LEH draw. Nothing is mailed. Log in to WILD.
> - Ordering deer licences online in September. They come by mail. Buy in person.
> - Thinking the 4 point rule is off all fall. It is off in October only.

> [!field]
> Put the Region 3 table on your phone as a photo. Before every trip, find your MU row and read the date, class and bag limit out loud to your partner.

## Rookie

```diagram lc-ml-rookie
```

```more the rookie goal in words
- Goal: safe, legal, and out there (Tip).
- Licensed, every firearm handled by [[ACTS]] and [[PROVE]], always hunting with a partner (Tip).
- Season one is for learning the ground, not filling tags (Tip).
```

```checklist s6-rookie
FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID), basic licence and species licences bought for this year
Read my MU (Management Unit) rows on synopsis page 34 out loud to my brother
Rifle zeroed and 10 shots in a 10 cm circle at 100 m (109 yd) from a rest
Shotgun patterned with my bird load
Compass bearing walked to a landmark and back
Trip plan left before every hunt
Five days in the field, journal filled for each
Wind checked with a puffer or milkweed every hunt
Field dressing watched or done with help
```

## Apprentice

```diagram lc-ml-apprentice
```

```more the apprentice goal in words
- Goal: first animals, clean shots, all the meat home (Tip).
- You plan most of the trip yourself. Your brother checks the plan (Tip).
```

```checklist s6-apprentice
First big game animal tagged on the spot, licence notched correctly
Edible portions packed out and kept cool
First limit or near limit of birds, every bird identified before the shot
Passed on at least one shot that was too far or too risky
Map scouted a hunt from home and found deer where the map said
Glassed up an animal before it saw me
Blood trailed a hit animal with a partner, or practised on a mock trail
One full season of journal entries reviewed in January
```

## Hunter

```diagram lc-ml-hunter
```

```more the hunter goal in words
- Goal: finds game alone, fills tags most years, never loses the plot on safety (Tip).
- You make the calls. You still hunt with a partner because it is safer (Tip).
```

```checklist s6-hunter
Three or more seasons on the same ground
Found and killed a mature animal by spot and stalk or sitting a crossing
Butchered a whole deer myself
Applied to LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) for a planned hunt, with a backup choice
Hunted a new MU from map scouting alone and came home with a plan for next year
Turned around for weather, light or a bad wind, and was glad later
Shot practice every month of the off season
Can explain my MU rules without opening the synopsis, then checked I was right
```

## Guide

```diagram lc-ml-guide
```

- Supervising is law: 3 prior licence years, at most two new hunters (99%, synopsis page 6).

```more the guide goal in words
- Goal: puts others on game and keeps them safe (Tip).
- In BC, supervising a youth or an initiation licence hunter needs, among other things, a hunting licence held in at least 3 of the licence years before this one (99%, synopsis page 6).
- A supervisor may not take more than two youth or initiation licence hunters at a time (99%, page 6).
- Taking someone hunting for pay or reward is guiding. It needs a guide licence (99%, synopsis page 11).
- So "Guide" here means unpaid mentor, not a paid guide (Tip).
```

```checklist s6-guide
Met the 3 licence year rule on synopsis page 6
Took a new hunter out for a full day, safety talk first
Let them make the shot decision and talked them through it
Taught a partner to read the wind and a topo map
Led a hunt where I carried no rifle and only spotted
Walked someone through field dressing and meat care
```

## Old Timer

```diagram lc-ml-oldtimer
```

- Old Timer is not about age. It is what you hand on (Tip).

```more the old timer goal in words
- Goal: teaches and gives back (Tip).
- Old Timer is not about age. It is about what you hand on (Tip).
- The synopsis ethics code: "Be aware that you are a mentor when youth are observing." (99%, synopsis page 77)
```

```checklist s6-oldtimer
Mentored two or more new hunters through their first season
Volunteered on a habitat or wildlife project
Gave a talk, a range day or a CORE (Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education) practice day
Shared meat with someone who does not hunt
Reported a violation, or helped someone report one
Kept a journal for ten seasons and can show what it taught me
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The ladder has no top. The best hunters I knew still called themselves learners.

> [!mistake]
> - Ticking a box because you read the session. The box is for the field.
> - Rushing to Guide before you meet the 3 licence year rule for supervising.
> - Measuring yourself in antlers. Measure in clean kills, meat home and safe days.

> [!field]
> Tonight, go through Rookie with your brother. Each tick needs a date and a place from your journal. If you cannot name it, leave it blank.

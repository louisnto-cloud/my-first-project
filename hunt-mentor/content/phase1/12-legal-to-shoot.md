> [!why]
> You get about 3 seconds. The 9 questions must be a habit before the animal shows up.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Same 9 steps as the "Is it legal to shoot?" card in the Field tab.
- Every answer must be **yes**. One no, or one "not sure", and you do not shoot.
- Practise saying them in order on every walk until they are automatic.

- Yes goes down. No goes right, to DO NOT SHOOT.

## Steps 1 to 3: paper, clock, season

1. **Licences on me?** Photo ID, [[FWID]] (Fish and Wildlife ID) number, the deer species licence, or the federal bird permit for ducks (85%).
2. **Legal hours?** Deer and quail: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (85%). Ducks: 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset (85%).
3. **Season open for this species, sex and antler type, in this [[MU]] (Management Unit), today?** Check the synopsis table for your exact MU. Dates: VERIFY in the Rules tab.

- Antler rules like "4 point" mean you count points **before** the shot. Tip.

## Steps 4 to 6: target, road, buildings

4. **100% sure what it is?** Species, sex, antler points. For ducks: species before you shoot. Not sure means no. Tip.
5. **Off the road, out of the vehicle?** No shooting from a motor vehicle or motorboat (85%). No shooting across a road or on the road allowance (80%). No loaded firearm in or on a vehicle or [[ATV]] (all terrain vehicle) (85%).
6. **Over 100 m (109 yd) from any occupied house, farm or ranch building?** (85%). This includes your brother's house on the property (80%, my reading).

## Steps 7 to 9: land, backstop, shot

7. **Allowed on this land?** [[Crown land]] that is open, or owner's permission. Not a park closed to hunting, an ecological reserve or a No Shooting Area (85%).
8. **Safe [[Backstop]]?** Earth behind the animal. No skyline. No people, road, livestock or building beyond. Tip, and your safety course rule.
9. **Clean shot inside your tested range?** Broadside or quartering away, still animal, steady rest. Tip.

- Steps 8 and 9 are about safety and ethics. They are not optional.

> [!rule]
> When in doubt, don't. The deer will be there next year. A bad shot follows you forever.

> [!mistake]
> - Counting antler points after the shot.
> - Forgetting that ducks start 30 minutes later than deer in the morning.
> - Shooting from the ATV seat "because the engine is off".

> [!field]
> Walking in, say the 9 steps out loud once. At the stand, rangefind the nearest building and the road.

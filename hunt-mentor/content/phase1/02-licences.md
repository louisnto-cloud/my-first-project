> [!why]
> No licence, no hunt. Some tags take 14 days by mail. Buy in person now.

## What you need, by animal

| Hunting | You need |
|---|---|
| Deer | BC hunting licence + deer species licence (tag) for that species |
| Ducks and geese | BC hunting licence + federal bird permit |
| Quail and grouse | BC hunting licence only |

- Residents need no species licence for quail or grouse (80%).
- Ducks and geese need no BC species licence, only the federal permit (80%).
- Mule deer and white tailed deer are **separate** species licences (80%).

## Your hunter number (FWID)

- Your old hunter number is now the [[FWID]] (Fish and Wildlife ID) (85%).
- It is 9 digits: your old number with 3 digits added at the front (85%).
- **There is no physical card.** You do not need to carry a printout (85%).
- You must carry **photo ID** and be able to give your FWID number (85%).
- Not sure of yours? Log in to WILD (Wildlife Information and Licensing Data) and tap **Find my FWID** (85%).
- No luck? FrontCounter BC: 1 877 855 3222 (80%).

## The hunting licence

- One per licence year. Licence year runs 1 April to 31 March (85%).
- Adult resident cost: $25 + $7 surcharge (80%). GST (Goods and Services Tax): VERIFY.
- It is **electronic only**. No paper copy exists (85%).
- Buy online in WILD, or at Service BC or FrontCounter BC (85%).

## Deer tags (species licences)

- Mule deer: $12 + $3 surcharge. White tailed deer: $12 + $3 surcharge (80%).
- **Cannot be printed at home.** They are special waterproof paper (85%).
- Online orders are mailed. About 14 days (85%).
- Hunting in the next 2 weeks? **Buy in person** at Service BC, FrontCounter BC or a licence vendor (85%).
- Some species have a 2 day wait after purchase. For deer: VERIFY.
- Carry **all** your species licences for the year, used and unused (85%).

> [!lean]
> Buy both a mule deer and a white tailed deer licence. $30 total. You will not know which deer walks out first.

## Federal bird permit (ducks and geese)

- Name: Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit. The habitat stamp is included (80%).
- Cost: $17 + tax (80%).
- 2026 e permit valid 1 August 2026 to 30 June 2027 (80%).
- Buy online any time with a credit card. Save it on your phone (85%).
- Carry it every time you hunt ducks or geese, paper or phone (85%).

## Your brother

- He can come along and watch **without a licence**, as long as he carries no firearm or weapon (80%).
- He can help pack meat out. You must stay with the meat, or fill in a transport record (80%).
- He should not call, chase or drive game toward you. That can count as hunting (65%, my reading of the law).
- To hunt himself he needs CORE (Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education), a PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) for firearms, and a hunting licence. That takes weeks. Not this October.

## Buy it this week

```checklist s2-buy
Find my FWID in WILD
Buy BC hunting licence (online is fine, it is electronic)
```

> [!rule]
> Licences go in a zip bag in the same pocket every time. Check the pocket before you leave the truck.

> [!mistake]
> - Ordering deer tags online a week before the hunt. They arrive too late.
> - Thinking the hunting licence covers ducks. You also need the federal permit.
> - Leaving used tags at home. Carry them all.

> [!field]
> Open WILD tonight. Find your FWID. Write it on the back of your PAL card in pencil.

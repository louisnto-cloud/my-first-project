> [!why]
> No licence, no hunt. Some tags take 14 days by mail. Buy in person now.

## What you need, by animal

| Hunting | You need |
|---|---|
| Deer | BC hunting licence + deer species licence (tag) for that species |
| Ducks and geese | BC hunting licence + federal bird permit |
| Quail and grouse | BC hunting licence only |

- Residents need no species licence for quail or grouse (99%).
- Ducks and geese need no BC species licence, only the federal permit (99%).
- Mule deer and white tailed deer are **separate** species licences (99%).

## Your hunter number (FWID)

- Your old hunter number is now the [[FWID]] (Fish and Wildlife ID) (99%).
- It is your old hunter number with 3 extra digits (99%).
- No card or printout needed. Knowing the number is enough (95%).
- You must carry **photo ID** and be able to give your FWID number when an officer asks (99%).
- Not sure of yours? Log in to WILD (Wildlife Information and Licensing Data) and use **Find my FWID** (99%).
- No luck? FrontCounter BC: 1 877 855 3222 (99%).

## The hunting licence

- It is good for the licence year, 1 April to 31 March. It expires 31 March (99%).
- Adult resident cost: $32. That is a $25 fee plus a $7 conservation surcharge (99%).
- GST (Goods and Services Tax) is not included in that price (99%).
- It is **electronic only**. You need no paper copy while hunting (99%).
- Buy online, or at Service BC, FrontCounter BC or a participating store (99%).

## Deer tags (species licences)

- Mule deer: $15. White tailed deer: $15. Each is a $12 fee plus a $3 surcharge (99%).
- Plus 5% GST (Goods and Services Tax) (99%).
- **Cannot be printed at home.** They are printed on special paper with unique numbers (99%).
- Online orders are mailed. Allow about 14 business days to a BC address (95%).
- Hunting in the next 2 weeks? **Buy in person** at Service BC, FrontCounter BC or a licence vendor. You get them right away (99%).
- **No waiting period for deer.** The 2 day wait applies only to bobcat, caribou, cougar, lynx, mountain goat, mountain sheep and wolverine (99%).
- Carry **all** your species licences for the year, used and unused (99%).

> [!lean]
> Buy both a mule deer and a white tailed deer licence. $30 plus GST. You will not know which deer walks out first.

## Federal bird permit (ducks and geese)

- Name: Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit (99%).
- The habitat conservation stamp must be on it. You pay for it with the permit (99%).
- Cost: $17. That is $8.50 for the permit plus $8.50 for the stamp (99%). Plus tax (80%).
- It expires 30 June after you buy it (99%). The 2026 e permit runs 1 August 2026 to 30 June 2027 (80%).
- Buy online (99%). Save the e permit on your phone (85%).
- Carry it every time you hunt ducks or geese (99%). Paper or phone both count (85%).

## Your brother

- He has CORE (Conservation and Outdoor Recreation Education), a PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) and an FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID). He can hunt too.
- He needs his **own** BC hunting licence, his **own** deer species licences and his **own** federal bird permit (99%).
- Licences cannot be lent, shared or transferred (99%).
- Each hunter fills **their own** tags. The hunter who kills cancels his own licence, right away (99%).
- You cannot notch your tag for his deer. BC has no party hunting rule (95%).
- Anyone without a licence can still come along to watch and help pack out, if they carry no firearm or other weapon (99%).
- The licensed hunter stays with the meat, unless the helper carries a Record of Receipt (99%).

## Buy it this week

```checklist s2-buy
Find my FWID in WILD
Buy BC hunting licence (online is fine, it is electronic)
Buy mule deer licence IN PERSON

Buy federal bird permit online, save to phone

```

> [!rule]
> Licences go in a zip bag in the same pocket every time. Check the pocket before you leave the truck.

> [!mistake]
> - Ordering deer tags online a week before the hunt. They arrive too late.
> - Thinking the hunting licence covers ducks. You also need the federal permit.
> - Leaving used tags at home. Carry them all.

> [!field]
> Open WILD tonight. Find your FWID. Write it on the back of your PAL card in pencil.

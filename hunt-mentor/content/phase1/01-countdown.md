> [!why]
> About 2 weeks to go. Gear and practice take longer than you think. This is the order that works.

## The big picture

- **Week 1 (now):** licences, buy the shotgun, buy and mount the scope.
- **Week 2:** sight in the rifle, pattern the shotgun, buy clothing, scout on the map.
- **Hunt week:** final checks, pack, go.
- Dates are a plan, not a rule. Move them to fit your life.

> [!lean]
> Licences and the scope come first. Without a sighted in rifle there is no deer hunt. Without a federal permit there is no duck hunt.

## Week 1: paperwork and the big buys

```checklist cd-w1
Find my FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID) in WILD online
Buy BC hunting licence online
Buy mule deer and white tailed deer licences IN PERSON
Buy the federal Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit online
Pick a shotgun (Session 3) and buy it
Buy steel shot for ducks and lead or steel for quail (Session 6)
Pick a scope and Picatinny rings (Session 4) and buy them
Have the shop mount the scope, or mount it with a torque wrench
Buy .308 ammo: 3 to 4 boxes of the SAME load
Ask your brother: is his own house on the 20 acres? Where are the neighbours?
```

## Week 2: practice and kit

```checklist cd-w2
Bore sight, then zero the rifle at 100 m (Session 5)
Shoot 3 shot groups from a rest until they are tight
Shoot from sitting and kneeling with shooting sticks
Pattern the shotgun on paper with your duck load (Session 5)
Practice on clay targets, at least 1 session
Buy clothing, boots, waders (Session 7)
Build the day pack and car kit (Session 8)
Download offline maps of the area to your phone
Scout on the map: slopes, benches, water, roads (Sessions 9 to 11)
Practice the legal to shoot flowchart in Field Mode
```

## Hunt week

```checklist cd-w3
Check in season regulation changes and closures
Check fire restrictions and road closures
Check legal light times in Field Mode
Leave a trip plan with someone
Pack: licences, ID, permit, ammo, knife, game bags, first aid
Cooler and ice ready for meat
Go slow. Watch more than you walk.
```

## Time savers

- Buy where you can handle the gun and get help mounting the scope. (Tip)
- Ask the store about a range near Kamloops. Many have sighting in days. (Tip)
- One ammo load for the rifle. Do not mix brands after zeroing. (Tip, 85% the zero changes between loads)
- Practice loading and unloading at the car until it is boring. (Tip)

> [!rule]
> Buy once, practice twice. The rifle you shot 60 times beats the fancy one you shot 6 times.

> [!mistake]
> - Mounting the scope the night before the hunt.
> - Buying 5 kinds of ammo and never zeroing any of them.
> - Spending every evening shopping and none on the range.

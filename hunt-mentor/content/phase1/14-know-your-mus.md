> Seasons and limits change from one MU (Management Unit) to the next. Know the number, and you know which rules to read.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Tap any MU number, region or place name in the app. A small map pops up, even with no signal.

## What an MU is

- BC (British Columbia) is split into 9 hunting regions (99%, BC MU map layer).
- Each region is split into numbered areas called [[MU]]s (Management Units) (99%, BC MU map layer).
- BC has 225 MUs (99%, count of the official MU layer, data date 2026-10-03).
- The synopsis lists seasons, bag limits and closures by MU (95%, synopsis season tables, see the Rule Book).
- So the first question on any trip is: which MU am I in? (Tip)

## How the numbers work

- An MU number is region, dash, unit. MU 3-27 means Region 3, unit 27 (99%, BC MU map layer).
- Read it like an address: the region first, then the unit (Tip).
- Region 7 has two halves: 7A Omineca and 7B Peace. Both use 7 in their MU numbers, like MU 7-20 (99%, BC MU map layer).
- Units do not always start at 1. Region 3 runs from MU 3-12 to MU 3-46 (99%, BC MU map layer).
- Close numbers are not always neighbours. MU 3-27 touches 3-20, 3-26, 3-28, 3-37 and 3-38 (95%, BC MU map layer).

## Home 1: Heffley Creek, MU 3-27

```mumap 3-27
```

- Heffley Creek and Sun Peaks are both in MU 3-27, Region 3 Thompson (95%, BC MU map layer and BC Geographical Names).
- Kamloops itself sits in MU 3-20, just south (95%, same sources).
- West across the North Thompson River is MU 3-28 (85%, Heffley Creek local plan).
- Next door to 3-27: MUs 3-20, 3-26, 3-28, 3-37 and 3-38 (95%, BC MU map layer).

## Home 2: Mission, MU 2-8

```mumap 2-8
```

- Mission is in MU 2-8, Region 2 Lower Mainland (95%, BC MU map layer and BC Geographical Names).
- South of the Fraser River, Abbotsford and Chilliwack are in MU 2-4 (95%, same sources).
- MU 2-8 is big: it touches 8 other units, from 2-4 to 2-19 (95%, BC MU map layer).

## Home 3: South Okanagan, MU 8-1

```mumap 8-1
```

- Oliver and Osoyoos are both in MU 8-1, Region 8 Okanagan (95%, BC MU map layer and BC Geographical Names).
- Penticton is in MU 8-9. Kelowna is in MU 8-10 (95%, same sources).
- Next door to 8-1: MUs 8-2, 8-8, 8-9 and 8-12 (95%, BC MU map layer).

## Find your MU on the Hunt Map

- Open the Map tab. Tap Hunt Map Layers. Turn on Management Units and Management Unit numbers.
- Tap inside a unit. The card shows the MU number and the region.
- Insights (bottom bar) shows the MU and region at the map centre.
- Search: type an MU number like 3-27. The map flies there.
- In a lesson: tap an MU number, then Open on Hunt Map.

```more near a line
- The small maps in lessons are simplified, about 1 km. Use the Hunt Map or the official map for the exact line (95%, how the small maps are made).
- On the ground, an MU line can follow a river, a ridge or a road you cannot see on a phone screen (Tip).
- Close to a line and not sure? Hunt where you are sure, well inside the unit you checked (Tip).
```

## Why it matters

- Season dates and limits are set by MU in the synopsis tables (95%, synopsis season tables).
- The same animal can be open in one MU and closed in the MU next door. Always read your exact MU row (95%, synopsis season tables).
- This lesson has no dates on purpose. The Rule Book has them, with sources:
  - [Region 3 Thompson rules](#/s/rb-region-3)
  - [Region 2 Lower Mainland rules](#/s/rb-region-2)

## Memory hooks

> [!rule]
> Know the number before you load the rifle.

- "Three twenty seven is home." Heffley Creek and Sun Peaks (Tip).
- "Two eight, north of the river." Mission is 2-8. Abbotsford, south of the Fraser, is 2-4 (Tip).
- "Eight one for quail." Oliver and Osoyoos are 8-1 (Tip).
- Region first, unit second: the first number is always the region (Tip).

> [!mistake]
> - Reading the season row for the wrong MU.
> - Thinking close numbers mean neighbours, or the same rules.
> - Trusting a small lesson map for an exact boundary.

> [!field]
> Before you leave the truck: open the Hunt Map, check the MU at your spot, then read that MU's row in the Rule Book.

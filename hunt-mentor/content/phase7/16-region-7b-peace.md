> [!rule]
> In the Peace, a quarter section line can change the legal elk. Know whether you stand inside the farm area or out.

> [!mistake]
> - Hunting moose on 27 October in MU 7-20. The window is 25 and 26 October, then 10 and 11 November.
> - Shooting an antlerless white tail in the bush part of 7-21. Antlerless is only in the Map H12 farm area.
> - Riding a quad above 1,400 m in MU 7-31 in September. That area is closed to motor vehicles.

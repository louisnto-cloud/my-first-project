## Regional bag limits

> [!law] Regional Bag Limits, page 67
> "DEER: The aggregate bag limit for deer is 3. Two deer may be antlerless but only one antlerless deer of each species may be taken. The bag limit for mule deer is 1. Antlerless mule deer are available only through LEH. The bag limit for white-tailed deer is 2, only one of which may be a buck, and one of which may be antlerless."
>
> "GROUSE: The daily aggregate bag limit for grouse: blue (dusky), spruce (Franklin), and ruffed is 5."
>
> "TURKEY: The regional aggregate bag limit for turkey is 2. Only one turkey may be taken during each open season."

- Deer: 3 in Region 8 in one licence year, all species together (99%, page 67, 2 October 2026 edition).
- Up to 2 of the 3 may be [[Antlerless|antlerless]], but only 1 antlerless of each species (99%).
- Mule deer: 1. Antlerless mule deer only with an [[LEH]] (Limited Entry Hunting) draw (99%).
- White tailed deer: 2, no more than 1 buck and no more than 1 antlerless (99%).
- Grouse: 5 a day in total, dusky ([[Blue grouse|blue]]), spruce ([[Franklin's grouse|Franklin]]) and ruffed together (99%). Not 5 of each, as in Region 3 (99%, page 34).
- Turkey: 2 a year, 1 in spring and 1 in fall (99%).
- Province wide, only 1 mule deer buck from Regions 3, 4, 5, 6, 7A, 7B and 8 combined (99%, page 14). A Heffley Creek buck uses up your Okanagan mule deer buck (95%, my reading).

>
> "▲ Motor Vehicles for Hunting Closed Areas (See page 10): The operation of all motor vehicles, including electric bicycles, for the purpose of hunting, to transport wildlife, to transport equipment, firearms and supplies which are intended for or in support of hunting or to transport hunters to and from the location of wildlife is prohibited in these areas."

- No sign does not mean no closure (99%, page 67).
- ★ [[Motor Vehicle Closed Area]]: no driving at all. Snowmobiles too, unless the line says otherwise (99%).
- ▲ Motor Vehicles for Hunting Closed Area: no vehicle to hunt, carry hunters, gear or game (99%).
- ✖ [[ATV]] (all terrain vehicle), electric bicycle and snowmobile closure: those vehicles are out all year unless dates are given (99%).
- Closures cover private land too, but not highways (99%, page 10).

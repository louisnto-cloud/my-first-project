## Regional bag limits

> [!law] Regional Bag Limits, page 56
> "Deer: The bag limit for mule (black-tailed) deer in region 7A is 1. The bag limit for white-tailed deer in region 7A is 2, only one of which may be a buck, and one of which may be antlerless."
>
> "Grouse: The regional daily aggregate bag limit for grouse: blue (dusky), spruce (Franklin), ruffed is 10. The regional daily bag limit for ptarmigan is 10."

- Mule deer: 1 in Region 7A (99%, page 56).
- White tailed deer: 2, no more than 1 buck and 1 [[Antlerless|antlerless]] (99%).
- Page 14 table for 7A: mule antlerless 1, mule total 1, white tail bucks 1, antlerless 1, total 2, all deer 3 (99%, page 14).
- Province wide: only 1 mule deer buck from Regions 3, 4, 5, 6, 7A, 7B and 8 combined (99%, page 14).
- Grouse: 10 a day for dusky, spruce and ruffed together. Ptarmigan: 10 a day (99%). [[Franklin's grouse]] is the spruce grouse (99%, glossary).

## Vehicle closures: how to read them

> [!law] Access Management Areas, page 56
> "Information signs may be posted at the points of closure for road and vehicle restrictions. These signs are for the benefit of hunters, but it is the hunters’ responsibility to recognize closures whether a sign is in place or not."
>
> "★ Motor Vehicle Closed Areas (See page 10)."
>
> "▲ Motor Vehicles for Hunting Closed Areas (See page 10): The operation of all motor vehicles, including electric bicycles, for the purpose of hunting, to transport wildlife, to transport equipment, firearms, and supplies which are intended for or in support of hunting or to transport hunters to and from the location of wildlife is prohibited in these areas."
>
> "◆ ATVs for Hunting Closed Areas (See page 10): The operation of ATVs (including motorcycles and electric bicycles) for the purpose of hunting, to transport wildlife, to transport firearms, or equipment and supplies which are intended for or in support of hunting, or to transport hunters to or from the location of wildlife is prohibited in these areas."
>
> "● Snowmobiles for Hunting Closed Areas (See page 10): The operation of snowmobiles for the purpose of hunting, to transport wildlife, to transport firearms or equipment and supplies which are intended for or in support of hunting, or to transport hunters to and from the location of wildlife is prohibited in these areas."

- No sign does not mean no closure (99%).
- ★ [[Motor Vehicle Closed Area]]: no motor vehicle for any purpose (99%, page 10).
- ▲, ◆ and ●: that vehicle type may not be used to hunt or to carry hunters, gear or game (99%).
- [[ATV]] (all terrain vehicle) closures here include motorcycles and electric bicycles (99%).

> [!rule]
> In the Omineca, the bull you can shoot is a spike fork. Count tines twice, pull the trigger once.

> [!mistake]
> - Shooting a 3 point bull moose because it "looked small". Only spike fork bulls are open.
> - Running the quad out at 6 am in September in MU 7-12. The ATV hours ban covers that.
> - Shooting a 2 point mule deer buck near Prince George in October. MUs 7-12 to 7-14, 7-24 and 7-25 are 4 point only.

> [!field]
> Before each trip: name your MU, read its rows, then the access hours. Note the time the ATV ban lifts that day.

## Grizzly bears on Vancouver Island

> [!law] Notice!, page 19
> "There have been a number of Grizzly Bear sightings on northern Vancouver Island in recent years. Appropriate safety precautions should be taken, and hunters are reminded that there is no open season for Grizzly Bears in B.C. Please report any sightings of a Grizzly Bear on Vancouver Island (which would be brown in colour compared to the uniformly black colour of Black Bears on Vancouver Island) to the Nanaimo Regional office, (250) 751-7220, or the 24-hour call center line at 1-877-952-7277. For more information on bear safety visit: www.gov.bc.ca/bear-smart"

- No [[Grizzly bear]] season anywhere in BC (99%, page 19).
- Island black bears are uniformly black. A brown bear on the Island is likely a grizzly: do not shoot, report it (99%).
- Report to Nanaimo, 250 751 7220, or the 24 hour line, 1 877 952 7277 (99%).

## Regional bag limits

> [!law] Regional Bag Limits, page 20
> "Deer: The bag limit for mule (black-tailed) deer is 3, of which only two may be antlerless from Special Areas and only two may be bucks."
>
> "The bag limit for antlerless mule (black-tailed) deer taken during the youth only season or the youth bow only season in MUs 1-1, 1-2, 1-4, 1-5, and 1-6 is one."
>
> "Grouse: The daily bag limit for blue (sooty) and ruffed grouse is 5 each. See page 12 for additional information on bag limits."

- Up to 3 black tailed deer a licence year in Region 1. No more than 2 bucks. No more than 2 [[Antlerless]], and those only from the Special Areas (99%, page 20).
- Youth antlerless deer in MUs 1-1, 1-2, 1-4, 1-5 and 1-6: 1 per youth season (99%).
- Grouse: 5 sooty (blue) and 5 ruffed a day (99%).
- The page 14 table agrees: Region 1 mule deer 2 bucks, 2 antlerless, 3 total; white tailed deer 0; regional limit 3, provincial limit 3 (99%, page 14).
- Black tails on the Island are not mule deer bucks from Regions 3 to 8, so an Island buck does not use up your one Interior mule deer buck (95%, my reading of page 14). The provincial cap of 3 deer still counts all of them (99%, page 14).

## Ungulate health: AHD and CWD

> [!law] Ungulate Health, page 20
> "AHD was first identified in the fall of 2020 on several Gulf Islands (Galiano, Mayne, Pender, and Salt Spring) and southern Vancouver Island as far north as Merville. AHD is a highly contagious and often fatal disease, particularly in black-tailed deer."
>
> "Although there is no known risk to human health from AHD, hunters are advised not to consume meat from animals found dead, obviously ill or acting abnormally prior to death. To help prevent the spread of AHD, dispose of carcasses by deep burial, do not move infected, dead or live deer to new areas, and prevent aggregations of deer at artificial feeding or water sources."
>
> "Help protect B.C. ungulates by understanding these risks, helping to increase awareness, and submitting your deer, elk, moose or caribou for testing. Learn more about the BC CWD Program at www.gov.bc.ca/chronicwastingdisease and AHD at www.gov.bc.ca/wildlifehealth."

- AHD (Adenovirus Hemorrhagic Disease): found in 2020 on Galiano, Mayne, Pender and Salt Spring, and south Island to Merville (99%).
- Signs: hard breathing, foaming or drooling, diarrhea, seizures; often found dead in good condition (99%, page 20).
- Do not eat meat from an animal found dead, sick or acting oddly (99%).
- Do not move deer, dead or alive. Bury carcasses deep (99%).
- [[CWD]] (Chronic Wasting Disease) is not known on the Island, but urine scents and carcasses could bring it in. Submit heads for testing (99%).

> [!rule]
> On the Island, the map decides the gun. Rifle, shotgun slug, shot only or bow: find your spot on the map before you load.

> [!mistake]
> - Shooting an antlerless black tail in rifle country because "the deer bag is 3". Antlerless is Special Areas, youth seasons or a draw only.
> - Setting a wireless trail camera in Region 1 in July, thinking the 1 August date applies. Region 1 bans them for hunting with no dates.
> - Hunting a Gulf Island without the Special Area Licence, the $100,000 insurance or the owner's permission.

> [!field]
> Before each Island trip: name your MU, check the bow only and shot only maps for that MU, then read its rows on the season screens.

## Vehicle closures: how to read them


| Symbol | Region 3 closure (99%, pages 10 and 33) |
|---|---|
| ★ | No driving at all |
| ▲ | No vehicle for hunting |
| ◆ ❄ | No ATV, or no snowmobile, for hunting |

```more the page 10 and 33 words
> [!law] Access Management Areas, page 33
> "Region 3 Motor Vehicle Prohibition maps are available online at www.gov.bc.ca/thompsonaccess."
>
> "Information signs are posted at the points of closure for the benefit of hunters, but it is the hunter’s responsibility to recognize closures whether a sign is in place or not. Contact Kamloops Regional Office for details."

> [!law] Motor Vehicle Prohibitions and Restrictions, page 10
> "Unless specifically exempted, all motor vehicle prohibitions include private land. Unless specifically included, motor vehicle prohibitions do not include highways (as defined in the Motor Vehicle Act)."

- No sign does not mean no closure (99%, page 33).
- ★ [[Motor Vehicle Closed Area]]: no driving at all, for any purpose (99%, page 10).
```

> [!why]
> Everything in Reading the Land ends up on one map: the law, the roads, the pressure, the habitat, the terrain, the wind and your own sign. Build it once, keep it alive, and every trip starts with a plan.

```diagram rq-map-stack
```

- Six layers, bottom up: law first, your sign last (Tip).
- Use the app's Hunt Map at [#/map](#/map), plus the Rule Book.

```more what this session covers
- A method, not a single map: the same 6 layers work for Heffley Creek deer, Okanagan quail or a Fraser Valley slough.
- Tools: the Hunt Map in this app (layers, waypoints, lines, areas, range rings, scent cone, elevation profile, slope and aspect shading, offline areas), iMapBC from the BC government, the Rule Book, and your Journal.
- Each layer links back to the session that teaches it.
- Start small: one area you can walk across in a morning, about 2 to 5 km2 (Tip).
- Next session puts it to work: [Field drill: read a piece of land](#/s/land-drill-heffley/0).
```

## Watch it build

```anim rq-map-layers-anim Six layers, one map.
```

- Each layer answers one question (Tip).
- Can I? Can I get there? Will they be there? How do I come in?

```more the questions each layer answers
1. Legal base: can I hunt here at all? (MU, land status)
2. Closures: can I shoot here, and where can I not drive?
3. Access and pressure: how do I get in, and where will other hunters be?
4. Habitat and season: will the animal be here this month?
5. Terrain and wind: where exactly, and from which side do I come in?
6. Your sign and seats: what did I actually see, and where do I sit?
```

## Your tools

```diagram rq-map-tools
```

- Hunt Map for building; Rule Book for checking; Journal for truth (Tip).
- Download the area offline before you go (Tip).

```more the tools in full
- Hunt Map layers in this app include: private land, parks, reserves, city and town limits, WMAs (Wildlife Management Areas), MUs (Management Units), LEH (Limited Entry Hunting) zones, motor vehicle closures, Forest Service roads, recreation sites and trails, winter range by species, burns by year, cutblocks by age, wetlands, habitat zones, month layers for big game, duck waters, quail habitat, candidate spots, routes and camps. Each shows its source, data date and certainty (90 to 95% for official layers, 60% for pattern layers).
- Hunt Map tools: waypoints with hunting icons (parking, glassing point, bed, rub, stand and more), lines with distance and climb, areas in hectares, range rings at 100, 200 and 300 m, scent cone from the forecast wind (Tip), elevation profile, slope and aspect shading, legal light and moon at a point, GPX and KML import and export, offline areas.
- iMapBC: the BC government map viewer for land status and official layers (90%).
- No Shooting maps (C7, C11, C12, C17 and others) live in the synopsis and the [Region 3 Rule Book](#/s/rb-region-3/0). Draw them on your map yourself (Tip).
```

## Layer 1: the legal base

```steps Build the map in 6 layers
rq-map-step-1 | Legal base: MU lines and land status.
rq-map-step-2 | Closures, No Shooting areas, 100 m rings.
rq-map-step-3 | Open roads and the pressure band.
rq-map-step-4 | Habitat and season.
rq-map-step-5 | Terrain and wind.
rq-map-step-6 | Your sign, seats and routes.
```

- Layer 1: which MU, and whose land (99% rules).
- Near the base, the North Thompson is the MU line: 3-27 east, 3-28 west (85 to 90%).

```more layer 1 in full
- Turn on Management Units and their numbers, then private land, parks, reserves, city limits and WMAs.
- Your MU sets your season and class: see [Local plan: Heffley Creek](#/s/local-heffley/0). In October any mule deer buck is legal in your MUs; from 1 November a buck needs 4 points (99%, synopsis page 34).
- River rule: where a river is an MU line, the line follows the right bank as you face downstream (98%, Management Unit Regulation). At Heffley Creek the water is in 3-27 (80%, interpretation).
- Private land: no hunting without permission. Cultivated land and grazing leases with livestock need consent (99%, page 9). "Indian Reserves are private land." (99%, page 9)
- Parks: some allow hunting in season (Lac du Bois outside the city line, Roche Lake, Bonaparte), some do not (Paul Lake: treat as closed) (95 to 99%).
- Conservation lands and WMAs: "Before hunting in a Conservation Land, please contact your regional office." (99%, page 9)
```

## Layer 2: closures and No Shooting

```diagram rq-map-step-2
```

- Draw the No Shooting maps and a 100 m ring round every house and barn (99%).
- Mark motor vehicle closures (99%).

```more layer 2 in full
- No hunting or shooting within 100 m of a dwelling, or a farm or ranch building occupied by people or animals (99%, page 10). The Hunt Map range rings (100 m) make this quick (Tip).
- Not within 15 m of the centre of a numbered highway or 2 lane public road (99%, page 10).
- Near the base: Kamloops city limits (no firearm discharge at all), Map C7 Kamloops band, Map C11 Sun Peaks (400 m from lifts, runs and facilities), Map C12 McQueen Lake (1 km), Map C17 Tranquille (99%).
- Motor vehicle closures: Map C15 Lac du Bois and Dewdrop, Map C16 Dewdrop Rosseau Creek WMA, Map C13 Skull Mountain (99%). ATV (All Terrain Vehicle) for hunting closed in 3-28, 3-29, 3-30 from 1 September to 10 December (99%).
- Paint all of it the same "no go" colour (Tip).
```

## Layer 3: access and pressure

```diagram rq-pr-road-bands
```

- Mark every open road, gate and parking spot (Tip).
- Shade about 1 km around open roads: that is where most hunters are (90%).

```more layer 3 in full
- Forest Service roads and recreation sites from the Hunt Map; check closures and road notices before each trip (99% for the need to check; see the Local plan for links).
- Pressure band: 73% of hunters within 0.5 km of a road, average farthest 0.84 km (90%, Pennsylvania). See [Hunting pressure](#/s/pressure-push/0).
- Route in and route out: draw them as lines, and check the climb with the elevation profile (Tip).
- Parking: wide pullouts, never a blind corner (Tip).
```

## Layer 4: habitat and season

```diagram rq-map-step-4
```

- Winter range, burns, cutblocks, wetlands, water (95% layers).
- Then the month: where will they be this week? (60 to 85%)

```more layer 4 in full
- Mule deer in October: mid slope fir and burn edges if mild; lower south and west slopes after the first real snow (75 to 85%). See [Migration routes in BC](#/s/bc-migration-routes/0) and [Winter ranges](#/s/winter-range-legal/0).
- Burns and cutblocks by age: a south facing burn or cutblock with 5 to 15 years of regrowth is a classic deer glassing target (Tip).
- Water in a dry fall, and edges where food meets cover: see [Food, water and cover](#/s/food-water-cover/0).
- Ducks: plateau ponds until freeze up, then valley water (75%). Upland: aspen and creek bottoms for ruffed grouse, rock for chukar (75 to 85%).
- Month layers on the Hunt Map are a general pattern (60%). Your sign beats them.
```

## Layer 5: terrain and wind

```diagram rq-map-step-5
```

- Aspect, steepness, benches, saddles, draws (Tip).
- Thermals: up in the morning, down in the evening (80%).

```more layer 5 in full
- Turn on aspect shading: south and west faces warm first and hold winter range (85% for winter range aspect).
- Turn on slope shading: steep, broken ground is where pushed deer and chukar go (Tip).
- Mark benches, saddles and draws: the funnels deer travel. See [Maps 1: reading terrain](#/s/maps-terrain/0) and [Trails and funnels](#/s/funnels/0).
- For each glassing point, write the wind it needs. Morning: glass from above; evening: from below or across (Tip). See [Wind and thermals](#/s/wind-thermals/0).
- Scent cone tool: shows where your scent goes with the forecast wind. A guide, not a promise: valley winds swirl (Tip).
```

## Layer 6: your sign, seats and routes

```diagram rq-map-legend
```

- Pin every track, rub, bed, scat pile and sighting (Tip).
- Two seats per area: one for each common wind (Tip).

```more layer 6 in full
- Waypoint icons: glassing point, bed, rub, stand, parking and more. Add the date and a note (Tip).
- Range rings from a seat: 100, 200 and 300 m (109, 219, 328 yd). Know your distances before an animal shows up (Tip).
- Sign tells you more than any layer. Fresh sign moves your seat (Tip). See [Reading sign](#/s/sign/0).
- Rules for gadgets: no drone on the trip, and no drone information from less than 6 hours before it (99%, page 11, item 36). No wireless (cell) trail camera for hunting from 1 August to 10 December outside Region 1 (99%, item 30). No hunting on location info from an aircraft (99%, item 34).
```

## A finished map

```diagram rq-map-example
```

- Red: no go. Tan: pressure. Green: habitat. Pins: your sign (Tip).
- This example is a made up area.

```more how to read the example
- The glassing point sits across from the south facing burn, with a note: "AM: N wind". Assumption in the example: a north wind carries your scent away from the burn.
- The trail from the burn to the road has two fresh sign pins: the route is in use.
- The private block is painted no go, so the deer that feed there are hunted only where they cross onto Crown land, and only with a safe backstop (Tip).
- Park well away from the trail so your truck and scent stay off it (Tip).
```

## Keep the map alive

```diagram rq-map-gonogo
```

- Check it the night before; update it the night after (Tip).
- 1 November changes the mule deer class in Region 3 (99%).

```more what changes during your season
- 1 November: mule deer bucks need 4 points in your MUs (99%).
- Road closures and fire restrictions change during the fall: check the Thompson Rivers road safety page and BC Wildfire Service (99% for the need; see [Local plan](#/s/local-heffley/0)).
- Snow moves deer and freezes ponds (75 to 85%).
- Wind changes which seat you use (Tip).
- Download the area for offline use before every trip (Tip).
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): A good map is half law, half dirt. Draw the law at home and the dirt with your boots.

## Common mistakes

```diagram rq-map-gonogo
```

> [!mistake]
> - Building the habitat layer before checking it is legal ground.
> - Trusting the month layer over fresh sign.
> - Never updating the map after a trip.

```more why each one hurts
- A perfect bench on private land or inside a No Shooting map is worth nothing (99% rules).
- Month layers are a general pattern (60%). Fresh tracks are today (Tip).
- An old map leads you to last month's deer (Tip).
```

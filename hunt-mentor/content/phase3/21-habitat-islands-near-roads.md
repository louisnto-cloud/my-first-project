- Close to a road does not mean empty, if it is legal and safe (Tip).

## Where hunters actually go

```diagram rl-is-road-chart
```

- In one Pennsylvania forest, 73% of hunters were within 0.5 km of a road (85%).
- Hunters thought they went 2.5 times farther than they did (80%).

```more the study in full
- Luloff, Diefenbach, Stedman and others tracked deer hunters with GPS (Global Positioning System) units on Sproul State Forest, Pennsylvania, 2001 and 2002 (85%).
- 56% of the study area was within 0.5 km of a road open to vehicles, and the model estimated 73% of hunters hunted there: hunter density near roads was over 2 times higher (85%).
- "The average maximum distance hunters reached from a road open to public vehicles was 0.84 km"; they walked 5.48 km a day (85%).
- Hunters were 1.25 times more likely to use an area for every 10 degree drop in slope (85%).
- Hunters reported going more than 2.5 times farther from the road than their GPS showed (80%, journal abstract via search preview).
- This is one forest in Pennsylvania. BC patterns are likely similar but not measured here (60%, Tip).
```

## What an island looks like

```diagram rl-is-map
```

- A timber patch, a brushy draw, a slough, a steep pocket (Tip).
- Small, close, awkward, or between two busy spots (Tip).

```more more island types
- A small patch of fir or aspen in open grassland (Tip).
- A brushy draw or creek strip running down to a road or a field (Tip).
- A cattail slough or beaver pond in a farm or ranch corner: often private (99%, permission needed).
- A steep pocket of cover just below a road where nobody wants to climb back up (Tip).
- An island in a river or the far shore of a lake, reached by your kayak or boat (Tip). See the water rules below.
- The far side of a creek or a fence nobody wants to cross (Tip).
- A thicket between two popular parking spots (Tip).
```

## Why good spots get skipped

```diagram rl-is-skipped
```

- Too small, too close, too awkward, in between (Tip).
- Each is a reason to look twice (Tip).

```more how animals use them
- Pressured deer and elk move away from people, often into thick cover, steep ground or areas far from roads (75%). See [Hunting pressure](#/s/pressure-push/0).
- Some animals do the opposite: they sit tight in small cover close to roads while hunters walk past (Tip, hunter observation).
- Elk security area ideas in US planning focus on big blocks far from open roads (60%, Hillis 1991, debated). Small islands work better for deer, birds and bears than for elk (Tip).
```

## The rules come first

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram rb-road-allowance
```

- No shooting across or within a road allowance, and no hunting within it (99%).
- 15 m each side of the middle on roads under 3 lanes (99%).

```more every rule that bites near roads
- Road allowance: no discharge across or within it, and no hunting within it, on numbered highways and 2 lane or greater public roads maintained by the province or others (99%). See [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/0).
- Listed highways also have 400 m bands. Example: Hwy 5 between Hope and Kamloops bans single projectiles within 400 m of the road allowance; Hwy 5 north of Kamloops is not on that list (95%, my reading in the Rule Book).
- 100 m: no hunting or shooting within 100 m of a dwelling, an occupied farm or ranch building, a school, a church, a playground or a regional district park (99%).
- Inside a BC Park: no hunting or shooting within 400 m of a park road or highway (99%).
- No hunting from a motor vehicle (99%, item 22). No loaded firearm in or on a vehicle (99%, item 28).
- Private land needs permission (99%). Check the Private land layer and Reserves on the Hunt Map (99%, app layers).
- Local No Shooting Areas and bylaws: check the Rule Book region pages (99%).
```

## Safe shots near roads

```diagram hf-backstop
```

- Earth backstop behind every shot. Never toward a road, house or trail (Tip).
- People see and hear shots near roads (Tip).

```more safety and ethics
- A .308 bullet can travel kilometres if it misses or passes through. Only shoot into a solid earth backstop (Tip). See [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/0).
- Walk well past the road allowance before you load, and unload before you return (99% for the allowance rule; Tip for the habit). See the road walk animation in the Rule Book.
- Gut piles in view of a road upset people. Move them or hide them where allowed (Tip).
- Close spots get checked by conservation officers too. Have your licence and tags ready (Tip).
```

## Water as your access

```photo hab-osoyoos-wetland Calm marsh edges: a kayak reaches what boots cannot.
```

- A kayak or your 10 ft boat reaches islands and far shores others skip (Tip).
- Boat rules are strict: read them first (99%).

```more boat rules and safety
- No hunting wildlife other than migratory birds from a boat being propelled by a motor (99%, item 23).
- Migratory birds: no shooting from or with a moving boat with a motor or sail; a boat still gliding from the motor counts as moving (99%, item 24).
- Since 1 October 2026 a loaded firearm in a boat is banned except when it is not being propelled by its motor (99%, FVPA (Firearm Violence Prevention Act), as carried in the Rule Book). See [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0).
- River islands may be private, parks, reserves or sanctuaries. Check land status before you land (99%).
- Cold water kills. Wear a PFD (Personal Flotation Device) and know the 1 10 1 principle (Tip). See [Water safety](#/s/water-safety/0).
```

## Find islands on the Hunt Map

```steps Find islands in 5 steps
rl-hm-sat | Satellite: small dark patches in pale ground.
rl-hm-layers | Layers: Private land, Parks, Reserves, closures.
rl-hm-tools | Measure: how far from the road and the nearest house.
rl-hm-tools | Slope steepness: steep pockets near the road.
rl-hm-spot | Waypoint it, then check it on foot.
```

- Look for cover between 100 m and 500 m from a road, legal and safe (Tip).

```more map tips
- Range rings from your truck spot show 100, 200 and 300 m (99%, app). Anything inside 100 m of a house is out (99%, Rule Book).
- Forest Service roads and Recreation trails layers show where other people drive and walk (99%, app layers).
- What can I see from here shows whether a road or a house can see your spot: if they can, so can people, and your shots point that way (Tip).
```

## How to hunt an island

```steps Hunt a small island
sg-stand-2 | Check the wind for the hour you go.
rl-ed-sit | Sit the edge of it, downwind, with a backstop.
sb-upland-pinch | Or one walks it slowly while one watches the end.
```

- Hunt it once and well. Small cover burns out fast (Tip).

```more options
- Option A, sit: watch the island's edge at dawn or dusk from downwind (Tip).
- Option B, walk it: one hunter walks slowly through into the wind while your brother watches the far exit, both with agreed zones of fire (Tip). See [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/0).
- Option C, glass: glass it from the road or a pullout without a firearm in hand, then plan (Tip). Searching for wildlife with a firearm in your possession counts as hunting (99%, definition in the Rule Book), so never do it from the truck.
- My lean: start with A. It disturbs the least.
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

```photo rl-edge-grass-aspen A small patch of cover can hold a big deer.
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The spot everybody drives past is the spot nobody hunts.

> [!mistake]
> - Shooting toward a road, a house or a trail.
> - Hunting inside the road allowance or 100 m of a house.
> - Walking a small patch over and over until it is empty.

```more the mistakes in full
- No earth backstop, no shot (Tip).
- Road allowance and 100 m rules apply to every island near a road (99%).
- Small cover holds few animals. Pick the right wind and hunt it rarely (Tip).
```

## Do this in the field

> [!field]
> On your next drive, list three small patches of cover within 500 m of the road. Check each on the map for rules and land status before you set foot.

```checklist rl-is-field
List three islands within 500 m of a road
Check private land, parks, reserves and No Shooting Areas
Measure distance to every house and the road allowance
Pick a backstop and a wind for each
Hunt the best one once, at dawn or dusk
```

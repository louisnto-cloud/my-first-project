## The 10 minute map read

```diagram rl-rt-read
```

- Same 8 checks, same order, every new piece of ground (Tip).
- Law first. Wind last. Everything else in between.

```more each check in detail
1. **Law:** MU (Management Unit), open season, land status, parks, reserves, No Shooting Areas. See [Rule Book](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/0).
2. **Access:** roads, gates, Motor vehicle closures, the 1,700 m rule in Regions 3 and 8 (99%, Rule Book).
3. **Shape:** ridges, spurs, draws, saddles, benches, knobs, bowls. See [Landforms 101](#/s/landforms-101/0).
4. **Face:** aspect for today's weather. See [Slope and aspect](#/s/slope-aspect/0).
5. **Water:** blue lines, dashed lines, green spots. See [Water on the land](#/s/water-on-land/0).
6. **Cover:** edges, cutblocks by age, burns by year. See [Edges](#/s/edges/0).
7. **Pressure:** roads, trails, rec sites, where trucks park. See [Islands of habitat](#/s/habitat-islands/0).
8. **Wind:** forecast wind and thermals for your seat and approach. See [Wind and thermals](#/s/wind-thermals/0).
```

## From the truck: what to notice

```photo hab-north-thompson-burn A burn above the North Thompson: new feed under dead trees.
```

- Faces: grass on warm faces, timber on cool ones (80%).
- Burns, cutblocks, benches, saddles on the skyline (Tip).

```more the driving scan
- Name the aspect of each face you pass and what grows on it (Tip). See [Slope and aspect](#/s/slope-aspect/0).
- Find the snowline and put a height on it (Tip). See [Elevation bands](#/s/elevation-snowline/0).
- Look for green in brown: springs, seeps, brushy draws (Tip).
- Note burns and cutblocks, and how old they look (Tip).
- Note where other trucks park: that is where the pressure starts (Tip).
- Note gates, closure signs and road conditions for the next trip (Tip). See [Forest roads, vehicles and radio](#/s/fsr-vehicles-radio/0).
```

## Tracks on the road

```diagram rl-rt-tracks
```

- A dirt road is a tracking strip: count the crossings (Tip).
- Fresh crossings mark live trails between bed and feed (Tip).

```more reading road crossings
- Best right after rain or fresh snow: every track is new (75%). See [Snow tracking](#/s/snow-tracking/0) and [Aging tracks](#/s/rs-track-aging/0).
- Mark each fresh crossing with a waypoint and the direction of travel (Tip).
- Two crossings close together often mean a pinch nearby (Tip). See [Pinch points](#/s/pinch-points/0).
- Drive slowly and pull fully off the road to look. Logging trucks have the right of way on many Forest Service roads (Tip). See [Forest roads, vehicles and radio](#/s/fsr-vehicles-radio/0).
```

## The rules for the truck

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram rl-rt-pullout
```

- No hunting from a motor vehicle. No loaded firearm in or on one (99%).
- Searching for wildlife with a firearm in your possession counts as hunting (99%).

```more the exact rules and my reading
> [!law] Item 22
> "To hunt wildlife from a motor vehicle."

> [!law] Item 28
> "To discharge, carry or have in possession a firearm containing live ammunition in its breech or in its magazine attached to the firearm, in or on a railway car, motor vehicle, sleigh, aircraft, bicycle or other conveyance."

> [!law] Definition of hunt
> "HUNT AND HUNTING - includes shooting at, attracting, searching for, chasing, pursuing, following after or on the trail of, stalking, or lying in wait for wildlife or attempting to do any of those things, whether or not the wildlife is then or subsequently wounded, killed or captured: (a) with intention to capture the wildlife, or (b) while in possession of a firearm or other weapon."

- My reading (85%, VERIFY with a conservation officer): glassing for game from the truck with a firearm in it can count as hunting from a motor vehicle. Scout from the truck without a firearm, or step away from the truck to glass with the rifle unloaded and cased inside.
- When you decide to hunt: walk away from the truck and out of the road allowance before you load (99% for the allowance rule).
- Rules carried in full: [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0), [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions/0), [Rule Book: where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/0).
- No hunting with a light: no spotlight, headlamp or truck lights to hunt by (99%, item 38).
```

## Glass from a pullout

```steps Glass from a pullout in 4 steps
rl-rt-pullout | Park fully off the road, safely.
hf-bino-brace | Brace your binoculars on the hood or a post.
glassing-grid | Glass in strips: benches, edges, draw heads.
rl-hm-spot | Drop a waypoint on what you find.
```

- Glass the faces that fit the time and weather first (Tip).

```more glassing tips from the road
- Early morning: sunny east faces and edges. Midday: shade and benches. Evening: west faces and feeding edges (Tip). See [Glassing](#/s/glassing/0).
- Use the truck hood, a fence post or a tripod for a steady view (Tip).
- Put a number on every animal you see: where, when, which face, which way it went (Tip). It goes in the Journal.
- Other people use pullouts too. Never point binoculars or a scope at houses or people (Tip).
```

## Map and truck together

```diagram rl-eb-read-snow
```

- Match what you see to the map: the snow edge, a burn, a bench (Tip).
- The Hunt Map height readout puts a number on it (99%, app).

```more matching view to map
- Centre the map on the feature you see, tilt it in 3D, and rotate it to face the same way you do. The match becomes obvious (Tip).
- Use your phone compass: point the top of the map where you are looking (Tip). See [Compass mastery](#/s/compass-mastery/0).
- Draw an elevation profile from the road to the bench you see: it tells you the climb before you leave the truck (99%, app tool).
```

```steps Turn a drive into a plan

```

- Write the plan in the Journal and your trip plan (Tip).

```more planning details
- Route: follow benches and contours, approach from downwind, stay off the skyline and out of the bedding cover (Tip). See [Route planning](#/s/route-planning/0).
- Time: be at the seat before first light or 2 hours before dark (Tip).
- Leave a trip plan with someone at home (Tip). See [Bush safety](#/s/bush-safety/0).
- Plan B for the opposite wind (Tip).
```

## The scouting loop

```diagram rl-rt-loop
```

- Map at home, drive, mark, walk, journal, back to the map (Tip).
- Each lap makes your map truer (Tip).

```more why the loop matters
- Maps go out of date: new cutblocks, new burns, closed roads, new houses (Tip).
- Your Journal is the real map: where you saw animals, sign, other hunters, the snowline by date (Tip).
- After three laps you will know a piece of ground better than most people who drive past it (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

```photo hab-lac-du-bois-grassland Read it from the road, then walk it slow.
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The truck is for looking. Your boots are for hunting. Never mix them up.

> [!mistake]
> - Glassing from the cab with a rifle beside you.
> - Driving the same road at dawn and calling it scouting.
> - Trusting the map over fresh tracks on the road.

```more the mistakes in full
- With a firearm in your possession, searching for wildlife is hunting, and hunting from a motor vehicle is unlawful (99%; applying it to the cab is my reading, 85%).
- Driving at prime time moves animals and wastes the best light. Drive midday, hunt dawn and dusk (Tip).
- Fresh sign beats old maps (Tip).
```

## Do this in the field

> [!field]
> Do the 10 minute map read for one new area at home. Then drive it slowly at midday, without a firearm, and drop five waypoints.

```checklist rl-rt-field
Do the 8 check map read at home
Save the area in Offline Maps on wifi
Drive at midday; name faces, burns, benches, saddles
Count fresh track crossings on the road
Drop five waypoints and write the plan in the Journal
```

## Memory hooks

- **Law first, wind last.**
- **Law, Access, Shape, Face, Water, Cover, Pressure, Wind:** "Lucky Archers Shoot Fast When Calm, Patient, Wise".
- **Truck to look, boots to hunt.**
- **The road is a tracking strip.**
- **Drive midday, hunt the edges of the day.**

```more using the hooks
- Say the 8 checks out loud on every new map until you do them without thinking (Tip).
```

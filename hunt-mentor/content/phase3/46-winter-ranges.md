## What winter range is

```photo g-md-doe-sage-snow Winter range: sage, shrubs and shallow snow.
```

- Steep south and west faces, broken rock, shrubs, old Douglas fir (85%).
- Near Kamloops the studied range is below about 760 m (80%).

```more the biology in full
- BC deer account: Interior winter range is "shrublands in the dry forest zone and on steep south and west facing sites with broken terrain" (85%).
- Snow decides it: deer have "difficulty moving through snow deeper than 30 cm" (85%). Sunny slopes melt first; broken rock and steep ground shed snow (75%, my reading).
- Kamloops study (Willms 1976): Dewdrop range below 760 m north of Kamloops, and Jamieson Creek. Douglas fir in the diet peaked in December and January; shrubs early fall and late winter (80 to 85%).
- Browse on Kamloops winter range: Douglas fir, saskatoon, Oregon grape, red osier dogwood, rose, bitterbrush, rabbitbrush, sagebrush (80%).
- Aspect: south and west, old Douglas fir (85%, Armleder 1994 via research notes).
- Moose winter "along river valleys that cut through the plateaus and in burns, logged areas, and wetland complexes" (85%).
```

## Snow interception cover

```diagram rq-wr-sic
```

- Big fir crowns catch snow; the ground below stays walkable (80%).
- That is why winter range orders protect old fir (99% for the order wording).

```more why the cover matters
- Order u-3-003 makes forest users keep enough "snow interception cover (SIC)" in each winter range planning cell (99%, order read directly).
- Plain English: old, big crowned Douglas fir catches snow, so deer can walk and feed under it in deep snow years (80%, my reading of the BC deer account and the order).
- Fir is also food: Kamloops deer ate the most Douglas fir in December and January (80%).
- In the field: a south slope with scattered big fir and a shrub understory is the classic look (Tip).
```

## Four rule books, not one

```diagram rq-wr-legal
```

- A UWR (Ungulate Winter Range) order covers logging and roads (99%).
- Hunting is set by the Wildlife Act and its synopsis (99%).

```more who makes which rule
- UWR: made under "sections 9 and 12 of the Government Actions Regulation" of the Forest and Range Practices Act (90%, BC Ministry of Environment UWR page, read directly).
- A 2004 order lists the species a winter range may be made for: mule and black tailed deer, white tailed deer, elk, mountain goat, caribou, bighorn sheep, thinhorn sheep and moose (99%, order read directly, 6 May 2004).
- Seasons, bag limits, closed areas and No Shooting areas: the BC Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis, 2026 to 2028 (99%).
- WMAs (Wildlife Management Areas) and other conservation lands: "Before hunting in a Conservation Land, please contact your regional office." (99%, synopsis page 9)
- Motor vehicle closures: synopsis maps by MU (Management Unit) (99%). See [Where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/0).
- So: a UWR on the map does not by itself close hunting (85%, my reading of the order and synopsis). Check the other three every time.
```

## What a real order says

```diagram rq-wr-order
```

- Order u-3-003, Merritt TSA (Timber Supply Area), mule deer (99%).
- Rules for timber harvest, silviculture and roads. Nothing on hunting (99%).

```more the order, read directly
- "ORDER: UNGULATE WINTER RANGE #U-3-003, Merritt TSA, Mule Deer", given under "sections 9(2) and 12(1) of the Government Actions Regulation" (99%).
- "Primary forest activity" means timber harvesting, silviculture treatments, and road construction, maintenance and deactivation (99%).
- General Wildlife Measure 1: forest activities must not drop snow interception cover below the order's Table 1 amounts (99%).
- Signed 21 January 2008 by the Deputy Minister of Environment (99%).
- The order has no hunting, firearm or motor vehicle wording (99%, read all 2 text pages). Other orders may differ: read the one for your area (Tip).
```

## Can I hunt here?

```diagram rq-wr-flow
```

- Season, closures, land owner, vehicles: in that order (Tip).
- Then hunt on foot, quietly.

```more each question and its rule
- Season: Region 3 mule deer in your MUs: any buck 1 to 31 October; 4 point bucks only 1 November to 10 December (99%, synopsis page 34). White tailed bucks 10 September to 10 December; antlerless white tails 10 to 31 October (99%).
- No Shooting and closed areas: Map C7 Kamloops, C11 Sun Peaks, C12 McQueen Lake, C17 Tranquille; Kamloops city limits (99%). See [Local plan: Heffley Creek](#/s/local-heffley/0).
- Land owner: "A person is not permitted to hunt on cultivated land or on Crown land which is subject to a grazing lease while the land is occupied by livestock, without the consent of the owner, lessee, or occupant of the land." (99%, page 9) Leased land falls under the Trespass Act whether or not cattle are there (99%). Much Kamloops grassland is grazing lease (Tip: check before you walk it).
- Reserves: "Indian Reserves are private land. Permission must be obtained" (99%, page 9).
- Vehicles: ATV (All Terrain Vehicle) for hunting closed in 3-28, 3-29, 3-30, 1 September to 10 December; snowmobile for hunting closed in 3-28 from 1 April to 14 December (99%).
```

## Rules that matter most on winter range

| Rule | Plain English | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Item 25 | No vehicle, boat or machine to herd or harass wildlife | Page 11 (99%) |
| Shooting from a vehicle | Never, moving or parked | Page 11 (99%) |
| 4 point rule | From 1 November, Region 3 mule deer bucks | Page 34 (99%) |
| Season ends | 10 December for Region 3 deer | Page 34 (99%) |
| Conservation lands | Call the regional office first | Page 9 (99%) |

- Winter deer chased with a machine is an offence and a cruelty (Tip).

```more the exact words
> [!law] Item 25, page 11
> "To use a power boat, aircraft, or motor vehicle or other mechanical device to herd or harass wildlife."

- A snowmobile or quad run at deer on winter range is that offence (95%, my reading).
- No loaded firearm in or on a motor vehicle, and no shooting from one (99%, page 11).
- After 10 December, Region 3 deer seasons in your MUs are closed (99%, page 34). Winter is for scouting from a distance (Tip).
- Full list: [It is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0).
```

## Every flight costs fat

```anim rq-wr-disturb-anim A chased deer burns reserves it needs.
```

- Disturbance makes deer burn energy and lose feeding time (70%).
- Hunt the edges, take your shot, leave (Tip).

```more the fact sheets
- Utah and Wyoming winter range fact sheet: human disturbance forces mule deer to use more energy and "can reduce their body condition to levels that influence survival or reproduction" (70%, search preview).
- US Forest Service winter range page: deer that flee use reserves needed to get through winter (70%, search preview).
- Wyoming: as few as 2 to 6 vehicle passes a day displaced mule deer on average 2.61 km (60%, Sawyer and others 2009 as summarized by Backcountry Hunters and Anglers).
- Some studies found mule deer hide in cover rather than run, which still costs feeding time (60%, Wisdom and others 2004 via the same summary).
- The fat bar in the animation is an illustration, not a measurement.
```

## What disturbs them most

```diagram rq-wr-disturb
```

- Dogs strongest, then machines, then people off trail (70%).
- Leave the dog home on winter range (Tip).

```more details
- Fact sheets: ATVs and mountain bikes cause a stronger flight response than hiking or horseback riding; off trail use causes more avoidance than on trail; dogs increase stress (70%, agency fact sheets, search previews).
- Dogs in some WMAs must be leashed unless lawfully hunting (99%, WMA Use and Access Regulation, research notes). Check the WMA's own rules.
```

## Hunt the edge, legally

```steps Hunt a winter range edge in 5 steps
rq-wr-step-1 | Find it on the Hunt Map.
rq-wr-step-2 | Check the season and the class.
rq-wr-step-3 | Park legally, walk in.
rq-wr-step-4 | Glass the edge from across the draw.
rq-wr-step-5 | Take your shot or leave quietly.
```

- Glass, do not walk the range (Tip).

```more the 5 steps in full
1. Hunt Map: winter range layers, MU lines, closures, private land and WMAs (95% for the layers).
2. Season and class for today: from 1 November a Region 3 mule deer buck needs 4 points on one antler (99%, page 34). Count before you shoot: see [Legal to shoot](#/s/legal-to-shoot/0).
3. Park at an open road. No ATV for hunting in 3-28, 3-29, 3-30 until 10 December (99%).
4. Glass the edge where timber meets open slope at first and last light, from 400 to 800 m across a draw (Tip). See [Glassing](#/s/glassing/0).
5. One clean shot within your range, or back out the way you came. Do not push the same deer twice (Tip).
```

## Common mistakes

```diagram rq-wr-flow
```

> [!mistake]
> - Thinking a winter range layer means "no hunting".
> - Walking a grazing lease with cattle on it without asking.
> - Driving roads on winter range to spot deer, then chasing them.

```more why each one hurts
- UWR orders set forestry rules (99% for the order read). Hunting is set by the synopsis (99%).
- Grazing lease with livestock: consent needed (99%, page 9).
- Chasing with a vehicle is item 25 (99%). Repeat flights cost deer fat (70%).
```

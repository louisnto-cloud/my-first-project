> [!why]
> Big animals still fool beginners: a cow moose for a bull in thick willows, a brown black bear for a grizzly, a coyote for a wolf. One wrong call can be a protected animal.

- Each screen shows one animal or one comparison, from several angles.
- Read the caption, then find the mark it names in the photo.
- Bears: colour does not tell you the species. Shape does (85%).
- Photos come from western North America unless the caption says otherwise.
- Photos are illustrations. The written field marks are the source of truth.

## Bull moose

```gallery
g-moose-bull-side | Bull, side view. Tall shoulders, hump, long legs, flat antler palms.
g-moose-bull-front | Bull, front view, Grand Teton. Antlers spread like two open hands.
g-moose-young-bull | Young bull with small antlers, Grand Teton. Count every point.
```

- Biggest deer in the world; black brown, with a long face and a [[Bell|bell]] under the throat (85%).
- Young bulls carry spikes or small forks; big bulls carry wide palms (85%).
- Some seasons are for [[Spike fork bull|spike fork bulls]] only: no more than 2 points on one antler (99%, page 3, 2 October 2026 edition).

## Sow with cubs

```gallery
g-bb-sow-cubs | Black bear sow with a black cub and a cinnamon cub, Yellowstone.
g-griz-sow | Grizzly sow with a yearling cub, Yellowstone.
```

> [!warn] Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Unlawful: "To hunt a black bear less than 2 years old or any bear in its company" (98%, page 11, item 14).
- Plain English: no cubs, no yearlings, and not the sow with them (98%). Watch any bear for several minutes before you decide (Tip).

## Coyote versus wolf

```gallery
grey-wolf | Grey wolf, Yellowstone. Long legs, big feet, broad muzzle.
coyote | Coyote, Yosemite. Slim, pointed muzzle, tall ears.
@@CANIDS@@
```

- Wolf: 30 to 50 kg in BC; coyote: 9 to 23 kg (85%).
- Wolf: broad blocky muzzle, short rounded ears. Coyote: narrow pointed muzzle, tall pointed ears (85%).
- Coyotes run with the tail down; wolves carry it straight out (Tip).

## Grandpa's rule

> [!rule]
> Bears: look for the hump and the face, never the colour. If you are not sure, it is a grizzly, and you do not shoot.

## Common mistakes

> [!mistake]
> - Calling a brown or cinnamon bear a grizzly, or a black grizzly a black bear. Check hump, face and ears.
> - Shooting a cow moose in willows because only the body showed. Wait to see the head.
> - Shooting a lone bear without waiting for cubs to show up behind it.

## Do this in the field

> [!field]
> - Every bear you see: say "hump or no hump, dish or straight, ears tall or round" before anything else.
> - Every moose: find the head and count points on one antler.
> - Every canid: weigh it in your head. Mallard sized head and slim legs, coyote. Big blocky head, wolf.

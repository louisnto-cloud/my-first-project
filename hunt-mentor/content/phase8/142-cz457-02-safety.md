> [!why]
> A safety is a mechanical part and parts fail. Your habits are the real safety; this chapter wires them to this rifle.

```diagram cz-safety
```

- Back, white dot showing: ON. It locks the trigger (95%).
- Forward: ready to fire (95%).
- The bolt still opens with the safety ON (95%).

```more the safety in the manual's words
- "Shift the safety backward to its “Safety-On” position (Fig. 4) until the white dot is uncovered. This procedure locks the trigger mechanism, and so, it is not possible to discharge a cartridge" (95%, CZ manual page 7).
- Two positions only: on or off (80%, all4shooters 2020: "two position safety behind the bolt handle, acting directly on the trigger").
- The manual's Fig. 4 drawing also shows a red dot behind the safety. My reading: red showing means the safety is forward, off (75%, my reading of the drawing). VERIFY on your rifle, unloaded.
- CZ lists "the possibility to pull the bolt out even with the safety engaged (safe handling when unloading)" as a feature (95%, page 4). So you unload with the safety ON.
- Assumption: Hunt Mentor teaches "safety ON, then unload" because the manual says the bolt opens with it on.
```

## Every handling step starts here

```steps Make the CZ 457 safe in 5 steps
cz-prove-1 | Point it safe. Finger out of the guard.
cz-prove-2 | Safety back. White dot shows.
cz-prove-3 | Press the catch back. Magazine out.
cz-prove-4 | Lift and pull the bolt fully back.
cz-prove-5 | Look and feel: chamber and well empty.
```

- This is [[ACTS]] and [[PROVE]] on your CZ (85%).

```more ACTS and PROVE, the words
- ACTS (the Vital Four): Assume every firearm is loaded. Control the muzzle direction at all times. Trigger finger off the trigger and out of the trigger guard. See that the firearm is unloaded: PROVE it safe (85%, Canadian Firearms Safety Course wording, via the [firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety) lesson).
- PROVE: Point the firearm in the safest available direction. Remove all cartridges. Observe the chamber. Verify the feeding path. Examine the bore (85%).
- CZ's own rules say the same: "Always handle your firearm as if it were loaded" and "Never take anyone's word that the firearm is unloaded" (95%, CZ manual page 2, rules 1 and 3).
- Do it every time you pick up, hand over, case or clean the rifle (80%, firearm safety lesson).
```

## The cocking indicator

```diagram cz-cocking
```

- Sticks out = striker cocked (95%).
- Cocked tells you nothing about a round in the chamber. PROVE it (Tip).

```more the indicator in words
- "When the striker is cocked (activated), its rear part protrudes over the plug (Fig. 5). This protrusion can be checked both by sight and by touch" (95%, CZ manual page 7).
- CZ safety rule 5: "Always store the firearm unloaded with the striking mechanism released" (95%, page 2). That means not cocked, so the indicator is flush.
- To release it on an empty rifle: the manual's unloading steps end with close the bolt, then press the trigger, pointed in a safe direction (95%, page 6). Chapter 3.
- Feel it with your thumb in the dark at the end of a hunt (Tip).
```

## CZ safety rules that matter in the field

| CZ rule | In short |
|---|---|
| 2 | Never point it at anything you will not shoot |
| 12 | Safety on until ready; muzzle safe when you push it off |
| 14 | Finger out of the guard until aiming |
| 15 | Know the target and what is behind it |
| 16 | Never at rock or water |
| 20 | Unloaded before cleaning, storing, travelling |

- All from the CZ manual, pages 2 and 3 (95%).

```more more of the 23 CZ safety rules
- Rule 15: "The bullet can pass through or past the target up to several hundred meters" (95%). Other sources put a .22 at over 1 km (65%, [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22)).
- Rule 16: "Never shoot at a hard surface such as rock, or a liquid surface such as water" (95%).
- Rule 4: unloaded before you lay it down or hand it over (95%).
- Rule 7: "Never leave a firearm unattended" (95%).
- Rule 10: no alcohol or drugs before or during shooting (95%).
- Rule 11: wear eye and ear protection whenever practical (95%).
- Rule 13: nobody in the path of the ejected case (95%).
- Rule 22: "Never alter any parts of your firearm" (95%). Chapter 9 has the legal side.
- Rule 23: a dropped or corroded rifle can be damaged where you cannot see it: have a gunsmith check it (95%).
```

## Click but no bang

```diagram cz-hangfire
```

- Keep it pointed safe. Wait 30 seconds (95%).
- Then eject and look at the case (95%).

```more misfire steps in full
- CZ rule 19: "In case it fails to fire, hold the firearm keeping it pointed in a safe direction and wait 30 seconds. If a hangfire (slow ignition) has occurred, the cartridge will fire within 30 seconds" (95%, CZ manual page 3).
- "If the cartridge does not fire, eject the cartridge and examine it. If the firing pin indent on the cartridge is light, or non-existent, have the firearm examined by a competent gunsmith" (95%).
- A shot that sounds "weak": unload and check the bullet has not stuck in the barrel. "Any subsequent shot could cause damage to the barrel" (95%, page 11).
- Tip: put the dud in a pocket, not on the ground where kids or dogs find it. Dispose of it at a range or gun shop.
```

## Safe with a partner and in the truck

```diagram zones-of-fire
```

- Your zone of fire is in front of you only (Tip).
- In or on any vehicle: chamber and magazine empty (99%).

```more the rules
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- BC: no loaded firearm in or on a vehicle; a firearm is loaded if a cartridge is in the chamber or in a detachable magazine attached to it (99%, synopsis pages 3 and 11, item 28, see [unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts)).
- So with the CZ: magazine out of the rifle and chamber empty before you reach the truck (95%, my reading).
- Zones of fire with a partner: [hunting with a partner](#/s/hunting-partner).
- Storage at home: unloaded, locked or bolt out, ammunition apart: see [firearm care](#/s/firearm-care) (99%, federal storage regulations s. 5).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-safety
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The safety is a switch. Your finger and your muzzle are the brakes.

> [!mistake]
> - Trusting the safety and pointing the muzzle sloppily.
> - Seeing the indicator flush and thinking the rifle is unloaded. It only says "not cocked".
> - Opening the bolt right after a click. Wait the full 30 seconds.

> [!field]
> At the range: run the 5 step safe drill out loud, 10 times, before the first shot.

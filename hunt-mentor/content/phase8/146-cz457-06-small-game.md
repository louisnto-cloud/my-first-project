## Hours, bullets and the bird you keep

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

| Rule | Plain English |
|---|---|
| Hours | 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (99%) |
| Bullets | No full metal jacket non expanding bullets (99%) |
| Grouse meat | Both breasts go home (99%) |

```more the rules in full
- Item 38, page 11: no hunting game, except migratory birds, from one hour after sunset to one hour before sunrise (99%, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)).
- Page 12: "It is unlawful to hunt or trap with a rifle using a full metal jacketed non-expanding bullet" (99%). Most .22 LR hunting loads are lead or copper plated lead round nose or hollow point, not full metal jacket (80%, maker product pages). Read the box (Tip).
- Edible portion of a game bird: both breasts go home (99%, [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts)).
- No light to hunt by, no infrared optics on the trip (99%, page 11).
```

## The shot: earth behind, head only

```diagram cz-backstop
```

- Earth behind every shot (Tip; CZ rule 15, 95%).
- Grouse: head or upper neck (Tip).

```more the shot in full
- A .22 can travel well past 1 km (65%). CZ: "several hundred meters" past the target (95%).
- Never at water or rock (95%, CZ rule 16).
- Grouse, hare: inside about 40 m from a rest (Tip). See [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22) and [grouse: how to hunt](#/s/mc-grouse-09).
- Unload before you walk to the bird (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-legal-grid
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Know the bird, the season, the place and the backstop before the safety comes off.

> [!mistake]
> - Taking the .22 on a quail or duck trip. Shotgun only.
> - Hunting hare in MU 2-4 or 2-3: no hare season there.
> - Shooting from a road edge inside a shot only strip.

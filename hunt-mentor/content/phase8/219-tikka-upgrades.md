## Worth it, in order

```diagram tk-upgrades
```

- 1 Scope, rings and a torque driver (Tip).
- 2 Snap caps and a cleaning kit (95%, the manual asks for both).

```more why each one, with my reasons
1. **Scope, rings, torque driver:** the biggest gain in dawn light and at 150 m plus (Tip). [Chapter 4](#/s/mc-tikka-04).
2. **Snap caps and a cleaning kit:** the manual recommends snap caps for dry fire and lists the cleaning kit (95%, Tikka manual pages 9 and 18). Cheap and used every week.
3. **Shooting sticks:** most Fraser Valley shots will be sitting or standing, not prone (Tip). Sticks make those steady.
4. **Sling:** carry hands free on long walks and a steadier hasty sling position (Tip; see [field positions](#/s/field-positions/0)). Your rifle has standard sling studs front and rear (95%, page 16).
5. **Spare magazine:** a second loaded magazine in a pocket, and a spare if one is lost (Tip). Use only a magazine made for your Tikka (Tip; the manual recommends original parts, 95%).
- Bipod: useful prone or on a bench; heavy and slow in timber. The front stud takes one (95%, page 16). My lean: sticks first, bipod later.
```

## Not worth it yet

```diagram tk-upgrades
```

- The factory trigger is good. Leave it (95%).
- A muzzle brake replaces your front sight and adds noise (75%).

```more why to wait
- **Aftermarket trigger:** Tikka says the action's precision parts "should not be altered, modified or changed" and that a replaced trigger, sear or safety needs the trigger returned to the maker (95%, Tikka manual page 15). Using parts that are not Sako or Tikka can cause malfunctions not covered by the warranty (95%, page 4).
- **Muzzle brake:** the Arctic front sight sits on the muzzle thread (95%, page 11). Recoil's reviewers noted they could not fit a brake "without losing the front sight" (70%). Brakes are louder for you and your partner (Tip). Thread size on your rifle: 5/8 x 24 (70%), VERIFY.
- **New stock or chassis:** the laminate stock is stable in wet weather (Tip). Learn this one first.
- **Bigger scope:** 15x or more is heavier and darker at dusk for close black tails (Tip).
- **Hand loading:** voids the Tikka warranty (95%, page 9).
```

## The legal lines

```diagram tk-legal-lines
```

- A suppressor is a prohibited device in Canada (99%).
- Never shorten the barrel or stock (99%).

```more the law
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "prohibited device means ... (c) a device or contrivance designed or intended to muffle or stop the sound or report of a firearm"

- Source: Criminal Code s. 84(1), current to 30 September 2026, read 2026-10-23 (99%). The Tikka manual covers suppressor fitting because some countries allow them; it also says follow national law (95%, page 17).
- Cutting a rifle so it is under 660 mm long, or its barrel under 457 mm: a prohibited firearm (99%, s. 84(1)). Chapter 1 has the full text.
- Magazines: the 5 round limit is for magazines designed for semi automatic firearms (99%, SOR/98-462 Part 4 s. 3). Never alter a magazine (Tip). Chapter 2.
- Muzzle brakes are not named in s. 84 (95%). Ask the store and a qualified gunsmith before fitting one (Tip).
- Anything that changes how the action works, its barrel length, or its magazine: ask a qualified gunsmith first. Never alter a gun in a way that could change its legal class (Tip).
```

## Fit the rifle to you: spacers and the knob

```diagram tk-lop-spacers
```

- Spacers set length of pull. Over 2 need longer screws (95%).
- An oversized bolt knob helps with gloves (95%).

```more the manual's steps
- Length of pull (95%, Tikka manual page 15): loosen the two recoil pad screws (soapy water around the pad screw holes stops tearing), add or remove spacers, tighten the screws. "Should you need to stack more than two spacers, you will need to replace the recoil screws with longer ones."
- "Make sure to use screws of appropriate length and do not overtighten them. In other case the stock may be damaged beyond repair" (95%).
- The Arctic came with 3 light metal spacers in the box (75%, all4shooters). Check your box.
- Fit test (Tip): rifle mounted in your hunting jacket, your nose about two fingers behind your thumb on the grip. Winter jacket means fewer spacers.
- Oversized bolt knob: push it over the original knob and tighten the screw inside it, 5 to 10 turns, holding the knob still (95%, page 17).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram tk-legal-lines
```

- **"Glass, practice, then parts."**
- **"Can is a crime."** A suppressor is a prohibited device in Canada.
- **"Two spacers, short screws; three, long screws."**

```more mistakes, rule and the field task
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Buy ammunition before accessories."

> [!mistake]
> - Buying a trigger kit before a box of practice ammunition.
> - Ordering a suppressor online from a US site. It is prohibited here.
> - Over tightening the recoil pad screws and cracking the stock.

> [!field]
> Write your shopping list in the order on this page, with a price checked date. Buy the first two items before the next range day.
```

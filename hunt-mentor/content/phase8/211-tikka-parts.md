## The action, right side

```diagram tk-action-right
```

- Safety catch: just behind the bolt handle, right side (95%).
- Magazine release: a two winged button at the front of the trigger guard (75%).

```more each part in plain words
- **Bolt handle:** lift, back, forward, down. Lifting cocks the firing pin (95%, Tikka manual page 7).
- **Bolt shroud:** the cap at the back of the bolt. Stays on for normal cleaning (95%, page 13).
- **Cocking indicator:** shows when the firing pin is cocked, by sight and by feel (95%, page 8).
- **Safety catch:** "located on the side of the receiver just behind the bolt handle" (95%, page 8). Chapter 2 covers it in full.
- **Rear sight drum:** six range steps, 100 to 600 m (95%, page 12). Chapter 3.
- **Picatinny rail:** about 122 mm (4.8 in) long, flush with the front of the receiver (75%, all4shooters test). Chapter 4.
- **Ejection port:** cases leave here. Never put a finger in it while the bolt is closing (Tip).
- **Magazine release:** "the button located at the front of the enlarged trigger guard"; two winged, so it works from either side (75%, all4shooters test).
- **Magazine:** a removable double stack steel box (75%); 10 rounds (70%, retailers and the Recoil review).
- **Trigger:** two stage (95%, Sako). Factory weight 13 to 15 N (newtons), about 3 lb (95%, page 15).
- **Trigger guard:** enlarged at the bottom so you can shoot with gloves (75%, all4shooters).
```

## The left side and underneath

```diagram tk-action-left
```

- Bolt stop: press it to take the bolt out (95%).
- Serial number: left side. Write it down at home (Tip).

```more the left side in full
- Bolt stop or bolt release (part A3 in the manual): to remove the bolt, lift the handle, press the bolt stop and pull the bolt fully out (95%, Tikka manual pages 8 and 13).
- Model and serial number (A4): stamped on the receiver (95%, page 7 figure 3).
- Receiver screws (A5): two Torx screws hold the barrelled action in the stock. Tighten to 7 Nm (newton metres), 62 in lb, with a metal trigger guard; max 5 Nm (44 in lb) with a plastic one (95%, page 13). Chapter 7 shows when to touch them.
- The notice on page 13: the pin in the bolt stop is spring loaded and can come out "if the components are handled carelessly" (95%). Do not pry at it.
- Tip: photograph the serial number and keep it with your licence number. If the rifle is ever stolen, the police need it.
```

## Barrel length and the law

```diagram tk-barrel-legal
```

- Factory barrel: 508 mm. Never cut it (99%).
- The law counts the chamber, not a muzzle device (99%).

```more the law, word for word
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "prohibited firearm means ... (b) a firearm that is adapted from a rifle or shotgun, whether by sawing, cutting or any other alteration, and that, as so adapted, (i) is less than 660 mm in length, or (ii) is 660 mm or greater in length and has a barrel less than 457 mm in length"

- Source: Criminal Code s. 84(1), current to 30 September 2026, read 2026-10-23 (99%).
- How it is measured: "the distance from the muzzle of the barrel to and including the chamber, but does not include the length of any component, part or accessory including any component, part or accessory designed or intended to suppress the muzzle flash or reduce recoil" (99%, s. 84(2)).
- Plain English: your factory 508 mm barrel is well over 457 mm. Cutting a rifle barrel or stock short can make it a prohibited firearm (99%). A gunsmith job that shortens a barrel is a legal question first: ask a qualified gunsmith and the RCMP (Royal Canadian Mounted Police) Canadian Firearms Program before anything is cut (Tip).
- Measure it yourself (Tip): unloaded and proved, bolt closed, slide a cleaning rod down the bore from the muzzle until it touches the bolt face, mark the rod at the muzzle, measure the mark. Expect about 508 mm (20 in), the length you confirmed.
```

## Before the first shot

```diagram tk-first-prep
```

- New rifle? Six checks from the manual (95%).
- Already shooting it? Do them once anyway (Tip).

```more the manual's list in full
> [!warn]
> All assembly, disassembly and maintenance: firearm unloaded, chamber, receiver and magazine empty. Look through the barrel from the rear. Never point it at anyone or at hard, flat surfaces (95%, Tikka manual page 7).

1. Wipe the oil from the barrel with a dry patch, "starting from the breech end" (95%, page 7).
2. Clean extra oil from the bolt, chamber and breech area (95%).
3. Check the bolt space and locking lugs are clean. Lightly grease the locking lug surfaces, cocking cam and head of the cocking piece (95%). Chapter 7 has the picture.
4. Check the sights are clean and free of debris (95%).
5. Check the tightness of the receiver screws (95%).
6. Insert the bolt. Fit scope mounts by the mount maker's instructions (95%).
- The manual's notice: work over a table to catch parts that drop (95%, page 7).
- Also register the rifle on the Tikka website for the third warranty year: 2 years standard, 3 when registered (95%, page 19).
```

```more more hooks, mistakes and the field task

- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Learn it blind." If you cannot find the safety with your eyes shut, you do not know your rifle yet.

> [!mistake]
> - Calling the bolt stop "the safety". They are different parts on different sides.
> - Guessing the barrel length. Measure it, chamber included.
```

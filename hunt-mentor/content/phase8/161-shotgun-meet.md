## Every handling step starts here

```diagram sg2-acts
```

- Point it safe. Finger off. Check it is unloaded (85%).
- Every step in this course starts with these three (Tip).

```more ACTS and PROVE
- [[ACTS]]: Assume it is loaded, Control the muzzle, Trigger finger off, See that it is unloaded (85%, Canadian Firearms Safety Course wording, see [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/1)).
- [[PROVE]]: Point safe, Remove all shells, Observe the chamber, Verify the feeding path, Examine the bore (85%, [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/3)).
- On a shotgun the feeding path is the magazine tube and the carrier, or both chambers on an over under (Tip).
- Remington's manual: point the gun in a safe direction "at all times, even when your firearm is unloaded" (95%, Remington 870 manual).
```

## The parts

```diagram sg2-parts
```

- Tap each part. Learn the names before you touch one (Tip).
- The rib is your sight line. The choke squeezes the shot (85%).

```more the parts in words
- Remington's 870 manual names these parts: stock, receiver, breech bolt, ejection port, trigger, safety mechanism, fore end, magazine, magazine cap, barrel, front sight and muzzle (95%, Remington 870 manual, Picture A).
- Most hunting shotguns have a rib on top of the barrel and a bead at the muzzle. You look along the rib; there is no rear sight (85%, [Shotgun mastery](#/s/shotgun-mastery/13)).
- Screw in choke tubes sit inside the muzzle (95%, Remington, Beretta and Browning manuals).
- The magazine tube under the barrel holds the shells on pumps and semi autos. Over unders have no magazine (95%).
```

## Inside the shell

```diagram sg2-shell
```

- Primer, powder, wad, shot, crimp (70%).
- The box tells gauge, length, shot type and size (95%).

```more the shell in full
- Remington's manual labels the head, the primer and the gauge marking on a shotshell (95%, Remington 870 manual, Picture B).
- The wad cushions the shot and carries it down the barrel (70%, [Cartridge anatomy](#/s/cartridge-anatomy)).
- Hunter education: "The shotshells must match exactly the gauge and shell length specified by the manufacturer" (85%).
- How to read a box is in [Chapter 7](#/s/mc-shotgun-07).
```

## When you pull the trigger

```diagram sg2-fire
```

- Hammer, pin, primer, powder, gas, wad, shot (70%).
- The shot leaves as a cloud that spreads with distance (85%).

```more why it matters
- A cloud means you point, not aim. You learn that in [Chapter 8](#/s/mc-shotgun-08).
- The choke decides how fast the cloud spreads ([Chapter 6](#/s/mc-shotgun-06)).
- A weak bang or soft recoil: stop, unload, check the barrel for a stuck wad before you fire again (95%, Remington 870 manual).
```

## Three actions

```diagram sg2-actions
```

| Action | You do | Shots |
|---|---|---|
| Pump | Slide the fore end | 1 plus magazine |
| Semi auto | Pull the trigger | 1 plus magazine |
| Over under | Break it open | 2 |

- Bird law: 3 shells in the gun at most, plug in (99%).

```more the plug rule
- Migratory birds: the shotgun holds no more than three shells in the magazine and chamber combined, using a one piece filler that cannot come out unless the gun is taken apart (99%, Migratory Birds Regulations, 2022, s. 37(1)(c), [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/32)).
- BC: a pump, repeating or auto loading shotgun must have a plug so the magazine holds no more than 2, for all hunting except slugs on big game (99%, page 12, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/2)).
- An over under holds two. It needs no plug (95%, my reading).
```

## Never cut or change a shotgun

```diagram sg2-legal-class
```

- A cut down shotgun can become a prohibited firearm (99%).
- Changes to barrels, stocks or triggers: ask a qualified gunsmith first (Tip).

```more the law, word for word
> [!law] Criminal Code, s. 84(1), "prohibited firearm" (b)
> "a firearm that is adapted from a rifle or shotgun, whether by sawing, cutting or any other alteration, and that, as so adapted, (i) is less than 660 mm in length, or (ii) is 660 mm or greater in length and has a barrel less than 457 mm in length"

> [!law] Criminal Code, s. 84(1), "restricted firearm" (b)
> "a firearm that (i) is not a prohibited firearm, (ii) has a barrel less than 470 mm in length, and (iii) is capable of discharging centre-fire ammunition in a semi-automatic manner"

- Plain English: never saw a barrel or a stock. A semi auto shotgun with a barrel under 470 mm (18.5 in) is a restricted firearm, not a hunting gun you can buy on a regular licence (95%, my reading).
- Taking the plug out for slugs on big game is legal in BC; it does not change the gun's class (95%, my reading of page 12).
- Remington: "never alter or modify your firearm in any way" (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- Read directly 2026-10-23, Criminal Code current to 2026-09-30.
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): A shotgun is simple. The people around it are not. Learn the gun so your hands are free to watch the people.

> [!mistake]
> - Picking up a gun and talking before you open it.
> - Calling every part "the thing". Learn the names: it makes the manual readable.
> - Thinking a shotgun is safe at range. Shot can still hurt people far past where it kills a duck.

> [!field]
> At a gun shop, ask to hold a pump, a semi auto and an over under. Open each one first, then name five parts on each.

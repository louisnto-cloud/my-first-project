## Grouse: walk, stop, shoot

- Walk slow along edges, stop every 20 to 30 steps. The stop flushes the bird (Tip).
- Swing through, keep moving, only with sky behind it (Tip).

```more grouse with a shotgun
- Most ruffed grouse shots come inside about 25 m (27 yd) through trees (Tip, [Grouse Masterclass](#/s/mc-grouse-09/4)).
- Improved cylinder for close cover, modified for open country and late season (60%).
- Lead No. 7.5 or 6 is legal for grouse (99%). If you also hunt ducks that day, carry only steel (Tip).
- Plug in: in BC a pump or semi auto must be plugged for grouse too (99%, page 12).
- Carry: two hands, muzzle up, safety on, finger off. Safety off as the gun comes up (Tip).
- Grouse seasons and limits near you: [Grouse Masterclass rules](#/s/mc-grouse-10).
```

## Ducks: set the range, then wait

```diagram sg2-duck-plan
```

- Steel, steel rated choke, plug in, 35 m line (99% law; Tip on the rest).
- Let birds commit. Shoot the ones inside your far decoy (Tip).

```more ducks with a shotgun
- Non toxic shot only, and none other in your pockets or boat (99%, s. 38(1)).
- 3 shells in the gun with the plug (99%, s. 37(1)(c)).
- No slugs, one shotgun; any other gun unloaded and cased or taken apart (99%, s. 37).
- Legal hours for ducks: half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset (99%, s. 28(3)(b)).
- Range: 35 m (38 yd) is a long shot with steel (70%, [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills/5)).
- Rules near Mission: [Migratory birds Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds).
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## The 35 metre decoy

```diagram sg2-range-card
```

- Range the farthest decoy at 35 m (38 yd) (Tip).
- Inside it: shoot. Past it: watch (Tip).

```more why a range marker
- Ducks look close because the eye judges by size; a marker keeps you honest (Tip).
- Big ducks need about 100 pellets in the 76 cm circle at your longest range. That, not pellet energy, sets the limit (70%, [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills/5)).
- No dog yet: shorten your range. A sailed bird over big water is lost (70%).
- You must make every reasonable effort to retrieve a bird you kill or wound (99%, [Geese Masterclass](#/s/mc-geese-09)).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Decide your range in the truck. In the blind your eyes will lie to you.

> [!mistake]
> - Changing chokes in the blind instead of on paper.
> - Shooting the 45 m bird because the last one at 30 m fell.
> - Grouse shells and duck shells in one vest.

> [!field]
> Write two cards: grouse (shell, choke, 25 m) and ducks (shell, choke, 35 m). Tape them inside your gun case lid.

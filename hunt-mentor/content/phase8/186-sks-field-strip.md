> [!why]
> The SKS (Simonov self loading carbine) strips with no tools in about 2 minutes. That is how you clean out the grease and the salt, and how you check the firing pin.

```diagram sks-strip-overview
```

- 7 parts come out: cover, spring, carrier, bolt, gas tube, piston, trigger group (95%).
- "No further disassembly is necessary or desirable" (95%).

```more what the manual says
- Order from US Army TC 9-56 (1969): clear the weapon; tool case and cleaning rod out; receiver cover pin; cover and driving (recoil) spring assembly; bolt carrier and bolt; gas cylinder tube and piston; trigger group only if necessary (95%).
- "No further disassembly is necessary or desirable" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Army standard: strip in 2 minutes, reassemble in 4 (95%, TC 9-56 training packet). You have no clock. Go slow (Tip).
- Lay a towel on the table and put parts down in order, left to right (Tip).
```

## Field strip in 7 steps

Point in a safe direction. Finger off the trigger. Unloaded and proved. No ammunition on the bench.

```steps Field strip the SKS in 7 steps
sks-strip-1 | Unload and prove it. Safety on.
sks-strip-2 | Cover pin up, then out to the right.
sks-strip-3 | Slide the top cover off to the rear.
sks-strip-4 | Pull the recoil spring out of the carrier.
sks-strip-5 | Magazine open, handle back, lift out.
sks-strip-6 | Lift the bolt off the carrier.
sks-strip-7 | Gas lock up. Tube and piston off.
```

```more each step in the manual's words
1. "Clear the weapon" (95%). [Chapter 3](#/s/mc-sks-03/2) unload.
2. "Rotate the receiver cover pin to a vertical position and pull it out as far as possible." Note: "It may be necessary to pry the receiver cover pin handle away from the receiver before it can be rotated" (95%).
3. "Remove the receiver cover to the rear" (95%).
4. "Pull the driving spring assembly out of the bolt carrier" (95%). It is under light spring tension: control it (Tip).
5. "Open the magazine by pulling the magazine catch to the rear. Pull the operating handle fully rearward, and lift the bolt carrier and bolt out of the receiver" (95%).
6. "Separate the bolt from the bolt carrier" (95%).
7. "Rotate the gas cylinder tube lock to its uppermost position and lift the rear of the handguard to remove the gas cylinder tube and the gas piston. The tube can be upended and the piston will slide out" (95%). The Type 56 packet says turn the lock to about 45 degrees to the rear (95%). Turn it until the tube frees; do not force it (Tip).
- Cleaning rod and tool kit come out first if you will clean: [chapter 5](#/s/mc-sks-05/4) (95%).
```

## Inside the bolt

```diagram sks-bolt-detail
```

- The firing pin floats free. It must rattle (70%).
- Do not drive the pin out unless you must. Gunsmith if unsure (Tip).

```more the bolt in detail
- Most SKS have a free floating firing pin with no spring; a stuck pin can slam fire (70%, Wikipedia "SKS"). [Chapter 1](#/s/mc-sks-01/5) has the slam fire screen.
- The pin is triangular in section and fits one way, notches up (70%). Wrong way can cause slam fires (70%).
- Extractor at the front right: "Place the bolt into the receiver, with the extractor to the right front" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Grease in the pin channel is the target. Spray solvent through the channel, shake, blow dry, shake again: it must rattle (Tip).
```

## Trigger group: only if needed

```diagram sks-trigger-group
```

- Safety ON first, or the catch will not press (95%).
- Hammer is under spring force. Do not strip it further (95%).

```more removing and refitting the trigger group
- Remove: "If necessary, the trigger group can be removed by depressing the catch located behind the loop of the trigger guard and pulling the trigger group down and out of the rifle. The safety must be in the safe position before the catch can be depressed" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Refit: "Invert the rifle and engage the pins at the front of the trigger group with the notches behind the magazine box. Swing the trigger group into position in the stock and seat it by a blow of the hand on the loop of the trigger guard. Move the safety to the fire position to insure that the catch is fully engaged" (95%).
- Remove it only to clean out cosmoline or after a soaking rain (Tip). The trigger parts themselves: gunsmith (Tip).
```

## Reassembly in 6 steps

```steps Put the SKS back together in 6 steps
sks-re-1 | Piston into the gas tube, small end first.
sks-re-2 | Tube on. Lock lever down.
sks-re-3 | Bolt in: extractor right front, 10 mm back.
sks-re-4 | Carrier on: press, rock, slide forward.
sks-re-5 | Recoil spring in, stepped end first.
sks-re-6 | Cover on, pin in, pin down.
```

```more reassembly in the manual's words
1. "Slide the gas piston, small end first, into the gas cylinder tube" (95%).
2. "Fit the large end of the tube over the gas cylinder. Swing the rear end of the gas cylinder tube into the rear sight base and turn the gas cylinder lock downward" (95%).
3. Place the bolt in the receiver with the extractor to the right front and its rear about three eighths of an inch from the inner rear wall (95%, TC 9-56, paraphrased). Three eighths of an inch is about 10 mm (99%, arithmetic).
4. "Lay the bolt carrier on the bolt and while pressing down on the carrier, move it slightly back and forth until it mates with the bolt. Slide the bolt and bolt carrier fully forward" (95%).
5. "Insert the driving spring assembly, stepped end first, into the bolt carrier" (95%).
6. "Insure that the receiver cover pin is pulled fully out to the right, then slide the receiver cover into place from the rear. Secure it with the receiver cover pin and rotate the pin down to the locked position. Close the magazine, and replace the cleaning rod and the combination tool case" (95%).
- Then the [function check](#/s/mc-sks-02/4) (95%).
```

## Function check, every time

```diagram sks-function-check
```

- Safety on: hammer must not fall. Safety off: it falls (95%).
- Any fail: strip again, then gunsmith (Tip).

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Parts down in a row, parts back in reverse. Never rush the last screw.

> [!mistake]
> - Forcing the bolt carrier down before it mates with the bolt.
> - Putting the recoil spring in the wrong end first.
> - Skipping the function check after reassembly.

> [!field]
> Strip and rebuild 5 times at the kitchen table, unloaded. Time the fifth one. Then the function check.

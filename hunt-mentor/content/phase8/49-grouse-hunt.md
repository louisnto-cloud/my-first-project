> [!why]
> Grouse hunting is simple: walk the right cover slowly and be ready. The skill is in the details, and the rules are strict on roads.

```diagram mgr-methods-grid
```

- Two tools: shotgun for flushing birds, .22 for sitting birds (95% legal; Tip on use).
- Every method below is legal in BC as written (99%).

```more legal tools for grouse
> [!law] Legal Hunting Methods table, page 13, row "Upland Game Birds"
> Air rifle "Yes", Rimfire "Yes" with note 2, Centrefire "Yes" with note 3, Shotgun "Yes", Archery "Bow B, C, D". Note 2: "Grouse, ptarmigan, or turkey only." Note 3: "Grouse or ptarmigan only."

- So your CZ 457 .22 and your 12 gauge are both legal for grouse and ptarmigan (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/7)).
- Handguns are not legal for hunting; nor are full metal jacket rifle bullets (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/1)).
- Unleashed dogs are allowed for small game, grouse included (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/15)).
- Lead shot is legal for grouse; it is banned for ducks and geese (99%).
```

## Scouting

```video
yt-ruffed-grouse-tips | Find young forest, edges and food. That is where grouse are.
```

- April: listen for drummers at dawn (Tip).
- Summer: note broods on roads; fall: walk with no gun first (Tip).

```more a scouting year
- April and May: drive or walk quiet roads at dawn, stop every few hundred metres, listen for drums and dusky hoots. Mark each on the Hunt Map (Tip). Drums carry about 400 m (60 to 70%).
- June to August: hen and brood sightings on roads mean a good fall nearby (Tip).
- September: walk one Forest Service road edge at first light with no gun. Count birds, note time, tree type and height (Tip).
- Map work: young cut blocks, aspen draws, creek bottoms, berry slopes; then plan a loop away from traffic (Tip). See [Where they are](#/s/mc-grouse-07).
- Check the MU, season, closures and private land layers every time (99% rules).
```

## Walk the edges with the shotgun

```anim sb-grouse-flush Walk, stop, wait. The stop flushes the bird.
```

- Walk slow along edges, stop every 20 to 30 steps (Tip).
- Stop beside an opening with room to swing (Tip).

```more step by step
1. Pick an edge: aspen against conifer, a cut block margin, a creek bottom, an old road through young forest (Tip).
2. Walk with the wind in your face or across it so your noise goes behind you (Tip).
3. Gun at ready, safety on, finger off the trigger (Tip; safety habit).
4. Every 20 to 30 steps stop for 10 to 20 seconds. Many flushes come during the stop (hunter lore, 60%).
5. When a bird flushes: mount, swing, shoot only with sky or dirt behind and no person in front (Tip). Next screen.
6. Miss or no shot: mark where it lands and follow up (Tip).

- Pros: legal, simple, all forest grouse. Cons: snap shots, many misses at first.
- Old hand's rule (tradition): "Hunt slow enough to be bored."
```

## The .22 rimfire approach (your CZ 457)

```steps Head shot with the .22 in 4 steps
mgr-rim-1 | Name the bird, the MU, the season, the bag.
mgr-rim-2 | Look past it: dirt behind, never sky.
mgr-rim-3 | Rest the rifle on a tree or a knee.
mgr-rim-4 | Aim at the head or upper neck, inside 25 m.
```

- Legal for grouse and ptarmigan only (99%).
- Head or nothing: the breast is the meat (Tip).

```more the full rimfire method and rules
- Rule: rimfire for upland birds is legal only for grouse, ptarmigan and turkey, so never for quail, pheasant or partridge (99%, page 13 note 2).
- Rule: no full metal jacket bullets in a rifle (99%). Tip: carry hollow point .22 LR (Long Rifle) hunting loads; whether plated round nose counts as full metal jacket: VERIFY.
- Rule: 400 m single projectile bands along listed highways (for example Hwy 5, the Coquihalla, Hope to Kamloops) ban your .22 there; shotgun with shot is allowed past the road allowance (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/19)). Hwy 5 north of Kamloops past Heffley Creek is not on that list (95%, my reading).
- Rule: no shooting within 100 m of an occupied house or farm building, school, church, playground or regional district park (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/15)).
- Range: hunters who use .22s on grouse keep head shots short; inside about 25 m (27 yd) is a sensible limit for most shooters (Tip, 55 to 60%). Practise on a 2.5 cm target at that range before the season (Tip).
- Aim point: the head, or the base of the neck if the head bobs (Tip, hunter advice).
- Backstop: a .22 bullet can fly over 1 km. Only shoot with dirt behind the bird; never at a bird on a skyline branch (Tip; safety). See [Backstop](#/s/firearm-safety).
- Ethics: shooting a sitting grouse is legal. Some hunters see it as less sporting; it is also the cleanest meat (opinion).
- Spruce grouse often stay put after a shot; count your bag by species before the next shot (99% bag rules, Tip).
```

## Shotgun flush shooting

```anim mgr-flush-swing Mount on the bird, swing through, keep moving.
```

- Lead 6 or 7.5 shot, improved cylinder choke (Tip, 60%).
- Most shots are close and fast: under about 25 m (Tip).

```more loads, chokes and the swing
- Load: 12 gauge, 28 to 32 g (1 to 1 1/8 oz) of No. 7.5 or No. 6 lead. No. 7.5 is the most common grouse load; No. 6 for later, bigger birds like dusky (60%, Project Upland; app gear notes 65%).
- Lead is legal for grouse (99%). If you also carry steel for ducks: steel 6 works on grouse (Tip).
- Choke: improved cylinder for close cover; modified for open country, sharp tails and late season (60%).
- Swing: start the bead behind the bird, move through it, shoot as the bead passes the beak, keep moving (Tip). See [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills).
- Most ruffed grouse shots come inside about 25 m through trees (Tip).
- Safety: low bird, no shot. Never swing toward a partner (Tip). See the video below.
- Old hand's rule (tradition): "Shoot where the bird is going, not where the bird was."
```

## A partner and a dog

```diagram sb-upland-pinch
```

```anim gr-zone-swing Each hunter shoots only in their own zone.
```

- Partner: one walks the cover, one waits at the end (Tip).
- Dog: unleashed is legal for grouse (99%).

```more working with your brother and a dog
- Walker and blocker: the walker pushes along a draw or hedgerow; the blocker stands in the open just past the end, never facing back into the cover (Tip).
- Each hunter owns a zone of fire and never swings past it (Tip). Talk often so you always know where the other is (Tip).
- Orange on both hunters in thick cover (Tip).
- Dogs: "Unleashed dogs may be used to hunt small game" (99%). Training on game birds under supervision: 1 August to 30 April (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/15)).
- Flushing breeds (spaniels, labs) push birds up in range; pointing breeds hold them while you walk in (Tip).
- Work the dog into the wind (Tip). See [The senses](#/s/mc-grouse-03).
- A dog finds wounded birds you would lose (Tip).
```

## Roads and Forest Service roads: the rules

```anim rb-road-walk Walk out of the road allowance before you load.
```

```diagram mgr-road-flow
```

- No hunting from a vehicle, no loaded gun in it (99%).
- No shooting or hunting in the road allowance of covered roads (99%).

```more the exact Rule Book words
> [!law] Highway No Shooting or Hunting Areas, page 10
> "It is unlawful to discharge a firearm across or to discharge a firearm or hunt within the road allowances of all numbered highways and any two lane or greater public road in B.C. that is maintained by the Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (or their contractors), the federal government or another province or territory. The road allowance extends:"
> "(a) 15 m on either side of the middle of a road with less than three lanes, or"
> "(b) 15 m from the edge of the paved surface of a highway with three lanes or more, or"
> "(c) to the boundary of private or cultivated land,"
> "whichever comes first."

> [!law] Item 22, page 11
> "To hunt wildlife from a motor vehicle."

> [!law] Item 28, page 11
> "To discharge, carry or have in possession a firearm containing live ammunition in its breech or in its magazine attached to the firearm, in or on a railway car, motor vehicle, sleigh, aircraft, bicycle or other conveyance."

> [!law] ATV on a Forest Service Road
> "While travelling on a Forest Service Road, operators of ATVs are required to hold a valid driver's licence and carry a minimum of $200,000 third-party liability insurance."

- Plain English: on numbered highways and two lane or wider public roads kept by the Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure, its contractors, the federal government or another province or territory, you may not shoot across, shoot within, or hunt within the road allowance (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/17)).
- Every road, every vehicle: no hunting from it (item 22) and no loaded firearm in or on it (item 28) (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/11)).
- [[FSR]] (Forest Service Road): kept by the Ministry of Forests, not Transportation. The quick lesson reads the road allowance wording as not naming FSRs (70%, interpretation): VERIFY with a conservation officer. Items 22 and 28 apply there too (99%).
- ATV (All Terrain Vehicle) on an FSR: valid driver's licence and at least $200,000 third party liability insurance (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/10)).
- Region 3 above 1,700 m: motor vehicles except snowmobiles only on existing roads and trails (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-region-3/22)). Check the Motor vehicle closures layer (99%).
- Safe habit on every road, FSR included (Tip): stop, engine off, unload check, walk out past 15 m from the middle and off the road surface, then load; never shoot along or across the road.
```

## Sharp tailed grouse and ptarmigan hunts

```gallery
sharp-tailed-grouse | Sharp tail country: grass, shrubs, aspen.
g-ptarmigan-summer | Ptarmigan country: alpine rock.
```

- Sharp tails: walk grass and shrub draws; late flocks flush far (60%).
- Ptarmigan: glass first, climb high, light gun (Tip).

```more how each hunt works
### Sharp tailed grouse (MUs 3-30 and 3-31 only)
- Early season: singles, pairs and small family groups hold closer (60%).
- Late season: big flocks that flush at sight; a modified choke helps (60%).
- In fall they feed on roadsides and landings rich in clover and dandelion, then in berry draws (85%); in late fall some feed high in aspen and birch (75%).
- Not in the part of 3-30 south of the Scottie Creek Forest Service Road, Map C18 (99%).
- Bag 5 a day, 10 in possession (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-region-3/12)).

### Ptarmigan
- Glass rocky slopes and willow basins; their camouflage is excellent, so look for movement (Tip, 60%).
- Fitness and weather: alpine hunts are long climbs with early snow (Tip).
- A light shotgun is easier to carry; your .22 is legal too (99% legal; Tip).
- Region 3 ptarmigan: 5 a day, 15 in possession, 1 September to 30 November in MUs 3-12 to 3-20 and 3-26 to 3-44 (99%).
- Sun Peaks: no hunting or shooting within 400 m of lifts, runs and buildings (99%).
```

## Decide on the day: the old hand's way

```diagram mgr-day-decide
```

- Weather and season pick the cover (Tip).
- Walk past the road band, follow every flush (Tip, 75% study basis).

```more what a 40 year hunter does differently (hunter wisdom, opinion)
- Hunts the edges and the thick young stuff, not open timber (classic grouse hunting advice).
- Walks slower than feels right and stops often (common advice).
- Walks away from roads: the Pennsylvania road data backs this (75%).
- Marks every flush and follows up at once (common advice).
- Circles lone evergreens and blowdowns (Outdoor Canada tip).
- Opens crops and hunts the food they show (common practice).
- Keeps a log: date, weather, where birds were. Over years that log is the "40 years of knowledge" (Tip).
- Passes on shots with no safe background, every time (safety).
- Knows the bag by species before the second bird (99% rule).
- These lines come from hunting writers and educators; they are opinion and tradition, not science.
```

## The parts, right side

```diagram cz-parts-right
```

- Names follow Fig. 1 of the CZ manual (95%).
- Safety (6) sits behind the bolt handle (95%).

```more every part in words
- **Barrel (1):** cold hammer forged on CZ 457 models (85%, CZ USA product pages). Swappable at "the highest user level" (95%, CZ manual page 4). Chapter 9.
- **Rear sight (2):** a folding leaf with distance marks every 25 m from 25 to 200 m, on models with iron sights (95%, CZ manual page 8). Some models, like the American, have no sights (85%, CZ USA).
- **Dovetail (3):** an 11 mm dovetail on top of the receiver for scope rings, or a Weaver rail (95%, CZ manual page 8).
- **Bolt handle (4):** a 60 degree lift, shorter than many rifles, for faster reloading (95%, CZ manual page 4).
- **Cocking indicator (5):** the rear of the striker sticks out over the bolt plug when cocked; check it by sight or touch (95%, CZ manual page 7).
- **Safety (6):** two positions, behind the bolt handle, acting on the trigger (80%, all4shooters test 2020; 95% CZ manual page 7 for how it works).
- **Trigger (7):** adjustable for weight and travel (95%, CZ manual page 7). Chapter 9.
- **Magazine (8) and magazine catch (9):** the catch is at the front of the magazine; press it rearward (95%, CZ manual page 5).
- **Stock (10):** held to the action by two connecting screws, parts 73 and 74 (95%, CZ manual pages 9 and 12).
```

## The left side: the bolt catch

```diagram cz-parts-left
```

- The bolt catch (1) lets the bolt come out (95%).
- The receiver is the part the law calls the firearm (95%).

```more the left side in words
- Bolt catch: "Open the bolt, and depressing the bolt catch (Fig.10) ... pull the bolt out of the gun" (95%, CZ manual page 9). Fig. 10 shows it on the left of the receiver (95%).
- Front sight (2): a blade in a hood; two screws set its height (95%, CZ manual page 8, Fig. 9).
- Receiver (3): the Criminal Code definition of "firearm" includes "any frame or receiver" (99%, Criminal Code s. 2, current to 30 September 2026, read 2026-10-23). So a barrel swap does not change which rifle you own; the receiver carries that.
```

## The bolt, out of the rifle

```diagram cz-bolt-parts
```

- Six parts you can see and name (95%).
- Striker and spring stay inside: gunsmith only (95%).

```more the bolt in words
- Names from the parts list: bolt (32), extractor (33), striker (36), bolt handle (37), bolt handle ball (38), cocking indicator (39), striker spring (41), bolt guide (31) (95%, CZ manual page 12).
- The manual stops at taking the bolt out: "No further disassembly is needed for routine maintenance" (95%, page 9).
- After "a great number of cartridges", CZ advises a gunsmith cleans the bolt (95%, page 9).
- Chapter 8 shows how to take the bolt out and put it back.
```

## Work the bolt: 4 moves

```diagram cz-bolt-cycle
```

- Up, back, forward, down (95%).
- Closing the bolt feeds a round and cocks the striker (95%).

```more the cycle in words
- CZ: open the bolt by raising the handle and pulling it to the rear until it stops; push it forward and rotate it down. "This procedure inserts the cartridge into the chamber and cocks the striker mechanism" (95%, CZ manual page 6).
- The handle turns only 60 degrees, which clears a big scope's eyepiece (95%, page 4; scope clearance is a Tip).
- Work it firmly, all the way. A short stroke can fail to pick up the next round (Tip).
- More on how bolt actions work: [rifle mechanics](#/s/rifle-mechanics).
```

## Which CZ 457 do you have?

| Model (examples) | What differs |
|---|---|
| American | 24 in (610 mm) barrel, no sights (85%) |
| Lux | Iron sights, walnut stock with cheekpiece (85%) |
| Varmint, Varmint MTR | Heavy 20.5 in (521 mm) barrel; MTR has a match chamber (85 to 95%) |
| Scout | Youth stock, 12 in length of pull, leaf sight (85%) |
| Synthetic, Thumbhole, LR Precision | Plastic or thumbhole stocks, threaded on some (80%) |

- VERIFY: which model and which chambering is yours?

```more the family, and how to check yours
- CZ: "Several versions are available." All share the barrel swap system, the 60 degree bolt, the adjustable trigger, the positive safety, the cocking indicator, bolt removal with the safety engaged, and barrels and magazines that fit the CZ 455 (95%, CZ manual page 4).
- Calibres CZ lists for barrel kits: .22 LR, .22 WMR (Winchester Magnum Rimfire) and .17 HMR (Hornady Magnum Rimfire) (80%, all4shooters 2020; CZ USA retailer listings). VERIFY your chambering on the barrel stamp.
- Match chamber: "For CZ 457 cal .22 LR MATCH models (e.g. version CZ 457 Varmint MTR) Match ammunition is recommended" because the chamber is cut to the minimum size; high power or longer cartridges are "not recommended" (95%, CZ manual page 5).
- Two trigger types exist: standard and Comfort, with different pull ranges (95%, page 7). Which one your model has: VERIFY.
- How to check: read the left of the barrel and receiver, and your box or receipt. Do not guess (Tip).
- Owner question: which CZ 457 is it (American, Lux, Synthetic, Varmint, Jaguar, Training, Pro Varmint or another), which calibre, and does it have iron sights, a scope or a threaded muzzle?
```

## Memory hooks and mistakes

```diagram cz-parts-right
```

- **"Catch in front, safety behind."** Magazine catch ahead of the magazine; safety behind the bolt handle.
- **"Sixty and done."** The 60 degree bolt lift.

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Learn a rifle with it empty on the kitchen table, never at the truck.

> [!mistake]
> - Calling every rimfire round ".22". A .22 WMR will not chamber safely in a .22 LR barrel. Read the stamp.
> - Short stroking the bolt, then wondering why it did not feed.
> - Guessing the model when buying parts. Barrels, stocks and sights differ.

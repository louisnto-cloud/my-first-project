## Match three stamps

```diagram tk-ammo-match
```

- The calibre is marked on the side of the barrel (95%).
- Headstamp and box must match it (95%).

```more why it matters
- "The caliber designation for your rifle is marked on the side of the barrel" (95%, Tikka manual page 9). "Be certain that each round you use is in the proper calibre" (95%, page 6).
- 7.62x39 (your SKS round) does not belong anywhere near the Tikka. Keep each rifle's ammunition in its own labelled box (Tip).
- .308 Winchester and 7.62x51 NATO are close but not the same: use what your barrel says (Tip; the manual says use the right calibre, 95%). VERIFY with a gunsmith before shooting any 7.62x51.
- Headstamp: [How a cartridge works](#/s/cartridge-anatomy/0).
```

## Tikka's ammunition rules

```diagram tk-ammo-rules
```

- Factory new, CIP or SAAMI, inspected, under 10 years old (95%).
- Never steel jacketed bullets (95%).

```more the manual's words
- "Use only high quality, commercially manufactured ammunition that is manufactured in accordance with C.I.P. ... or SAAMI ... standards" (95%, Tikka manual page 9). CIP is the European proof commission; SAAMI is the Sporting Arms and Ammunition Manufacturers' Institute.
- "Carefully inspect each cartridge before it is loaded in the magazine. Be certain the cartridge cases are not split, deformed, or the cartridges do not possess any other dents or defects (this applies even to factory ammunition)" (95%).
- "Do not fire old (age over 10 years) ammunition in this firearm" (95%).
- "DO NOT ever shoot with steel jacketed bullets!" (95%).
- "Before purchasing a large batch, make sure that the rifle functions reliably with it" (95%).
- "The use of reloaded, handloaded, remanufactured or other non standard ammunition voids all warranties" (95%).
```

## The legal bullet

```diagram tk-bullet-types
```

- Full metal jacket and other non expanding bullets: unlawful to hunt with (99%).
- Soft point, bonded or all copper: legal (95%).

```more the rule word for word
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

> [!law]
> "It is unlawful to hunt or trap with a rifle using a full metal jacketed non-expanding bullet, or a tracer, incendiary, or explosive bullet."

- Source: BC synopsis, as quoted in [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0) (99%).
- Deer may be hunted with a centrefire rifle; your .308 qualifies (99%, page 13 table, via the Rule Book).
- Your hunting area rules (MUs, Management Units, seasons, no shooting areas near Mission and Harrison): [Region 2](#/s/rb-region-2/0).
- Bullet types (85%, [How a cartridge works](#/s/cartridge-anatomy/0)): cup and core soft points expand fast and are cheap; bonded bullets hold together through bone; all copper bullets are lead free and penetrate deep.
```

## Pick one load

```diagram tk-load-pick
```

- 150 to 165 gr expanding bullets suit black tails (65%).
- One load, one zero (75%).

```more choosing for a black tail
- Black tailed bucks weigh about 48 to 90 kg, does 40 to 65 kg (90%, BC deer account, via the [mule deer Masterclass](#/s/mc-mule-deer-01)).
- A 150 gr cup and core soft point is plenty for deer (65%, [ammo guide](#/s/ammo-guide/0)). A 165 gr bonded bullet is a good all round choice if you may meet a black bear (65%).
- Your 1 in 10 twist suits 150 to 180 gr hunting bullets (65%, [How a rifle works](#/s/rifle-mechanics/0)).
- Your 20 inch barrel is about 80 to 100 fps (feet per second) slower than the box, which is tested from a longer barrel (70%, [.308 ballistics](#/s/ballistics-308/0)).
- Every load hits a different spot. Switch loads and you must zero again (75%).
- Write the load name and lot number on your zero card (Tip). Chapter 10 has the card.

> [!lean]
> My lean: buy one box of a 150 gr soft point and one of a 165 gr bonded load. Shoot both at the range day. Keep the one that groups best and feeds cleanly. Buy 3 more boxes of the same lot.
```

## Energy and the black tail

```diagram tk-energy
```

- A 150 gr load carries over 1,356 J (1,000 ft lb) past 300 m (85%).
- Your skill sets the limit, not the energy (70%).

```more the numbers
- Federal Power Shok 150 gr from the [.308 ballistics](#/s/ballistics-308/0) lesson: 3,590 J at the muzzle, 2,840 J at 100 m, 2,210 J at 200 m, 1,700 J at 300 m (75%).
- The 1,000 ft lb rule is a rule of thumb with no clear origin (70%).
- A good bullet through both lungs matters more than energy (70%, [shot placement](#/s/shot-placement/0)). The deer vital zone with a margin is about 20 to 25 cm (75%).
- Your real limit: the plate test in [chapter 5](#/s/mc-tikka-05) (Tip).
```

## Storing and carrying ammunition

```diagram tk-ammo-rules
```

- Locked, separate from the rifle (95%).
- Dry, cool, in its box (Tip).

```more storage and the field
- Tikka: store the ammunition in a separate locked location (95%, Tikka manual pages 5 and 8). The storage law is in [chapter 8](#/s/mc-tikka-08).
- Keep rounds in their box with the lot number. Loose rounds in a wet pocket corrode and pick up grit (Tip).
- On the hunt: one loaded magazine in the rifle when you are set up, a few spare rounds in a closed pocket (Tip). No live ammunition in an attached magazine in a vehicle (99%, [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0)).
- Lead warning: wash your hands after handling ammunition and after cleaning (95%, Tikka manual page 6, rule 12).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram tk-bullet-types
```

- **"Three stamps, one name."** Barrel, headstamp, box.
- **"It must open up."** Expanding bullets only.
- **"One load, one zero."**

```more mistakes, rule and the field task
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Buy one load by the case, not ten loads by the box."

> [!mistake]
> - Mixing loads in a pocket and missing because the zero moved.
> - Shooting old surplus or steel jacketed bullets in the Tikka.
> - Taking full metal jacket "practice" rounds on the hunt.

> [!field]
> Before the range day: buy the two test loads, write their names and lot numbers in the journal, and check every round for dents and split necks.
```

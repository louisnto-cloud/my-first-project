> [!why]
> A moose spends most of the day lying down chewing. Be at the willow edge when it stands up, not when it is bedded in the timber.

## A day in October and November

```anim sa-moose-day Feed at dawn, bed at midday, feed at dusk.
```

- Dawn: out to willow, wetland edge or cutblock (75%).
- Dusk: back out to feed (75%).

```more the daily loop
- Minnesota GPS and activity study, 9 adult moose (75%, thesis via search): activity was crepuscular, peaking about 2 hours after sunrise and around sunset; most bouts of activity and rest lasted about 20 minutes.
- Captive moose fed about 10 hours a day in about 5 bouts (70%).
- Alaska radio collars: rest periods averaged 105 minutes in summer and 171 minutes in winter (70%, via search). So moose get up and feed several times a day and at night, not only at dawn and dusk (70%).
- Midday: bedded in shade or thick cover, often close to the food (Tip, [Moose](#/s/moose)).
- Legal hunting hours for moose: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (99%, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)).
```

## Bed

```photo hab-subalpine-forest
```

- Beds in thick conifer or willow near water and food (75%).
- Hot days: shade, wet ground, even water (70%).

```more beds in detail
- App summary: moose bed in thick conifer or willow near water and browse (75%, [bedding terrain](#/s/bedding-cover)).
- Research on moose bed sites found they are chosen as thermal refuges (cooler spots) in warm weather (65%, Wildlife Biology 2016 paper title via search).
- A moose bed is a big flattened oval (Tip). Size in centimetres: VERIFY (no source found).
- Bedded moose chew cud for long spells; in winter rumination can reach 12.6 hours a day (70%, captive study).
- Chapter 8 shows how to read a bed.
```

## Feed

```anim rp-moose-browse A moose breaks a stem down to reach the tips.
```

- Reaches twigs up to 2.5 m and breaks taller stems down (90%).
- October menu: last leaves, then twigs (85%).

```more feeding in detail
- BC account (90%): moose "can reach twigs up to 2.5 m above the ground, chew off large diameter twigs, and often break down branches with their jaws to get at tips that are out of reach".
- Moose feed in the same rich browse patch for days (Tip). Fresh browse = come back at dawn or dusk.
- Feeding spots in fall: willow flats, wetland edges, young cutblocks and burns with willow, aspen and birch (85%, chapter 7).
- Water plants are mostly a summer food; by November twigs rule (90%).
```

## Water

```photo g-moose-cow
```

- Wetlands are kitchens as much as drinking spots (85%).
- Snow and twigs supply much winter water (Tip).

```more water in detail
- Moose live by water: willow swamps, lake edges and beaver ponds (85%, [food, water and cover](#/s/food-water-cover)).
- Summer: they feed in shallow ponds and dive for pondweeds (85 to 90%).
- Hot days: standing in water cools them (70%, chapter 2).
- No moose study on daily drinking times was found: VERIFY. Do not plan a hunt around a "drinking hour" (Tip).
- Hunting a moose while it swims is unlawful unless it was already wounded (99%, page 11 item 26, [unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts)).
```

## Heat

```anim mc-moose-heat-day A warm day: into the shade.
```

```photo bg-moose-cow-calf-water
```

- Warmer than about 14°C: less moving in the open (70%).
- Hunt the edges early and late on warm days (Tip).

```more heat in detail
- Captive moose showed heat stress signs from about 14°C in summer and about minus 5°C in late winter (70%, chapter 2). Wild moose vary (70%).
- Minnesota study: as the temperature rose, rest bouts got shorter and active bouts longer up to a limit (70%, via search). The study is about summer.
- A warm, sunny November afternoon in the Thompson can still pass 10°C (Tip). Moose will sit tight in cool cover.
- Evidence level: strong for heat sensitivity; weak for exact numbers (70%).
```

## Weather

```diagram mc-moose-weather
```

- Strong: heat, deep snow (70 to 90%).
- Weak or none: moon, air pressure (VERIFY).

```more weather in detail, with evidence levels
| Weather | What changes | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Heat | Beds in shade or water, less midday movement | 70% (studies above) |
| Deep snow | Moves down slowly; easy to 40 cm | 90% (BC account) |
| Calm, cool, clear | Calls carry far; best calling days | 65% (Outdoor Canada) |
| Heavy wind, rain | Calls muddied; hearing limited | 65% (Outdoor Canada); moose behaviour: Tip |
| Cold front | No moose study found | VERIFY; hunters report more movement after a front (Tip) |
| Air pressure | No moose study found | VERIFY |
| Moon | No moose study found | VERIFY |

- For deer, the app found no reliable moon effect (70%, [Mule deer Masterclass 6](#/s/mc-mule-deer-06)). Do not plan moose days by the moon (Tip).
- Cold calm mornings after the first frosts keep moose on their feet longer: hunter lore (60%, [Moose](#/s/moose)).
```

## Hunting pressure

```anim rq-pr-push Pressure pushes animals into cover.
```

- Pressure pushes moose into thick cover by day (Tip).
- Roads and open cutblocks raise risk to moose (90%).

```more pressure in detail
- BC moose research: roads and open cutblocks made moose more vulnerable to hunters and predators (90%, chapter 4).
- November in Region 3: deer hunters are everywhere in the woods (Tip). Expect moose to bed deeper and move at the edges of light.
- Walk in before light, away from roads; sit or glass where cover meets food (Tip).
- Evidence for moose specific pressure responses in BC: VERIFY (no study read). Treat as Tip.
```

## Plan your day

| Time | Do this (Tip) |
|---|---|
| 1 h before sunrise | Walk in quietly; legal light starts (99%) |
| Dawn to 3 h after | Glass and still hunt edges |
| Midday | Scout sign, eat, rest; still hunt timber slowly |
| 3 h before sunset | Sit a wetland or cutblock edge |
| Last light | Count carefully: poor light, big dark shape |

```anim rb-sunrise-hours Legal light starts 1 hour before sunrise.
```

```more the plan in words
- Hours: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset for big game (99%, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)).
- A downed moose at last light means a night of work: shoot early enough to deal with it (Tip, chapter 10).
- Field Mode shows legal light for your spot: [legal light](#/field/light).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mc-moose-day-clock
```

- **Rhyme:** "Up at dawn and up at dark; noon is bed and chew and park."
- **Number trick:** 10 and 5: about 10 hours feeding in about 5 bouts (70%).
- **Old hand's rule** (tradition): "Hunt the edges when the light is on the edge."

```more more hooks
- **Rhyme:** "Hot sun high, moose lies by; cold calm dawn, moose moves on."
- **Number trick:** 14: degrees Celsius where summer heat stress starts (70%).
- **Number trick:** 1 and 1: one hour before sunrise, one hour after sunset (99%).
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Do not shoot a moose at last light unless you are ready to work all night."

> [!mistake]
> - Driving roads at midday looking for moose in the open.
> - Planning around the moon.
> - Shooting at last light with no plan to recover and cool the meat.

> [!field]
> Pick one wetland edge. Sit the last 2 hours of light there twice this season. Write what you see and when in your Journal.
```

> [!why]
> Most accidents happen while loading and unloading. Do these steps the same way every time and your rifle never surprises you.

- Red dot showing: it can fire (95%).

## The safety, 3 states

```diagram tk-safety-3pos
```

- 1 Ready: cocked, safety forward, red dot (95%).
- 2 Transport: unloaded, uncocked (95%).
- 3 ON: cocked, safety back, no red dot (95%).

```more the manual's words
- "The safety catch is located on the side of the receiver just behind the bolt handle" (95%, Tikka manual page 8).
- The 3 conditions in figure 7 (95%, page 8):
  1. "The rifle is cocked and the safety is in the OFF position (READY TO FIRE)"
  2. "The transport position. The rifle is unloaded, uncocked and the safety is in the OFF position."
  3. "The rifle is cocked and the safety is in the ON position"
- "The safety can only be engaged when the rifle is cocked" (95%, page 8). So an uncocked, empty rifle sits with the safety forward. That is normal.
- The cocking indicator under the bolt shroud shows cocked, by sight and by feel (95%, page 8). Feel for it with a thumb in the dark (Tip).
- Some Tikkas have a three position safety with a bolt release button. Sako lists a two position safety on the Arctic (95%). Assumption: yours is two position. Check: if there is no small button beside the bolt handle in front of the safety, it is two position.
```

## What safety ON locks

```diagram tk-safety-locks
```

- Bolt, firing pin, sear and trigger (95%).
- To open the bolt: safety forward first (95%).

```more why the order matters
- When the safety is ON it locks "the bolt in the closed position, the firing pin to the cocked position, movement of the sear and trigger blade" (95%, Tikka manual page 8).
- So on your Arctic, unloading always starts with the safety forward, muzzle pointed safe. That is the moment of most risk: finger out of the guard, eyes on the muzzle (Tip).
- "The manual safety is merely a mechanical device and is in no way a substitute for the Basic Safety Rules" (95%, page 8).
- Your CZ 457 is different: its bolt opens with the safety ON. Say it out loud when you switch rifles (see [How a rifle works](#/s/rifle-mechanics/0)).
```

## The bolt in 4 moves

```diagram tk-bolt-cycle
```

- Up, back, forward, down (95%).
- Full stroke, firm, every time (Tip).

```more each move
1. **Up:** unlocks the two locking lugs and cocks the firing pin: "The firing pin is cocked when the cocking piece moves backwards while lifting the bolt" (95%, Tikka manual page 7). The Arctic handle lifts 70 degrees (95%, Sako).
2. **Back:** all the way. The extractor pulls the case; the spring loaded plunger ejector throws it out of the port (95% for the plunger ejector, Sako).
3. **Forward:** the bolt face strips the top round from the magazine into the chamber (95%, page 10).
4. **Down:** the action is closed and the rifle is cocked (95%, page 10).
- Short stroke (not pulling fully back) is the classic jam (Tip). Practise with snap caps, chapter 5.
```

## Load check, step by step

```steps Prove it unloaded in 4 steps
tk-check-1 | Muzzle safe, finger off. Safety forward: red dot.
tk-check-2 | Magazine out first.
tk-check-3 | Lift, press the bolt stop, bolt out. Look.
tk-check-4 | Bolt in, closed. Muzzle safe: trigger to decock.
```

- This is the manual's load check (95%).
- Do it every time you pick the rifle up (Tip).

```more the manual's load check in full
- Precautions (95%, Tikka manual page 8): never assume the gun is unloaded; never point or push a firearm toward yourself or another person; always visually check the chamber; magazine out and bolt open before handing it over.
1. Finger away from the trigger, barrel in a safe direction.
2. Disengage the safety: move it forward, red dot shown.
3. Remove the magazine with the magazine release button.
4. Remove the bolt: lift the handle, press the bolt stop, pull the bolt fully out.
5. Check the chamber is empty. If not, go to "Unloading the rifle".
6. Put the bolt back and close it. "THE FIRING PIN IS NOW COCKED." With the barrel pointed safe, decock it by pulling the trigger.
- Notice: "The firing pin can be decocked only when the bolt is in the closed position" (95%, page 9).
- Bolt out is also the moment for the obstruction check: look through the bore from the rear. Any obstruction, even minor, is a gunsmith job (95%, page 9).
```

## Load the magazine

```diagram tk-mag-load
```

- On the follower, press down, slide back (95%).
- Never force an extra round (95%).

```more the steps and the law on capacity
- Take the magazine out of the rifle. Place a round on the follower in front of the lips, press it down and slide it fully under the lips. Repeat (95%, Tikka manual page 10, figure 10).
- "Do not try to force more cartridges than the maximum capacity of your magazine. An overloaded magazine may not function properly" (95%).
- The manual also says: do not load more cartridges than local law permits (95%, page 4).
- The Canadian 5 round limit for long gun magazines applies to magazines designed for semi automatic firearms (99%, [SOR/98-462](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462/FullText.html), Schedule, Part 4, s. 3(1), current to 30 September 2026, read 2026-10-23). A magazine made for a bolt action rifle is not on that list (90%, my reading). So your 10 round Tikka magazine is legal as sold (90%).
- Never alter a magazine or buy one made for a semi automatic rifle that happens to fit: that is where the law changes. Ask the store or the RCMP (Royal Canadian Mounted Police) Canadian Firearms Program (Tip).
- Hunting rules for rifles in BC: the Rule Book lessons show no rifle magazine limit (85%, my reading of [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0)). The 3 shell limit is for shotguns.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Empty the magazine

```diagram tk-mag-unload
```

- Thumb pushes the rim forward, finger tips it up (95%).
- Over a table, rounds into the box (Tip).

```more the manual's words
- "Grasp it with one hand around the magazine box, bottom down and front end forward. With the tip of the thumb firmly press down on the cartridge rim and push towards the front of magazine box. As the cartridge moves forward, tip it slightly upward with the index finger. Repeat until the magazine is unloaded" (95%, Tikka manual pages 10 and 11).
- Check each round as it comes out: dents, split necks, pushed in bullets go in a separate pocket (95%, page 9 says inspect every cartridge).
```

## Load the rifle, step by step

```steps Load the Tikka in 5 steps
tk-load-1 | Muzzle safe, finger off. Loaded magazine in.
tk-load-2 | Bolt up, then fully back.
tk-load-3 | Forward and down: chambered and cocked.
tk-load-4 | Safety back: ON. No red dot.
tk-load-5 | Only on a sure target: safety forward, fire.
```

- Load only at the firing line or on the hunt, never in a vehicle (99%).
- Wipe the chamber dry before shooting (95%).

```more the manual's loading steps and the rules
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Before loading: obstruction check done, correct cartridges, chamber wiped dry, eye and ear protection on (95%, Tikka manual page 10).
- Insert the loaded magazine, lift the bolt and pull it completely back, push it forward to feed a round, turn the handle down: the action is closed and cocked (95%).
- "Pull the safety catch backwards to the ON position" (95%).
- To fire: safety forward, red dot visible, press the trigger. Not taking a second shot? Safety ON, muzzle safe, fingers away from the trigger (95%).
- "This rifle can have a live round in the chamber and can fire even if the magazine is empty or has been removed" (95%, page 10).
- A firearm is loaded with a round in the chamber or in a detachable magazine inside or attached to it (99%, [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions/0), page 3).
- No live ammunition in the breech or an attached magazine in or on a motor vehicle or other conveyance (99%, [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0), item 28).
- Tip: on a hunt, carry with the chamber empty and a loaded magazine in, until you are set up for a shot. Bolt forward, down, safety ON takes 2 seconds and you will practise it in chapter 5.
```

## Unload the rifle, step by step

```steps Unload the Tikka in 4 steps
tk-unload-1 | Muzzle safe. Safety forward: red dot.
tk-unload-2 | Lift and pull back. The live round comes out.
tk-unload-3 | Bolt stays back. Magazine out.
tk-unload-4 | Look: empty. Close, muzzle safe, decock.
```

- Order: round out, then magazine out (95%).
- Do not push the bolt forward until the magazine is out (Tip).

```more the manual's unloading steps
- "Always unload the firearm immediately after firing is completed. Never store a loaded firearm" (95%, Tikka manual page 10).
1. Keeping the firearm pointed in a safe direction, disengage the safety by pushing it forward (red dot visible).
2. Lift and pull back the bolt to extract the live cartridge from the chamber.
3. Remove the magazine by pushing the magazine release button.
4. After checking the rifle is unloaded and empty of cartridges, close the bolt, point the rifle in a safe direction and pull the trigger to uncock the firing pin.
- Why the bolt stays back in step 3: pushing it forward with the magazine still in feeds the next round, and you are loaded again (Tip).
- Catch the ejected round in your hand or on a jacket on the ground. Count the rounds back into the box: the count must match what you loaded (Tip).
- The rifle is now in the transport position: unloaded, uncocked, safety forward (95%, page 8).
```

## Misfire: click, no bang

```diagram tk-misfire
```

- Muzzle safe. Wait at least 1 minute (95%).
- Then unload with extreme care (95%).

```more the misfire drill
- "If the rifle doesn't fire after pulling the trigger, wait at least one minute and unload the rifle with extreme care" (95%, Tikka manual pages 10 and 19).
- Why: a slow ignition (hang fire) can fire late (70%, see [How a rifle works](#/s/rifle-mechanics/0)).
- A misfire is on the manual's stop shooting list: have the rifle checked by an authorised gunsmith (95%, page 19).
- Never look down the muzzle of a doubtful rifle (95%, basic safety rules page 5).
- An unusual noise when shooting: stop, safety ON, unload, and check the bore for a stuck bullet (95%, page 6).
```

## Handing it over

```diagram tk-handover
```

- Magazine out, bolt open, chamber checked (95%).
- Never take it any other way (95%).

```more the manual's rule and the field habits
- "Never take from or give the rifle to another person unless the magazine is removed, the bolt is open, and the chamber has been visually checked to confirm it is unloaded" (95%, Tikka manual page 8).
- Before a fence, a log, a ditch or a tree stand: "Open and empty the chamber(s) of your firearm and engage the manual safety" (95%, page 6, rule 9). On your Arctic the safety only engages when cocked, so: chamber empty, magazine out, bolt open (Tip, my reading of pages 6 and 8).
- With your brother: say "bolt open, mag out, chamber clear" and let him look too (Tip). See [two armed hunters](#/s/firearm-safety/0).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram tk-safety-3pos
```

- **"Red is ready."** Red dot: it can fire.
- **"Round, then mag."** Unload the chamber first, then the magazine.
- **"Forward to open."** On the Tikka, safety forward before the bolt lifts.

```more more hooks, mistakes and the field task
- **"Open, out, look."** Bolt open, magazine out, look in the chamber: the hand over rule.
- **"One minute, one click."** After a misfire, wait a full minute.
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Count them out, count them in." The rounds you load must match the rounds you unload.

> [!mistake]
> - Pulling the magazine and calling the rifle unloaded with a round still in the chamber.
> - Pushing the bolt forward again before the magazine is out, and reloading by accident.
> - Short stroking the bolt under pressure.

> [!field]
> With an empty rifle and 5 dummy rounds: 10 full load and unload cycles, saying each step out loud. Then 10 with your eyes on a target, not the rifle.
```

```checklist tk-loading-drill
Load check done, bolt out, bore clear
5 dummy rounds into the magazine
Load: magazine in, up, back, forward, down, safety ON
Unload: safety forward, round out, magazine out, look, decock
Rounds counted back: 5 of 5
```

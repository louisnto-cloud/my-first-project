> [!why]
> The goose year decides which birds are in front of you: big local families in September, migrants in October, wary survivors in December.

- Nest in spring, moult in summer, move in fall (80%).
- October and November change the most (Tip).

## January to March: winter and the spring move

```diagram sb-goose-seasons
```

- Winter flocks hold open water and fields (80%).
- Spring moves start as early as February (75%).

```more the months in full
- Winter: geese feed a few hours in early morning and late afternoon in fields, then rest on water (80%, Hinterland Who's Who).
- Some populations start north in February; northern breeders arrive late April or early May (75 to 80%).
- Pairs form in a goose's second year; spring is when pairs settle on territories (80%).
- In temperate areas nesting can start as early as mid March (80%).
- Late season days quoted from the Rule Book (99%): District 3 (Kamloops), Canada and cackling geese 1 to 10 March. District 8 (Okanagan), 21 February to 10 March in a year that is not a leap year. District 2 (Mission), 10 February to 10 March 2027.
- Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## April and May: eggs and goslings

| Stage | Number (80%) |
|---|---|
| Eggs | 4 to 7, sometimes 2 to 11 |
| Sitting (incubation) | 25 to 28 days, hen only |
| Leave the nest | 1 to 2 days after hatch |
| First flight | 6 to 9 weeks |
| Survive to fly | About half |

- The hen sits; the gander guards (80%).
- Families walk several kilometres in a few days after hatch (80%).

```more the nest in full
- The hen picks a slightly raised dry spot near water with a good view and builds a shallow bowl lined with down (80%, Audubon).
- Most geese do not nest until they are 2 or 3 years old (80%, Hinterland Who's Who).
- In BC Canada geese nest mostly below 500 m (85%, BC Breeding Bird Atlas via the quick lesson).
- Okanagan towns addle eggs within 14 days of incubation to control introduced geese (85%, Regional District of Okanagan Similkameen). That is a permit program, not hunting.
- Spring is a time to stay away from nests. No goose season runs in April or May in any BC district (95%, my reading of the Rule Book tables).
```

## June to August: the moult

```diagram mgs-moult
```

- All flight feathers drop at once: 4 to 6 weeks flightless (70%).
- Non breeders fly north to moult in late May and June (80%).

```more moult in full
- Hinterland Who's Who gives a 4 to 5 week flightless moult (80%); a city naturalist guide gives 4 to 6 weeks, about the same time the goslings cannot fly (70%).
- Timing: usually June into August (70%).
- Non breeding geese make a moult migration, late May to early June (80%, Hinterland Who's Who).
- Goslings fly at 6 to 9 weeks, the biggest races later (80%, Audubon).
- You will find moulted flight feathers on lake shores in summer. Good sign for next fall (Tip). See [Chapter 8](#/s/mc-geese-08).
```

## September: the local birds

```diagram re-r3-goose-bars
```

- September geese are mostly local, big and in family groups (Tip, 65%).
- District 3 Canada geese close 21 to 30 September (99%).

```more September in full
- Early season dates (99%, Rule Book): District 3, 8 to 20 September. District 2, 5 to 15 September 2026. District 8 opens 20 September.
- Young birds fly by late summer and look like adults by fall (80%).
- Families are still feeding on hay, pasture and the first grain stubble near their home water (Tip, 65%).
- Hunter lore: early season birds have not been shot at yet and decoy best (Tip).
- Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## October and November: what changes

```diagram mgs-oct-nov
```

- Migrants and cacklers join the locals (75%).
- High lakes freeze and birds crowd the valley water (75%).

```more October and November in full
- Northern geese leave their breeding areas late August to September and reach wintering areas in October or November (75%, Ducks Unlimited). Some linger in the north until early October (80%, Hinterland Who's Who).
- Snow geese reach the Fraser delta by early October and peak mid October to mid December (80%, [How animals move](#/s/migration)).
- Fall migration is well under way by late September and mostly over by late November, when Interior lakes freeze (75%, Ducks Unlimited via the migration lesson).
- High plateau lakes freeze first; birds then crowd onto open valley water and the coast (60%, my reading in the migration lesson).
- After weeks of shooting, flocks flare sooner and land farther out (Tip, 65%). Change the spread. See [Chapter 4](#/s/mc-geese-04).
- Heffley Creek in October: District 3 Canada and cackling geese open 1 October to 23 December, 10 a day (99%). Mission: District 2 opens 10 October 2026 (99%).
- Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Which goose, which month

```diagram mgs-species-calendar
```

- Local Canada geese: all year (85%).
- Snow geese: Fraser delta October to April, Skagit in deep winter (80%).

```diagram sg-snowgoose
```

```more the details and the gaps
- Snow geese: Fraser delta by early October, peak mid October to mid December, most shift to the Skagit in Washington late December to February, return mid March to mid April, then fly to Wrangel Island in April (80%).
- Cackling geese pass BC on the way to the Willamette and lower Columbia valleys (75%); exact Interior dates: VERIFY.
- White fronted geese: passing flocks in early fall and spring, a Tip (60%). No BC count found.
- Migrant Canada geese in the Interior: from the first hard frosts of October (Tip, 65%, quick lesson).
```

## December: cold and the late season

```anim re-r2-goose-week Mission: Canada geese close for part of late fall while ducks stay open.
```

- Cold days: geese feed in the warm afternoon (80%).
- District 2 Canada geese close 23 November to 18 December (99%).

```more December in full
- Below about minus 9°C (15°F) at sunrise, a big wintering flock usually skipped its morning flight; on the coldest days only a few birds fed (80%, Raveling 1972).
- In bitter cold geese roost through the morning and feed when it is warmest (65%, Ducks Unlimited goose guide).
- Snow on fields: geese feed where wind or cattle bared the ground (Tip).
- District 3 closes 23 December; District 2 reopens 19 December 2026 to 10 January 2027; District 8 reopens 20 December to 5 January (99%, Rule Book).
- Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Memory hooks

> [!tip] Memory rhymes (made for this app)
> - "March they nest, June they rest, fall they fly, winter they try."
> - "September: big and local. October: new and vocal. December: few and fickle."

- Number trick: **28, 6, 9**. About 28 days sitting, about 6 weeks flightless, young fly by 9 weeks (80%).

> [!rule] Old hand's rule (tradition, opinion)
> First hard frost, look up. Hunters across the north watch for the first cold snap to bring new birds. A Tip, not a measured trigger.

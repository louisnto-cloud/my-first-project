## Habitat: edges and river brush

```photo hab-kamloops-pond-farm
```

- Bed in willow, cottonwood, young conifers (75%).
- Feed on hay, alfalfa and browse at field edges (80%).

```more habitat detail
- BC account: "Within areas that have a suitable climate, whitetails prefer valley bottoms with farmland" (85%).
- US Forest Service species review: in BC, riparian brushlands and young Douglas fir stands are among the most important habitats (85%).
- Riparian means the green strip along rivers and creeks (glossary: [[Riparian]]).
- Idaho Fish and Game: woodlands, dense brush, marshy areas, and edges where meadow meets forest; rarely open desert (80%).
- Staudenmaier and others 2021: white tails use lower, flatter, brushier ground than mule deer (85%).
- They live close to houses and roads. Shooting distance rules matter (99%, see chapter 10).
```

- Summer: grass, forbs, crops. Winter: shrubs (80%).
- Favourites: willow, dogwood, chokecherry, saskatoon (80%).

```more food table
| Season | Main foods |
|---|---|
| Spring | New grass and forbs on fields and south slopes (75%) |
| Summer | Forbs, crops, hay and alfalfa (80%) |
| Fall | Hay regrowth, last forbs, rose hips, browse (75%) |
| Winter | Browse: willow, dogwood, chokecherry, saskatoon, Douglas fir (80%) |

- Idaho: summer mostly grass and soft plants; winter mostly shrubs, favourites willow, dogwood, chokecherry (80%).
- Montana and South Dakota preferred browse: chokecherry, kinnikinnick, serviceberry (saskatoon), skunkbush sumac, snowberry, dogwood (80%, US Forest Service species review).
- White tails are mainly browsers; grass alone cannot give them the 16% protein they need to grow and breed (80%, Montana State University Extension).
- Range wide diet: browse 46%, forbs 24%, mast 11%, grass 8% (85%, US Forest Service species review).
- Douglas fir needles fill the stomach in deep snow but give little nutrition (80%, Idaho).
```

## Elevation by month

```diagram mwt-elev-months
```

- About 300 to 900 m most of the year (60 to 70%).
- Moves are short: along or across the valley (85%).

```more elevation detail
- Hunt Mentor's general pattern (data/migration.json): about 300 to 900 m in most months, up to about 1,000 m in September and October (60 to 70%, built from the BC account and habitat zone elevations).
- BC account: seasonal moves are mostly up or down the main valleys, or across the valley from north facing slopes in summer to south facing sites in winter (85%).
- Summer: floodplains and adjacent terraces (85%).
- Idaho: in winter white tails move below about 900 m (3,000 ft), onto southeast and southwest slopes (80%).
- US Forest Service species review: migrations to winter range are usually under 16 km (10 miles) (85%).
- Compare: southern Interior mule deer average about 47 to 55 km one way (85%, BC Wildlife Federation).
- Local valley elevations near Heffley Creek: VERIFY on the Hunt Map elevation readout.
```

## Region 3: Heffley Creek

```diagram sa-wt-valley
```

- North Thompson hayfields and cottonwood bottoms (75%).
- MU (Management Unit) 3-27: about 100 to 145 white tails a year (90%).

```more Region 3 detail
- Local reading: North Thompson hayfields and cottonwood bottoms north of Kamloops, and river bottoms on the Salmon River side of MU 3-20 (75%, local plan).
- Resident kills 2022 to 2024 by MU (Management Unit): 3-36 led with 486, then 3-27 with 389, 3-26 with 349, 3-20 with 221, 3-19 with 195 (90%, harvest statistics, 3 years combined).
- MU 3-27 in 2024: 771 hunters, 133 kills, about 17% success (90%).
- Region 3 overall 2024: about 7,900 resident white tail hunters, 1,091 kills, about 14% (90%).
- Most valley bottom land is private. Ask first: [Asking for permission](#/s/landowner-permission).
```

## Region 2: Fraser Valley

```photo hab-salmonberry
```

- No white tail season in Region 2 (97%).
- Deer you see near Mission are black tailed deer (85%).

```more Region 2 detail
- The synopsis page 14 table gives white tailed deer 0 for Region 2 (97%).
- Page 27 warns "no open season for white tailed deer" at Herrling Island near Hope (MU, Management Unit, 2-3) (99%).
- Coastal deer are black tailed deer, a kind of mule deer, with their own rules (85%).
- So from Mission, your nearest white tail hunting is Region 3 or Region 8 (Tip).
- Local plan: [Mission and the Fraser Valley](#/s/local-mission).

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Find them on the Hunt Map

```steps Find white tail country in 4 steps
mwt-map-1 | Turn on White tailed deer by month. Pick the month.
mwt-map-2 | Add Private land. Orange means ask first.
mwt-map-3 | Use Slope direction: south faces for late season.
mwt-map-4 | Drop your stand. Check the Scent cone.
```

- Open the [Hunt Map](#/map) and follow the 4 steps.
- Layers show patterns, not deer. Boots confirm (Tip).

```more more map tools
- **White tailed deer by month** (general pattern, 60%): valley bottoms by month from studies.
- **White tailed deer winter range**: official ungulate winter range polygons where they exist (95% for the polygons).
- **Private land**: you need the owner's permission (95%).
- **Cutblocks by age** and **Burns by year**: young openings 5 to 20 years old grow browse (Tip, map guide).
- **Habitat zones**: Ponderosa Pine and Interior Douglas fir zones are low country (85%).
- **Slope steepness** and **Slope direction**: find benches and south faces.
- **Sun and shade**: where snow melts and deer sun themselves in winter (Tip).
- **What can I see from here**: check a glassing point's view of a field (Tip).
- **Scent cone** and **Weather and wind**: plan the stand for the forecast wind.
- How to use the map: [Map scouting](#/s/map-scouting).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mwt-valley-section
```

- **Rhyme:** "Brush to bed, field to feed, river near for all they need."
- **Number trick:** 300 to 900 m. Region 2: 0.
- **Old hand's rule** (tradition): "Find the food, then find the nearest thick cover to it."

```more more hooks, mistakes and field drill
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion, widely repeated): "Edges hold deer." Matches the habitat studies: white tails use the meeting line of cover and food.
- **Rhyme:** "Summer low by the river's flow, winter south where the sun melts snow."

> [!mistake]
> - Hunting high open slopes for white tails (that is mule deer country).
> - Assuming a field is open because there is no sign. Check Private land.
> - Looking for white tails in Region 2.

> [!field]
> On the Hunt Map, mark 3 spots in MU (Management Unit) 3-27 where a hayfield meets river brush on Crown land or land you can get permission for. Visit each once before the season.
```

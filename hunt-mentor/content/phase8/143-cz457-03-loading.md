> [!why]
> Most firearm accidents happen while loading and unloading. Learn the exact CZ steps until your hands do them without you.

```diagram cz-mag-parts
```

- 5 round magazines on most models; 10 round ones exist (80%).
- CZ 455 magazines fit the 457 (95%).

```more magazines in full
- "Barrels and magazines are compatible with the CZ 455 (same interface)" (95%, CZ manual page 4).
- Capacity: the all4shooters test rifles came with a "box magazine with a capacity for 5 cartridges" (80%). Canadian shops sell CZ 10 round .22 LR magazines for the 457, 455, 452 and 512 in steel and polymer (65%, Rangeview Sports listing).
- Some models ship with a single shot adapter: it sits where the magazine goes and you lay one cartridge on its follower; CZ says it protects bullets from damage when feeding, for the best accuracy (95%, CZ manual page 6).
- Magazines are made for one calibre. A .22 LR magazine is not a .22 WMR magazine (Tip, VERIFY with CZ for your barrel kit).
- Is a 10 round .22 magazine legal? The 5 round limit for rifle magazines applies to semi automatic centre fire firearms; magazines designed for rimfire firearms are excluded (95%, SOR/98-462 Part 4 s. 3(1)(a) and 3(2)(a)(i), my reading). Your CZ is a bolt action rimfire, so 10 is legal (95%, my reading).
```

## Load the magazine, 3 steps

```steps Load a CZ magazine in 3 steps
cz-magload-1 | Lay a round on the follower, base ahead of the lips.
cz-magload-2 | Press down and slide it back to the rear wall.
cz-magload-3 | Next one on top, same way, until full.
```

- Load the magazine away from the rifle (Tip).

```more the manual's words
- "With the open end uppermost, grasp the magazine in one hand. Place a cartridge on the magazine follower with the base just forward of the retaining lips. Press the cartridge down and back until it touches the rear edge of the magazine. Place the next cartridge on top of the previous cartridge and slide in as previously described" (95%, CZ manual page 5).
- Rim over rim: on rimfire, make each new rim sit ahead of the one below, or it can jam (Tip, common rimfire advice).
- Check each round: no dents, no green corrosion, right calibre on the box (95%, CZ rule 9: never cracked, deformed or dented cases or damaged bullets).
```

## Magazine out

```diagram cz-mag-out
```

- Catch is in front of the magazine. Press it rearward (95%).

```more the manual's words
- "Press the magazine catch located at the front of the magazine, in a rearward direction (Fig. 2), and remove the magazine (single-shot adapter) from the firearm" (95%, CZ manual page 5).
- Cup your other hand under the magazine so it does not drop in mud or snow (Tip).
```

## Load the rifle, 4 steps

```steps Load the CZ 457 in 4 steps
cz-load-1 | Point safe, finger off, safety ON.
cz-load-2 | Magazine in. Push until the catch holds.
cz-load-3 | Lift the bolt and pull it back.
cz-load-4 | Forward and down. Safety ON until the shot.
```

- Load only where you may shoot (Tip).

```more the manual's words and the law
- "ALWAYS MAKE SURE THE RIFLE IS POINTED IN A SAFE DIRECTION WHEN LOADING!" (95%, CZ manual page 6, capitals in the original).
- "Insert a loaded magazine into the rifle until it is physically secured by the magazine catch ... Open the bolt by raising the bolt handle and pulling it to the rear until it stops. Then push the bolt continuously to its forward position again and ... close the bolt action. This procedure inserts the cartridge into the chamber and cocks the striker mechanism ... If you do not intend to fire immediately ensure the firearm safety is applied" (95%).
- Tip: with the safety ON, the CZ bolt can still be worked to chamber a round (95%, CZ page 4 says the bolt opens with the safety engaged).
- The magazine in the rifle already makes it loaded under BC law, even with the chamber empty (99%, synopsis page 3, loaded firearm).
- Single shot adapter: leave it in, open the bolt, lay one cartridge on its follower, close the bolt (95%, page 6).
```

## Unload, 4 steps

```steps Unload the CZ 457 in 4 steps
cz-unload-1 | Point safe, finger off, safety ON.
cz-unload-2 | Magazine out first.
cz-unload-3 | Open the bolt. Catch the round.
cz-unload-4 | PROVE it: look and feel the chamber and well.
```

- Magazine first, then the bolt (95%).
- Wrong order feeds another round (Tip).

```more the manual's words, and storing it
- CZ manual, page 6: "Remove the magazine in the manner already described. Open the bolt and check to ensure that the cartridge has been extracted from the chamber and ejected. Close the bolt. Depress the trigger (dry fire)." (95%)
- Plain English: magazine out, open the bolt, check the round came out, close, then press the trigger pointed safe to release the striker for storage (95%).
- CZ also says it "does not recommend frequent dry firing" and to use snap caps for dry training (95%, pages 5 and 6). One press to uncock at the end of the day is what the manual itself describes (90%, my reading).
- Rimfire tip: a [[Snap cap]] in the chamber protects the firing pin when you do press the trigger (Tip).
- Count rounds: the number in your pocket plus the magazine should equal what you started with (Tip).
```

## Problems feeding

| Problem | Likely cause (CZ) | Fix (CZ) |
|---|---|---|
| Round does not feed | Magazine not fully in, or dirty | Seat it, clean it, or change magazine |
| Case not ejected | Dirty chamber or extractor, wrong cold lube | Clean, dry, light suitable oil |
| Weak sounding shot | No powder | Unload; check the barrel is clear |

- All rows: CZ manual page 11 (95%).

```more troubleshooting in full
- "CAUTION - if a malfunction occurs, the possibility of an accidental discharge is substantially higher ... Keep the rifle pointed in a safe direction, do not turn the rifle against your body or anyone else's. Do not place your hands in front of the barrel!" (95%, CZ manual page 11).
- "If you are not able to clear the malfunction ... entrust the firearm to a competent gunsmith" (95%).
- Stuck round you cannot clear: magazine out, safety ON, muzzle safe, and take it to a gunsmith cased and unloaded as far as you can make it (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-mag-out
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Magazine out first, every time. Then the bolt. Then your eyes and your finger.

> [!mistake]
> - Opening the bolt before the magazine is out: it feeds the next round.
> - Leaving the magazine in at the truck. That is a loaded firearm in BC.
> - Mixing .22 LR and .22 WMR in the same pocket.

> [!field]
> With snap caps or empty: load and unload 20 times. Say "mag out, bolt open, look, feel" each time.

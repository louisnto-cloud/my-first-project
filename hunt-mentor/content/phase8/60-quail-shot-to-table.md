> [!why]
> A clean kill, a found bird, a cool carcass and a legal bag. This chapter ties the whole Masterclass together and ends with a one page cheat sheet.

```diagram mql-shot-window
```

- Shoot high birds with sky behind, about 15 to 30 m (Tip).
- Lead 7.5 or 8, steel 6 or 7; skeet or improved cylinder choke (75%).

```more shot placement and range in full
- Aim for the head and front half: brain, neck, heart and lungs (Tip, anatomy from chapter 2, 70%).
- "One small pellet is often enough to bring down a quail." (75%, Washington Fish and Wildlife)
- Loads: "Size 7 1/2 and 8 lead shot are standard among quail hunters, and some go as small as 9's. If you're shooting steel, 6's and 7's are the norm." (75%)
- Chokes: "A fairly open choke, skeet or improved cylinder, is well suited to most quail shooting situations." (75%) Your 12 gauge works and puts more pellets in the pattern (75%).
- The quick lesson's loads (Tip): quail and partridge, lead 7.5 or 6, or steel 6; chukar and pheasant, lead 5 or 6, or steel 3 or 4. Huns: lead 6 or 7.5 at 30 to 45 yards (75%, Washington).
- Range: 15 to 30 m is a Tip, not a rule. Pattern your gun at 20 and 30 m first: [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills) and the pattern math diagram there.
- Lead shot is legal for upland birds (99%). Plug: no more than 3 shells in total (95%).
```

## After the shot

```anim mql-mark-fall Eyes on the spot. Drop your hat. Walk straight to it.
```

- Mark the fall before you look for a second bird (60%).
- Retrieval is the law (99%).

```more after the shot in full
- Mentally mark where the bird falls before swinging on another, and drop a hat or handkerchief where you stood (60%, Missouri Conservationist).
- Walk straight to the spot with your eyes on it (Tip, from the quick lesson).
- A hit bird may run. Get to it fast; a wing tipped quail can hide in brush (Tip).
- Dispatch a live bird at once, quickly and humanely (Tip).
- No dog: shoot only over short cover so every bird falls where you can find it (Tip).
- Search in a widening circle from the last feather patch (Tip).

> [!law]
> "No person shall kill, cripple or wound game without making all reasonable effort to locate, dispatch, retrieve and include it in their bag limit." (99%)
```

## Field care

```steps Care for a quail in 4 steps
mql-care-1 | Cool it fast: mesh vest, shade, air around it.
mql-care-2 | Draw it soon if warm: finger out the guts (Tip).
mql-care-3 | One feathered wing stays on until home (99%).
mql-care-4 | Ice in the cooler, then cook to 74 °C.
```

- Cool birds fast in October sun (Tip).
- One feathered wing stays on each bird until it is home (99%).

```more field care in full
- "One feathered wing stays on each bird until it is home (99%). Both breasts must come home (99%)." From the quick lesson and the [Rule Book: after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill).
- Page 15: "Anyone who possesses or transports a game bird, other than a migratory game bird, must leave attached to the carcass one feathered wing" (99%).
- Breasting birds in the field: keep the wing attached to the breast you carry (85%, my reading in the Rule Book).
- A game vest with a mesh back beats a plastic bag (Tip).
- Count birds by species as you clean them and write the tally in the Journal with the MU (Management Unit) (Tip).
- Cooler with ice for the drive home (Tip, from the trip checklist).
```

## Meat and cooking

```diagram sb-bird-yield
```

- A quail gives about 60 to 80 g of meat (Tip, 60%).
- Cook birds to 74 °C (165 °F) inside (85%).

```more meat and cooking in full
- Quail: pluck or skin, gut with a thumb. About 60 to 80 g of meat, one per person as a starter (Tip, 60%).
- Chukar and partridge: skin and breast, keep the legs. About 150 to 250 g of meat per bird (Tip, 60%).
- Food safety: USDA lists all poultry at 165 °F (74 °C) (85%, search preview of the USDA table). The app's meat care lesson: cook birds to 74 °C (80%).
- Breast meat is lean and dries fast. Cook it quick and hot, or braise whole birds slowly (Tip).
- Plucked, skin on, the meat stays moister (Tip).
- Lessons: [Meat care](#/s/meat-care) and [Cooking wild game](#/s/cooking).
```

- Region 8 quail: 1 October to 30 November, 10 a day, 30 in possession (99%).
- BC hunting licence only; shotgun, air gun or bow; no rifle (95 to 99%).

## Memory hooks

```diagram mql-care-3
```

- **Rhyme:** "Mark it, walk it, cool it, keep a wing; cook to seventy four, then let the kitchen sing." (Tip, 85 to 99% for the facts)
- **Number trick:** "10, 30, 74": 10 a day, 30 in possession (99%), cook to 74 °C (85%).

```more more hooks
- **Load hook:** "Seven and a half for the little ones." Lead 7.5 is standard for quail (75%).
- **Old hand's rule (opinion):** "A bird you cannot find is a bird you should not have shot." Hunting educators teach shooting only where you can recover the bird (Tip).
```

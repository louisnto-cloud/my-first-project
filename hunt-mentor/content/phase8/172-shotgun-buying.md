## Options, pros and cons

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Gas semi auto (Beretta A300) | Softest recoil with heavy steel. Factory 3 shell reducer | More parts to clean. Gas parts must stay clean |
| Inertia semi auto (Stoeger M3000) | Runs clean. Cheaper | Sharper recoil. Needs real loads to cycle |
| Pump (Benelli Nova, Remington 870) | Cheapest. Cycles any load. Simple | Most recoil. Short stroking under stress |
| Over under (Browning Citori) | Safest to show empty. Two chokes | Two shots. Costs more. Check steel rating |

> [!lean]
> My lean: a **Beretta A300 Outlander**, 12 gauge, 3 in, 28 in barrel. Gas softens 3 in steel, it ships with steel proof chokes and a 2 shell reducer, and the manual is clear.
> Budget pick: **Stoeger M3000** (inertia). Try both on the same day. Your call.

```more why each, with sources
- A300: gas system that vents excess pressure, "Every A300 Outlander has been tested with special proof test ammunition at 1320 bar with steel shot," steel proof Mobilchoke tubes, factory reducer to 3 shots (95%, Beretta manual).
- A300 watch out: the 2011 manual says not to use European "High Performance" steel loads with pellets over 3.5 mm. BB is 4.57 mm. VERIFY with Beretta Canada which goose loads suit an A300 before you buy BB.
- M3000: inertia, minimum 1 oz 3 dram loads, plug installed from the factory, full and improved modified not recommended with steel (95%, Stoeger manual).
- Pump: reliable and cheap, kicks hardest (70%, [Choosing your shotgun](#/s/shotgun-guide/2)).
- Over under: weak as a one gun for ducks plus upland; check the steel rating (65%, same lesson).
```

## Fit, and try before you buy

```diagram sg2-fit
```

- Wear your hunting jacket. Eyes shut, mount, open: straight down the rib (Tip).
- Pattern the gun you buy at 35 m before the hunt ([Chapter 6](#/s/mc-shotgun-06)).

```more fit in full
- LOP (length of pull) is trigger to the end of the stock. Most adults need about 14 to 14.75 in (65%, [Choosing your shotgun](#/s/shotgun-guide/7)).
- The A300 Outlander ships set for a right handed shooter with 55 or 60 mm drop; spacers and a plate change drop and cast. Beretta: that change "must be carried out by a competent gunsmith" (95%).
- Ask the shop to let you mount 3 guns 10 times each. The one that points where you look wins (Tip).
- A shooting club or clays range may let you try guns before you buy: VERIFY with a local club.
- Left eye dominant and right handed? Find out first: [Shotgun mastery](#/s/shotgun-mastery/12).
```

## Buying used: 7 checks

```diagram sg2-used
```

- Seller shows it unloaded; you prove it too (95%).
- If in doubt, pay a gunsmith to look before you pay the seller (Tip).

```checklist sg2-used-check
Seller opens it. I prove it unloaded myself
Bores from both ends: no rust, pits, bulges or dents
Choke tubes come out with the wrench; threads clean
Choke wrench and the tubes come with it
Action cycles 10 times, smooth, no grinding
Pump: fore end locks and unlocks. Semi auto: piston, seal, recoil spring present
Over under: opens and closes firmly, top lever snaps back
Plug present
Stock and recoil pad not cracked; barrel stamp readable
Serial number present
PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) checked; seller gets a reference number
```

```more the law on a private sale
- For a private sale of a non restricted firearm, the seller must get a reference number from the Registrar of Firearms (RCMP Canadian Firearms Program) before the transfer; you give the seller your licence details so they can ask (99%, [Firearms Act s. 23](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/F-11.6/section-23.html), via [Choosing your shotgun](#/s/shotgun-guide/9)).
- PAL means Possession and Acquisition Licence. You need yours to buy any firearm (99%).
- Home storage: unloaded, locked or bolt out or in a locked container, ammunition not readily at hand unless locked too (99%, [Firearm care](#/s/firearm-care/13)).
- Never buy a cut down shotgun: under 457 mm barrel or 660 mm overall can be a prohibited firearm (99%, [Chapter 1](#/s/mc-shotgun-01/6)).
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Buy the gun that fits, not the gun that wins the magazine test.

> [!mistake]
> - Buying online without ever mounting the gun.
> - A 3 1/2 in magnum "for geese" that you then flinch with all season.
> - A cheap used gun with a seized choke and no plug.

> [!field]
> Shop on a weekday morning, in your hunting jacket. Mount the A300 and the M3000 side by side, ask about the plug and the chokes in the box, then go pattern before the opener.

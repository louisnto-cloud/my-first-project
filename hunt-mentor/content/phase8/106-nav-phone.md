## Save the truck, start the track

```steps At the truck, before you walk
fn-nt-before-1 | Waypoint the truck before you leave it.
fn-hm-wpt-2 | Icon: Parking. Name it.
fn-ph-setup-4 | Airplane mode where there is no signal.
```

- Pin first. Track only if the walk is new or confusing (Tip).
- Note the battery percent as you leave (Tip).

```more pin and track in full
- Waypoint: Tools, Waypoint, At my location, or press and hold the map, then Drop waypoint (99%, app).
- Go & Track: Start recording, Pause, Stop and save. It shows distance, moving time, climb and points (99%, app).
- "Keep the screen on. iPhone web apps cannot record with the screen off." The app keeps the screen awake while recording (99%, app text).
- So a track costs battery all day; a waypoint costs almost nothing (Tip, [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery)).
- My lean: always the truck pin; a track on new ground, or start it at the last blood or when the light goes (Tip).
```

## Back to start

```diagram fn-nt-back
```

- Go & Track shows Back to start: distance and heading to where you began (99%).
- The green dot marks the start (99%).

```more Back to start in full
- The line reads like "Back to start: 1.9 km, heading 214° (SW). The green dot marks the start" (99%, app code).
- Under 15 m away it says you are at the start (99%).
- Headings are from true north. A compass set to 15 degrees east reads the same numbers (90%).
- The track also shows "GPS good (8 m)" or "GPS is weak (... m). Points are skipped until it improves." Points worse than 30 m are skipped (99%, app code).
- Only a waypoint, no track? Tools, Draw and measure, Measure: tap your blue dot, then the truck pin. It shows distance and the bearing of the leg (99% for the tool; using it this way is Tip).
```

## No signal: walk home in 4 steps

```steps Home with no bars
mc-nav-ns-1 | Airplane mode on. GPS still works.
mc-nav-ns-2 | Open the Hunt Map: find your blue dot.
mc-nav-ns-3 | Read Back to start, or measure to the pin.
mc-nav-ns-4 | Set the heading on the compass and walk.
```

- Walk with the compass; check the phone every 15 minutes (Tip).
- Aim off if the truck is on a road ([Chapter 4](#/s/mc-nav-04)).

```more why compass plus phone
- Airplane mode stops the phone searching for a tower; GPS keeps working (85%, [Maps 2](#/s/maps-navigation)).
- Walking with eyes on a screen trips you and burns battery. Walking with eyes on a tree is safer (Tip).
- Re read the heading at each check. It changes as you go round obstacles (Tip).
- Prefer handrails to a straight line in the dark ([Night navigation](#/s/night-navigation)).
```

## How accurate is the dot?

```diagram mc-nav-accuracy
```

- Open sky: about 5 m. Timber, cliffs and canyons: worse (90%).
- The blue circle around your dot shows the accuracy (99%).

```more accuracy in full
- "GPS enabled smartphones are typically accurate to within a 4.9 m (16 ft.) radius under open sky." Worse near buildings, bridges and trees (90%, gps.gov).
- Accuracy also depends on satellite geometry, signal blockage, the atmosphere and the receiver (90%, gps.gov).
- Hunt Map locate button: the blue circle is GPS accuracy (99%, app).
- Stand still in the open for 30 seconds before you mark something you need in the dark (Tip).
- Terrain data in the Hunt Map is about 24 m apart, so small cliffs and cut banks may not show (99%, app). The dot can be right and the map still miss a drop (Tip).
```

## Battery: warm, dim, offline

```diagram fn-bat-cold
```

- iPhones are made for 0 to 35°C. Colder, they can shut off early (95%).
- Inside chest pocket. Keep 30% for the walk out (Tip).

```more battery in full
- Apple: in the cold, battery life drops for a while and the phone may turn off; it returns to normal when warm (95%).
- Do not charge a frozen lithium battery; warm phone and bank first (75%, Battery University).
- Battery savers (Tip): screen dim and off, airplane mode in dead zones, Low Power Mode, Bluetooth and wifi off, no all day track.
- A 10,000 mAh (milliamp hour) bank refills most phones about twice (Tip). Keep it warm too.
- Battery budget and a one Saturday test: [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery).
```

## Satellite help when there are no bars

```diagram mc-nav-sat-compare
```

- iPhone 14 or later: Emergency SOS via satellite in Canada (95%).
- A messenger texts both ways on a plan; a beacon only sends SOS (60 to 65%).

```more the three kinds compared
- **iPhone 14 or later:** Emergency SOS via satellite in Canada with iOS 16.1 or later, only where there is no cell or wifi, outside with a clear view of the sky and horizon (95%, Apple). It shares location, elevation, battery and Medical ID if set (95%). Free for two years after activation (95%, Apple support page published 5 October 2026, checked 2026-10-17). The page does not say what it costs after that: VERIFY.
- **Satellite messenger** such as a Garmin inReach: SOS over the Iridium network to a staffed 24/7 response centre, two way texting, needs an active subscription (65%, Garmin).
- **PLB (personal locator beacon):** one way SOS on 406 MHz, no monthly plan for the SOS function, often with a 121.5 MHz homing signal and GPS (60%, gear guides). Registration in Canada: free on the Canadian Beacon Registry. Its page says registering a 406 MHz beacon "is required in most situations" and names PLBs only "in some situations" (90%, canada.ca beacon registry page, modified 18 February 2026, read 2026-10-17). Register yours anyway so rescuers get your contact details (Tip).
- [[Satellite messenger]] gear tiers and prices: [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery) (60 to 65%).
- My lean (Tip): if both of you carry iPhone 14 or later, that covers SOS. A messenger adds texting home "running late, all fine", which stops a needless search.
- Practise Apple's satellite Demo mode at home (Tip).
```

## When the phone dies

```diagram fn-ph-fail-flow
```

- STOP. Warm the phone 15 minutes. Then map and compass (95% for warming).
- Truly lost: stay put ([Chapter 7](#/s/mc-nav-07)).

```more the fall back plan
1. STOP: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan (75%, AdventureSmart).
2. Warm the phone inside your jacket for 15 minutes; a cold shut off often recovers (95%, Apple: returns to normal when warm).
3. Still dead? Paper map, oriented. Escape bearing from your hand ([Chapter 2](#/s/mc-nav-02)). Handrail to the baseline. Aim off ([Chapter 4](#/s/mc-nav-04)).
4. Not sure of any of that? Stay put, shelter, signal; your trip plan's call time brings help (75%, [Chapter 7](#/s/mc-nav-07)).
- Two is one, one is none: carry the paper map and compass every time (Tip).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram fn-nav-backup
```

- **Rhyme:** "Satellites are free, tiles are not: save the map before the spot."
- **Number trick:** 5 m in the open, 30 m and the track skips the point (90 to 99%).
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion, from [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery) Grandpa's rule): "Treat the phone like your water. Ration it, keep it warm."

```more more hooks
- **Rhyme:** "Pin the truck, then trust your luck."
- **Number trick:** 0 to 35°C: iPhone's comfort range (95%).
- **Number trick:** 14 and 16.1: iPhone 14, iOS 16.1 for satellite SOS in Canada (95%).
- **Rhyme:** "Two is one, one is none."
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion, BCSARA's "only a cell phone" warning): "A phone is a tool, not a plan."

> [!mistake]
> - Driving out of signal before saving Offline Maps.
> - Phone in the pack lid on a frosty morning.
> - Walking with eyes glued to the screen.
```

> [!why]
> Find the food in shallow, calm water near a safe place to rest, and you have found the ducks.

```diagram mdk-wetland-section
```

- Puddle ducks: shallow edges, under about 30 cm (75%).
- Divers: deeper, open water (80%).

```more water depth and cover
- Dabbling ducks feed best in water less than about 30 cm (12 in) deep; teal and pintail like the shallowest edges, 5 to 10 cm (70 to 75%).
- Divers use bigger, deeper lakes and rivers and dive to the bottom for plants, snails and clams (80%).
- Cattail and bulrush bands give cover and nest sites (80%).
- Beaver ponds are duck ponds: shallow, full of food, sheltered (Tip).
- Hunter meaning: on a big lake, puddle ducks sit in the shallow bays; divers raft off the points (Tip).
```

## Food by season

```diagram mdk-food-calendar
```

- Fall: marsh seeds, pondweed, waste grain (80%).
- Divers add snails, clams and insects (80%).

```more food in your areas
- Fall foods: seeds of marsh plants, pondweed, waste grain in cut fields (80%).
- Mallards and pintails feed in harvested grain fields at dawn and dusk (80%).
- Shovelers sieve tiny animals with their comb edged bill (75%).
- Laying hens need protein: snails and insects in spring (75%).
- Fraser delta waterfowl need food from September to April, much of it on farm fields (80%).
- Hunter meaning: a cut grain field next to a pond is a mallard magnet. Ask the farmer (Tip).
```

## Elevation by month

```diagram mdk-elev-month
```

- Summer: 300 to 1,500 m wetlands (60%).
- Winter: low valley water under about 700 m (65%).

```more the pattern
- Breeding: Interior wetlands and potholes from about 300 to 1,500 m (60%).
- Lesser scaup, canvasback and redhead breed on plateau lakes at 500 to 1,500 m (85%).
- Plateau lakes start to freeze in October; birds pack onto valley water in November (60 to 65%).
- Winter: open water from sea level to about 700 m (65%).
- These are general bands from the app migration data, not counts (55 to 65%).
```

## Find them on the Hunt Map

```steps Use the Hunt Map in 4 steps
mdk-map-1 | Turn on Duck waters and Wetlands.
mdk-map-2 | Add Private land, Parks and MU lines.
mdk-map-3 | Use Sun and shade: sun behind your hide.
mdk-map-4 | Use What can I see from here to pick a glassing knoll.
```

- Layers find the water; land status says if you can hunt it (Tip).
- Open the [Hunt Map](#/map).

```more every layer and tool, and how to use it for ducks
- Layer "Duck staging and winter waters": valley lakes and wetlands of 20 ha or more where ducks stop in fall and spring, plus low open water in winter (app layer guide).
- Layer "Wetlands": marshes, swamps and bogs from the Freshwater Atlas. Small potholes for puddle ducks (app layer guide).
- Land status: Private land, Parks and protected areas, Reserves, MU (Management Unit) lines and numbers. A pond in private land needs permission (99%).
- Terrain tools: Slope steepness (find a gentle walk in for a heavy decoy bag), Slope direction (aspect: south facing shores warm and open first), What can I see from here (a knoll to glass the water from), Sun and shade (put the rising sun behind you, in the ducks' eyes) (all Tips).
- Scent cone and wind: check which shore will have the wind at your back or side for the landing (Tip).
- Insights on a spot shows the month band for ducks from the app migration data (55 to 65%).
- Field task: drop a waypoint on each pond you scout, with a note of what you saw.
```

## The night after rain

```diagram tk-wet-night
```

- Never leave it zipped in a soft case (95%).
- Dry first, then the routine clean (95%).

```more the manual's words
- Wet conditions: "dry it using compressed air or let it dry in a warm / dry place without any protective cover i.e. drag bag" (95%, Tikka manual page 18).
- "Corrosion can begin within 24 hours if the rifle is not cleaned after use" (95%, page 18).
- "Do not store the rifle in a leather or fabric case. These materials attract moisture, even though they may appear to be perfectly dry" (95%, page 19).
- "Before storage, always check the condition of the rifle and ensure that it is perfectly dry" (95%, page 19).
- Wipe the stock dry (95%, page 18). Laminate is many thin layers of wood glued together, which resists swelling (Tip). Oil finish or not on your stock: VERIFY before you use stock oil.
- Soaked through, or the zero walked after a wet week? The action can come out to dry (chapter 7), then confirm zero (Tip).
```

## Rain on the hunt

```diagram tk-rain-field
```

- Muzzle covered, scope caps on (Tip).
- Dipped the muzzle? Unload and look through (95%).

```more field tips
- Muzzle cover or a bit of tape keeps rain and needles out (Tip). Firing through thin tape is common practice; Tikka does not mention it. VERIFY with the maker before you rely on it.
- Scope caps on until you need the scope. Wipe lenses with a lens cloth, not your shirt (Tip, [Optics](#/s/optics-mastery/0)).
- Carry with the muzzle pointed safe and slightly down in rain; water runs off, not in. Muzzle control still comes first (Tip).
- A wet magazine and wet rounds: dry them tonight too. Check rounds for green corrosion before the next hunt (Tip; the manual says inspect every round, 95%).
- Quick wipe at lunch: dry cloth over the action and bolt handle (Tip).
```

## Cold days

```diagram tk-cold
```

- Oil in the bolt? Clean it out for cold weather (95%).
- Gloves: the big trigger guard and an oversized knob help (75 to 95%).

```more cold weather in full
- "If you have reason to believe that oil may have penetrated the bolt, you should clean it in order to retain its smooth operation. This is particularly important if you use the rifle in cold or harsh weather" (95%, Tikka manual page 13). Bolt strip: chapter 7.
- The Arctic trigger guard is "enlarged at the bottom to allow shooting with gloves" (75%, all4shooters).
- An oversized bolt handle knob is a Tikka accessory: push it over the original knob and tighten the screw inside it, 5 to 10 turns (95%, Tikka manual page 17). Price: [chapter 9](#/s/mc-tikka-09).
- Practise the safety, the magazine release and the bolt with your real hunting gloves (Tip).
- Condensation: cold steel brought into a warm room collects water. Either leave it cased in a cold, secure, lawful place overnight, or uncase it, let it warm, then dry and oil it (Tip; no manual covers this).
- Scope fog: breathe down and away from the eyepiece (Tip).
```

## Snow or mud in the barrel

```diagram tk-cold
```

- Any fall or muzzle dip: unload, bolt out, look through (95%).
- Any obstruction: gunsmith before you fire (95%).

```more the obstruction check
- "Check the barrel to ensure there are no obstructions in the chamber and bore. This is extremely important because serious injury can result to the user or to nearby persons if a cartridge is fired in an obstructed barrel or chamber" (95%, Tikka manual page 9).
- Steps: magazine out, bolt out, look through the barrel from the rear. "If an obstruction in the barrel is detected, a competent gunsmith must remove the obstruction" (95%).
- Field reality: loose snow you can see may clear with a cleaning rod pushed from the breech, rifle unloaded. Mud or anything stuck: do not shoot (Tip, as in [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0)).
- A take down rod in the pack makes this possible on the hill (Tip).
```

## Storage and transport: the law

```diagram tk-storage-law
```

- Home: unloaded, and locked, bolt out, or locked up (99%).
- Vehicle: never loaded (99%).

```more the rules, as the app's safety lessons state them
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Home storage of a non restricted rifle: unloaded, and either a secure locking device, the bolt removed, or a locked container or room that cannot readily be broken into; ammunition not readily at hand unless locked up too (99%, [Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals Regulations](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209/FullText.html), s. 5(1), via [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0), checked 2026-10-17).
- Bolt out storage: your Tikka bolt comes out in 2 seconds (chapter 2). Store the bolt in a different locked place (Tip).
- Unloaded means no cartridge in the chamber and none in an attached or inserted magazine (99%, federal transport rules, via [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0)).
- Leaving a vehicle: non restricted firearms locked in the trunk or a lockable compartment; no trunk, out of sight and the vehicle locked (99%, same).
- BC: no loaded firearm in or on a vehicle (99%, [firearm safety refresher](#/s/firearm-safety/0)); hunting synopsis item 28 (99%, [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0)).
- Tikka's advice: "keep your firearm locked away and store the ammunition in a separate locked location" (95%, Tikka manual page 19).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram tk-rain-field
```

- **"Wet rifle, open case."** Out of the bag the same night.
- **"Cold and dry, oil goodbye."** Clean the oil out of the bolt for the cold.
- **"Fell? Look through."**

```more mistakes, rule and the field task
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "Rain doesn't rust a rifle. Neglect does."

> [!mistake]
> - Zipping a wet rifle into a soft case for the drive home and forgetting it till the weekend.
> - An oil soaked bolt on a minus 20 C morning.
> - Firing after a slip in the snow without looking through the bore.

> [!field]
> Before the next wet hunt: pack a muzzle cover, scope caps, a dry cloth, a take down rod and a spare dry bag for the drive home.
```

```checklist tk-wet-kit
Muzzle cover or tape
Scope caps on
Dry cloth in a zip bag
Take down cleaning rod
Rifle out of the case tonight, dry, clean
```

## Scouting

```steps Scout a new area in 5 steps
mmd-map-1 | At home: layers for deer, burns, land status.
mmd-map-2 | Mark south and west slopes for late fall.
mmd-map-4 | Pick seats that see several openings.
glassing-grid | At dusk: glass from your seats.
sa-md-sign-grid | Midday: walk for sign, beds, trails.
```

- Scout in the off season and the week before (Tip).
- Log every deer: time, slope, which way it faced (Tip).

```more scouting in detail
- Use the [Hunt Map](#/map) at home (chapter 7), then confirm on foot (Tip).
- Drive the Heffley Lake road at dusk with binoculars; note every deer, the time, the slope and which way it faced (Tip, quick lesson field task).
- Summer scouting shows where bucks live, but snow can move them before your hunt (85%, chapter 5). Scout again the week before (Tip).
- Walk midday for sign: tracks, pellets, beds, rubs, browse (chapter 8). Stay off the slopes you plan to hunt at dawn (Tip).
- Check land status, closures and No Shooting areas for every spot (Tip, [land status](#/s/maps-land-status)).
- Use the Journal: after 10 trips you have your own pattern (Tip).
```

## Glassing

```diagram glassing-grid
```

- Sit on a rest before first light; glass shadows and edges (Tip).
- Look for white rumps, ears, antler tips, a flick (65%, Tip).

```video
yt-glassing-mule-deer | Sit, rest the optics, and pick the slope apart.
```

```more glassing in detail
- Be on the seat 45 minutes before legal light (Tip, quick lesson). Legal light starts 1 hour before sunrise (99%, page 11, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)).
- "Glass from a distance and let the spotting scope do the walking" (60%, Mule Deer Foundation, M. Luby). Set up before first light and glass into the shadows (60%).
- In snow free country you often find mule deer by their whitish rumps; in snow, look for brown bodies (65%, Rinella).
- Bedded deer: scan for heads, necks, ears and antler tips above brush (65%, Rinella).
- Grid method: split the slope into squares, near to far, 1 to 2 minutes per square (Tip, [Glassing](#/s/glassing)).
- Steady optics on a tripod or pack; 10x42 binoculars are a good first optic (Tip, Mule Deer Foundation).
- Glass again the same slope; deer stand up and appear all morning (Tip).
```

## Spot and stalk

```steps Spot and stalk in 5 steps
sa-stalk-1 | Glass from high ground at first light.
sa-stalk-2 | Pick 2 landmarks near the deer.
sa-stalk-3 | Check the wind before every move.
sa-stalk-4 | Close in out of sight, behind cover.
sa-stalk-5 | Get a rest. Wait for broadside.
```

- Plan with landmarks, wind and sun before you move (60%, Tip).
- From above if you can: they bed facing down (65%).

```video
yt-stalking-mule-deer | Plan the stalk from where you glassed: landmarks, wind, cover.
```

```more stalking in detail
- Use terrain, wind direction and the sun to your advantage, and stay out of sight lines (60%, Mule Deer Foundation).
- Mule deer bed facing downhill, so "starting the stalk above the deer" is a huge advantage (65%, Rinella).
- Mule deer bed on the leeward side of rolls; plan for the wind they are using (65%).
- Landmarks look different up close. Pick 2 or 3 near the deer (a dead tree, a rock, a bush) and photograph them from the glassing seat (Tip).
- Morning thermals rise once the sun is up: come from above or the side then. Evening: from below or across (80% thermals; Tip approach).
- Leave the pack, slow to a crawl the last 100 m, check the wind every 20 m (Tip).
- Bedded buck: wait for it to stand if the angle is bad; deer usually stand to stretch in the afternoon (80%, chapter 6).
- If the wind turns: back out and come back another day (Tip).
```

## Still hunting

```anim hf-step-cadence A few steps, then a long look.
```

- Slow walk into the wind through bedding cover (Tip).
- Works midday and in timber; deer often see you first (Tip).

```more still hunting in detail
- A few steps, stop, look for a minute; walk like a deer, not a person (Tip, [Still hunting](#/s/still-hunting)).
- Into or across the wind only (Tip).
- Look for parts: an ear, a leg, a horizontal back line in vertical timber (Tip).
- Best on damp, quiet ground, in wind or light rain (Tip).
- Shots are quick and close: shoot only standing deer you have fully identified (Tip).
- Mule deer in rough broken ground are better glassed than still hunted (Tip, hunter consensus).
```

## Sit a trail or an edge

```diagram ld-glass-seats
```

- Sit between beds and feed at dawn and dusk (Tip).
- Quiet, steady, great for a first season (Tip).

```more sitting in detail
- Deer feed on open slopes at dawn and dusk and drift to beds (85%, chapter 6). Sit the edge where they cross (Tip).
- Pick a seat with the wind blowing from the deer to you, a rest, and a safe backstop (Tip).
- In the rut, sit a saddle or bench between doe groups from first light (Tip, [the rut](#/s/the-rut)).
- Pros: quiet, rested shot, learn deer behaviour. Cons: needs the right spot and patience (Tip).
- My lean for your first trip: sit and glass a feeding slope at dawn and dusk; scout midday (Tip, quick lesson).
```

## Calling and rattling

```diagram sg-calls-legal
```

- Mouth calls are allowed; electronic calls are not (95 to 99%).
- Rattling works less well for mule deer (50%, Tip).

```more calls in detail
> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Recorded or electronic calls are unlawful for hunting wildlife except wolf, coyote, cougar, lynx, bobcat and Schedule C birds (99%, page 12, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)).
- Mouth calls, grunt tubes and rattling antlers are not recorded or electronic, so they are allowed (95%, my reading in the Rule Book).
- Rattling pulled bucks in about 65% of sessions in a Texas white tail study (70%), but it is less reliable for mule deer (50%).
- A soft grunt or doe bleat can stop a walking buck for a shot (60%, hunter lore).
- Mule deer does respond to fawn distress calls (70%, Lingle). That is a reason not to blow distress calls carelessly: you may pull does, not bucks (Tip).
- Deer urine lures: banned if from outside BC; urine scents can carry CWD (Chronic Wasting Disease) (99%, page 12 item 51, inside back cover).
```

## Hunting with a partner

```diagram mmd-partner
```

- One glasses and signals, one stalks (Tip).
- Agree zones of fire and signals before you split (Tip).

```diagram mmd-hand-signals
```

```more partner tactics in detail
- **Spotter and stalker:** the spotter stays on the glassing seat and guides with hand signals; the stalker follows landmarks (Tip).
- **Two seats:** you and your brother glass two slopes and meet at 10:00 (Tip).
- **Push a draw:** one hunter walks a draw with the wind at his back while the other sits the escape route up the slope; mule deer often flee uphill (70% for uphill flight; Tip for the tactic). Never with either hunter in the other's [[Zone of fire]].
- Blaze orange on both of you when moving (Tip; not required by the BC synopsis, 90%).
- Only one shooter at a time (Tip). The hunter who kills the deer cancels his own species licence right away (99%, page 11 item 1, via the [quick lesson](#/s/mule-deer)).
- Dogs: allowed for deer only on a leash (99%, page 12, [legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods)). That includes a dog tracking a wounded deer (90%, my reading in the Rule Book).
```

## Decide on the day

```diagram mmd-day-decision
```

- Wind wrong? Change slope (Tip).
- Not sure? No shot (Tip).

```more decisions in detail
- Before dawn: check the forecast wind and thermals; pick the slope where both work (Tip).
- First light: glass. No deer by about 10:00? Walk for sign or move to the next seat (Tip).
- Deer bedded: plan the stalk, or note the spot and come back for the evening (Tip).
- Snow fell overnight: drop elevation and look for fresh tracks (85% for deer moving down; Tip for the tactic).
- Before any shot: legal species, sex, points counted on one side, safe backstop, inside your tested range, broadside or quartering away (99% for the legal parts via the [Rule Book](#/s/rb-region-3); Tip for the rest).
- Long day plan: dawn seat, midday sign walk, afternoon stalk or seat, dusk seat (Tip).
```

## What a 40 year hunter does differently

```diagram mmd-old-hand
```

- Sits and glasses more, walks less (Tip).
- Lets a bad chance go (Tip).

```more the old hand's habits and ethics
- Glasses for hours, walks for minutes (Tip, widely taught).
- Checks the wind all day and walks away from a bad wind (Tip).
- Walks 500 m to 1 km past roads and pullouts (Tip).
- Knows each slope by month and snow (chapter 5) and keeps a journal (Tip).
- Waits for a standing broadside deer inside a tested range; passes on running, skylined or quartering to shots (Tip, [shot placement](#/s/shot-placement)).
- Counts points twice in a 4 point season (99% rule; Tip habit).
- Expects the look back, and is ready on a rest (65%).
- In the rut, hunts the does and stays out all day (65%, Rinella).
- Ethics: fair chase, no lights (99%, page 11), and a real effort to recover every hit animal (95%, [blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing)). The full list of unlawful acts: [Rule Book](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts).
- Respect land: ask permission on private land and ranches (Tip, [landowner permission](#/s/landowner-permission)).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mmd-method-picker
```

- **Rhyme:** "Glass at first light, stalk at noon, sit at dusk under the moon." (Tip)
- **Number trick:** 45, 10, 100: be seated 45 minutes before light; glass till 10:00; crawl the last 100 m (Tip).
- **Old hand's rule** (tradition): "Glass more, walk less."

```more more hooks
- **Rhyme:** "Wind in your face, landmarks in mind; from above the bed, you'll be hard to find."
- **Number trick:** 2 landmarks near the deer, 1 shooter at a time (Tip).
- **Old hand's rule** (widely taught, opinion): "The deer you see first is the deer you can stalk."
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "If the wind is wrong, go home or go elsewhere."
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "When it beds, you plan; when it stands, you shoot."

> [!mistake]
> - Walking ridgelines all day and seeing only rumps.
> - Starting a stalk without landmarks.
> - Talking on the glassing seat.

> [!field]
> First trip plan: dawn seat, midday sign walk, dusk seat. Agree 4 hand signals with your brother before you leave the truck.
```

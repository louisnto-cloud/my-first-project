> [!why]
> Quail live in a narrow band: low, brushy, close to food and water, often next to people. Chukar live on the rock above them. Learn the band and you stop walking empty country.

```diagram mql-habitat-section
```

- Quail: valley floor and first benches, brush beside food (85%).
- Chukar: steep rock and cheatgrass slopes above (80%).

```more habitat in full
- BC: quail mostly breed below 750 m, most common from 250 to 500 m, some as high as 1,250 m (85%, BC Breeding Bird Atlas).
- They prefer the Ponderosa Pine and Bunchgrass zones in the Interior (85%).
- Habitat: mixed farm and suburban areas with lots of cover for feeding, nesting and roosting, plus riparian, grassland and open pine or broadleaf woods (85%).
- They need "a mosaic of low, brushy vegetation, with grass and forb openings, taller shrubs, and trees, interspersed with water" (80%, California habitat records).
- Washington lists: thick tangles near stream beds, small ponds and wetlands; valley bottoms with Russian olive or tall sage; tree lines and fence lines; low brush and tall grass; edges of grain fields (75%).
- Chukar: open, rocky, dry slopes and canyon walls, usually steep; grass and brush cover with cheatgrass a key plant (75 to 80%, Audubon and FEIS).
- Huns: cultivated land, hedgerows, bushy pastures and stubble (75%, Audubon).
```

## Plants that hold quail

```gallery
hab-woods-rose-slope | Wild rose along a dry creek: thorny hiding cover.
hab-blackberry | Blackberry thicket: a covey can vanish in it.
hab-chokecherry | Chokecherry in fruit: brushy draws with berries.
hab-snowberry | Snowberry: berries hang on into winter.
```

- Thorny brush beside open feeding ground (Tip).
- Food: seeds and green leaves, legumes first (80%).

```more foods by season
| Season | Main food (80%, California records) |
|---|---|
| Fall and winter | Seeds, waste grain, some fruit |
| Spring | Green leaves, new shoots, flowers |
| Summer | Seeds; chicks eat many insects |

- Adults eat mostly seeds and green plants; legume seeds lead (80%). Insects are under 5% of the adult diet (70%).
- Also acorns, berries, flowers and bulbs (75%, Audubon).
- In BC yards they take garden seed and sprouting peas (65%, BC Outdoors).
- Plant names for the Okanagan draws are from the app's [habitat tour](#/s/habitat-upland): rose, blackberry, chokecherry, snowberry (Tip for which ones hold birds).
```

## Chukar ground

```gallery
hab-kamloops-sagebrush | Sagebrush slope near Kamloops: bare soil between grey shrubs.
hab-bitterbrush-slope | Bitterbrush on a dry slope: chukar country shrub.
hab-lac-du-bois-grassland | Open bunchgrass in October.
```

- Steep, rocky, dry, with cheatgrass and sage (75 to 80%).
- Water within reach in dry weather (75%).

```more chukar ground in full
- The chukar's key plant in North America is cheatgrass (80%, FEIS). Also sagebrush, bitterbrush, currant and rabbitbrush slopes (80%).
- Slopes are usually steep: over 7% grade with at least 60 m (200 ft) of rise (80%).
- Chukar need water unless they can eat lots of green leaves (75%, Audubon). Broods in California stay within about 3.2 km of water (80%).
- BC: best on low slopes of the Thompson from Kamloops to Lytton; also in the South Okanagan and Similkameen (65%, BC Outdoors).
- The quick lesson: mostly 250 to 500 m in the Thompson (75%); start at springs and creek mouths below cliffs (Tip).
```

## Elevation by month

```diagram mql-elev-months
```

- Quail: about 250 to 750 m all year. No migration (60 to 85%).
- Chukar: move lower in deep snow (80%).

```more elevation in full
- The app migration data puts quail at 250 to 750 m in every month, in Bunchgrass and Ponderosa Pine zones (60 to 75% by month).
- Most BC breeding birds sit at 250 to 500 m (85%).
- Snow pushes quail lower and closer to houses (Tip, from the trip plan).
- Chukar: altitudinal moves from higher to lower ground in heavy snow (80%, FEIS). No BC elevation numbers found by month: VERIFY.
```

- South Okanagan (Region 8): quail, chukar and Huns, all open (99%).
- Heffley Creek (Region 3): no quail row. Mission (Region 2): no quail row (97%).

```more each area in full
- Region 8: quail, chukar, gray partridge and pheasant cocks have seasons in MUs (Management Units) 8-1 to 8-15 and 8-21 to 8-26 (99%, Rule Book).
- Region 3, Heffley Creek: chukar and gray partridge rows in MUs 3-12 to 3-20 and 3-26 to 3-44, but **no quail row** (97%). Quail at the property are not legal game. Heffley Creek is outside the main quail range (60%).
- Region 2, Mission: pheasant cocks only, MUs 2-4 and 2-8; no quail, chukar or partridge rows (97%). The Fraser Valley quail population appears gone anyway (85%, BC atlas).
- Chukar near Heffley Creek: the dry Thompson valley slopes toward Lytton hold the best birds (65 to 75%).
```


- Turn on: California quail habitat, Private land, City and town limits (Tip).
- Then Reserves, Parks and protected areas, Management Units (Tip).

```more how to use the layers
- Open the [Hunt Map](#/map) and tap **Layers**.
- "California quail habitat (no migration)": low Bunchgrass and Ponderosa Pine country near farms and brushy creeks, all year. General pattern (60%, app layer note). Loaded for areas C and D.
- "Private land": you need the owner's permission. Not every parcel is fenced or signed (90%, app layer note).
- "City and town limits": most ban shooting inside their limits by bylaw (95% for the boundaries; bylaws in chapter 9).

- "Parks and protected areas", "Wildlife Management Areas", "Management Units", "Management Unit numbers": rules differ by place (95%).
- Download area C, "South Okanagan and Similkameen (quail trip)", before you go: phone signal is patchy (Tip).
```

## Hunt Map: terrain tools

```steps Four terrain tools for birds
mql-map-1 | Slope steepness: gentle for quail, steep for chukar.
mql-map-2 | Slope direction: south slopes warm and melt first.
mql-map-3 | What can I see: pick a spot to glass a slope.
mql-map-4 | Sun and shade: shady slopes at midday, sunny edges at dawn.
```

- Tap **Terrain tools** on the right of the Hunt Map (95%).
- Slope, direction, view and sun all work offline in downloaded areas (90%).

```more terrain tools in full
- The Terrain sheet has four tiles: Slope steepness, Slope direction, What can I see, Sun and shade (95%, the app).
- Slope steepness colours ground by angle, from gentle to over 45°. Chukar like steep slopes (80%); stay off slopes you cannot walk safely (Tip).
- Slope direction: on cold early season nights chukar may roost on warmer south facing slopes; later they gather where snow is thin (75%, Washington Fish and Wildlife).
- What can I see from here: find a glassing point over a chukar face, glass for feeding birds, then plan a stalk from above (75%).
- Sun and shade: lights the terrain for a time today and shows sunrise and sunset. Chukar sit in shade in the heat of the day (80%). For quail, look for brushy edges that catch the first sun (Tip).
- Lessons: [Maps 1: reading terrain](#/s/maps-terrain) and [Map scouting from home](#/s/map-scouting).
```

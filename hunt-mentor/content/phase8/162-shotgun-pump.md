> [!why]
> A pump is the simplest repeater. It also punishes a lazy hand: half a stroke and it jams when the bird is in front of you.

```diagram sg2-pump-parts
```

- Steps follow the Remington 870 manual. Other pumps are close, not identical (95%).
- Your own gun's manual wins (Tip).

```more how a pump works
- Pulling the fore end back opens the bolt and throws out the shell in the chamber. Pushing it forward picks up the next shell and closes the bolt (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- On the 870, the fore end is locked when the gun is cocked. You push the action bar lock to open it (95%).
- "When the fore-end is forward, the action is closed and the firearm can be fired" (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- Pros and cons of a pump as your first gun: [Chapter 12](#/s/mc-shotgun-12).
```

## The safety

- 870: a cross bolt button behind the trigger (95%).
- Red band hidden = safe. Red band showing = it can fire (95%).

```more the safety in full
- Remington: "To engage the safety mechanism, push the button so the RED BAND MARKING CANNOT BE SEEN" (95%).
- "Never pull the trigger when the safety mechanism is engaged" (95%).
- A safety is "a mechanical device" and can fail. "Don't rely on your gun's safety" (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- Some 870s also have a key lock in the safety button. Keep the key away from the gun and the shells (95%).
```

## Load, step by step

```steps Load a pump in 4 steps
sg2-pump-load-1 | Point it safe. Safety on. Push the lock, pull back.
sg2-pump-load-2 | Drop one shell into the port.
sg2-pump-load-3 | Fore end fully forward. Now loaded.
sg2-pump-load-4 | Push shells into the magazine past the latch.
```

- Load only where you will hunt, after you check the barrel is clear (95%).
- Plug in: the magazine takes 2 (99%).

```more the manual's words
1. Point the firearm in a safe direction.
2. Engage the safety mechanism.
3. Push upward on the action bar lock.
4. Pull the fore end fully rearward.
5. Put one shell of the correct gauge and length through the ejection port.
6. Push the fore end forward to close the action. "THE FIREARM IS NOW LOADED."
7. Press a shell against the carrier and push it fully forward into the magazine.
- "Be sure the rim of each shell snaps past the shell latch" (95%, Remington 870 manual, how to load).
- Without a plug the 870 tube holds 4 shells of 3 in or 2 3/4 in (95%). With the plug: 2 (99% law; 95% manual).
- Check the barrel stamp against the box before you load ([Chapter 5](#/s/mc-shotgun-05)).
```

## Unload, step by step

```steps Unload a pump in 4 steps
sg2-pump-unload-1 | Point it safe. Safety on. Lock, then slowly back.
sg2-pump-unload-2 | Lift the chamber shell out of the port.
sg2-pump-unload-3 | Work it again until no shell comes out. Count.
sg2-pump-unload-4 | Look and feel: chamber and magazine empty.
```

- Count out what you counted in (Tip).
- Leave the action open until it is cased (Tip).

```more the manual's words
1. Point the firearm in a safe direction.
2. Engage the safety mechanism.
3. Push upward on the action bar lock.
4. Pull the fore end slowly rearward until the front of the shell is even with the ejection port.
5. Lift the front of the shell outward and remove it.
6. Pull the fore end fully rearward until the next shell is released from the magazine.
7. Tip the gun sideways so the shell drops from the port.
8. Close and open the action until all shells are removed.
- "Look into the ejection port. Check the chamber and magazine to make sure there are no shells in the firearm" (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- Never pump live shells out by racking the gun fast over and over with the muzzle wandering. Slow and pointed safe (Tip).
```

## What goes wrong

```diagram sg2-pump-fails
```

- Short stroke: the top beginner jam. Rack it all the way, hard (70%).
- A shell slid back over the carrier: safety on, muzzle safe, open hard (95%).

```more fixes in full
- Short stroking: under stress beginners pull the fore end only part way, so the next shell does not feed (70%, [Choosing your shotgun](#/s/shotgun-guide/2)).
- Shell slid back over the carrier while loading: "Engage the safety mechanism... Keep the firearm pointed in a safe direction. Forcefully OPEN the action" or take out the trigger plate and remove it (95%, Remington 870 manual).
- Wrong gauge or length: [Chapter 5](#/s/mc-shotgun-05). A 20 gauge shell in a 12 gauge can lodge in the barrel (95%).
- Rim not past the latch: the shell can slide back. Push each one until it clicks (95%).
- Gun went click, no bang: keep it pointed safe, wait, then unload. Never look down the muzzle (Tip; Beretta's manual says wait at least one minute, 95%).
```

## Practice at home, unloaded

- 20 slow load and unload drills with snap caps, not live shells (Tip).
- Then 20 full strokes, eyes on a spot on the wall (Tip).

```more drill notes
- Snap caps are dummy shells. Live ammunition stays out of the room when you drill (Tip; Browning manual keeps ammunition away from cleaning areas, 95%).
- Say ACTS and PROVE out loud each time (Tip).
- A pump is legal for grouse and ducks with the plug in (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/2)).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Pump it like you are angry at it. A gentle pump is a jammed pump.

> [!mistake]
> - Short stroking the fore end.
> - Loading the magazine with the action open and the muzzle drifting.
> - Forgetting the round in the chamber when you "unload" the magazine.

> [!field]
> On the range, load 2, fire 2 at a clay, unload and count. Do it 10 times before your first hunt.

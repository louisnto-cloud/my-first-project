## Pack the kit

```diagram tk-range-kit
```

- Eye and ear protection for you and anyone near you (95%).
- Two test loads, one box each (Tip).

```more why each item
- "Always wear protective glasses and earplugs whenever you shoot, and make sure that persons close to you are similarly protected" (95%, Tikka manual page 10).
- Snap caps for warm up dry fire at the bench (95%, page 9).
- Torx bits and a torque driver: if a screw is loose, fix it to the right number on the spot (Tip; numbers in [chapter 4](#/s/mc-tikka-04)).
- Your firearms licence: the PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) is shown at club sign up (70%, [sighting in](#/s/sighting-in/0)).
- Transport: unloaded, chamber and magazine empty, in the vehicle (99%, [chapter 8](#/s/mc-tikka-08)). Cased and ammunition separate is the good habit (Tip).
```

Firearms licence, journal, pen

## The timeline

```diagram tk-range-timeline
```

- Get on paper close, then zero at 100 m (95%).
- Finish from field positions, not the bench (Tip).

```more each block in detail
1. **Arrive:** read the range rules; learn the cease fire call; load check at the firing line only (95% load check, page 8; range rules Tip).
2. **On paper:** bore sight at 25 to 30 m, bolt out (95%, page 12). Then one shot, then a group.
3. **Zero at 100 m:** 3 shot groups from a solid rest; adjust to the group centre; repeat; one confirm group (95%, page 12). Irons: 7 mm a click. Scope: read the turret.
4. **Load test:** 3 groups of 3 with each load. Keep the tighter one that feeds and ejects cleanly (95% for testing function, page 9; group choice Tip).
5. **Field positions:** sitting with sticks, kneeling, standing on a post. 5 shots each at 100 m (Tip).
6. **Plate test:** 5 shots per position per distance (Tip, [chapter 5](#/s/mc-tikka-05)).
7. **Finish:** unload, prove it, case it. Clean that night (95%, page 18).
- Barrel heat: let it cool between groups; a hot barrel can shift shots (Tip, [sighting in](#/s/sighting-in/0)).
- Any change in sound, a sticky bolt, a flattened or leaking primer: stop shooting (95%, page 19).
```

## One sheet, five bulls

```diagram tk-target-sheet
```

- Two zero groups, a confirm group, load B, and a cold bore bull (Tip).
- Write the details beside each group (Tip).

```more how to use the sheet
- Bull 1 and 2: zero groups. Write the clicks you made between them (Tip).
- Bull 3: the confirm group after the last adjustment (95%, the manual says repeat until the group is near the bull).
- Bull 4: the second load, same aim point (Tip).
- Bull 5: keep it blank. Next range day, your very first shot from a cold, clean barrel goes here. That is your hunting shot (Tip). The manual says a cleaned .22 bore needs a few rounds to settle; whether your .308 shifts on the first shot after cleaning is something only this bull tells you (Tip).
- Photograph the sheet for the journal (Tip).
```

## Your zero card

```diagram tk-zero-card
```

- Load, lot, zero distance, date (Tip).
- Plate test limits per position (Tip).

```more what goes on it and why
- Rifle and sights: irons or which scope (Tip).
- Load and lot number: a new lot can shift the zero (Tip).
- Zero distance and date (Tip).
- Clicks from bore sight: lets you get back fast if a scope is knocked or a sight refitted (Tip).
- Plate test limits from [chapter 5](#/s/mc-tikka-05): your personal maximum range from each position (Tip).
- Next check: before every hunt, a few shots to confirm zero (Tip). After any fall, flight or rough ride: confirm (Tip).
```

## After the range

```diagram tk-range-kit
```

- Clean tonight (95%).
- Log rounds fired, groups, limits (Tip).

```more the evening routine
- Routine clean from [chapter 7](#/s/mc-tikka-07): rust can start within 24 hours (95%, page 18).
- Check the receiver screws and ring screws (95%, page 18 for receiver screws).
- Write the zero card. Put a copy in the case (Tip).
- Count the remaining rounds of your chosen load. Buy 3 more boxes of the same lot (Tip, [chapter 6](#/s/mc-tikka-06)).
- Add the session to the app's journal and the My guns log (Tip).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram tk-target-sheet
```

- **"Close, then far."** 25 m on paper, then 100 m.
- **"Bench to zero, field to learn."**
- **"Log it or lose it."**

```more mistakes, rule and the field task
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion): "The range is where you make mistakes cheaply."

> [!mistake]
> - Chasing one flyer shot with the turrets.
> - Shooting only from the bench and calling it ready.
> - Leaving without writing the load, lot and clicks down.

> [!field]
> Book the range day this week. Print this chapter's target sheet idea on plain paper with 5 bulls, and fill the zero card the same evening.
```

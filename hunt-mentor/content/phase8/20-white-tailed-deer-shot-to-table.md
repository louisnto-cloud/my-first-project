## Shot placement and range

```diagram quartering
```

- Wait for broadside or quartering away (Tip).
- Your range: where you hit a 15 cm (6 in) plate 9 of 10 from a rest (Tip).

```more placement and range detail
- Aim up the back of the front leg, one third up the chest: both lungs and the top of the heart (75%, hunter education).
- Quartering away: aim so the bullet exits the far shoulder (75%).
- Quartering toward, straight on, running, through brush: beginner, pass (Tip).
- Valley shots are often 50 to 150 m (55 to 164 yd) (Tip).
- Your .308 Winchester with a 150 to 165 grain expanding bullet is plenty for white tails (Tip).
- Most beginners: about 200 m (219 yd) maximum (Tip).
- Backstop: solid earth behind the deer every time. Flat fields, roads and houses are everywhere in white tail country (Tip, firearm safety).
- Full detail: [Shot placement](#/s/shot-placement) and [.308 ballistics](#/s/ballistics-308).
```

## Read the reaction

```diagram mwt-shot-reactions
```

- Watch the deer, not the rifle (Tip).
- The reaction is a clue. Blood and hair confirm (Tip).

```more reactions detail
- Heart or lungs: often a kick or hard bolt, then a crash within sight or about 50 to 137 m (55 to 150 yd) (70%, National Deer Association via [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing)).
- Liver: often hunched, runs a short way, then walks (60%).
- Gut: tail clamped, back hunched, slow walk (60%).
- A white tail that flags and bounds off looking normal may still be hit. Always check for sign (Tip).
- Listen after the shot: a crash, a cough or a fading run (Tip).
- Mark exactly where it stood and the last spot you saw it (Tip).
```

## Wait, then track

```diagram sg-blood-wait
```

| What you saw | Wait |
|---|---|
| Heart or lungs, out of sight | 30 min plus (70%) |
| Liver | 4 to 6 h (70%) |
| Gut | 6 to 8 h, or overnight if cool (70%) |

- You must make all reasonable effort to find it (95%).

```more tracking detail
- Wait times from hunter education via [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing). National Deer Association gives shorter liver and gut times (70%). Opinion, not law.
- Bright red with bubbles: lungs. Dark red: liver or muscle. Green or brown with smell: gut (70%).
- Track beside the sign, mark it, look ahead (chapter 8).
- A dog may track your deer on a leash only (99%, synopsis page 12).
- Rain coming? Go sooner (Tip).
- Law: "No person shall kill, cripple or wound game without making all reasonable effort to locate, dispatch, retrieve and include it in their bag limit" (95%, via [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing)).
```

## Tag first, then field care

```steps Field dress in 8 steps (after you notch the licence)
sk-gut-1 | On its back, head uphill, gloves on.
sk-gut-2 | Cut around the anus and tie it off.
sk-gut-3 | Belly cut, blade edge up, two fingers guiding.
sk-gut-4 | Roll the guts out to one side.
sk-gut-5 | Cut the diaphragm, then the windpipe high.
sk-gut-6 | Pull it all out through the back.
sk-gut-7 | Drain, wipe dry, prop it open.
sk-gut-8 | Cool it in shade, off the ground.
```

- Notch the licence before you touch the deer (99%).
- Cool it fast: open, out of the sun, air moving (Tip).

```more field care detail
- Cancel (notch) your white tailed deer species licence immediately after the kill, before you handle the deer, as printed on the licence (99%, page 11 item 1).
- Keep [[Evidence of sex]] naturally attached: the unskinned tail, plus a testicle or part of the penis for a buck, or part of the udder and teats for a doe (99%, page 15).
- Carry that species licence with the meat whenever you possess or transport it (99%, page 15).
- Gutless method works well when you must pack it out (Tip, see [Field dressing](#/s/field-dressing)).
- Valley white tails are often near a road: use a cart or drag on a tarp, with permission on private land (Tip).
- Steps shown are the app's field dressing frames; full detail in [Field dressing](#/s/field-dressing).

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Meat care

```anim sk-tarp-heat A tarp in the sun cooks meat. Shade and air cool it.
```

- Skin early and cool below about 4 C as fast as you can (Tip).
- October valley days can be warm. Do not leave it overnight in the hide (Tip).

```more meat care detail
- Heat, dirt and moisture spoil meat. Cool, clean, dry saves it (Tip).
- Hang in shade with air moving, game bags against flies (Tip).
- Gut shot: wash the cavity with clean water, dry it, trim (Tip).
- Do not move whole deer carcasses or high risk parts between regions without checking the [[CWD]] (Chronic Wasting Disease) rules (see [CWD](#/s/cwd)).
- Yield: about a third of live weight as boneless meat (Tip, assumption). A 91 kg buck gives roughly 30 kg (Tip).
- Full detail: [Meat care](#/s/meat-care), [Packing out](#/s/packing-out), [Butchering](#/s/butchering).
```

## Cooking notes

```diagram sk-meat-yield
```

- Lean meat: cook steaks hot and fast, medium rare (Tip).
- Trim fat and silver skin: that is where strong taste lives (Tip).

```more cooking detail
- Backstraps and tenderloins: hot pan, rest, slice thin (Tip).
- Shoulders and shanks: low and slow, braise (Tip).
- Grind trim with some pork fat for burgers and sausage (Tip).
- A farm family who gave you permission: bring them a package (Tip, the quick lesson's Grandpa's rule).
- Full detail: [Cooking wild game](#/s/cooking).
```

## Rules recap

```regs deerR3
```

- Region 3: 2 white tails, either sex. Region 8: 2, only one buck, one antlerless (99%).
- Region 2: no white tail season (97%).

```more the white tail rules in one place
| Region | Bucks | Antlerless | Bow either sex | Bag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (listed MUs) | 10 Sept to 10 Dec | 10 to 31 Oct | 11 to 20 Dec | 2, either sex (99%) |
| 8 (listed MUs) | 10 Sept to 30 Nov | 10 to 31 Oct | 1 to 20 Dec | 2: max 1 buck, 1 antlerless (99%) |
| 2 | none | none | none | 0 (97%) |

- Youth only seasons also exist in both regions (99%). MUs (Management Units), youth and bow only rows: [Rule Book: Region 3](#/s/rb-region-3), [Region 8](#/s/rb-region-8).
- Deer aggregate bag: 3 deer, only 1 mule deer (99%, pages 33 and 67).
- Licence: a white tailed deer species licence, separate from mule deer (99%, page 8).
- 100 m rule: no hunting or shooting within 100 m of a dwelling house or occupied farm or ranch building, and more (99%, page 10).
- Hunting cultivated land without consent of the owner, lessee or occupant is unlawful (99%, page 12 item 53).
- Legal hours: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (99%, page 11 item 38).
- Deer parts or scents from outside BC: unlawful for hunting (99%, page 12 item 51).
- Source: BC Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis 2026 to 2028, read directly 2026-10-02. Region 3 page 34, Region 8 page 68.
- Everything else: [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods), [after the kill](#/s/rb-after-the-kill), [bag limits](#/s/rb-bag-limits).

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mwt-shot-reactions
```

- **Rhyme:** "Watch it, mark it, wait it out; track it slow, there's no doubt."
- **Number trick:** 30 minutes, 4 to 6 hours, 6 to 8 hours: lungs, liver, gut.
- **Old hand's rule** (tradition): "Notch first, knife second."

```more more hooks, mistakes and field drill
- **Old hand's rule** (opinion, widely taught): "The work starts when the shot ends."
- **Rhyme:** "Cool it, clean it, keep it dry; good meat never asks you why."
- **Number trick:** 2 white tails in Region 3, 0 in Region 2, 10 to 31 October for does.

> [!mistake]
> - Running after a hit deer right away.
> - Handling the deer before notching the licence.
> - Leaving the carcass in the hide on a warm October night.

> [!field]
> Pack a kill kit now: flagging tape, headlamp, knife, game bags, gloves, a pen, and your licence in a zip bag.
```

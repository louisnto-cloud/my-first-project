> [!why]
> The CZ is already accurate. Spend on what makes you hit: glass you can see through, a sling you will use, and ammo it likes.


- Cheapest wins first: an ammo test (Tip).
- Then a scope and rings, a sling, a spare magazine (Tip).

```more why this order
- .22 accuracy changes a lot between ammo brands in the same rifle (Tip). How to test: [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22).
- Most of your grouse and practice shots are under 50 m; a modest rimfire scope does the job (Tip).
- A hard case and a lock help you meet the transport and storage rules (99% for the rules, via [firearm care](#/s/firearm-care)). Prices: VERIFY.
- Prices are Canadian dollars, from Canadian store listings found 2026-10-23 (65%, search previews). They change weekly.
```

## Legal and not legal

```diagram cz-legal-upgrades
```

- Suppressor: a prohibited device in Canada (99%).
- Infrared optics: banned on a BC hunt (99%).

```more the law behind the list
> [!law] Criminal Code s. 84(1), "prohibited device" (c)
> "a device or contrivance designed or intended to muffle or stop the sound or report of a firearm"

- So no suppressor, ever, even on a threaded CZ (99%).
- A thread protector or muzzle brake on a threaded model is not a sound suppressor (90%, my reading). Never one that muffles the sound.
- Bullpup stock: a prohibited device (99%, SOR/98-462 Part 4 s. 2). Ordinary replacement stocks and chassis that keep the action ahead of the trigger: no such rule found (85%, my reading). Ask a gunsmith.
- Barrel under 457 mm by alteration, or auto fire: prohibited (99%, chapter 9).
- Infrared optics: "To use or possess infrared optics on a hunting expedition" is unlawful (99%, synopsis page 11, item 39).
- Lights to hunt by: unlawful (99%, item 38).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-legal-upgrades
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Buy ammo before accessories, and practice before ammo.

> [!mistake]
> - Buying rings for a Picatinny rail. The CZ has an 11 mm dovetail unless a rail was added.
> - Ordering a "can" from a US site. Illegal to have in Canada.
> - A huge scope on a light .22: heavy and slow.

> [!field]
> Before you buy: take the rifle to the shop, unloaded and cased, and fit the scope and rings on it there.

> [!why]
> An over under is the easiest gun to show is empty: break it open and everyone can see. Two barrels, two chokes, two shots.

```diagram sg2-ou-parts
```

- Steps follow the Browning Citori manual (95%).
- Your own gun's manual wins (Tip).

```more how it works
- The top lever unlocks the action. The barrels pivot down on the hinge pin (95%, Citori manual).
- One trigger fires both barrels in turn. The selector on the safety picks which fires first: O for over, U for under (95%).
- Ejectors throw out fired hulls when you open it and lift live shells for you to pull (95%).
- No magazine, so no plug. Two shots is under the 3 shell bird limit (95%, my reading).
- Many older over unders are not rated for steel (65%, [Choosing your shotgun](#/s/shotgun-guide/2)). Check the maker's chart for yours.
```

## Safety and barrel selector

- Citori: slide the safety back, an S shows: safe (95%).
- Barrel selection only works with the safety on (95%).

```more the manual's words
- "The complete action (both barrels) can be put on safe by drawing the thumb 'safety' rearward... an 'S' appears" (95%, Citori manual).
- "Sliding the barrel selector to the left reveals an 'O' that indicates the top barrel (over) will fire first. Sliding the barrel selector to the right reveals a 'U'" (95%).
- Tip: put the more open choke in the barrel that fires first: close birds come first (Tip).
```

## Load, step by step

```steps Load an over under in 3 steps
sg2-ou-load-1 | Point it safe. Safety on. Top lever right, open.
sg2-ou-load-2 | Look through both barrels. One shell in each.
sg2-ou-load-3 | Lift the stock to the barrels. Now loaded.
```

- Close it by raising the stock, so the muzzles stay pointed down (Tip).
- Load only when shooting is near: Browning's own rule (95%).

```more the manual's words
1. "Move the top lever to the right and open the breech."
2. "Check the chambers and bores to be certain they are clear of obstructions."
3. "Insert a shell in each chamber, or in the chamber you intend to fire, and close the breech."
- "NEVER LOAD A SHELL INTO THE CHAMBERS UNLESS SHOOTING IS IMMINENT" (95%, Citori manual).
- Let the top lever snap back by itself. Do not hold it back with your thumb (95%).
```

## Unload, step by step

```steps Unload an over under in 3 steps
sg2-ou-unload-1 | Safety on. Open fully. Hulls fly, live shells lift.
sg2-ou-unload-2 | Pull live shells out by hand. Count them.
sg2-ou-unload-3 | Look through both. Carry it open.
```

- Tilt the gun so empty hulls fly away from your face (95%).
- An open over under over your arm is the safest carry in a group (Tip).

```more the manual's words
- "Using your thumb, move the top lever fully to the right to open the action. The action must be opened completely to reset the firing mechanism" (95%).
- "It may be necessary to pull downward slightly on the forearm to completely open the breech" (95%).
- Cant the breech to the right (left for left handers) "so empty hulls eject clear of your body and face" (95%).
- "Fired shells will be thrown completely out of the gun upon opening the action; and unfired shells will be elevated above the chambers for unloading" (95%).
- "Always inspect the chambers and barrels carefully after unloading" (95%).
```

## What goes wrong

```diagram sg2-ou-fails
```

- Second barrel will not fire: let the trigger all the way forward (95%).
- Will not close: unload, clean the breech, never force it (95%).

```more fixes in full
- Second shot fails: "If the trigger is not permitted to return to its fully forward position, the firing mechanism will not reengage." Common with gloves and fast shooting (95%, Citori manual).
- Debris on the breech can stop it closing: "Open the gun and unload it. Carefully examine the action surfaces and remove any foreign matter" (95%).
- Do not leave fired hulls in the chambers: moisture can "sweat" out of plastic hulls and cause rust (95%).
- New guns are tight. Pull down a little on the forearm to open fully (95%).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Broken open, everybody breathes easy. Carry it open between birds.

> [!mistake]
> - Swinging the barrels up to close the gun, sweeping your partner.
> - Holding the top lever so it does not lock fully.
> - Assuming any old over under is fine with steel shot.

> [!field]
> At a gun shop, open and close an over under 10 times with the stock coming up, muzzles down.

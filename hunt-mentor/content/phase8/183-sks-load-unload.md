> [!why]
> The SKS (Simonov self loading carbine) has a fixed magazine you cannot pull out. Unloading it wrong leaves a round in the chamber or rounds in the rifle.

```steps Load with a stripper clip in 5 steps
sks-load-1 | Muzzle safe. Safety on. Handle back.
sks-load-2 | Clip into the guides on the carrier.
sks-load-3 | Thumb presses the rounds down.
sks-load-4 | Clip out. Handle back, let go.
sks-load-5 | Safety on until the shot.
```

- Point in a safe direction, finger off the trigger, check it is unloaded before you start.
- Your magazine takes **5**. Never force more (99%).

```more loading in the manual's words
- "Pull it fully to the rear, and release it. If the magazine is empty, the bolt will remain open. Insert a stripper clip into the clip guides at the top front of the bolt carrier, and with the thumb as close as possible to the base of the top cartridge, press the cartridges off the clip and into the magazine. Remove the empty clip" (95%, US Army TC 9-56, 1969).
- "Pull operating handle rearward and release it; the bolt will run forward and chamber a cartridge. CAUTION: The rifle is now ready to fire!" (95%).
- "If the rifle is not to be fired immediately, apply the safety" (95%).
- Military stripper clips hold 10 rounds (95%, TC 9-56). Your magazine is limited to 5, so load 5 rounds on a clip, or load singly (99% on the limit; the clip method is a Tip).
- Load only where you may shoot: at the range, or in the field when hunting. Never in or on a vehicle (99%, [Rule Book item 28](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0)).
```

## No clip? One at a time

```diagram sks-load-single
```

- Round on the follower. Press down until it stays (95%).
- Repeat up to 5. Then handle back and let go to chamber one (95%).

```more single loading notes
- "If clipped ammunition is not available, individual cartridges can be placed on top of the magazine follower and pressed down into the magazine until it is full" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Hunting tip: load 4 in the magazine and chamber 1 when you start the stalk, so the magazine holds 4 plus 1 (Tip). The law limits what the magazine can hold, not the chamber (90%, my reading of SOR/98-462 Part 4).
- Only hunting ammunition in the rifle when hunting. Surplus full metal jacket rounds are illegal for hunting in BC (99%, [chapter 7](#/s/mc-sks-07)).
```

## Unload the fixed magazine

```steps Unload and prove it in 5 steps
sks-unload-1 | Muzzle safe. Finger off. Safety on.
sks-unload-2 | Catch back. Rounds drop into your hand.
sks-unload-3 | Close it. Handle back: the chamber empties.
sks-unload-4 | Look and feel: chamber, bore, magazine.
sks-unload-5 | Close the bolt pointing safe.
```

- This is [[PROVE]] for the SKS.
- Rounds fall out fast. Hand, hat or soft bag under the magazine (Tip).

```more the manual's unload, word for word
> "Place weapon on SAFE. Pull back magazine catch and allow magazine to swing open. Remove all cartridges. Close the magazine. Pull operating handle to the rear to eject any remaining rounds. Release the handle (bolt will remain open). Inspect to insure no cartridges remain in the magazine, chamber, or receiver."

- Source: TC 9-56 Type 56 training packet, unload and clear (95%).
- To close the bolt after: "Open magazine again and pull operating handle to rear and release", or press the follower down slightly and ease the carrier forward. Then safety to FIRE, press the trigger, back to SAFE (95%).
- Count the rounds: 5 in, 5 out, plus one if you chambered one (Tip).
- Hand the rifle to your brother only open and proved (Tip, same rule as the [firearm safety lesson](#/s/firearm-safety/0)).
```

## In the truck: magazine empty too

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram sks-vehicle
```

- No round in the chamber or an attached magazine in or on a vehicle (99%).
- The SKS magazine is always attached. So it must be empty (95%, my reading).

```more the law
> [!law] BC Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis, page 11, item 28
> "To discharge, carry or have in possession a firearm containing live ammunition in its breech or in its magazine attached to the firearm, in or on a railway car, motor vehicle, sleigh, aircraft, bicycle or other conveyance."

- Federal: unloaded means no cartridge in the chamber and none in a magazine attached to or inserted in the firearm (99%, transport regulations s. 1, via [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/13)).
- Leaving the truck: non restricted rifles locked in the trunk or out of sight with the vehicle locked (99%, same screen).
- Item 28 and the federal rules: see [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0) (99%).
```

## Click, no bang

```diagram sks-stoppage
```

- Muzzle safe. Wait, then clear it (Tip on the wait).
- A second failure: unload, strip and inspect (95%).

```more stoppage drill
- The manual's immediate action: "When rifle fails to fire, pull the operating handle back to eject bad round, watch for ejection, release handle to chamber new round. Aim and attempt to fire. If immediate action fails to reduce stoppage, unload, clear, disassemble, and inspect" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Hunting rule (Tip): a slow ignition (hang fire) can fire late. Keep the muzzle safe and count to 30 before you open the action. Your Tikka manual asks for a full minute (95%, Tikka manual).
- Keep the dud apart from your good ammunition (Tip).
- Bolt not fully closed is one cause of failure to fire (95%, TC 9-56 troubleshooting table). Never push the bolt shut with your hand on a live round; let it run home (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Count them out the way you counted them in.

> [!mistake]
> - Opening the magazine and walking away with a round still in the chamber.
> - Carrying the SKS in the truck with rounds in the fixed magazine.
> - Pressing a full 10 round clip into a 5 round magazine.

> [!field]
> With 5 dummy rounds: load by clip, unload, load singly, unload. Ten times. Say each step out loud.

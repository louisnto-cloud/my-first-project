## Is your SKS legal? Today's status

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram sks-legal-status
```

- A standard Type 56 SKS is **non restricted** (99%, RCMP Firearms Reference Table, valid 19 October 2026).
- Its magazine must hold **no more than 5** rounds (99%).
- Ottawa is reviewing the SKS. No decision yet (85%, news, 21 October 2026). VERIFY each season.

```more the legal detail, with dates
> [!law] RCMP Firearms Reference Table (FRT), record FRN (Firearm Reference Number) 33820, valid as of 2026/10/19
> "Legal Classification: Non-Restricted"

- The FRT lists several Norinco "Type 56 SKS" records, by arsenal number. Records with the standard 520 mm barrel are all non restricted (99%, read directly, FRT pages 66169 to 66181).
- The FRT warns: "do not confuse this firearm with the Chinese Type 56 AK and AKM (variants) which are copies of the Kalashnikov AK-47 series" (99%). The Type 56 AK is prohibited; your SKS is not (99%, FRT record 45186 and [SOR/98-462](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462/FullText.html) Part 1 item 64).
- Not on the bans: the SKS does not appear on the RCMP lists of models prohibited by the May 2020, December 2024 or March 2025 orders (99%, Hunt Mentor searched all three RCMP lists, dated 19 October 2026).
- Licence: a non restricted rifle needs a [[PAL]] (Possession and Acquisition Licence) (85%, RCMP class rules via the app's licence lessons).
- Magazine law: chapter 8 and below. 5 rounds for a semi automatic centrefire long gun magazine (99%, [RCMP](https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/classes-firearms/maximum-permitted-magazine-capacity), page modified 18 October 2024).
- The review: the federal government announced a review of firearm classification on 5 December 2025, with consultations "including on the SKS" (80%, The Canadian Press, 5 December 2025). On 21 October 2026 its expert group had not met yet; the Prime Minister said he would weigh its advice before any SKS decision (80%, The Canadian Press, 21 October 2026).
- Certainty on "non restricted today": 99% for the FRT record; the law can change after this date. VERIFY before every season ([chapter 8](#/s/mc-sks-08)).
```

## Inspection, step by step

Point in a safe direction. Finger off the trigger. Check it is unloaded ([chapter 3](#/s/mc-sks-03)).

```steps Inspect a surplus SKS in 6 steps
sks-insp-1 | Find and strip out the old grease.
sks-insp-2 | Shake the bolt. The pin must rattle.
sks-insp-3 | Bolt out. Look down a bright bore.
sks-insp-4 | Gas tube and piston: clean and free.
sks-insp-5 | Dummy rounds: exactly 5 fit.
sks-insp-6 | Bayonet locks. Stock not cracked.
```

```checklist sks-inspection
Unloaded and proved, no ammunition on the bench
Field stripped (chapter 6)
Cosmoline cleaned from bolt, carrier, firing pin channel, gas tube, trigger group
Firing pin rattles freely when the bolt is shaken
Bore bright, no pits, rust, bulge or ring
Gas piston slides freely in a clean tube
Magazine takes 5 dummy rounds and no more
Bayonet folds and locks; stock has no cracks
Function check passed (chapter 2)
Gunsmith check booked: headspace and a full look over
```

```more the inspection in detail
- **Cosmoline:** the thick storage grease on surplus rifles. "Cosmoline remnants in the bolt can impede pin movement" (70%, Wikipedia "SKS"). Degrease every part from chapter 6, and the firing pin channel in the bolt most of all (Tip).
- **Firing pin:** most SKS have a "free floating firing pin" with no spring; a pin stuck forward causes slam fires (70%, Wikipedia). Shake the cleaned bolt: a free pin rattles (Tip, common armourer and owner advice). Silent: clean again. Still silent: gunsmith.
- **Bore:** take the bolt out, shine a light in the breech, look from the muzzle (Tip). Bright and sharp is good. Dark pits, rust, a bulge or a ring: gunsmith.
- **Gas system:** chapter 6 step 7 takes it apart. Carbon and rust here slow the action (Tip).
- **Magazine:** use dummy rounds only (snap caps). Exactly 5 should go in. A 6th means the limit has failed: do not use it and see a gunsmith (99% on the law; the test is a Tip).
- **Bayonet and stock:** the bayonet locks by spring at both ends (95%, TC 9-56). Cracks in the wood behind the receiver can grow under recoil (Tip).
```

## Slam fire: why the pin matters

```diagram sks-slamfire
```

- No spring holds the pin back (70%).
- Stuck forward, it can fire as the bolt closes (70%).

```more slam fire in detail
- Early Soviet SKS (1949 to 1950) had spring loaded pins; "most variants have a free floating firing pin within the bolt" (70%, Wikipedia "SKS").
- "A pin stuck forward can cause slamfires" (70%). Old grease is the usual cause on surplus rifles (70%).
- The pin is triangular in cross section and fits one way only, notches up. Put in another way it can also cause slam fires (70%, Wikipedia). If your pin was ever out, a gunsmith refits it (Tip).
- A slam fire can fire more than one shot. Load only with the muzzle in a safe direction and only at a range or in the field (Tip).
- Some owners fit an aftermarket spring loaded pin. That is a gunsmith job (Tip).
```

## Headspace and the gunsmith rules

```diagram sks-headspace
```

```diagram sks-gunsmith
```

- Headspace: bolt face to the point that stops the case (85%).
- A gunsmith checks it with gauges. Do this on any surplus rifle (Tip).

```more why headspace matters
- Headspace is the space between the bolt face and the point in the chamber that stops the cartridge (85%, hunter education definition).
- Too much headspace lets the case stretch and split or rupture when fired (80%).
- Signs after firing: cracked, bulged or split cases, gas in your face. Stop shooting (Tip, same warning list as the [Tikka](#/s/rifle-mechanics/0)).
- Surplus rifles may have mixed parts from different rifles. A gunsmith inspection before the first shot is cheap insurance (Tip).
- Never send or carry the rifle loaded. Unload and prove it first (99%, federal transport rules via [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/13)).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): An old rifle has secrets. Make it tell you all of them before it hears a live round.

> [!mistake]
> - Loading straight from the box because "it looks clean".
> - Skipping the firing pin channel when degreasing.
> - Never counting the magazine with dummy rounds.

> [!field]
> Tonight: field strip it (chapter 6), degrease, shake the bolt, count 5 dummy rounds, then book a gunsmith look.

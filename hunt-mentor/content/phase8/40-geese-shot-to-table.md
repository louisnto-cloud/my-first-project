## Load, pattern, range and lead

```diagram sb-goose-range
```

- Steel BB for big Canada geese; BBB or T for longer shots (70%).
- Pattern: 60 pellets in a 76 cm (30 in) circle at your longest range (65%).

```diagram gr-shot-sizes
```

```more loads and chokes
- Non toxic shot only, and no lead in your pockets or boat (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds)).
- Steel BB is the all round pellet for large Canada geese; BBB and T for longer range (70%, Ducks Unlimited).
- Steel 1 or 2 for cackling geese and snow geese over decoys (Tip, quick lesson).
- Your 12 gauge with 3 inch steel is enough (Tip). Modified choke is a usual start with BB steel (Tip). Pattern it: [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills), [Shotgun guide](#/s/shotgun-guide).
- Max range for you this season: 35 m (38 yd). A goose looks close at 50 m because it is big (Tip).
- Cripple shells: steel 2 or 4, finish at about 14 m (15 yd) (65%).
```

```anim sk-leads Swing through, sustained lead, pull away.
```

- Swing slowly from behind; fire as you see daylight past the head (65%).
- Too fast a swing and too much lead is a common miss (65%).

```more shooting notes
- Big birds look slow, so hunters under lead or over lead. Guides suggest a slow swing from behind and firing when daylight shows between barrel and head (65%, Ducks Unlimited, Phil Bourjaily).
- Head shots at close decoying birds: the head is about the size of a dove's body and fewer pellets reach the breast (65%).
- On calm days take good overhead shots near 27 m (30 yd) with BB or larger; birds rarely commit fully (65%).
- Wear earplugs: magnum loads harm hearing (65%).
- Background: [Shot placement](#/s/shot-placement), [Shotgun skills](#/s/shotgun-skills).
```

## After the shot

```diagram mgs-after-shot
```

- Watch every bird that leaves until it is out of sight (Tip).
- Kill and retrieve wounded birds as soon as you can (99%).

```more cripples and retrieval
- A goose that drops its legs, lifts its head or sails off is hit. Mark the spot it lands with a landmark and a map pin (Tip).
- On the ground: finish it at once with a cripple shell to the head at close range (65%), or by hand if you know how (Tip).
- On water: you must have the means to retrieve before you shoot (99%, s. 42). A kayak or boat under paddle, or motor off with the gun unloaded until confirmed (80%, my reading in the Rule Book). VERIFY with a conservation officer.
- A bird you find dead and keep counts in your daily bag (99%, s. 43(3)).
- Make all reasonable effort to locate, dispatch, retrieve and count it (99%, page 12).
- Do not shoot again at the flock while a cripple is running (Tip).
```

## Field care

```steps Goose field care in 4 steps
mgs-care-1 | Gloves on. A feathered wing or head stays on.
mgs-care-2 | Cool it fast: breast or open it within 1 to 2 hours.
mgs-care-3 | Breast out, keep the legs.
mgs-care-4 | Label or preserve before you give a goose away.
```

- Wing or head stays on until preserved (99%).
- Bird flu care: gloves, clean outdoors, wash up (80%).

```more care in full
- Keep at least one fully feathered wing or the fully feathered head on each bird until it is preserved (99%, s. 52(1)).
- "Preserved" means plucked and frozen, made into sausage, cooked, dried, canned or smoked away from the hunting area, or mounted (99%, s. 1).
- Unpreserved birds count toward the possession limit everywhere, home fridge included (99%, s. 46(1)).
- Avian influenza: wear rubber or nitrile gloves, clean birds outdoors or in a ventilated place, wash hands, do not take birds that look sick, keep away from your own poultry (80%, Oklahoma and California wildlife agencies).
- A goose holds heat. Breast it or open it within 1 to 2 hours in mild weather (Tip).
- Giving birds away: label with your full name, address, phone, email if any, the date, your permit number and your signature, or preserve them first (99%, s. 50).
- Never let edible meat be wasted (99%, s. 55(1)).
```

## Meat and yield

```diagram sb-bird-yield
```

- About 1 kg of boned breast from a big Canada goose (60%).
- Legs and thighs: slow cook (Tip).

```more yield notes
- A mature Canada goose of about 3.6 kg gives roughly 1 kg of boned breast; single breast halves run about 280 to 450 g (60%, hunter figures, quick lesson).
- Cackling and snow geese give less (Tip).
- Pluck only if you will roast it whole; otherwise skin and breast, keeping a wing on until the freezer (Tip; wing rule 99%).
- Older resident geese can be tough (Tip). Brine, slice thin across the grain, or grind (Tip).
- More: [Meat care](#/s/meat-care), [Butchering](#/s/butchering).
```

## Cooking notes

| Cut | How (Tip unless shown) |
|---|---|
| Breast | Sear, slice thin across the grain |
| Legs and thighs | Braise or confit, low and slow |
| Tough or old bird | Grind for sausage or jerky |
| Food safety | Cook to 74°C (165°F) inside, agency advice for bird flu (80%) |

- Goose breast is dark, rich meat (Tip).

```more cooking in full
- Wildlife agencies advise cooking all game birds to an internal 74°C (165°F) because of avian influenza (80%, Oklahoma and Arkansas agency advice in search previews).
- Many cooks serve goose breast pinker. That is your choice; the agency advice is the safe line (Tip).
- Remove any shot and feather bits you find in the meat (Tip).
- More recipes: [Cooking wild game](#/s/cooking).
```

## Rules recap

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram mgs-rules-checklist
```

- All from the Rule Book. Read your district's page before every trip (99%).

```more every rule, with its Rule Book page
- Federal Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit ([[MGBHP]]) with the habitat conservation stamp, plus your BC hunting licence, on you (99%, [Rule Book: migratory birds](#/s/rb-migratory-birds)).
- Shotgun 10 gauge or smaller, 3 shells total with the [[Magazine plug]] (99%). One shotgun; no slugs (99%).
- [[Non toxic shot]] only, and none other in your possession while hunting (99%).
- Hours: half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset (99%).
- Bait: no hunting within 400 m (437 yd) of bait until it has been gone 7 days. Standing crops and grain spilled by normal farming are not bait (99%).
- No live birds, no electronic calls (99%).
- No hunting from or with a motor vehicle, aircraft, or a moving boat with a motor or sail (99%).
- Retrieve: adequate means at hand; kill and retrieve wounded birds (99%).
- Daily limit reached: stop for that species that day (99%). Possession is 3 days' worth, home included, until preserved (99%).
- Wing or head on; labels to give birds away (99%).
- Land: no hunting on [[Cultivated land]] without consent; Trespass Act on enclosed land; no shooting within 100 m of an occupied home or farm building; not in or across the [[Road allowance]] (99%, [Where you can hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt)).
- Mission and all of MU (Management Unit) 2-4: Fraser Valley Special Area licence ($10) plus $1,000,000 liability insurance (99%, [Region 2](#/s/rb-region-2)).
```

```more the dates and the District 2 snow goose wording

- District 2, 2026 to 2027: Canada and cackling 5 to 15 September, 10 October to 22 November, 19 December to 10 January, 10 February to 10 March. White fronted 10 October to 24 January. Snow and Ross's 10 October to 5 January, then 20 February to 10 March (99%).
- District 2 snow and Ross's limit, federal table: "10 (not more than 5 may be Ross's Geese). An additional 5 Snow Geese may be killed or taken in Provincial Management Units 2-4 and 2-5" (99%). Possession 30, not more than 15 Ross's, plus 15 more snow geese in 2-4 and 2-5 (99%).
- The synopsis page 12 prints 5 a day outside 2-4 and 2-5. The [Region 2 Rule Book](#/s/rb-region-2) marks Mission's 2-8 VERIFY and suggests taking 5 a day to stay safe (Tip).
- District 2: the MU 2-4 late Canada goose season does not apply 100 m either side of any dyke in Delta, other than Westham Island (99%, page 28).
- Hunt two districts in one day: the highest daily limit of the areas applies (99%, s. 43(2)).
```

## Memory hooks

> [!tip] Memory rhymes (made for this app)
> - "Feet down, near, clear: then shoot, not before, my dear."
> - "Wing on, gloves on, cool it, then home."
> - "Name the district, then load the gun."

- Number trick: **35, 60, 76**. Shots inside 35 m, 60 pellets in a 76 cm circle (Tip and 65%).

> [!rule] Old hand's rule (tradition, opinion)
> Mark every bird that falls before you shoot at the next one. Widely taught for waterfowl: one found goose beats two lost ones.

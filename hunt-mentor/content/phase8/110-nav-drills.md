## Drill 1: compass near home in Mission

```steps Compass drill, about 1 hour
mc-nav-dc-1 | Set 15° east at home and check it.
mc-nav-dc-2 | Pace a measured 100 m, three times.
mc-nav-dc-3 | Walk a triangle: 0°, 120°, 240°.
mc-nav-dc-4 | Measure the miss from the start flag.
```

- Bring: compass, flagging, notebook, a measured 100 m (Tip).
- Pass: finish within about 10 m of the start (Tip).

```more drill 1 in full
- **Where:** a big open park or field near home in Mission where walking off path is allowed (Tip). Away from cars, fences and power lines (80%, metal bends the needle).
- **Bring:** baseplate compass with declination set to 15 east (Mission 15.20 E, 95%), a flag or cap for the start, notebook, phone for the 100 m.
- **Steps:**
  1. Measure 100 m with the Hunt Map Measure tool along a straight path (99% tool; Tip use). Walk it three times counting each left foot. Average. Repeat on rough ground and uphill if you can (75%, pace counting).
  2. Put the flag down. Walk 100 m by your pace count on 0 degrees, then 100 m on 120 degrees, then 100 m on 240 degrees. Sight a target each leg; do not stare at the needle (95%).
  3. Measure how far you finished from the flag (Tip).
  4. Next: back bearings. Walk 50 m on any bearing, then walk the back bearing home (95% for the maths).
  5. Last: the two person leapfrog with your brother over 200 m ([Chapter 4](#/s/mc-nav-04)).
- **Pass:** triangle within about 10 m on 300 m walked (Tip). That is about 2 degrees of error (98%, maths).
- **Fail? Why:** metal nearby, phone in the compass hand, wrong ring setting, a sloppy pace count (Tip).
```

```checklist mc-nav-drill-compass
Declination set to 15° east and checked
My 100 m pace count, flat
My 100 m pace count, rough or uphill
Triangle walked, finished within 10 m
Back bearing walked home
Leapfrog with my brother over 200 m
```

## Drill 2: map vs land at Heffley Creek

```steps Map vs land drill, a scouting morning
mc-nav-dm-1 | At home: pick 5 features and pin them.
mc-nav-dm-2 | On the hill: phone away, map only.
mc-nav-dm-3 | Pencil dot where you think you are, then check.
mc-nav-dm-4 | Pass: 3 of 5 within 200 m. Swap roles.
```

- Bring: paper map of the block in a zip bag, compass, pencil, phone (Tip).
- Pass: 3 of 5 marks within about 200 m (Tip).

```more drill 2 in full
- **Where:** your Heffley Creek scouting block, on Crown land or with permission (Tip). Check private land and closures on the Hunt Map first (99% layers; [Maps 3](#/s/maps-land-status)).
- **At home:** pick five features you will see from the route: a ridge, a saddle, a creek fork, a cutblock edge, a road bend (Tip). Drop a waypoint on each (99%, app). Print or sketch the block on paper (Tip).
- **On the hill:** at each of three stops, with the phone in the pack:
  1. Orient the map by compass and by land ([Chapter 5](#/s/mc-nav-05)).
  2. Name three features you see; find them on the map; check their order.
  3. Pencil dot where you must be. Then take the phone out and check. Write the miss in metres.
  4. Read the slope under your feet: which way does it face? Does the map agree?
- **Add on:** one cross bearing fix from two landmarks about 90 degrees apart (85%).
- **Pass:** 3 of 5 dots within about 200 m (Tip). Then swap: your brother navigates, you check.
- **Safety:** daylight only, trip plan left, back by your turnaround time (Tip).
```

```checklist mc-nav-drill-maplands
Five features picked and pinned at home
Map oriented by compass and by land
Three stops, pencil dot, phone check, miss written
One cross bearing fix
3 of 5 within 200 m
Swapped roles with my brother
```

## Drill 3: phone and GPS with no signal

```steps Phone drill, 1 km out and back
mc-nav-dp-1 | At home: save the area, test in airplane mode.
mc-nav-dp-2 | At the truck: Parking pin, start Go & Track.
mc-nav-dp-3 | Walk 1 km out in airplane mode, phone warm.
mc-nav-dp-4 | Walk back by Back to start and the compass.
```

- Bring: charged phone, power bank and cable, compass, paper map (Tip).
- Pass: arrive at the truck, and note the battery used (Tip).

```more drill 3 in full
- **At home:** Offline Maps, My base area (Heffley Creek) or Draw a box for your spot. Tap Keep my saved maps. Airplane mode on; check the map loads (99%, app).
- **At the truck:** note the battery percent. Drop a Parking waypoint. Start Go & Track. Note "GPS good" and its metres (99%, app).
- **Walk out:** about 1 km on a new line, screen dim, phone in a chest pocket, check every 15 minutes (Tip). Drop a waypoint at the turn (99%).
- **Walk back, round 1:** read Back to start, for example "1.0 km, heading 200°" (99% format). Set it on the compass, put the phone away, walk tree to tree (Tip). Check twice on the way.
- **Walk back, round 2 (next trip):** no track. Use only the truck pin: Tools, Draw and measure, Measure from your dot to the pin; walk the bearing (99% tool; Tip use).
- **Round 3:** pretend the phone died at the turn. Walk back by the escape bearing and handrails only ([Chapter 2](#/s/mc-nav-02)).
- **Pass:** at the truck each round. Write the battery used per hour (Tip). Now you know your own battery budget ([GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery)).
- iPhone 14 or later: try Emergency SOS via satellite Demo mode at home, not a real SOS (95% that Demo mode exists via Apple; Tip).
```

```checklist mc-nav-drill-phone
Offline area saved and tested in airplane mode
Parking pin and Go & Track started at the truck
1 km out with the phone warm and dim
Back to start walked on the compass
Pin only, Measure walk back
Phone dead pretend: escape bearing and handrails
Battery used per hour written down
Satellite SOS Demo mode tried at home
```

## Drill 4: the lost scenario with your brother

```steps Lost scenario, about 1 hour
mc-nav-dl-1 | Your brother leads you in. You don't look.
mc-nav-dl-2 | "You are lost." STOP out loud. He times you.
mc-nav-dl-3 | Find yourself. Decide: stay or move, and why.
mc-nav-dl-4 | Signal, and start a shelter. 30 minutes.
```

- He leads, you follow without the map or phone, then you solve it (Tip).
- Pass: STOP, a fix or a stay decision, signal and shelter within 30 minutes (Tip).

```more drill 4 in full
- **Where:** a known block in daylight, both of you with radios or phones, trip plan left (Tip).
- **Steps:**
  1. Your brother leads you about 20 to 30 minutes off trail. You do not look at map or phone (Tip).
  2. He says "you are lost". You sit and do STOP out loud: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan (75%, AdventureSmart).
  3. Think: when were you last sure? How long have you walked? (4 km an hour off trail, 75%.)
  4. Observe: orient the map, name features, try a cross bearing ([Chapter 5](#/s/mc-nav-05)).
  5. Plan: say "stay" or "move" and why, using the stay or move list ([Chapter 7](#/s/mc-nav-07)).
  6. Say your practice 911 script out loud, coordinates first. Do not dial 911 for a drill (Tip).
  7. Signal: three whistle blasts, orange gear in the nearest opening (75%).
  8. Start a shelter and a bough bed (Tip). No fire unless legal and safe (80%).
- **Pass:** all within 30 minutes, and your fix within about 300 m if you chose to find yourself (Tip).
- **Swap roles** next time. The leader learns what a lost person does (Tip).
- **Hard mode** (Tip): start the drill one hour before dark, both with headlamps, close to the truck.
```

```checklist mc-nav-drill-lost
STOP said out loud
Last sure point named
Fix found or stay decision made, with the reason
Practice 911 script said, coordinates first
Three whistle blasts and orange in the open
Shelter and bed started within 30 minutes
Roles swapped
```

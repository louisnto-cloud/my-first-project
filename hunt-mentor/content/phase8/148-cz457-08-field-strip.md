> [!why]
> Taking the bolt out is the only strip CZ asks of you. Do it right and you clean better, store safer and never lose a part.

```diagram cz-stop-line
```

- You: magazine out, bolt out, stock screws (95%).
- Further than that: a gunsmith (95%).

```more what CZ allows
- CZ's whole stripping procedure: remove the magazine, shift the safety to off, open the bolt, depress the bolt catch and pull the bolt out (95%, CZ manual page 9).
- "No further disassembly is needed for routine maintenance" (95%).
- "CAUTION: Do not strip your firearm any further. If you assume that your firearm requires adjustment or repair, entrust this work to a competent gunsmith in a specialized workshop!" (95%).
- After "a great number of cartridges", have a gunsmith clean the bolt (95%).
- Removing the bolt is also one of the legal storage options for a non restricted rifle (99%, storage regulations s. 5, via [firearm care](#/s/firearm-care)).
```

## Bolt out, 4 steps

```steps Take the bolt out in 4 steps
cz-strip-1 | PROVE it. Magazine out.
cz-strip-2 | Safety to OFF, as the manual says.
cz-strip-3 | Open the bolt fully back.
cz-strip-4 | Hold the bolt catch in. Slide the bolt out.
```

- The bolt catch is on the left of the receiver (95%).

```more the steps in full
1. Point safe, finger off. Magazine out, bolt open, look and feel: empty (85%, ACTS and PROVE).
2. "Shift the safety to its "Off" position" (95%, CZ manual page 9, step 2). Only on a proven empty rifle.
3. Open the bolt all the way back (95%).
4. "Depressing the bolt catch (Fig.10) ... pull the bolt out of the gun" (95%). Fig. 10 shows the catch on the left side (95%).
- CZ also lists pulling the bolt out with the safety engaged as a feature (95%, page 4). The stripping steps still say safety off. My lean: follow the stripping steps (Tip).
- Lay the bolt on a clean cloth, not on the ground (Tip).
```

## The bolt you are holding

```diagram cz-bolt-parts
```

- Wipe it, light oil, put it back (95%).
- Do not pull the striker out (95%).

```more what you may clean on the bolt
- Outside of the bolt, the bolt face and around the extractor: cloth, old toothbrush, light oil, then wipe off (95%, CZ manual page 10, "cleaning of other parts").
- The firing pin hole: CZ's misfire remedy is "disassemble and clean the firing pin", but its stripping section says that is beyond routine maintenance (95%, pages 9 and 11). My reading: a gunsmith job (85%).
- Do not soak the bolt in solvent (95%, other parts rule).
```

## Bolt back in, 3 steps

```steps Put the bolt back in 3 steps
cz-rebuild-1 | Handle up, line up the guide, slide it in.
cz-rebuild-2 | Push it home and turn the handle down.
cz-rebuild-3 | Safety check: it must not fire.
```

- Then PROVE it again (Tip).

```more reassembly in full
- The manual does not print reassembly steps; it is the stripping steps in reverse (85%, my reading).
- Slide the bolt in with the handle up; if it stops, do not force it: check the bolt is cocked and lined up (Tip).
- Function check on the empty rifle, the same test CZ gives for the trigger: open and close the bolt several times and move the safety on and off. "During this safety check, the rifle must not discharge" (95%, CZ manual page 7). On an empty rifle: the cocking indicator must still stick out afterwards (85%, my reading).
- Then with the safety off, pointed safe, press the trigger once to release the striker for storage (95%, unloading steps page 6).
```

## Stock screws

```diagram cz-stock-screws
```

- Check them. Tighten to 3.5 Nm (95%).

```more more
- "Check tightening of the stock fixing screws (pos. 73 and 74) and tighten them as necessary with the torque of 3.5 Nm" (95%, CZ manual page 9). Part 73 is the front connecting screw, 74 the rear (95%, page 12).
- 3.5 Nm (newton metres) is about 31 inch pounds (95%, unit conversion).
- Use a small torque driver with the right bit; loose screws open groups (Tip).
- Wood stocks swell and shrink with Harrison rain; check before season (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-strip-4
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Take apart only what you can put back blindfolded. For the CZ, that is the bolt.

> [!mistake]
> - Taking the striker out of the bolt. CZ says gunsmith.
> - Forcing the bolt back in when it is uncocked or out of line.
> - Overtightening stock screws by feel.

> [!field]
> At the kitchen table, empty: bolt out and back in 5 times. Then the safety check.

> [!why]
> The SKS (Simonov self loading carbine) can take a black tailed deer cleanly, inside a short range, with the right bullet. Outside that, it wounds.

```diagram sks-energy
```

- 7.62x39 has about half the energy of your .308 at 200 yd (180 m) (75%).
- It drops under the 1,000 ft lb rule of thumb past about 130 m (145 yd) (75%, computed).

```more the numbers
| Distance | 7.62x39, Hornady 123 gr SST | .308, 150 gr (app table) |
|---|---|---|
| Muzzle | 1,508 ft lb (2,045 J) | 2,648 ft lb (3,590 J) |
| 100 | 1,136 ft lb (1,540 J) at 100 yd | 2,090 ft lb (2,840 J) at 100 m |
| 200 | 842 ft lb (1,142 J) at 200 yd | 1,630 ft lb (2,210 J) at 200 m |

- 7.62x39: Hornady data, 20 inch test barrel, muzzle velocity 2,350 fps (feet per second) (75%, maker data via a retailer listing).
- .308: from the app's [.308 ballistics](#/s/ballistics-308/0) lesson (75 to 85%).
- Crossing 1,000 ft lb: about 146 yd, or 134 m, by straight line between Hornady's 100 and 200 yd figures (75%, Hunt Mentor arithmetic).
- The 1,000 ft lb figure is a rule of thumb with no clear origin (70%, from the [ballistics deep dive](#/s/ballistics-deep/0)). Placement matters more (85%).
- TC 9-56 lists a practical range of 400 m for soldiers (95%). That is a military number, not a deer number.
```

## My lean: 150 m and under

```diagram sks-range
```

- Most coastal timber shots are close (Tip).
- Your limit: 150 m (164 yd) with a soft point, and only if you pass the plate test (Tip).

```more why 150 m
- Energy falls under the rule of thumb around 130 m (75%, above).
- Military surplus SKS rifles with iron sights are not tack drivers. Many owners report groups of several inches at 100 m (Tip, owner reports; test yours).
- Black tailed bucks weigh 48 to 90 kg, smaller than mule deer (90%, BC (British Columbia) deer account, via the [mule deer Masterclass](#/s/mc-mule-deer-01/4)).
- Harrison and Fraser Valley deer country is thick second growth and clearcut edges. A 150 m limit covers most chances (Tip).

> [!lean]
> My lean: the Tikka .308 is your deer rifle. The SKS is a fine backup or a thick timber rifle inside 100 m, after a gunsmith check and a zero with soft points.
```

## The plate test

```diagram sks-group-test
```

- 5 shots, all in a 15 cm (6 in) plate at 100 m: OK to 100 m (Tip).
- Miss the plate? Shorten your range until you do not (Tip).

```more how to run it
- From a field rest you would use hunting: sitting with sticks, or kneeling against a tree (Tip). [Field positions](#/s/field-positions/0).
- Cold barrel first shot counts most. It is the one the deer gets (Tip).
- Repeat at 150 m. Pass both or keep your limit at 100 m (Tip).
- Placement: [shot placement](#/s/shot-placement/0). Heart and lungs, broadside or quartering away (85%).
```

## BC rules for the SKS

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram sks-bc-legal
```

- Centrefire rifle: legal for deer. No calibre minimum in the table (99%).
- Expanding bullets only. No full metal jacket (99%).

```more the rules, with sources
- Deer: "centrefire rifle, an air rifle of .35 calibre or larger, a 20 gauge or larger shotgun with No. 1 Buck or larger, or bow class B, C or D" (99%, synopsis page 13, [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0)). The table sets no minimum calibre for centrefire rifles (95%, my reading).
- No full metal jacket, tracer, incendiary or explosive bullets for hunting (99%, page 12).
- No firearm with an electronically controlled firing mechanism, and no long gun with a barrel under 305 mm (99%, page 12). Your 520 mm barrel is fine.
- The BC synopsis has no special rule for semi automatic rifles. The magazine plug rule is for shotguns only (95%, my reading of page 12).
- The 5 round magazine limit is federal law (99%, SOR/98-462 Part 4, see [chapter 8](#/s/mc-sks-08/2)).
- No round in the chamber or attached magazine in or on a vehicle (99%, page 11 item 28; [chapter 3](#/s/mc-sks-03/3)).
- No lights to hunt by; hunting hours apply (99%, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0)).
- Harrison Mills and Nicomen lowland: no rifles (99%, Closed Areas Regulation, via [your Harrison deer hunt](#/s/local-harrison-deer/0)).
- Your licence, deer species licence and tags: [licences](#/s/licences/0).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Know your rifle's distance. Then cut it by a quarter for the deer.

> [!mistake]
> - Taking a 250 m shot because the leaf says 1000.
> - Hunting with surplus full metal jacket.
> - Zeroing with one load and hunting with another.

> [!field]
> Run the plate test at 100 and 150 m with your soft points. Write your limit on tape on the stock.

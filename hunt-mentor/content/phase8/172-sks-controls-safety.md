> [!why]
> Five controls run the whole SKS (Simonov self loading carbine). Know them blind and you never fumble with a loaded rifle.

```diagram sks-controls
```

- Tap each number: safety, handle, magazine catch, cover pin, gas tube lock.
- Point in a safe direction, finger off the trigger, check it is unloaded before you touch any of them.

```more the controls in the manual's words
- **Safety (1):** "apply the safety by rotating it forward and upward"; to fire, "rotate the safety rearward until it is parallel to the trigger" (95%, US Army TC 9-56, 1969).
- **Operating handle (2):** "Grasp the operating handle, pull it fully to the rear, and release it. If the magazine is empty, the bolt will remain open" (95%).
- **Magazine catch (3):** "pull back the magazine catch and allow the magazine to swing open" (95%).
- **Cover pin (4):** rotate it to vertical and pull it out to free the top cover (95%). Chapter 6.
- **Gas tube lock (5):** rotate it up to free the gas tube; down to lock (95%). Chapter 6.
- Part names come from [Know the parts](#/s/mc-sks-01/2) in chapter 1.
```

## The safety: two positions

```diagram sks-safety
```

- Forward and up: SAFE. It blocks the trigger (95%).
- Rearward and down, against the guard: FIRE (95%).

```more what the safety does and does not do
- "The safety, when rotated up to the safe position, places a block behind the trigger and prevents the trigger from being pressed" (95%, TC 9-56).
- It blocks the trigger. The manual does not say it locks the hammer or the firing pin (95%, my reading). A hard knock or a stuck pin can still fire a loaded rifle (Tip).
- The safety must be on before the trigger group catch can be pressed (95%). Chapter 6.
- Every manual says the same in its own words: a safety is a mechanical part, never a substitute for safe handling (95%, see [how a rifle works](#/s/rifle-mechanics/0)).

> [!warn]
> Three rifles, three safeties. Tikka: safety off to work the bolt. CZ 457: bolt opens with safety on. SKS: lever behind the trigger, forward is safe. Say it out loud when you switch.
```

## Bolt hold open

```diagram sks-bolt-open
```

- Empty magazine: the bolt stays open after the last shot (95%).
- Open bolt is a quick visual check, not proof. Look in the chamber (Tip).

```more how the hold open works
- "When the last round is fed from the magazine, a nib on the magazine platform contacts the catch and forces it upward." The catch then holds the bolt open (95%, TC 9-56).
- To close an empty rifle: open the magazine and pull the handle back and let go, or press the follower down slightly and do the same (95%).
- Ride the handle forward only when unloaded and pointing safe. Live rounds: let the bolt slam home by itself so it locks fully (Tip).
```

## The magazine catch

```diagram sks-mag-catch
```

- Pull the catch back. The magazine swings open (95%).
- Every round in it drops out. Have a hand or bag under it (Tip).

```more why this is the SKS unload
- The fixed magazine is part of the rifle. It is not removed; it swings open to empty (99%, RCMP FRT: "fixed magazine with swing-down body assembly").
- Opening it does NOT empty the chamber. That needs the operating handle too (95%, TC 9-56 unloading steps). [Chapter 3](#/s/mc-sks-03) has the full unload.
```

## Function check

Point in a safe direction. Finger off the trigger. Unloaded and proved.

```diagram sks-function-check
```

- Do it after every rebuild and before every season (Tip).
- Hammer falls with the safety on? Stop. Gunsmith (95%).

```more the manual's function check
- From the Type 56 training packet in TC 9-56 (95%):
  1. Pull the operating handle to the rear and hold it.
  2. Press down on the magazine follower.
  3. Allow the bolt to go forward.
  4. Place the safety lever on SAFE.
  5. Press the trigger: the hammer should not go forward.
  6. Place the safety on FIRE.
  7. Press the trigger: the hammer should go forward.
- Dry fire on an SKS strikes the free floating pin. Use a snap cap if you have one (Tip).
- Extra check (Tip): after step 7, hold the trigger back, cycle the handle, ease off the trigger. You should feel a click as it resets, and the hammer must stay cocked.
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The safety is a part. Safe is a habit.

> [!mistake]
> - Thinking an open magazine means an empty rifle. The chamber can still hold a round.
> - Trusting the safety to stop a slam fire. It only blocks the trigger.
> - Mixing up the Tikka, CZ and SKS safety directions on the same trip.

> [!field]
> Unloaded, at home: 20 times, safety on and off without looking, then 5 function checks.

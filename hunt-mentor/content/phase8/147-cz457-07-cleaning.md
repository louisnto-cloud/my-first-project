> [!why]
> Harrison rain rusts steel overnight. Ten minutes after the hunt keeps the CZ accurate for decades.

```diagram cz-clean-when
```

- Each use, when wet, soon after shooting (95%).
- At least once a year (95%).

```more when, in the manual's words
- "Clean the Rifle: each time you use it; if you get your rifle wet; as soon as possible after shooting; at least once a year in a temperate climate; as often as once a month in a tropical climate, or other demanding environments" (95%, CZ manual page 9).
- CZ safety rule 8: "Always clean the firearm after use" (95%, page 2).
- Rimfire tip: .22 LR ammunition carries a wax that coats the bore; a freshly scrubbed bore may need 10 to 20 rounds before groups settle (85%, Tikka manual note on its rimfire models, via [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22)). Many rimfire shooters clean the bore less often than the action for this reason (Tip).
```

## Clean it in 5 steps

```steps Clean the CZ 457 in 5 steps
cz-clean-1 | PROVE it. Magazine out. Bolt out.
cz-clean-2 | Dry patches from the chamber until one is clean.
cz-clean-3 | Very dirty: solvent on a brush, wait, scrub.
cz-clean-4 | Dry patches until no residue.
cz-clean-5 | Thin oil on metal. Wipe off the excess.
```

- Always from the chamber end (95%).
- Brush all the way through before you pull back (95%).

```more the manual's words
- Few or no shots: "clean the barrel and cartridge chamber with a clean patch attached to the cleaning rod. Repeat the cleaning, changing patches, until the last patch comes out clean" (95%, CZ manual page 9).
- Very dirty: "apply a gun cleaning solution using cleaning brush. Let the solvent act for about 10 minutes ... Then thoroughly scrub out the barrel" (95%).
- Then "dry the barrel and cartridge chamber with a clean patch and check that all powder residues, or fouling have been removed" (95%, page 10).
- "The cleaning rod and the brush must be inserted into the barrel from the chamber, to prevent wear or damage to the muzzle. Pass the brush all the way through the barrel before reversing the movement. If you try to change directions with the brush in the barrel, the brush could stick" (95%).
- The bolt comes out first: chapter 8.
```

## Five cleaning rules

```diagram cz-clean-rules
```

- Bore cleaner stays in the bore (95%).
- One dry patch before shooting (95%).

```more the rules in full
- Other parts: "a dry cloth, old toothbrush, copper bristle brush or a wooden scraper. Preserving oil or paraffin oil can be used ... Never use the agent designed for barrel bore cleaning! These solutions could remain in the joints, and after some time could cause corrosion" (95%, CZ manual page 10).
- Check no cloth threads or bristles stay in the action (95%).
- Stock: a dry cloth or furniture polish (95%).
- Preserve: wipe clean, dry parts with an oily cloth or spray, remove all excess, and "Before shooting always pull through the barrel with dry patch" (95%).
- Cold: "At very low temperatures remove the oil from all accessible parts or apply a lubricant especially for these conditions" (95%).
- Long storage in severe climates: thick preserving grease, removed fully before use (95%).
```

## Wet day routine (Harrison rain)

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| At the truck | Unload, PROVE, wipe dry, case it open or loose (Tip) |
| At home | Bolt out, dry everything, patch the bore, light oil (95%) |
| Next day | Check for rust spots on screws and the muzzle (Tip) |

- A wet case holds water against steel (Tip).

```more more wet weather care
- CZ: clean "if you get your rifle wet" (95%, page 9).
- Wood stocks: let them dry slowly at room temperature, not by a heater (Tip).
- Condensation, cold and storage: [firearm care](#/s/firearm-care).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-clean-rules
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Put it away cleaner than you took it out.

> [!mistake]
> - Pushing the rod in from the muzzle and nicking the crown.
> - Reversing the brush halfway down the bore.
> - Bore solvent on the trigger and bolt, where it sits and rusts.

> [!field]
> After the next wet hunt: run the 5 steps the same night. Time yourself.

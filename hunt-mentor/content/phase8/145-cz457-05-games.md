> [!why]
> Your Tikka teaches recoil. Your CZ teaches everything else, hundreds of shots a season, for the price of a few .308 boxes.

```diagram cz-scale
```

- Shrink the deer's vital zone to 50 m (95%).
- A 5 cm dot at 50 m = the vitals at 200 m (95%).

```more why scaled targets work
- A deer's vital zone is about 20 to 25 cm (8 to 10 in) across (70%, [shooting skills](#/s/shooting-skills)).
- Same angle, shorter distance: dot size = 20 cm x 50 m / deer distance (95%, geometry). 10 cm = 100 m, 5 cm = 200 m, 3.3 cm = 300 m, 2.5 cm = 400 m.
- What it does not train: recoil, wind at long range and the .308's drop. Mix in Tikka sessions (Tip).
- A .22 round costs a small fraction of a .308 round (65%, [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22) estimate). VERIFY current prices at your shop.
- Every game here is a Tip: a training idea, not a fact.
```

## Game 1: the dot card

```diagram cz-dot-game
```

- 6 shots, 6 dots, a new position each (Tip).
- Log the score. Beat it next week (Tip).

```more how to play
- Print or draw three 5 cm dots and three 2.5 cm dots on one sheet (Tip).
- At 50 m: one shot per dot. Prone for dot 1, then sitting, kneeling, standing with sticks (Tip).
- Score 1 per hit. 5 or 6: you are a 200 m deer shot from those positions with that rifle and you; 3 or less: practise that position (Tip).
- Start with the safety ON between shots; push it off only when the sights settle (95%, CZ rule 12).
```

## Game 2: four positions against the clock

```diagram cz-positions
```

- Get into position and fire one aimed shot inside the par time (Tip).
- A deer will not wait for a bench (Tip).

```more how to play
- Start standing, rifle slung, magazine in, chamber empty, safety ON (Tip). Range rules may say otherwise: follow them.
- On "go": get into position, chamber a round, safety off on target, one shot at a 5 cm dot at 50 m (Tip).
- Score: hit inside the par = 2, hit over the par = 1, miss = 0 (Tip).
- Positions: [field positions](#/s/field-positions). [[Shooting sticks]] count as a field rest (Tip).
- Grandpa's twist: one shot in each position after a 50 m jog. Heart rate up, like after a climb (Tip).
```

## Game 3: one cold shot a day

```diagram cz-cold-shot
```

- Only the first shot counts (Tip).
- The deer always gets your first shot (Tip).

```more how to play
- One shot, no warm up, from the position you would use in the bush, at a 5 cm dot at 50 m (Tip).
- Where you can do this: a range or a legal place to shoot. Never in a no shooting area or a bylaw area (99%, see [Region 2](#/s/rb-region-2) and [Local plan: Mission](#/s/local-mission)).
- Mark the calendar: 1 or 0. Ten in a row: move to the 2.5 cm dot (Tip).
```

## Game 4: catch a flinch

```diagram cz-flinch
```

- A dip on a snap cap shows a flinch (Tip).
- Squeeze, follow through, call the shot (Tip).

```more how to play
- A partner loads the magazine with live rounds and one [[Snap cap]], out of your sight (Tip).
- Snap caps are what CZ says to use for dry training (95%, CZ manual page 5).
- Rimfire snap caps wear out; replace dented ones (Tip).
- Call the shot: say where the crosshair was when it broke, then check the paper (Tip).
- Then do the same with the Tikka. Flinch shows up on the big gun first (Tip).
```

## Game 5: grouse walk, the safe way

```diagram cz-backstop
```

- Walk a range trail with paper "grouse" heads (Tip).
- Before each shot, say the backstop out loud (Tip).

```more how to play
- Staple 4 cm squares (about a grouse head) at 10 to 40 m along a range walking course, some on the ground, some high (Tip).
- Rule: no shot unless earth is behind the target. High targets with sky behind: say "no shot" and score a point for the call (Tip).
- This builds the habit for real grouse, where the backstop rule saves lives (Tip; CZ rule 15, 95%).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram cz-scale
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Small dot, small miss. Practise on the dot, not the deer.

> [!mistake]
> - Only shooting off a bench. The deer shot will be off sticks or a knee.
> - Shooting fast groups. Train one perfect shot at a time.
> - Ignoring wind at 50 m. A breeze moves a .22 several cm (75%).

> [!field]
> This week: one dot card at 50 m. Write the score in the app's journal.

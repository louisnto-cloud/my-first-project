> [!why]
> Old military ammunition leaves salt in your rifle. Left overnight in Fraser Valley damp, salt turns into rust.

```diagram sks-corrosive
```

- Corrosive primers leave salt after firing (65%).
- Salt pulls water from the air. Water plus steel is rust (65%).

```more the chemistry
- Many older military primers use potassium chlorate. Fired, it becomes potassium chloride, a salt (65%, AccurateShooter, "Coping with Corrosive Ammo", 3 March 2018).
- The salt attracts moisture; with oxygen it rusts and pits steel (65%).
- Oil alone does not remove salt. Water dissolves it (65%).
- Your bore is likely chrome lined, which resists this rust (70%, Wikipedia "SKS"). The gas tube, piston, bolt face and carrier still rust (Tip).
- Most SKS ammunition sold in Canada today is non corrosive. Old surplus is the risk (Tip).
```

## Spot surplus ammunition

```diagram sks-ammo-id
```

- Steel case, sealed tins, military headstamp: think surplus (Tip).
- Unknown? Treat it as corrosive (Tip).

```more how to tell, honestly
- No outside mark proves a round is corrosive or not (Tip).
- Signs of old military surplus: sealed metal tins, steel cases, Berdan primers (two small flash holes inside a fired case), military headstamps with a year code (Tip, common surplus guides).
- New factory boxes usually say "non corrosive" on the box (Tip). Read it.
- Rule: if you cannot tell, clean as if it was corrosive. It costs 10 minutes (Tip).
```

## Surplus is not deer ammunition

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram sks-fmj-vs-sp
```

- Surplus rounds are almost all full metal jacket (Tip).
- [[Full metal jacket]] bullets are illegal for hunting in BC (British Columbia) (99%).

```more the law
> [!law] BC Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis, page 12
> "It is unlawful to hunt or trap with a rifle using a full metal jacketed non-expanding bullet, or a tracer, incendiary, or explosive bullet."

- Use surplus only for practice at the range. Hunt with a soft point or other expanding bullet (99%, [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0)).
- Keep range ammunition and hunting ammunition in different boxes, and only hunting rounds in your hunting bag (Tip).
```

## Clean the same day

Point in a safe direction. Finger off the trigger. Unloaded and proved. Field stripped ([chapter 6](#/s/mc-sks-06)).

```steps Clean after corrosive ammunition in 4 steps
sks-clean-1 | Bore: wet patches, brush, dry patches.
sks-clean-2 | Bolt face and carrier: wet and scrub.
sks-clean-3 | Gas tube and piston: wet and scrub.
sks-clean-4 | Dry everything. Light oil.
```

```more the method and the sources
- The manual's routine (95%, US Army TC 9-56): wet a patch with bore cleaner and run it through the bore several times; brush the bore and chamber several times; dry patches until clean; inspect; clean other parts and soak small parts; dry with clean rags; "cover all parts with a light coat of oil".
- For corrosive salts, water based cleaning first (65%, AccurateShooter 2018). One method: window cleaner with ammonia or hot water through the bore and over the bolt, carrier and gas parts, scrub with a toothbrush, then dry fully and oil (60%, Paul Markel via AccurateShooter). Label: Tip.
- Water left in the rifle also rusts. Dry everything, then oil (65%).
- Do it the same day. Run a dry patch again over the next few days and look for orange (Tip).
- Do not forget the gas piston and tube: the hot gas carries residue there (Tip, owner advice).
- Routine cleaning and oil choices are in [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0).
```

## The built in cleaning kit

```diagram sks-tool-kit
```

- Tool kit in a trap in the butt. Rod under the barrel (95%).
- Unlock the bayonet to free the rod (95%).

```more the kit in the manual
- "Press in on the cover in the butt plate until the combination tool case is ejected. Unlock the bayonet, let it hang vertically, and after disengaging the head of the cleaning rod from the front sight base, pull the cleaning rod out of the rifle" (95%, TC 9-56).
- Kit: combination tool, sling, charger clips, oiler (95%). When used as a rod guide, the tool case cap twists onto the front sight base (95%).
- Tip: a coated one piece rod and a bore guide are kinder to the bore than the steel army rod.
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Shoot salt, wash with water, finish with oil. Same day.

> [!mistake]
> - Oiling a corrosive bore without washing it first.
> - Cleaning the bore and forgetting the gas tube and piston.
> - Taking surplus full metal jacket rounds hunting.

> [!field]
> Pack a range kit: water spray bottle, patches, toothbrush, rag, oil. Clean before you drive home.

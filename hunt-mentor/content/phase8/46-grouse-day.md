> [!why]
> The same patch of bush can be empty at noon and full at 4 pm. Time and weather move grouse as much as habitat does.

```diagram mgr-day-clock
```

- Feed early and late, rest midday, roost at night (hunter lore, 75%).
- Legal: 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset (99%).

```more the day, with the evidence level for each claim
| Claim | Evidence | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| Feed early morning and late afternoon | Agency and hunter accounts | 75% |
| Pick grit and dust on road edges | Agency accounts, sign | 75% |
| Rest and dust bathe midday | Hunter accounts | 70% |
| Spruce and dusky sit in trees midday | Hunter accounts | 65% |
| Roost in trees, ground or snow at night | Agency accounts | 75% |
| Adults move only a few hundred metres a day | Pennsylvania Game Commission | 75% |

- Hours rule: "To hunt game, except migratory game birds from one hour after sunset to one hour before sunrise" is unlawful (99%, item 38, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-legal-methods/13)).
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Morning: grit and breakfast

```gallery
hab-grass-fir-edge | Grass and fir edge: morning feeding ground.
hab-snowberry | Snowberry: a fall and winter food.
```

- First light to mid morning: edges, trails, road shoulders (75%).
- They need grit for the gizzard (85%).

```more morning detail
- Grouse pick small gravel to grind food in the gizzard (85%). Road edges and trails supply it (75%).
- Young birds in September and October feed on clover and berries along road edges and cut blocks (65 to 75%).
- Sharp tailed grouse use roadsides and landings with clover, dandelion and yarrow heavily in fall (85%, BC species account).
- Roads are also where the rules bite. See [How to hunt](#/s/mc-grouse-09/6) before you hunt a road edge.
- Tip: walk an old closed road or a trail away from traffic at first light.
```

## Midday: rest, dust, sit

```diagram mgr-find-sitting
```

- Birds loaf in cover and dust bathe (70%).
- The best time to still hunt sitters with the .22 (Tip).

```more midday detail
- Dust bathing: shallow bowls in dry dirt on road edges and banks, used to clean feathers (80%).
- Spruce grouse spend fall and winter days up in conifers (80%, Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife, earlier lesson).
- Dusky grouse sit in big Douglas fir (Tip).
- Tip: slow down at midday; look up, not ahead.
```

## Evening: feed again, then roost

```anim sg-thermal-day Thermals: up in the morning, down in the evening.
```

- Last 2 hours of light: birds feed again (hunter lore, 70%).
- Legal light ends 1 hour after sunset (99%).

```more evening detail
- Late afternoon feeding mirrors the morning: edges, berries, clover, buds (70%).
- In bitter cold, some hunters see birds feed in the warmest hours, about 1 to 4 pm (hunter account, 50%: Tip).
- Roost: in trees (often conifers), on the ground, or under soft snow (75%).
- Thermals sink in the evening, so a dog working uphill gets scent from above (Tip).
- Shooting light fades fast in timber; your watch says legal, the woods may say no (Tip).
```

## Weather

```diagram mgr-weather-grid
```

- Wind and rain move birds into shelter (hunter lore, 55 to 60%: Tip).
- No grouse study found on pressure fronts or the moon.

```more each weather type, with evidence
| Weather | What birds do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Strong wind | Wild, flush early; shelter in low, thick cover | Hunter lore (Tip) |
| Rain | Under middle aged conifers | Hunter lore (Tip) |
| Calm overcast | Moving and feeding; good hunting | Hunter lore (Tip) |
| Fresh soft snow | Snow roosts; tracks show everything | 70 to 75% |
| Crust, deep cold | Into thick conifers; weight loss | 85% |
| Falling pressure, cold front | No grouse study found | VERIFY |
| Moon phase | No grouse study found | VERIFY |

- Hunting magazine and blog advice says calm overcast days are best and windy days make grouse skittish (55 to 60%).
- Keep your own record in the Journal: date, weather, flushes. Your data beats lore (Tip).
- See also [Weather and the moon](#/s/weather-moon) for deer.
```

## Hunting pressure through the week

```diagram mgr-200m-band
```

- Birds close to open roads get shot more (75%).
- Walk past 200 m from the road (Tip, based on the study).

```more detail
- Pennsylvania: 48% harvest rate near open roads versus 19% farther away; male survival 23% within 201 m of roads versus 36% beyond (75%).
- Opening weekend and the first snow bring the most hunters (Tip).
- Weekday mornings on gated roads see the least traffic (Tip).
- The flip side: birds far from roads are less shot, not less wary (Tip).
```

## Memory hooks

```diagram mgr-day-clock
```

- Rhyme: "Gravel at dawn, dust at noon, berries at dusk, roost by the moon."
- Number trick: "1 and 1": 1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset.
- Old hand's rule (tradition): "Wind day, low and thick. Rain day, under the firs."

```more more hooks
- "Look up at noon."
- "200 m past the road."
- "No study on the moon": do not plan around it.
- Rhymes are made for this course; the wind and rain rules are common hunter advice, not science.
```

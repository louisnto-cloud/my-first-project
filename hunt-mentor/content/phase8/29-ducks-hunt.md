> [!why]
> Most duck hunts are won the evening before: scouting, wind and a hide the birds cannot see into.

```diagram sb-duck-tactics
```

- Four methods: decoys, jump shooting, fields, pass shooting (Tip).
- Your best start: decoys on a small pond, or a creek jump (Tip).

```more the four methods, pros and cons (opinion)
- **Decoys on water.** Pros: close, slow, committed birds; best ID; fits a 10 ft boat or waders. Cons: gear, a hide, a way to fetch.
- **Jump shooting.** Pros: no gear, fits two hunters on foot. Cons: fast flushing shots, birds can fall in deep water.
- **Field hunting.** Pros: big flocks, dry feet, easy pick up. Cons: you must find yesterday's field and get permission (99% for permission on private land).
- **Pass shooting.** Pros: nothing to set. Cons: long crossing shots, more cripples. Skip it this season.
- My lean: decoys on a pond you can wade, then jump shoot creeks on the way out.
- Assumption: no dog, so every shot must fall where you can fetch it.
```

## Scout: four steps

```steps Scout a duck spot in 4 steps
mdk-scout-1 | Pick 2 or 3 waters on the Hunt Map.
mdk-scout-2 | Watch from far off, first and last light.
mdk-scout-3 | Write where, how many, what, when, wind.
mdk-scout-4 | Ask permission, then plan for tomorrow's wind.
```

- Scout the evening before (Tip).
- Never walk onto the roost to look (Tip).

```more scouting like an old hand (opinion, widely taught)
- Glass from the truck or a knoll. Count birds, name species, note the time they came in.
- Check the wind forecast for the morning. Ducks land into the wind (80%).
- Pick the hide spot now: cover behind you, sun at your back, shallow water in front, fetchable.
- Rest spots: hunt a pond once, then leave it a week (75%).
- Use the [planner](#/plan) to write the trip plan.
```

## Decoys on water

```steps Set a J hook spread in 4 steps

hf-jhook-2 | Set the main bunch upwind, close to your hide.
hf-jhook-3 | Run a tail line out downwind.
hf-jhook-4 | Leave an open pocket 20 to 25 m out.
```

```video

```

- 6 to 12 decoys is plenty on a small pond (Tip).
- Leave a clear landing hole inside 25 m (Tip).

```more decoy details (opinion)
- Use a [[J hook]] or a simple U. The landing pocket is the [[Landing zone]] (Tip).
- Small family groups of 3 to 6 look calm and real (Tip).
- Farthest decoy no more than 35 m (38 yd) from you: it is your range marker (Tip).
- Mix in a few divers' decoys on big water if you hunt divers; divers like long lines (Tip).
- Plastic decoys and mouth calls are legal; live birds and electronic calls are not (97 to 99%). [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/19).
```

## Wind, hide and the sun

```diagram mdk-crosswind
```

- Wind at your side: ducks cross in front, not at you (Tip).
- Sun behind you, in their eyes (Tip).

```more hide rules (opinion)
- A Ducks Unlimited writer angles the spread so birds approach on a crossing angle; it keeps the hide less obvious and gives follow up shots as birds flare downwind (Tip).
- On calm mornings, puff talc powder to read the wind (Tip).
- Birds look past the decoys too; tuck into the smallest patch of grass or cattails and stay very still (Tip).
- A roof or a hat brim stops them seeing straight down (Tip). See [Masterclass 3](#/s/mc-ducks-03).
- Check the Sun and shade tool on the [Hunt Map](#/map) to place the sun behind you (Tip).
```

## Calling

```video
yt-duck-call-basics | Hold the call right, then learn the basic quack.
yt-duck-call-quack | The basic quack, slowly.
```

- Call less than you think. Watch the birds, not the call (Tip).
- Mouth calls only; no electronic calls (97%).

```more calling (opinion)
- Learn three sounds: the single quack, the greeting call and the feed chuckle (Tip).
- Call to birds that are far off or leaving. Stop when they cup their wings and come (Tip).
- A wrong note matters less than movement in the hide (Tip).
- Late season birds have heard it all: call softly and rarely (Tip).
- BC also bans recorded calls for game birds (99%). [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/19).
```

## Jump shooting creeks

```anim mdk-jump Jump a creek: bend to bend.
```

- Sneak bend to bend, out of sight (Tip).
- Make them flush into the wind (65%).

```more step by step (opinion, widely taught)
1. Pick a winding creek or a chain of small sloughs.
2. Come in from downwind and behind cover, so birds that flush have to fight a headwind (65%).
3. Look before every bend. Spot them before they spot you.
4. Agree the plan: who goes left, who goes right, rise together on a word.
5. Think "one drake". Pick one bird and let it gain a little room before you mount the gun.
6. Shoot only where the bird will fall on land or shallow water you can wade.
- Use [[Jump shooting]] on small water only; big water birds flush out of range (Tip).
```

## Fields and pass shooting

```photo hab-geese-stubble Stubble fields feed mallards at dawn and dusk.
```

```diagram sk-steel-range
```

- Fields: find yesterday's field and get permission (99% for permission).
- Pass shooting: only inside 35 m (38 yd) (Tip).

```more field and pass shooting (opinion)
- Field mallards come to the exact spot they fed yesterday. Set up there before light, lie flat, cover your face (Tip).
- Hide in the stubble with a layout blind or natural cover; small spreads of 1 to 2 dozen decoys (Tip).
- Bait rules apply near fields: [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/19) (99%).
- [[Pass shooting]]: crossing birds at range cause most cripples. Beginners skip it (Tip).
- Most hunters overestimate range. Use a decoy or a stake at 35 m as a marker (Tip).
```

## Partner, boat and kayak

```diagram gr-blind-boat
```

```diagram hf-duck-zones
```

- You take your side, your brother takes his. Never swing across (Tip).
- Shoot from a boat only when it is stopped: beached, anchored or tied (99%).

```more two hunters and a boat (opinion and rules)
- Agree zones before the first bird. One person calls the shot (Tip).
- In the 10 ft boat: sit back to back or side by side, shoot sitting, only one person shoots at a time if space is tight (Tip).
- The kayak is a retrieval boat, not a shooting platform (Tip).
- Boat rules (no shooting from a boat moving under motor or sail, loaded gun rules): [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/20) (99%).
- Cold water safety: [Boat and kayak safety](#/s/first-duck-hunt/10).
```

## Shoot or pass: the decision

```diagram mdk-shoot-flow
```

- Five yeses, then pick one bird (Tip).
- Any "no" means let it go (Tip).

```more the five questions
1. Can I name it? Species and sex if a restricted species (99% that restricted limits apply).
2. Is it under its limit today: 8 ducks, inside that 4 pintail, 4 canvasback, 2 goldeneye, 2 harlequin (99%).
3. Is it inside 35 m (38 yd)? (Tip)
4. Is it safe: not over my partner, not low over the water toward a house or road? (Tip)
5. Can I fetch it? (99% that you must have a means of retrieval)
- Old hand's rule (opinion): "The bird you let go comes back next week. The one you cripple does not."
```

## What a 40 year hunter does differently

| Beginner | Old hand (opinion) |
|---|---|
| Hunts where ducks were | Hunts where they will be |
| Calls a lot | Calls little, watches a lot |
| Looks up at birds | Eyes up, head down |
| Shoots the flock | Picks one drake |
| Hunts the same pond daily | Rotates and rests spots |
| Shoots at 50 m | Waits for 25 m |

```more more old hand habits (opinion, widely taught)
- Scouts more than he hunts.
- Sets up before the first bird can see, packs up after the last legal minute.
- Knows his range by heart and marks it with a decoy.
- Keeps a hunt journal: wind, weather, birds, what worked. See [Your hunt journal](#/s/hunt-journal-guide).
- Leaves birds in the area: never hunts the roost.
```

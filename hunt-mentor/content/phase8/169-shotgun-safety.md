> [!why]
> Bird hunting means fast swings near other people. Most shotgun accidents happen when a bird pulls a muzzle across a partner.

```diagram sg2-line
```

- Each hunter shoots only in a slice about 45 degrees straight ahead (75%).
- Between and behind the line: never (75%).

```more zones of fire in full
- A [[Zone of fire]] is the only slice of ground or sky where you may shoot (75%, hunter education, [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/4)).
- Walk side by side, always in sight. One gets ahead or behind: both stop and fix the line (75%, [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/5)).
- Lose sight of each other: muzzles up or down, no shooting, call out (75%).
- Bright orange helps you see each other in timber. BC law does not require it (95%). My lean: wear it (Tip).
- Full partner plan: [Hunting with a partner](#/s/hunting-partner) and [zones of fire, together](#/s/hunting-partner/4).
```

## In a blind

```diagram sg2-blind
```

- Agree left and right before first light (70%).
- Never swing across your partner (70%).

```more blind rules
- Shoot only birds on your side. Muzzles out the front, never across a partner (70%, [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/7)).
- Guns rest muzzle up or out the front. Never leaned on the wall behind you (Tip).
- Count birds out loud as they fall: you each need to know when the limit is reached (99% on the limit, Tip on the method, [Geese Masterclass](#/s/mc-geese-09/8)).
- Earplugs: magnum loads damage hearing (65%).
```

## In a boat

```diagram sg2-boat
```

- Sit back to back. Sit to shoot. Never stand (70%).
- Motor pushing the boat: every gun unloaded (95%).

```more the boat law and rules
- BC Firearm Violence Prevention Act, in force 1 October 2026: no loaded firearm in or on a boat, with a hunting exemption when the boat has no motor or the motor is not moving it (95%, [Water safety](#/s/water-safety/4)).
- Ducks and geese: no hunting from a moving boat with a motor or sail; drifting after you cut the motor still counts as moving (99%, s. 41, [Rule Book](#/s/rb-migratory-birds/34)).
- One person moves at a time. Unload before anyone changes seats (70%).
- Recoil plus standing tips small boats (Tip, [Water safety](#/s/water-safety/5)).
- My lean (from [Water safety](#/s/water-safety/4)): use the boat as a ride to a shore blind. Shoot from land.
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Low bird, no shot

```diagram sg2-low-bird
```

- Only shoot with sky behind the bird (Tip).
- A low bird toward a partner, a dog or a decoy setter: never (Tip).

```more why
- Shot that misses keeps going. Sky is the only safe backdrop for a flying bird in a group (Tip).
- Grouse Masterclass: "low bird, no shot. Never swing toward a partner" (Tip, [Grouse Masterclass](#/s/mc-grouse-09/4)).
- Grouse in timber: stop and let the bird go if a partner is anywhere near your swing (Tip).
```

## Handing over, fences and ditches

```diagram sg2-handover
```

- Open, empty, muzzle safe. They check it too (80%).
- Unload before a fence, a ditch or a log (95%).

```more in full
- Do ACTS and PROVE every time you pick up, put down, hand over or receive a firearm (80%, [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/3)).
- Browning: "ALWAYS UNLOAD YOUR FIREARM'S CHAMBERS BEFORE CROSSING A FENCE, CLIMBING A TREE, JUMPING A DITCH OR NEGOTIATING OTHER OBSTACLES" (95%, Citori manual).
- Browning also: never lean a gun on a fence, tree or car (95%).
- Beretta: never take a gun from another person unless the action is open and checked empty (95%, A300 manual).
- Fences and ditches step by step: [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/8).
```

## Blocked barrel

```diagram sg2-bore-check
```

- Snow, mud or a stuck wad can burst a barrel (95%).
- Unload, look from the breech, push it out with a rod (95%).

```more the manual's words
- Remington: "Even a small amount of snow, mud, excess lubricant or grease in the bore can dangerously increase pressure and cause the barrel to bulge or burst" (95%).
- "NEVER try to remove an object from the barrel by loading a cartridge and firing" (95%, 870 manual).
- If a rod will not move it: a gunsmith (95%).
- Beretta: never look into the barrel from the muzzle (95%).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The bird you let go is never the one you regret.

> [!mistake]
> - Swinging past your partner's shoulder on a bird.
> - Standing up in the boat for a better angle.
> - Crossing a ditch with a shell in the chamber.

> [!field]
> Before first light, both say out loud: my side, your side, no low birds, unload to move.

> [!why]
> October water in the Interior is cold. A small boat tips fast. The cold, not swimming skill, is what kills.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Cold water shock and 1 10 1

```diagram gr-1-10-1
```

- Use the 10 minutes for one job: get out, or onto the hull (Tip).

```more where 1 10 1 comes from
- The "1 10 1 principle" is a name, not exact timing. It was made by physiologist Dr. Gordon Giesbrecht (80%).
- **1 minute:** cold shock. You gasp and breathe hard. Get your breathing under control (80%).
- **10 minutes:** of useful muscle movement before hands and arms stop working (80%).
- **1 hour:** before hypothermia makes you unconscious (80%).
- Real times vary with water temperature, body and clothing. Some experts say it is too neat (70%).
- The first gasp is the killer. If your head is under water when you gasp, you inhale water (75%).
- Tip: use the 10 minutes for one job: get out of the water or onto the hull. Do not swim for shore unless it is very close.
```

## PFD worn, not stowed

```diagram gr-pfd-worn
```

- A [[PFD]] (personal flotation device) only works if you wear it. My lean: wear it, zipped, every minute.
- Try it on over all your hunting layers.

```more what the law says
- Canadian law requires an approved PFD or lifejacket of the right size **on board** for each person (99%).
- The law requires carrying it, not wearing it (95%). One exception: an inflatable PFD must be worn in an open boat (99%). My lean: wear it, zipped, every minute on the water.
- About 87% of boaters who drowned were not wearing one, per the Lifesaving Society (75%).
- Pick one that fits over your hunting jacket. Try it on with all layers before the trip.
```

## The boat and the kayak

```diagram gr-boat-load
```

- Any motor, even a small electric: carry a PCOC (Pleasure Craft Operator Card) or other proof of competency (95 to 99%).
- Never lean out for a bird. Bring it along the hull to the middle.

```more boat, kayak and retrieving
- Any boat fitted with a motor needs the operator to have proof of competency on board, usually a PCOC (99%). The rule sets no minimum motor size, so a small electric counts (95%).
- 10 ft boat: check the capacity plate. Count you, your brother, gear, guns and a deer (VERIFY plate numbers on your own boat).
- Load low and in the middle. Heavy items on the floor, centred side to side.
- Kayak: one person, guns cased and strapped, dry bag clipped in. Hard to shoot from and easy to tip.
- No dog? Retrieve birds with the boat, motor off and gun unloaded, or with a long handled landing net (Tip).
- Never lean out over the side to grab a bird. Bring it along the hull to the middle.
```

## Guns in the boat

```diagram gr-boat-gun-flow
```

- Motor pushing the boat: every gun **unloaded** (95%). Ducks: no hunting from a moving motor boat (99%).

```more the exact rules, BC and federal
- BC [[FVPA]] (Firearm Violence Prevention Act), in force 1 October 2026, section 4: you must not transport, carry or store a **loaded** firearm in or on a vehicle or boat (95%).
- Section 5 of the same Act: no shooting "in, on or from a vehicle or boat" (95%).
- Hunting exemption, in force the same day: a loaded gun, and shooting, are allowed in a boat with no motor, or in a motor boat while the motor is not propelling it, when you are hunting or retrieving wildlife (95%). For ducks and geese, the federal moving boat rule below also applies (99%).
- So while the motor pushes the boat, every gun aboard is unloaded (95%).
- BC synopsis, page 11, item 23 (2 October 2026 edition), deer and other game: no hunting from a boat unless it is **not being propelled by a motor**, or is held still in current, tide or wind by the motor (99%). Item 22: no hunting wildlife from a motor vehicle (99%).
- Federal, ducks and geese: no hunting from a **moving** boat with a motor or sail. Drifting on momentum from the motor still counts as moving (99%).
- BC synopsis, page 11, item 24 (2 October 2026 edition), ducks and geese: no hunting "from or by using a moving boat that is equipped with a motor or sail" (99%). Same as the federal rule. This replaced the old power boat rule on 1 October 2026 (99%, B.C. Reg. 66/2026).
- Safe habit: gun unloaded and in a case while moving. Load only once you are stopped and set (Tip). Federal transport rules require a non restricted firearm to be unloaded, not cased (95%).
```

> [!lean]
> Use the boat as a ride to a shore blind or a point. Shoot from land. It removes most of the legal and tipping risk.

## Never stand to shoot

```anim gr-boat-tip Recoil plus standing: over you go.
```

- Sit square, feet flat. Shoot only inside your arc. One person moves at a time.

```more why boats tip
- Small boats and canoes tip when weight goes high or to one side.
- Recoil pushes you back. Standing turns that into a fall over the side.
- Sit square, feet flat, shoot only inside your own arc. See the firearm safety session.
- One person moves at a time. Everyone else sits still.
```

## If you capsize

```steps If you go in
gr-cap-1 | Expect the gasp. Float and breathe, about 1 minute.
gr-cap-2 | Stay with the boat.
gr-cap-3 | Climb onto the hull.
gr-cap-4 | Can't get out? HELP (Heat Escape Lessening Posture).
gr-cap-5 | Two of you? Huddle. Let the gun go.
```

```more the steps in words
1. Expect the gasp. Mouth closed if you can. Float in your PFD and get breathing under control for about 1 minute (80%).
2. Stay with the boat. It is bigger to see and helps you float (75%).
3. Climb onto the hull to get as much of your body out of the water as you can (75%).
4. Can't get out? Use the HELP (Heat Escape Lessening Posture): arms crossed on chest, knees up, ankles crossed (75%).
5. Two people? Huddle, chests together (75%).
6. Once out, stay out. Water pulls heat away about 25 times faster than air (70%).
7. Let the gun go. It is replaceable.
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The PFD in the bow locker is a cushion. The PFD on your chest is a lifejacket.

> [!mistake]
> - PFD under the seat "to put on if we tip".
> - Motoring after a crippled duck with a loaded gun.
> - Standing up to shoot or to pull in a bird.

> [!field]
> Both wear PFDs over hunting layers. In shallow water, practise sitting back to back and reaching for a bird, gun unloaded.

```checklist ws-launch
Both PFDs on and zipped
PCOC on board if the motor is fitted
Guns unloaded and cased
Weight low and centred
Trip plan left with someone
Dry bag: spare warm layer, lighter, phone
```

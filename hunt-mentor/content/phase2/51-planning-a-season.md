## Key points

```diagram fl-plan-year
```

- Licences run 1 April to 31 March (99%).
- The main LEH draw closes in June (99%).
- No wireless trail cameras for hunting from 1 August to 10 December, except Region 1 (99%).

```more the key points in full
- Licence year: 1 April to 31 March; all hunting and species licences expire 31 March (99%, from [The BC hunter's year](#/s/bc-calendar/0)).
- 2026 main LEH draw deadline was 11:59 pm, 23 June 2026 (99%). The 2027 date: VERIFY on the LEH page each spring.
- LEH application fee: $6.30 including taxes, not refundable (99%).
- Prove BC residency every three years to keep buying resident licences and LEH (99%).
- Online licence orders are mailed; at a counter you get them at once (99%).
- Item 30: no wireless camera for hunting from 1 August to 10 December, except in Region 1 (99%, synopsis page 11).
- Planning tasks (when to train, scout, buy gear) are opinion (Tip).
```

## The licence timeline

```diagram lc-cal-paperwork
```

| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 April | New licence year starts | 99% |
| Spring | LEH synopsis posted | 95% |
| June | Main LEH deadline | 99% |
| July | LEH results in WILD | 99% |
| August | Buy species licences, bird permit | 99% |
| 31 March | Everything expires | 99% |

- Put every date in your phone calendar today (Tip).

```more the timeline in full
- LEH results are in [[WILD]] (Wildlife Information and Licensing Data); not mailed; email only if your profile has one (99%).
- Species licences: online orders are mailed, counter purchases are immediate (99%). Some species licences are not valid until 2 days after issue; deer is not one of them (90%).
- You must carry a valid federal [[MGBHP]] (Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit) for ducks and geese (99%).
- The Mandatory Hunter Report is due 31 March for anyone who bought an elk, moose or caribou species licence (99%).
- Next special draw (not for you this year, for awareness): 2027 Special Mountain Sheep draw closes 19 November 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific Time (99%).
- Prove residency every three years (99%).
- Full detail: [Rule Book: licences, FWID and fees](#/s/rb-licences-fees/0), [LEH draw strategy](#/s/leh-strategy/0).
```

## Scouting schedule

```diagram fl-plan-scout
```

- Winter: maps. Spring: sheds and winter range. Summer: glass at dusk (Tip).
- Wireless trail cams: not for hunting 1 August to 10 December, except Region 1 (99%).

> [!law] Item 30, page 11
> "To use a wireless camera for the purposes of hunting from Aug. 1 to Dec. 10, except in region 1 (see page 20 for further details)."

```more scouting in full
| Trip type | When | What you learn (Tip) |
|---|---|---|
| Desk (maps, satellite) | Any time, best in winter | Benches, saddles, water, access |
| Drive | Spring, summer | Roads, gates, closures, parking |
| Boots | Spring to early fall | Trails, sign, real terrain, travel times |
| Glass | Summer evenings, early fall | Where deer feed and bed |

- Walk winter range in March and April without pushing deer; they are at their weakest (Tip, from [The BC hunter's year](#/s/bc-calendar/0)).
- Region 3: all motor vehicles except snowmobiles are banned above 1,700 m except on existing roads and trails (99%).
- Map work: [Map scouting from home](#/s/map-scouting/0). Road status: [Maps 3: land status and access](#/s/maps-land-status/0).
```

## Trip fuel math

```diagram fl-plan-fuel
```

- Kilometres x your truck's use x today's price (Tip).
- Example numbers are made up. Use your own.

```more trip cost in full
- Fuel = (round trip km / 100) x litres per 100 km x price per litre (Tip, arithmetic).
- Measure your real route in a map app; add 10% for back roads and scouting loops (Tip).
- Add: food, ice, camp fees where they apply, and a spare day of food (Tip).
- Mission to Heffley Creek and Okanagan trips: measure them; the app does not give a distance to avoid a wrong number.
```

## A hunting week

```diagram fl-plan-week
```

- Weekdays prepare, weekends hunt, Monday clean up (Tip).

```more the week in full
- Monday: meat in the cold, clean the rifle, journal the trip (Tip).
- Tuesday and Wednesday: maps, weather, wind for the weekend; plan A, B and C spots (Tip).
- Thursday: pack, check in season changes and fire restrictions (99% that changes are posted online).
- Friday: drive, set camp, sleep early (Tip).
- Saturday and Sunday: hunt the edges of the day; drive home rested (Tip).
- The night before checklist: [Weather, moon and what moves game](#/s/weather-moon/0).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram fl-plan-mistakes
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): October is won in February. The calendar does not wait for you.

> [!mistake]
> - Missing a deadline.
> - No plan B spot for the wrong wind.
> - New gear the week of the hunt.

```more the mistakes in full
- Deadlines are hard. Miss the main LEH draw and you wait a year (99%).
- Wrong wind on the day: go to plan B, do not force plan A (Tip).
- New boots, new pack, new scope the week before: test in summer instead (Tip).
```

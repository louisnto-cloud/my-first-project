> [!why]
> The CZ 457 is your grouse rifle and your cheapest teacher. Used right it is deadly precise; used carelessly its bullet can travel past a kilometre.

```diagram ff-rf-cz457
```

- Legal for grouse, ptarmigan and turkey among game birds (99%).
- Not for quail, ducks or deer (99%).

```more what this lesson covers
- Your rifle: CZ 457 in .22 LR (long rifle). Model, barrel length and scope: Assumption, not yet confirmed; check yours.
- CZ 457 features from the CZ manual: modular barrel swaps "at the highest user level," handle rotation shortened to 60 degrees, adjustable trigger, cocking indicator, bolt removable with the safety on, 11 mm dovetail for scope mounts (95%).
- One CZ 457 model lists 1.8 to 3.4 lb trigger pull and "2 MOA accuracy" (85%, CZ product page, American model).
- Rules here are only what the [Rule Book: legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0) says. Seasons: see your region's Rule Book page.
```

## Key points

```diagram gr-legal-guns
```

- Grouse: head or upper neck shots (Tip).
- 50 m zero keeps you within 1.5 cm from 15 to 55 m (75%).
- Earth behind every shot. Never water or rock (Tip).

```more all key points
- Rimfire among game birds: grouse, ptarmigan or turkey only (99%, Rule Book note 2). So no .22 for quail (95%).
- Other small game: rimfire is a legal method in the methods table (99%, Rule Book). Open seasons and which species: check your region's tables (VERIFY by species and area).
- Deer, moose, elk, bear: no rimfire (99%, Rule Book).
- CCI Mini Mag 40 gr (grain) copper plated round nose: 1,235 fps (feet per second), BC (ballistic coefficient) .130 (85%, CCI).
- Wind matters: 10 km/h moves it about 3 cm at 50 m and 11 cm at 100 m (75%, model).
- A .22 bullet can carry well over 1 km (65%).
- CZ: dry fire only with snap caps, not frequently (95%).
```

## The cartridge

| Type | Speed | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Standard velocity | Lower, near the speed of sound | Target, often most accurate (Tip) |
| High velocity, e.g. CCI Mini Mag | 1,235 fps (376 m/s) | Hunting, all round (85%) |
| Subsonic | Below the speed of sound | Quiet, short range (Tip) |

- Rimfire priming sits in the rim. Low pressure. Not reloadable (85%).

```more the cartridge in full
- How rimfire works: [how a cartridge works](#/s/cartridge-anatomy/0) (85%).
- CCI Mini Mag, part 30: 40 gr copper plated round nose, 1,235 fps, BC .130 (85%, CCI product page, search preview).
- Speeds for standard and subsonic loads vary by brand: read the box (Tip). The speed of sound is about 1,125 fps at 20 C (85%, physics).
- Round nose bullets are what most .22 LR hunting loads use; hollow points expand more and can ruin small birds (Tip).
- Store .22 ammunition dry. Old or damp rimfire misfires more (Tip).
```

## Trajectory with a 50 m zero

```diagram ff-rf-trajectory
```

- Hold dead on from 15 to 55 m (75%).
- 75 m: 6 cm low. 100 m: 19 cm low (75%).

```more the table in full
40 gr at 1,235 fps, scope 4 cm above the bore (Hunt Mentor model, py ballisticcalc, G1 drag) (75%).

| Distance | 25 m zero | 50 m zero | 75 m zero |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 m | 1 cm low | 1 cm low | 0.5 cm high |
| 25 m | 0 | 0.5 cm high | 2.6 cm high |
| 40 m | 0 | 0.8 cm high | 4 cm high |
| 50 m | 1 cm low | 0 | 4 cm high |
| 75 m | 8 cm low | 6 cm low | 0 |
| 100 m | 21 cm low | 19 cm low | 10 cm low |

- Assumption: scope height 4 cm. A lower scope changes the close numbers a little.
- CZ open sights come zeroed at 50 m from the factory (95%, CZ manual).
- Speed at 100 m: about 980 fps; energy about 85 ft lb (115 J) (75%, model).

> [!lean]
> Zero at 50 m. Grouse heads are small, so keep shots inside about 40 m (opinion).
```

## Wind on a .22

```diagram ff-rf-wind
```

- A light breeze moves the bullet several cm at 50 m (75%).
- Close the distance instead of guessing (Tip).

```more wind in full
- 10 km/h full crosswind, Hunt Mentor model: 1 cm at 25 m, 3 cm at 50 m, 6 cm at 75 m, 11 cm at 100 m (75%).
- 20 km/h: about double (75%).
- A grouse head is a few centimetres wide (Tip). Past 40 m in wind, a head shot gets risky.
- Wind clock and reading wind: [ballistics deep dive](#/s/ballistics-deep/0).
```

## Find the ammo your rifle likes

```steps Test .22 ammo in 4 steps
ff-rf-test-1 | Buy one box each of 3 or 4 kinds.
ff-rf-test-2 | After cleaning, shoot 10 to 20 warm up rounds.
ff-rf-test-3 | Two 5 shot groups of each at 50 m.
ff-rf-test-4 | Keep the winner. Buy the same lot.
```

- Each .22 rifle has favourite ammunition (Tip).

```more why it matters
- .22 LR accuracy varies a lot between brands in the same rifle (Tip, common shooter experience).
- Tikka's manual notes .22 LR ammunition has a wax that spreads in the bore, so a cleaned bore needs 10 to 20 "cold rounds" before groups settle (85%, Tikka manual note for its rimfire models).
- CZ: "Match" chambered models are tight and prefer match ammunition (95%, CZ manual). Check whether yours is a match chamber.
- Zero with the winner, then do not switch (75%).
```

## Grouse: where to aim

```diagram ff-rf-grouse-head
```

- Head or upper neck (Tip).
- Body shots ruin breast meat or the bird runs (Tip).

```more shot placement in full
- With the CZ 457: head shots only (Tip, from the [ammo session](#/s/ammo-guide/0)).
- Grouse often sit still on a trail edge or low branch, which makes a rest shot possible (Tip). See [grouse](#/s/grouse/0) and [habitat tour: quail and grouse](#/s/habitat-upland/0).
- Edible portion rule for game birds: both breasts go home (99%, [Rule Book: unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0)).
- Bag limits and seasons: Rule Book regional pages only.
```

## Grouse with the .22, step by step

```steps A .22 grouse shot in 4 steps
ff-rf-hunt-1 | Spot the bird. Stop moving.
ff-rf-hunt-2 | Look past it: earth must stop the bullet.
ff-rf-hunt-3 | Rest on a tree or knee. Safety off on target.
ff-rf-hunt-4 | Head shot. Safety on, unload, then walk.
```

- No safe backstop, no shot (Tip).

```more the steps in full
1. When you see a grouse, stop. Many hold still (Tip).
2. Look past the bird. A bird on a branch above you has only sky behind it: no shot with a rifle (Tip).
3. Rest the rifle. Safety off only when the crosshair is on the head (95%, safety rule).
4. Squeeze. Then safety on, unload, and walk in. In BC, no loaded firearm in or on a vehicle (99%, Rule Book item 28).
- Unloading the CZ: magazine out, open the bolt, check the chamber. The CZ lets you open the bolt with the safety on (95%, CZ manual).
```

## Ricochets and distance

```anim ff-rf-ricochet A low bullet skips off water and keeps going.
```

- A .22 can carry well over 1 km (65%).
- Low angles off water, rock or hard ground skip (Tip).

```more danger range in full
- One technical model of a .22 at 1,100 fps fired at 30 degrees estimated about 1,500 yd (1,370 m) of travel (65%, Shooting Times).
- Box warnings commonly say a .22 can travel over a mile; that figure is often quoted, not confirmed here (55%, VERIFY on your box).
- Tikka: never point a firearm at hard or flat surfaces (95%).
- Know what is past the grouse: a road, a cabin, a cow, your brother. See [zones of fire](#/s/firearm-safety/0).
```

## CZ 457 handling notes

| Topic | CZ 457 (manual) |
|---|---|
| Safety ON | Back, white dot showing, locks trigger |
| Bolt with safety ON | Opens. Unload with safety on |
| Dry fire | Not frequently. Use snap caps |
| Trigger | Standard: 8 to 15 N. Factory set 11.1 to 14 N |
| Scope | 11 mm dovetail or Weaver rail |

- All rows: CZ 457 manual (95%).

```more more from the manual
- Comfort trigger option: 5 to 7 N, factory 6 N (95%).
- Do not set the trigger too light or too short; check by cycling the bolt and safety on an empty rifle. The rifle must not fire (95%).
- Stock screws: 3.5 Nm (newton metres) (95%).
- Magazines and barrels interchange with the CZ 455 (95%).
- Mounting the scope and sighting in "should be entrusted to a qualified gunsmith's workshop" per CZ (95%). Many owners do it themselves with care (Tip).
```

## Clean the .22

```diagram ff-rf-clean
```

- Rod in from the chamber end (95%).
- Brush all the way through before you reverse (95%).

```more cleaning in full
- CZ: clean "each time you use it," if wet, as soon as possible after shooting, at least once a year (95%).
- Few shots: clean patches until one comes out clean. Very dirty: solvent and brush, let it act about 10 minutes, scrub, dry (95%).
- "The cleaning rod and the brush must be inserted into the barrel from the chamber, to prevent wear or damage to the muzzle" (95%).
- Do not use bore cleaner on other parts: it can sit in joints and cause corrosion (95%).
- More: [cleaning, maintenance, cold and transport](#/s/firearm-care/0).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Grouse on a high branch, sky behind | Shotgun, or let it go |
| .22 for quail | Shotgun only for quail |
| Body shot on a grouse | Head or upper neck |

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The little gun has a long reach. Treat it like the big one.

```more the mistakes in full
- Shooting a bird on a branch with nothing behind it but sky.
- Taking the .22 on a quail trip. Rimfire is legal for grouse, ptarmigan and turkey only among game birds.
- Body shots that ruin meat.
```

> [!field]
> Before grouse season walks: three 5 shot groups at 25 m and 40 m from a knee rest. If they fit a loonie, you are ready (Tip).

> [!why]
> Most fines are for simple things: a round in the chamber in the truck, a shot from the road, a missing tag.

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

- Every rule below has a certainty %. Under 80% means: read the official text before you rely on it.
- Most rules here were read in search previews of official pages. Capped at 85%.
- Seasons, bag limits and fees live in the Rules tab, not here.

## Licences: what to carry

| Hunting | Carry |
|---|---|
| Deer | Photo ID, your [[FWID]] (Fish and Wildlife ID) number, every deer species licence for the year (85%) |
| Ducks and geese | Photo ID, FWID number, federal [[MGBHP]] (Migratory Game Bird Hunting Permit), paper or phone (85%) |
| Quail and grouse | Photo ID, FWID number. No species licence for residents (80%) |

- The BC hunting licence is electronic only. Be able to give your FWID number (85%).
- Carry your [[PAL]] (Possession and Acquisition Licence) any time you have a firearm. Tip: habit, not a hunting rule.
- Full detail: Phase 1 Session 2, Licences and tags.

## Legal hunting hours

| Game | Earliest | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| Deer, quail, grouse (all game except migratory birds) | 1 hour before sunrise | 1 hour after sunset |
| Ducks and geese | 30 minutes before sunrise | 30 minutes after sunset |

- Big game and upland hours: 85%. Ducks and geese hours: 85%.
- Ducks get **less** light than deer. Check both times every morning.
- Sunrise and sunset change by town. Use a local table, not a city 200 km away. Tip.

## "Loaded" in BC

- BC meaning: live ammunition in the **breech (chamber) or the magazine**. A clip with live rounds attached to the firearm counts as the magazine (85%).
- So a full magazine in the rifle with an empty chamber **is loaded** in BC (85%).
- Federal "unloaded": no cartridge in the chamber and none in a magazine attached to or inserted in the firearm (85%).
- Loose rounds in your pocket or a detached magazine: not loaded (80%, my reading of both definitions).

> [!law]
> No loaded firearm in or on a motor vehicle, railway car, sleigh, aircraft, bicycle or other conveyance. No shooting from one either (85%).

## Truck, ATV and boat

- **Truck:** magazine out, chamber empty before the firearm goes in the truck (85%).
- **ATV (all terrain vehicle):** same rule. It is a motor vehicle or conveyance. Unload before you get on (80%).
- Engine off is not enough. Get off the [[ATV]], step away, then load (80%). Federal rules also say load only where shooting is lawful (75%, secondary).
- **No shooting wildlife from a motor vehicle or a boat propelled by a motor** (85%).
- The 10 ft boat with motor: no deer shot from it, ever (85%).
- Kayak: no motor, so the BC motorboat rule does not apply. Ducks: see the duck screen. Deer from a kayak: VERIFY. Tip: do not.

## Roads

- No shooting **across** a road (85%).
- No hunting or shooting on the road allowance of a public road (80%).
- Road allowance: 15 m (16 yd) each side of the middle of a road with under 3 lanes. Or 15 m from the pavement edge on 3 lanes or more. Or to private or cultivated land, if closer (85%).
- Which roads count (for example a [[FSR]], Forest Service Road): VERIFY in the synopsis.
- Safe habit: walk at least 15 m off the road, then load. Tip.

## The 100 m rule

> [!law]
> No hunting or shooting within 100 m (109 yd) of a church, school building, school yard, playground, regional district park, or a dwelling house, farm or ranch building occupied by people or domestic animals (85%).

- "Occupied" includes animals in a barn (85%).
- 100 m is close. A rifle bullet travels kilometres. Use your rangefinder on buildings. Tip.
- Owners can slaughter their own livestock near their buildings. That exemption is not for hunting (85%).
- **Your brother's house on the 20 acre property counts.** No shot within 100 m of it, even with his permission (80%, my reading: the only owner exemption is livestock slaughter).
- Barns or sheds with animals inside count too (85%).
- Field job: rangefind the house from each likely stand. Mark a 100 m ring on your phone map. Tip.

## No Shooting Areas and closed areas

- A No Shooting Area: firing a firearm is banned, even outside hunting (85%).
- They are on the regional maps in the [[Synopsis]] (85%).
- Region 3 synopsis has Kamloops area maps with No Shooting and No Hunting Areas (80%). Exact boundaries near Heffley Creek: VERIFY.
- Some towns and regional districts add their own discharge bylaws. Sun Peaks and Heffley Creek: VERIFY.
- Check the map **before** you go, not on the slope.

## Parks and protected land

| Place | Hunting? |
|---|---|
| Ecological reserve | **No.** No hunting, no firing a firearm or bow (85%) |
| BC park | Only in parks listed for hunting, only in open season (80%) |
| BC park road | No shooting within 400 m of the centre line of a park road (80%) |
| Wildlife Management Area | Rules differ by area. VERIFY each one |
| First Nations reserve | Private land. Permission from the band office to hunt on **or cross** it (85%) |

- Park closed to hunting: you may not even carry a firearm there, unless a park officer allows it (80%).
- Check each park page on bcparks.ca before you go (80%).

## Private land and the Trespass Act

- Hunting private land needs the owner's consent (85%). Get it **in writing**. Tip.
- Under the Trespass Act, "enclosed land" includes land with a lawful fence, a natural boundary, or signs (85%).
- Signs must be at each normal access point and readable in daylight (85%).
- On enclosed land you are presumed to have **no** permission. You must prove you do (85%).
- Cultivated land (crops, hay, orchard): treat as no hunting without permission (70%). VERIFY wording.
- A "No Hunting" sign may not be a trespass sign, but respect it anyway (65%, secondary).

## Motor vehicle closures

- Motor Vehicle Closed Areas (old name: Access Management Areas) ban some or all vehicles, or only ATVs or snowmobiles (85%).
- Motor Vehicle Hunting Closed Areas ban using a vehicle to hunt, carry hunters, gear or game (85%).
- Region 3 maps: [gov.bc.ca/thompsonaccess](https://www.gov.bc.ca/thompsonaccess) (80%).
- Areas near Heffley Creek and Sun Peaks: VERIFY on the map.
- Fire season closures and gates also apply. Check BC Wildfire Service. Tip.

## Banned methods

| Method | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rimfire on big game | **Not allowed** (80%) |
| .22 rimfire on grouse or quail | VERIFY in synopsis firearm table |
| Lights or spotlights | **Never** hunt with a light (80%) |
| Night hunting | Banned outside legal hours (85%) |
| Drones | No drone on a hunting trip. No drone info from under 6 hours before (85%) |
| Electronic calls | Banned, except wolf, coyote, cougar, lynx, bobcat and some listed birds (85%) |
| Dogs for deer | Leash only (80%). Grouse and quail: unleashed dog OK (80%) |
| Feeding or baiting deer | Banned within 200 m of a home, school yard or playground in Regions 3 and 8 (80%). Bait for deer elsewhere: VERIFY |
| Electronic firing mechanism | Not allowed (80%) |

## Ducks and geese (federal)

- Non toxic shot only for ducks, geese, coots and snipe. No lead (85%).
- Shotgun no larger than 10 gauge (85%).
- Holds **3 shells max**, chamber plus magazine. Plug must need the gun taken apart to remove (85%).
- No hunting from a **moving** boat with a motor or sail (85%).
- OK when the boat is beached, anchored or tied off (80%).
- A motorboat may chase a wounded bird to retrieve it (85%).
- Possession limit: 3 times the daily limit, at all times, **including at home** (85%).
- Daily limits for 2026 to 2027: VERIFY in the Rules tab.

## Ducks: the bird in your cooler

- Unpreserved birds keep **one fully feathered wing or the fully feathered head** attached (85%).
- Store birds so each can be counted and identified. No frozen block of ducks (80%).
- "Preserved" means fully processed for storage, for example frozen and plucked. Exact definition: VERIFY.
- Tip: leave a wing on until the duck is in your home freezer.

## Deer: tag and evidence

- Notch the species licence right after the kill, before you move the animal (85%). See Phase 1 Session 13.
- Evidence of sex stays **naturally attached** until the meat is home, at a cutter, in cold storage, or inspected (75%, secondary).
- Buck: the part of the head with antlers, **or** testicles or penis plus the tail or a patch of hide (75%, secondary).
- Doe: the part of the head where antlers would be, **or** udder or teats plus the tail or a patch of hide (75%, secondary).
- Antler restricted season (like 4 point): keep antlers attached to part of the skull (75%).
- Exact hide patch size: VERIFY.

## Meat wastage

- You must remove the **edible portions** (85%).
- Deer edible portions: neck, ribs, all four quarters and the loins (85%).
- Game birds (ducks, quail, grouse): both breasts (85%).
- Leave guts and bones at the kill site, or take them to a landfill (85%).
- You may give the animal to someone who then takes the edible portions out (85%).

## CWD (Chronic Wasting Disease)

- [[CWD]] is confirmed in the Kootenay Region (85%).
- CWD Management Zone: [[MU]] (Management Unit) 4-1 to 4-8 and 4-20 to 4-25 (85%).
- In that zone: mandatory CWD test for every deer, elk and moose, within one week (85%).
- No brain or spinal column (tail OK) taken out of that zone (85%).
- Region 3 and Region 8 are **not** in the zone (85%, from the MU list).
- Bringing cervid (deer family) parts in from outside BC is restricted (65%, secondary). VERIFY before any hunt outside BC.
- Deer urine lures from outside BC: may be banned (60%). VERIFY. Tip: use none.

## Transporting firearms (federal)

- Unloaded while in a vehicle (85%).
- Non restricted rifle or shotgun: no case or trigger lock needed while you travel (85%).
- Leaving it in the vehicle: lock it in the trunk or a locked compartment. No trunk: out of sight in the **locked** vehicle (85%).
- No special permit to carry a non restricted firearm. Your PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence) is enough (85%).
- Ammunition can travel with it, just not in it (80%).

## Giving away meat, and helpers

- Anyone carrying your game without you needs a **Record of Receipt** (85%).
- Record of Receipt shows: date and place, your name and address, who receives it, your FWID (Fish and Wildlife ID), the species licence number, species and sex (85%).
- Your brother hunts too. He needs his **own** hunting licence, species licences and federal bird permit (85%).
- Each hunter notches only his own licence, for the animal he killed. Shooting a deer for the other person's tag: VERIFY. Treat as not allowed.
- Ducks: each hunter has his own daily limit. Do not pool birds. Tip.
- Carrying his deer while he is not with you: you need his Record of Receipt (85%).
- Friends you give meat to at home: write a short Record of Receipt anyway. Tip.
- Selling game meat: VERIFY. Treat as not allowed.

## Wounded animal on closed or private land

- The Trespass Act has no exception for tracking wounded game (70%, secondary).
- Posted or fenced land: ask the owner first. Written permission is best (70%).
- Owner says no, or can't be found: call a conservation officer. Tip.
- Animal ran into a park or No Shooting Area: do not fire there. Call a conservation officer. Tip.
- You must still make a real effort to recover wounded game (70%). VERIFY the exact rule.

## Reporting poaching

- RAPP (Report All Poachers and Polluters): 1 877 952 7277 (85%).
- Cell: #7277 (75%).
- Open 24 hours, 7 days. You can stay anonymous (85%).
- Note: plate, vehicle, place, time, what you saw. Never confront. Tip.

> [!rule]
> If you have to ask "is this legal?", the answer today is no. Find out at home, then come back.

> [!mistake]
> - A full magazine in the rifle in the truck. That is loaded in BC.
> - A quick shot from the road or the ATV seat.
> - Freezing ducks with no wing or head attached.

> [!field]
> Before each trip: open the synopsis map for your MU. Mark No Shooting Areas, closures and buildings on your phone map.

```checklist s5-rules
Magazine out and chamber empty before the truck or ATV
Walk 15 m off the road before loading
Rangefinder check on any building inside 150 m
100 m ring around my brother's house marked on my map
No Shooting Areas checked on the synopsis map
Motor vehicle closures checked for my MU
Written permission for any private land
RAPP number saved in my phone
```

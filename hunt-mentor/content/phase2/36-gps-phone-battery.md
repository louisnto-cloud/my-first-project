> [!why]
> Your phone is your map, your track home and your SOS. Cold, a bright screen and a hunt for cell signal can kill it by noon. Set it up right and it lasts the day.

## Key points

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| Download maps at home | GPS works with no signal, map tiles do not |
| Phone in an inside pocket | Below 0°C it can shut off |
| Airplane mode in dead zones | No battery wasted hunting for signal |
| Screen dim and off | Biggest single drain (Tip) |
| Waypoints, not all day tracks | Track recording keeps the screen awake |
| Power bank warm, cable packed | Lithium batteries hate charging in the cold |
| Map and compass anyway | Phones fail |

```more the key points in full
1. GPS needs no cell signal. Map tiles do, so save the area offline first (95%; Hunt Map Offline Maps, 99% app feature).
2. Phone GPS is typically within about 4.9 m (16 ft) in open sky, worse near trees, buildings and bridges (90%, gps.gov).
3. iPhones are designed for 0 to 35°C. In the cold the battery can drain fast and the phone can shut off; it recovers when warm (95%, Apple support).
4. Lithium ion batteries should not be charged below freezing (75%, Battery University).
5. The Hunt Map keeps the screen awake while recording a track, and iPhone web apps cannot record with the screen off (99%, read in the app).
6. iPhone 14 or later can text emergency services by satellite in Canada with no cell or wifi, with a clear view of the sky (95%, Apple support).
7. Carry a paper map and compass every time (Tip).
```

## Why GPS is sometimes off

```diagram fn-gps-sky
```

- Open sky: about 5 m (90%).
- Thick timber, cliffs and canyons: worse. Wait for the dot to settle (Tip).

```more GPS accuracy in full
- "GPS enabled smartphones are typically accurate to within a 4.9 m (16 ft.) radius under open sky." Accuracy worsens near buildings, bridges and trees (90%, gps.gov accuracy page; the site notes it was last updated February 2025).
- Accuracy also depends on satellite geometry, signal blockage, the atmosphere and the receiver (90%, gps.gov).
- The Hunt Map shows the accuracy: the blue circle around your dot, and Go & Track says "GPS good (8 m)" or "GPS is weak" and skips weak points (99%, app).
- Tip: stand still in the open for 30 seconds before you mark a waypoint you will need in the dark.
```

## Cold kills batteries

```diagram fn-bat-cold
```

- iPhone is designed for 0 to 35°C (95%).
- Below that it may shut off. Warm it and it comes back (95%).

```more cold in full
- Apple: iPhone and iPad are "designed for use where the ambient temperature is between 0º and 35º C" (95%, Apple support, read 2026-10-11).
- In cold conditions battery life can drop for a while, the device may turn off, charging may stop, and it returns to normal when it warms up (95%).
- Late October dawns near Kamloops are often near or below freezing (Tip; check the forecast).
- Fix: phone in an inside chest pocket, not the pack lid or a hip belt (Tip).
```

## Charging in the cold

```diagram fn-bat-charge
```

- Do not charge a frozen battery. Warm both first (75%).
- Keep the power bank inside your jacket too (Tip).

```more charging in the cold in full
- Battery University: lithium ion can be fast charged from 5 to 45°C; below 5°C reduce the current, and no charging at freezing temperatures, because metal lithium plates on the anode and permanently harms the battery (75%, BU 410).
- Most phones protect themselves and slow or stop charging when cold (95%, Apple says charging may stop).
- Practical: warm the phone and bank against your body for 15 minutes, then charge (Tip).
```

## Set up the phone at home

```steps Five minutes the night before
fn-ph-setup-1 | Save the hunt area in Offline Maps.
fn-ph-setup-2 | Charge phone and power bank to 100%.
fn-ph-setup-3 | Turn the brightness down.
fn-ph-setup-4 | Airplane mode where there is no signal.
fn-ph-setup-5 | Low Power Mode on.
```

- Then mark the truck as a waypoint before you walk (Tip).

```more setup in full
- Airplane mode stops the phone searching for a cell tower. GPS keeps working (85%, Maps 2). Turn it off on a ridge if you need to send a message.
- Low Power Mode reduces background app refresh, automatic downloads, mail fetch and some visual effects (65%, carrier support guides; check Apple's page).
- Close apps that use location in the background (Tip).
- Turn off what you do not need on the hunt: Bluetooth, wifi (Tip).
- Write your truck's coordinates on paper too (Tip).
```

## A battery budget

```diagram fn-bat-budget
```

- Plan to have 30% left when you start the walk out (Tip).

```more battery budget in full
- Your phone's numbers depend on model, age and cold. Test it: one Saturday, log the percent every 2 hours (Tip).
- Biggest savings: screen off while glassing, phone warm, airplane mode in dead zones (Tip).
- Reserve 30 percent for the walk out, a call from a ridge, and a satellite SOS (Tip).
- Older batteries hold less. iPhone shows Battery Health in Settings, Battery (90%).
```

## Track or waypoints?

```diagram fn-bat-track-wpt
```

- A recorded track keeps the screen awake all day (99%).
- Waypoints cost almost nothing (Tip).

```more tracks and waypoints in full
- Hunt Map Go & Track: "Keep the screen on. iPhone web apps cannot record with the screen off." The app keeps the screen awake while you record; dim it and carry a battery pack on long days (99%, app text).
- Go & Track shows Back to start: distance and heading to where you began, with a green dot at the start (99%, app). Great on the walk out.
- Battery smart plan (Tip): drop waypoints at the truck, the glassing seat and the bench. Record a track only for the last part, or when the walk is new and confusing.
- Waypoints: Tools, Waypoint, At my location; or press and hold the map (99%, app).
```

## Satellite SOS

```diagram fn-sos-sky
```

- iPhone 14 or later, iOS 16.1 or later in Canada (95%).
- Needs open sky and horizon, no cell or wifi (95%).

```more satellite SOS in full
- Apple Emergency SOS via satellite works in Canada on iPhone 14 or later; Canada needs iOS 16.1 or later (95%, Apple support, read 2026-10-11).
- It works only where there is no cellular or wifi coverage, outside with a clear view of the sky and horizon (95%).
- It texts emergency services and shares your location with elevation, battery level, Medical ID and emergency contacts if set up (95%).
- Free for two years after activating an eligible iPhone (95%). After that: VERIFY.
- Try it in Demo mode at home so you know the screens (Tip).
- Two way texting with family needs a satellite messenger or a paid plan. See [Bush safety](#/s/bush-safety/0).
```

## Backups: two is one

```diagram fn-nav-backup
```

- Every layer covers the one above it failing (Tip).

```more backups in full
- Phone with offline map and a track or waypoints.
- Power bank and the right cable, both warm.
- Paper map of the block in a zip bag, truck and bench marked.
- Compass with 15 east set. See [Compass mastery](#/s/compass-mastery/0).
- Satellite SOS on the phone, or a messenger. A trip plan with your brother or family (see Bush safety).
- Headlamp on lithium AA or AAA cells: Energizer lists its Ultimate Lithium AA from minus 40 to 60°C (75%, maker's sell sheet). Alkaline cells weaken in the cold (Tip).
```

## When the phone dies

```diagram fn-ph-fail-flow
```

- STOP first. Then warm, charge, or go to map and compass.

```more when the phone dies in full
- STOP: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan. See [Bush safety](#/s/bush-safety/0) (90%).
- Warm the phone inside your jacket for 15 minutes; it may come back (95%, Apple: battery returns when warm).
- No luck? Orient the paper map, find a handrail (road, creek) and aim off toward the truck. See [Compass mastery](#/s/compass-mastery/0).
- Truly lost: stay put. "If you become lost or disoriented, the best thing you can do is stay put." (75%, AdventureSmart and Whistler Search and Rescue guidance, search preview)
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram hf-nav-kit
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Treat the phone like your water. Ration it, keep it warm, and never drink the last of it until you are on the road home.

> [!mistake]
> - Phone in the pack lid at minus 5°C.
> - Recording a track all day with the screen bright.
> - Maps not downloaded, no signal at the trailhead.

```more the mistakes in full
- Phone in the pack lid or hip belt at minus 5°C. It reads 40 percent, then shuts off.
- Recording a track from 5 am with the screen bright. Dead by noon.
- Maps not downloaded. No signal at the trailhead and a blank grey screen.
```

> [!field]
> One cold morning, put a phone in your chest pocket and an old phone in the pack lid. Note both every hour. Now you know your phone.

```checklist fn-battery-night
Offline area saved and checked in airplane mode
Phone 100%, power bank 100%, cable packed
Lithium cells in the headlamp, spares in the pack
Truck coordinates written on paper
Trip plan left with someone
Satellite SOS demo tried once
```

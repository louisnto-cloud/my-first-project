> [!why]
> Hungry, thirsty hunters get cold, slow and careless. A pocket of snacks and a planned drink schedule keep your head clear for the shot that matters.

## Key points

```diagram fl-fw-clock
```

- Something to eat every 2 to 3 hours (Tip).
- Fat has 9 calories a gram, carbohydrate and protein 4 (95%).
- Treat all stream water (95%).

```more the key points in full
- Hiking with hunting gear is rated 9.5 METs (Metabolic Equivalents); deer hunting 6.0; stand hunting 2.5 (95%, 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities).
- Rough calories = METs x body weight in kg x hours (85%).
- Energy factors: fat 9 kcal per gram, protein 4, carbohydrate 4, alcohol 7 (95%, FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization) food energy report).
- Hunters often struggle to eat enough on hard days and forget to eat (65%, outfitter and boot maker blog). Set a snack timer (Tip).
- Weigh before and after a training hike: 1 kg of weight lost is about 1 L of fluid not replaced (65%, sports nutrition summaries).
- Boil water at least 1 minute (2 minutes above 2,000 m) or use 2 drops of household bleach per litre and wait 30 minutes (95%, HealthLink BC File 49b).
- Keep perishable food at 4 °C or colder; the danger zone is 4 °C to 60 °C; no more than 2 hours out (95%, HealthLink BC).
- Do not consume alcohol or drugs while hunting (99%, synopsis ethics code, page 77).
```

## How much energy does hunting take?

```diagram fl-fw-kcal
```

- Walking hills with a pack: about 760 kcal an hour for an 80 kg hunter (Tip, estimate).
- A sit in a blind: about 200 an hour (Tip, estimate).

```more the numbers and the maths
- MET values, read directly (95%, Compendium codes): 04085 stand hunting with limited walking 2.5; 04100 hunting, general 5.0; 04080 deer, elk, large game 6.0; 04125 hiking with hunting gear 9.5; 04081 dragging a carcass 9.8; 04110 pheasants or grouse 6.0; 04090 duck hunting, wading 2.5.
- Calories = METs x kg x hours (85%). For 80 kg (Assumption):
  - Stand: 2.5 x 80 x 1 = 200 kcal an hour.
  - Deer hunting: 6.0 x 80 = 480 an hour.
  - Hiking with gear: 9.5 x 80 = 760 an hour.
  - Dragging: 9.8 x 80 = 784 an hour.
- A day of 4 hours hiking with gear and 6 hours of slower hunting: about 760 x 4 + 400 x 6 = 5,440 kcal (Tip, estimate). Real numbers vary a lot.
- You will not eat all of that back on the day. That is fine for one or two days; eat well in camp at night (Tip).
- Your weight and speed: see [Fitness for hunting](#/s/hunt-fitness/0).
```

## Eat little and often

```diagram fl-fw-clock
```

- Breakfast before the walk in. Snacks every 2 to 3 hours (Tip).
- Eat on the sit, quietly, before you get cold (Tip).

```more timing in full
- Big meals make you sleepy and slow on a climb. Small and often keeps energy steady (Tip).
- Snack before a big climb, not at the top (Tip).
- Eat before you get cold: your body makes heat from food (Tip, see [Hypothermia, frostbite and cold weather](#/s/cold-injuries/0)).
- Quiet food: unwrap snacks at the truck into a soft bag, so you do not crackle wrappers on a sit (Tip).
- Example day times: legal light starts about 1 hour before sunrise; near Heffley Creek that is about 6:22 am on 15 October (90%, see [Weather, daylight and season timing](#/s/daylight-weather-bc/0)). Breakfast before that.
```

## What to pack: energy per gram

```diagram fl-fw-density
```

- Fat gives the most energy for the weight you carry (95%).
- Mix: carbohydrate for quick energy, fat and protein to last (Tip).

```more food choices in full
| Food | Good for (Tip) | Note (Tip) |
|---|---|---|
| Nuts, trail mix | Dense energy | Easy to eat with gloves |
| Cheese, salami | Fat and protein | Keeps for a day unrefrigerated in cold weather |
| Jerky | Protein | Salty, drink with it |
| Bars | Quick energy | Pick ones that do not freeze rock hard |
| Sandwich or wrap | Real lunch | Eat it first, it squashes |
| Chocolate | Quick and fat | Fine in cold weather |
| Instant soup, cocoa | Warmth | Thermos or stove |

- Energy factors: fat 9, carbohydrate 4, protein 4 kcal per gram (95%, FAO).
- Salt: you lose it in sweat. Salty snacks help on hot early season days (Tip).
```

## One day of food

```diagram fl-fw-menu
```

- Pack the day's food in one bag the night before (Tip).

```more planning a camp weekend
- Two days at camp, two people: plan each meal, then add one spare day of simple food in the truck (Tip).
- Hot supper every night: warms you before bed (Tip, see [Hunting camp](#/s/hunting-camp/0)).
- Food storage at camp: locked in the truck or hung, never in the tent (85%, WildSafeBC).
- Bring a little more than you think. Cold days burn more (Tip).
```

## How much water?

```steps Find your own sweat number
fl-fw-sweat-1 | Weigh before a training hike.
fl-fw-sweat-2 | Count every bottle you drink.
fl-fw-sweat-3 | Weigh after: 1 kg lost is about 1 L.
fl-fw-sweat-4 | Add them up: that is your hourly need.
```

- Your number beats any general rule (Tip).
- Start the day already drinking: half a litre at breakfast (Tip).

```more water in full
- Sweat rate method: weigh before, track drinks, weigh after; weight lost plus fluid drunk is roughly your sweat loss (65%, sports nutrition summaries of the ACSM (American College of Sports Medicine) approach).
- 1 kg of body weight lost is about 1 L of fluid (65%).
- Do this on two training hikes: one cool, one warm (Tip).
- On a hunt: carry enough for the day plus a way to treat water (Tip).
- Region 3 grassland and dry forest can have no water at all on a ridge. Carry it (Tip, see [Water: finding it and making it safe](#/s/water-safe-drinking/0)).
```

## Pee check

```diagram fl-fw-pee
```

- Pale: good. Dark yellow: drink now (60%).
- A rough guide, not a lab test (60%).

```more colour in full
- Pale yellow suggests enough fluid; dark yellow or amber suggests you need to drink (60%, sports nutrition article).
- Some vitamins and foods change the colour (Tip).
- Few trips to pee all day is another sign you are behind (Tip).
```

## Water in the cold

```diagram fl-fw-cold
```

- Bottles freeze in November. Insulate them (Tip).
- A warm drink at noon lifts body and mood (Tip).

```more cold water tips in full
- Kamloops in November: average daily maximum 5.8 °C, average minimum minus 1.1 °C, about 16 nights at or below 0 °C (95%, Environment Canada normals 1991 to 2020, Kamloops A).
- Store bottles upside down so ice forms at the bottom first, not in the lid (Tip).
- Keep one small bottle inside your jacket (Tip).
- A thermos of soup or cocoa at the noon sit is a morale tool (Tip).
- Hydration bladder hoses freeze first. Blow the water back into the bladder after each sip, or use bottles (Tip).
```

## Treat every stream

```diagram fl-fw-treat
```

- Boil 1 minute, or bleach 2 drops a litre and wait 30 minutes (95%).
- Cattle range: treat everything (Tip).

```more treatment in full
- Boil at least 1 minute; above 2,000 m (6,500 ft), at least 2 minutes (95%, HealthLink BC File 49b).
- Household bleach (about 5.25%): 2 drops (0.1 mL) per litre, cover, wait 30 minutes; a slight chlorine smell should remain (95%).
- Cloudy or colder than 10 °C: double the bleach and wait 1 to 2 hours (95%).
- Cloudy water: strain through a cloth or coffee filter and let it settle first (95%).
- Filters: jug filters are not built for pathogens; use a device certified to kill or remove them (95%, HealthLink BC, NSF certification).
- Boiling is the most effective way to disinfect water, especially for Giardia and Cryptosporidium (95%).
- Full lesson: [Water: finding it and making it safe](#/s/water-safe-drinking/0).
```

## Food safety at camp

```diagram fl-fw-danger
```

- Cold food: 4 °C or colder (95%).
- Danger zone: 4 °C to 60 °C. No more than 2 hours (95%).

```diagram fl-fw-cooler
```

```more food safety in full
- "Keep your fridge set at 4 °C (40 °F) or lower" (95%, HealthLink BC food safety).
- Germs grow rapidly between 4 °C and 60 °C, the danger zone (95%).
- Do not leave meat, dairy or cooked leftovers out more than 2 hours (95%).
- Cook to an internal 74 °C (165 °F) or hotter for leftovers and mixed dishes (95%).
- Cooler (Tip): ice on top, raw meat sealed at the bottom, ready food apart, cooler in shade, melt water drained.
- Wild game meat care: [Meat care](#/s/meat-care/0). Cooking temperatures for game: [Cooking wild game](#/s/cooking/0).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram fl-fw-mistakes
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): A hungry hunter shoots in a hurry. Eat a bite, sip some water, then decide.

> [!mistake]
> - Waiting until hungry or thirsty.
> - Drinking straight from a creek in cattle country.
> - One frozen bottle and nothing else.

```more the mistakes in full
- By the time you feel hungry or thirsty you are already behind. Use a snack timer (Tip).
- Streams can carry Giardia and other germs. Treat every one (95%, HealthLink BC).
- Bottles freeze. Insulate, store upside down, carry a thermos (Tip).
```

> [!field]
> On your next training hike, do the sweat test: weigh before, count every sip, weigh after. Write your litres per hour in the journal. Then pack the next hunt day's water to match.

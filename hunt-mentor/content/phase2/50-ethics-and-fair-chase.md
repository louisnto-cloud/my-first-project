## Key points

```diagram fl-eth-code
```

- Shoot only with a clear shot at a vital part (99%).
- Make all reasonable effort to recover wounded game (99%).
- Report an accidental kill promptly (99%).

```more the key points in full
- "It is extremely important for hunters and trappers to realize that they use a public resource!" (99%, synopsis page 77).
- "Strive to make every kill a clean one ... do not shoot unless you have a clear shot of a vital part of the animal. Remember: 'you can't pull the bullet back.'" (99%, page 77).
- "No person shall kill, cripple or wound game without making all reasonable effort to locate, dispatch, retrieve and include it in their bag limit." (99%, page 12, Retrieval).
- It is unlawful to kill or wound wildlife by accident and fail to promptly report it to an officer (99%, page 11, item 18).
- Fair chase: "the ethical, sportsmanlike, and lawful pursuit and taking of any free-ranging wild game animal in a manner that does not give the hunter an improper or unfair advantage" (90%, Boone and Crockett Club).
- Unfair advantage: "when the game does not have reasonable chance of escape" (90%, Boone and Crockett Club).
- Drones, aircraft information, lights, infrared optics and most recorded calls are banned on a hunt in BC (99%, page 11).
```

## The BC ethics code

> [!warn] Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram fl-eth-code
```

- Safety, respect for wildlife, meat care, pack out, no alcohol, permission, report, be a mentor (99%).

```more the code, word for word (synopsis page 77)
- "Safety for yourself, your companions, and the public is the most important aspect of hunter training."
- "Have respect for the sensitivities of others. Do not display your kill or firearms where it may offend other people."
- "Respect wildlife and the sanctity of the harvested animals' lives. Strive to make every kill a clean one. Prepare your firearm in advance of, and during, the season to ensure accuracy and dependability. Practice your marksmanship at a licensed firing range, and do not shoot unless you have a clear shot of a vital part of the animal. Remember: 'you can't pull the bullet back.'"
- "Prepare the meat properly. Proper handling of meat is easy to learn and to not do so is both illegal and wasteful."
- "Respect the environment by packing out everything you pack in!"
- "Do not consume drugs or alcohol while hunting or trapping."
- "Respect private property. Always seek permission from landowners and thank them for the opportunities given. Unless explicitly stated, permission granted once does not entitle you to access the property on subsequent occasions."
- "Please respect gates, fences, and equipment as they are there for a reason. Leave everything the way you found it."
- "Be the eyes and ears for the enforcement agencies and report those who break the law."
- "Be aware that you are a mentor when youth are observing."
- "Respect your fellow hunters. Work with one another to promote good relationships, safety, knowledge and a fair distribution of opportunity and harvest."
- "Lead by example, you are an ambassador for the public and media image of hunting."
- All quotes: 99%, BC Hunting and Trapping Regulations Synopsis 2026 to 2028, page 77.
```

## Fair chase

- Fair chase: lawful, sporting, and the animal keeps a real chance to escape (90%).
- Technology should not replace hunting skill (90%).

```diagram fl-eth-tech
```

```more fair chase in full
- Boone and Crockett Club: fair chase is "the ethical, sportsmanlike, and lawful pursuit and taking of any free-ranging wild game animal in a manner that does not give the hunter an improper or unfair advantage over the game animals" (90%, read directly).
- A fair chase hunter "defines 'unfair advantage' as when the game does not have reasonable chance of escape" (90%).
- A fair chase hunter "uses technology in a way that does not diminish the importance of developing skills as a hunter or reduces hunting to just shooting" (90%).
- In BC, many fair chase lines are also law. See the next screens.
```

## Shoot or pass?

```diagram fl-eth-shot
```

- Six yes answers, or no shot (Tip).
- Any doubt is a no (Tip).

```more the six questions in full
1. Legal here and now: open season, your MU (Management Unit), legal hours, not a No Shooting Area (99% that each is law; see [Is it legal to shoot?](#/s/legal-to-shoot/0)).
2. Sure what it is: species, sex, antler points for the season (99% that shooting the wrong class is unlawful).
3. Safe backstop: earth behind it, nothing and no one beyond (Tip, see [Firearm safety](#/s/firearm-safety/0)).
4. Clear vital shot: "do not shoot unless you have a clear shot of a vital part" (99%, page 77). See [Shot placement](#/s/shot-placement/0).
5. Inside your tested range: the distance you proved on paper (Tip, see [Shooting skills](#/s/shooting-skills/0)).
6. Steady and calm: rested, breathing slowed, solid rest (Tip).
```

## Six hard calls

```steps Six hard calls, with a lean
fl-eth-case-1 | A buck running flat out.
fl-eth-case-2 | Last light, edge of thick timber.
fl-eth-case-3 | 400 m away, you tested 200.
fl-eth-case-4 | A hit deer crosses into posted land.
fl-eth-case-5 | One deer behind another.
fl-eth-case-6 | It was the wrong animal.
```

- Each lean is opinion. The law parts are marked (Tip).

```more each call in full
1. **Running buck.** Lean: let it go, or wait for it to stop. A whistle or a bleat sometimes stops a deer (Tip). Running shots wound more often (Tip).
2. **Last light.** Legal hunting for big game ends 1 hour after sunset (99%, page 11, item 38). You may not use a light to hunt (99%, item 38). Lean: if you could not trail it before full dark, pass. Rules on tracking with lights: [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing/0).
3. **Beyond your range.** Lean: close the distance or pass. Never a "hope" shot (Tip).
4. **Into posted land.** The Trespass Act has no exception for tracking wounded game (99%, see [BC rules you must know](#/s/bc-rules/0)). Get the owner's permission, or call a conservation officer (Tip).
5. **One behind the other.** The bullet can pass through. Wait until yours stands clear (Tip).
6. **The wrong animal.** Stop, do not hide it, and report promptly. Item 18 covers accidental kills (99%); my reading is that the honest path for a wrong animal is the same report (80%). VERIFY with the conservation officer you call. It is also unlawful to make a false statement to an officer (99%, item 3).
```

## Wounded game

> [!warn] Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```steps Wounded game in 5 steps
fl-eth-wound-1 | Watch it go. Mark two spots.
fl-eth-wound-2 | Wait before you follow.
fl-eth-wound-3 | Trail it slowly. Mark the last blood.
fl-eth-wound-4 | Lost it? Grid search, get help.
fl-eth-wound-5 | Found: finish, tag, it counts.
```

> [!law] Retrieval, page 12
> "No person shall kill, cripple or wound game without making all reasonable effort to locate, dispatch, retrieve and include it in their bag limit."

```more wounded game in full
- Plain English: you must try hard to find it, finish it, bring it out, and count it in your bag limit (99%).
- Wait times by hit, reading blood, grid search: [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing/0).
- Big game: cancel your licence immediately after the kill and before handling it (99%, item 1).
- Into a park closed to hunting or a No Shooting Area: no shooting there; call a conservation officer (99% and Tip, see [BC rules you must know](#/s/bc-rules/0)).
- After every reasonable effort and no animal: write down what happened and what you will change (Tip). Many hunters stop hunting for the day after wounding an animal (Tip, opinion).
```

## Made a mistake? Report it

```diagram fl-eth-accident
```

- Accidental kill or wound: report promptly to an officer (99%).
- RAPP (Report All Poachers and Polluters): 1 877 952 7277 (99%).

```more reporting in full
> [!law] Item 18, page 11
> "To kill or wound wildlife by accident or to protect life or property and fail to promptly report the killing to an Officer."

- Plain English: if you kill or wound wildlife by accident, or to protect life or property, you must report it to an officer promptly (99%).
- RAPP line: 1 877 952 7277, any time (99%). The synopsis also lists rapp.bc.ca (99%, page 77).
- Wrong animal (wrong sex, too few points, wrong MU): report it the same way. My reading, 80%. VERIFY.
- Never move or hide it unless the officer tells you what to do (Tip).
- Honesty protects your licence, your name and other hunters (Tip).
```

## Technology: where BC draws the line

> [!warn] Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

```diagram fl-eth-tech
```

- No drones on a hunt. No hunting within 6 hours of a flight (99%).
- No lights, no infrared optics (99%).

```more the technology rules in full
- Item 30: no wireless camera for hunting from 1 August to 10 December, except in Region 1 (99%).
- Item 33: no hunting from an aircraft or within 6 hours of being airborne, other than a regularly scheduled commercial flight (99%).
- Items 34 and 35: no hunting with, or sharing, wildlife locations found from an aircraft (99%).
- Item 36: no drone on a hunting expedition, and no use of drone information from during or less than 6 hours before the hunt (99%).
- Item 38: no hunting with the aid of a light or illuminating device (99%).
- Item 39: no infrared optics on a hunting expedition (99%).
- Item 41: no recorded or electronic calls except for wolf, coyote, cougar, lynx, bobcat, or Schedule C birds (99%).
- All: synopsis 2026 to 2028, page 11 to 12, 2 October 2026 edition. Full text: [Rule Book: it is unlawful](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0).
- Rangefinders, GPS (Global Positioning System) and map apps are not named as banned (85%, my reading). Fair chase asks you to keep building skill anyway (Tip).
```

## Respect after the shot

```diagram fl-eth-respect
```

- Take the edible portions out, every time (99%).
- Photos: clean, respectful, no spot details (Tip).

```more respect in full
- Edible portions of deer: neck, ribs, all four quarters and the loins (99%, see [BC rules you must know](#/s/bc-rules/0)).
- "Do not display your kill or firearms where it may offend other people" (99%, page 77).
- Photos: wipe the blood, tongue in, a respectful pose, no kilometre sign or landmarks in the frame (Tip, see [Hunter etiquette](#/s/etiquette/0)).
- Carcass and parts: not where people are likely to be (99%, synopsis page 15).
- Feeling sad or quiet after a kill is normal. Many hunters take a moment of thanks (Tip).
- Learn whose land you hunt on and respect it: [Whose land you hunt on](#/s/indigenous-respect/0).
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram fl-eth-mistakes
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The shot you pass on is the one you never have to explain.

> [!mistake]
> - A hopeful shot past your tested range.
> - Giving up a blood trail too early.
> - Gory photos with your spot in the frame.

```diagram lc-gr50-ethics
```

```more the mistakes in full
- Hopeful shots wound animals. Close the distance or pass (Tip).
- All reasonable effort to recover is the law (99%).
- Photos are public forever. Respect the animal, the landowner and other hunters (Tip).
```

> [!field]
> Before opening day, write your own three shot rules in the journal. At the range, test your max distance from a field position. On the hunt, say the six LISTEN checks in your head before you take the safety off.

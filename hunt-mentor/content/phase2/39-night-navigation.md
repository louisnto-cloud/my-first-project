> [!why]
> In late October, legal light ends about an hour after sunset, when it is already dark. Almost every deer hunt ends with a walk out at night. Plan it like a second hunt.

```diagram fn-nt-route
```

- Night routes are big and simple: handrail to catching feature (Tip).
- Prepare in daylight: waypoint, back bearing, lamp.

```more what this lesson adds
- Light times and turnaround: [Planning a route](#/s/route-planning/0). Compass tricks: [Compass mastery](#/s/compass-mastery/0). Phone and battery: [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery/0).
- This lesson: night eyes, lights and the law, the before dark routine, walking out, finding north by the stars, and what to do when unsure.
- Mid October at Heffley Creek: sunset about 18:08, legal light ends about 19:08, civil twilight ends about 18:41 (85%, my calculation).
```

## Key points

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| Waypoint the truck in daylight | The phone leads you back |
| Write the back bearing on your hand | Works when the phone dies |
| Pick a catching feature | The road you cannot miss |
| Unload at legal light end | No hunting after hours, no hunting with a light |
| Red light, low | Keeps your night eyes |
| Short legs on handrails | Hard to get lost |
| Stop when unsure | Night mistakes grow fast |

```more the key points in full
1. Big game and upland bird hunting ends 1 hour after sunset (99%, synopsis page 11, see the Rule Book).
2. It is unlawful to hunt with the aid of a light (99%). A headlamp to walk in or out is not hunting with a light (90%, Rule Book, my reading).
3. Following a wounded animal with a firearm after hours, or with a headlamp, is hunting outside hours and with a light (85%, Rule Book reading in [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing/0)).
4. Night vision takes 20 to 40 minutes to build and a few seconds of bright light breaks it down (90%, US National Park Service).
5. Dim deep red light preserves night vision (90%, same).
6. In the dark, use handrails and catching features, not long straight bearings (Tip, from orienteering).
7. Lost: stop, stay put, shelter, call (75%, AdventureSmart).
```

> [!warn]
> Study aid only. The official regulations are the law.

## Night eyes

```diagram fn-nt-adapt
```

- 20 to 40 minutes to see well in the dark (90%).
- A few seconds of bright light resets it (90%).

```more night eyes in full
- Rods in the eye use a pigment, rhodopsin, that builds up in low light. It starts after about 20 minutes and full benefit takes 20 to 40 minutes (90%, NPS, dark adaptation article).
- "A few seconds of bright light" breaks it down and the rods stop working until it rebuilds (90%).
- Older aviation guidance gives 20 to 30 minutes for useful dark adaptation (70%).
- Tip: on a clear night with snow or a moon you may not need a lamp on an open road. In timber you will.
```

## Red or white light

```diagram fn-nt-redwhite
```

- White for walking hard ground. Red, low, for maps and near your brother (Tip).
- Never shine a light in another hunter's eyes (Tip).

```more lights in full
- Deep red light does not break down rhodopsin. Bright red still can, so dim red is best (90%, NPS).
- Red light makes red ink on a map hard to see (65%, older aviation handbook).
- Headlamp habits (Tip): beam on the trail at your feet, not at the horizon; turn your head, not your whole body; warn before you switch to bright.
- Animals react to a moving light as pure motion (Tip). See [Deer senses and mind](#/s/deer-mind/0).
- Two lights each, plus spare lithium cells (Tip). Energizer lists its lithium AA from minus 40 to 60°C (75%).
```

## A swinging beam spooks game

```anim fn-nt-beam A moving beam across a slope moves deer. Keep it low and steady.
```

- Walking in before light? Point the lamp at your boots (Tip).
- Using a light to find or hold game is illegal (99%).

```more lights and the law
- "To hunt wildlife by the use of, or with the aid of a light or illuminating device" is unlawful (99%, synopsis page 11, item 38, as quoted in the Rule Book).
- That covers a spotlight, headlamp, weapon light or truck lights used to find, hold or shoot game (95%, Rule Book, my reading).
- Rule Book: [Legal methods](#/s/rb-legal-methods/0) and [Unlawful acts](#/s/rb-unlawful-acts/0).
```

## Before dark: the routine

```steps Five things before the light goes
fn-nt-before-1 | Waypoint the truck before you leave it.
fn-nt-before-2 | Write the back bearing on your hand.
fn-nt-before-3 | Pick the catching feature: the road.
fn-nt-before-4 | Check the lamp, spare cells, second light.
fn-nt-before-5 | Legal light ends: unload, walk out together.
```

- Do steps 1 to 4 at the truck, every time (Tip).

```more the routine in full
- Truck waypoint: Tools, Waypoint, At my location, icon Parking (99%, Hunt Map).
- Start Go & Track at the truck if the route is new. Back to start gives distance and heading the whole way (99%). Battery plan: [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery/0).
- Write the bearing from your hunting spot back to the road or truck on the back of your hand (Tip).
- Unload at legal light end: chamber and magazine empty before walking out, and empty before the firearm goes in or on the vehicle (99% for the vehicle rule, Rule Book item 28; walking out unloaded is Tip).
- Walk out together. Agree a meet point if you are split up (Tip).
```

## Walking out: big, simple, short

```diagram fn-nt-route
```

- Walk to a handrail, follow it to the catching feature (Tip).
- Longer is fine. Lost is not.

```more walking out in full
- In the dark, prefer a creek, a road, a fence, a cutblock edge or a ridge over a long straight bearing (Tip, orienteering handrails).
- Shorter legs: check the compass and the phone more often (Tip).
- Count paces on legs where you have no handrail (75%, see Compass mastery).
- Aim off when heading for the road so you know which way to turn (85%).
- Slow down on steep ground: red slopes in Slope steepness are 30 to 45 degrees (99%, Hunt Map). Check it before dark.
- Stay away from cliff edges (touching contours) and creek banks in the dark (Tip).
```

## Back to start on the phone

```diagram fn-nt-back
```

- Go & Track: Back to start gives distance and heading (99%).
- Walk that heading with the compass, phone back in the warm pocket (Tip).

```more back to start in full
- Back to start shows something like "1.9 km, heading 214° (SW)". The green dot marks the start (99%, Hunt Map).
- Waypoint only, no track? Open the truck waypoint and use Measure from your location to read distance and bearing (Tip; Measure shows the bearing of the last leg, 99%).
- A heading on the phone is from true north. Your compass set to 15 east also reads true (90%).
- Weak GPS under timber? Wait for "GPS good" before you trust it (99% app wording; Tip).
```

## North from the stars

```diagram fn-nt-polaris
```

- Line from Merak through Dubhe, about 5 times the gap: Polaris (90%).
- Polaris sits about 51° up at Heffley Creek, due north (85%).

```more stars in full
- "Draw a line from Merak through Dubhe, and go about five times the Merak/Dubhe distance to Polaris." (90%, EarthSky)
- Polaris climbs higher as you go north, overhead at the pole, on the horizon at the equator (90%, EarthSky). Its height in degrees is about your latitude: about 51 degrees at Heffley Creek, 49 at Mission (85%, standard astronomy).
- Polaris marks true north, within about 1 degree (85%).
- A backup only. Clouds are common in October (Tip).
```

## Not sure where you are?

```diagram fn-nt-flow
```

- STOP. Lamp off a minute. Think.
- Lost for real: stay put and call. Do not walk down blind draws (Tip).

```more lost at night in full
- STOP: Stop, Think, Observe, Plan (90%, Bush safety).
- "If you become lost or disoriented, the best thing you can do is stay put." (75%, AdventureSmart guidance via search and rescue page)
- Shelter from wind, insulate from the ground, fire only if safe and allowed (Tip; check fire bans, see [Bush safety](#/s/bush-safety/0)).
- Satellite SOS or messenger: open sky, clear horizon (95%, Apple). See [GPS, phone and battery](#/s/gps-phone-battery/0).
- Your trip plan's call time brings help to the right place (75%).
- Tip: walking downhill in the dark to "get out" often ends at a cliff or creek in a steep draw.
```

## Shot near dark?

```diagram sk-wait-chart
```

- Mark the spot, the line and the time. Then follow the rules for after hours (Tip).

```more late shots in full
- Mark where you stood, where the animal was, and the direction it went with waypoints (Tip).
- After legal light, trailing with a firearm or a light is hunting after hours and with a light (85%, Rule Book reading). See [Blood trailing](#/s/blood-trailing/0) for what you can do and who to call.
- Tip: a late evening shot means a night walk with meat. Pack the headlamp, rope and game bags every time.
```

## Grandpa's rule and mistakes

```diagram fn-nt-before-2
```

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Learn the walk out in daylight. The dark only lets you use what you already know.

> [!mistake]
> - No truck waypoint and no back bearing.
> - Blasting a white lamp at your brother's eyes, then at the hillside.
> - Cutting straight down a dark draw to "save time".

```more the mistakes in full
- No truck waypoint and no back bearing. The phone dies at 18:50 and nothing else knows the way.
- Blasting a bright white lamp around: your night eyes are gone, and every animal on the slope sees it.
- Cutting straight down a dark draw instead of walking the road. Draws end in creeks, cliffs and blowdown.
```

> [!field]
> Pick a 1 km walk you know. Walk it at last light with only a red lamp and your compass. Your brother walks behind with the phone and says nothing unless you leave the route.

```checklist fn-night-out
Truck waypoint saved
Back bearing written on hand
Catching feature named out loud
Headlamp, second light, spare cells
Unloaded at legal light end
Walked out together
```

## The 10 day sprint, starting now

```diagram ff-pp-sprint
```

- Two short range days, eight dry nights (opinion).
- Do not change anything new before the hunt (Tip).

```more the sprint in full
| Day | Do | Rounds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm zero at 100 m, 3 shots, cold barrel | 3 to 6 |
| 2 to 4 | Dry fire 10 min: sticks, sitting, get downs | 0 |
| 5 | Range: 1 cold plate shot, then 3 at 150 m, 3 at 200 m from sticks | 7 to 10 |
| 6 to 8 | Dry fire 10 min. Pattern the shotgun once if ducks are in your plan | 0 rifle, 5 shotgun |
| 9 | Gear check. Inspect and wipe every round. Scope screws marked | 0 |
| 10 | Rest. Pack. Sleep early | 0 |

- Do not switch loads, scopes or rings this close to the hunt. Each change needs a new zero (75%).
- If the 200 m group is bigger than 20 cm from sticks, your limit this season is 150 m (opinion, from the [range test](#/s/shooting-skills/0)).
```

## Three pillars

```diagram ff-pp-pillars
```

- Dry fire: most of the reps, free (opinion).
- Range: confirms with real recoil (opinion).
- Field: teaches real ground (opinion).

```more why the mix matters
- Dry fire builds the trigger press and position habits with no ammunition (Tip). Tikka says use a snap cap (95%). CZ says it does not recommend frequent dry firing and to use snap caps made for it (95%).
- Range days add recoil, noise, real wind and real distance (Tip).
- Field practice adds slopes, grass, cold hands and a racing heart (Tip).
- A rough ratio that works: 5 dry sessions for every live session (opinion).
```

## Safe dry fire, every time

```steps Set up dry fire in 4 steps
ff-pp-dry-1 | All ammunition out of the room.
ff-pp-dry-2 | Prove it unloaded. Snap cap in.
ff-pp-dry-3 | Aim at a safe wall, never a window.
ff-pp-dry-4 | 10 minutes. Say finished. Lock it away.
```

> [!warn]
> Dry fire is only safe with zero ammunition in the room. Every session.

```more dry fire rules in full
1. Every round out of the room, magazines and pockets empty (Tip, from [shooting skills](#/s/shooting-skills/0)).
2. [[PROVE]] the rifle unloaded. Snap cap in the chamber. Tikka: "'Snap cap' cartridges with spring loaded 'primers' are ideal" (95%).
3. Aim at a spot on a wall that would stop a bullet, never at a window, door or person (Tip).
4. Ten minutes, then say "finished" out loud, snap cap out, rifle locked away before ammunition returns (Tip).
- Dummy rounds with empty primer pockets are good for loading practice but do not protect the firing pin (95%, Tikka manual).
- CZ 457: same idea, snap caps made for rimfire (95%, CZ manual).
```

## The dry fire menu

| Session | 10 minutes of | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| A | 10 presses each: prone, sitting, sticks | Crosshair still at the click |
| B | 10 get downs to sticks, timer on | Under 5 seconds |
| C | 10 NPOA checks, then 1 press each | Settles on target |
| D | Shotgun: 20 mounts to a corner of the room | Eye on the line, gun meets the eye |

- Rotate A, B, C, D (opinion).

```more the menu in detail
- A: watch the crosshair at the moment of the click. Any jump is the trigger finger or a flinch (Tip).
- B: from standing, rifle slung. Ends with the safety still on until you are on target (95% for the safety rule).
- C: natural point of aim drill from [positions and rests](#/s/field-positions/0) (80%).
- D: shotgun mounts, unloaded and proved. Eyes stay on a target, the gun comes to the cheek (Tip). See [shotgun mastery](#/s/shotgun-mastery/0).
```

## One range day

```diagram ff-pp-range-day
```

- Cold shot first. It is the only one a deer gives you (Tip).
- Write it all down before you leave (Tip).

```more the range day in full
1. **Cold shot (10 min):** first shot of the day, from sticks or sitting, at your usual hunting distance. Log it (Tip).
2. **Zero check (15 min):** 3 shots at 100 m from a rest. Adjust only if the group centre is off by more than about 2.5 cm (opinion).
3. **Positions (25 min):** the position ladder from the drills lesson (opinion).
4. **Distance (20 min):** 150 m and 200 m from sticks. Check your dope card numbers ([ballistics deep dive](#/s/ballistics-deep/0)).
5. **Shotgun or .22 (20 min):** clays, or .22 position practice at 50 m (opinion).
- Hunter education: confirm the zero from a solid bench with the forestock on a pad or sandbag; never rest the barrel itself (85%).
- Ranges near you: listed in [sighting in](#/s/sighting-in/0). Hours and fees: VERIFY before you drive.
```

```checklist ff-pp-range-bag
Rifle, bolt, magazine, snap cap
Hunting load, 20 rounds, same lot
Sticks and the pack you hunt with
Targets, 20 cm plates, stapler
Ear and eye protection
Spotting scope or binoculars
Journal and pencil
Rangefinder
Shotgun, plug in, shells, chokes and wrench
```

## Shoot the field, lawfully

```diagram ff-pp-field-course
```

- Only on a range or land where shooting is lawful and safe (95%).
- Walk between stations to raise your heart rate (Tip).

```more field shooting in full
- Shooting outside a range: you need a place where discharge is lawful and a safe [[Backstop]]. BC has discharge bylaws and No Shooting Areas (99%, [Rule Book: where you can and cannot hunt](#/s/rb-where-you-can-hunt/0)). If unsure, use a club range.
- Many clubs have field or silhouette courses. Ask at your club (Tip).
- Shoot in your hunting clothes, gloves and pack. Cold fingers change the trigger press (Tip).
- Steep ground: practise one angled shot each session if the range has a slope (Tip).
```

## The .22 as your cheap trainer

```diagram ff-pp-cost
```

- A .22 round costs about a fifteenth of a .308 round (65%).
- Same positions, same trigger habits, no recoil (Tip).

```more using the .22 in full
- Prices from the [ammo session](#/s/ammo-guide/0), checked 2026-10-01: .308 hunting $40.99 to $54.99 for 20, CCI Mini Mag .22 LR (long rifle) $13.79 to $16.99 for 100 (65%).
- Per shot: about $2.05 to $2.75 versus $0.14 to $0.17 (65%, arithmetic).
- Run the position ladder and get down drills with the CZ 457 at 50 m (opinion).
- No recoil means the .22 hides a flinch. Mix in a few .308 shots every session (Tip).
- More on the CZ: [the .22 rimfire](#/s/rimfire-22/0).
```

## Log it

```diagram ff-pp-log
```

- Date, position, distance, wind, result (Tip).
- 20 entries show your real pattern (Tip).

```more what the log tells you
- Low misses often mean a jerked trigger or a flinch (Tip).
- Misses left and right often mean wind or a bad natural point of aim (Tip).
- Cold shot results over a month are your truest number (opinion).
- Use the [hunt journal](#/s/hunt-journal-guide/0) or a page in a notebook.
```

## The full year

| Months | Focus | Live rounds a month |
|---|---|---|
| Jan to Mar | Dry fire 3 nights a week, .22 days | 0 to 20 .308 |
| Apr to Jun | Range twice a month, positions, clays | 20 to 30 |
| Jul to Sep | Field drills, range test, pattern, zero | 30 to 40 |
| Oct to Dec | Confirm zero, hunt, log every shot | 5 to 10 |

- Opinion: a plan, not a rule.

```more in season upkeep
- Confirm zero after any knock, fall or long drive, and after travel by air (Tip).
- Tikka: check scope mounts and screws as part of maintenance (95%). See [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0).
- After the season: write down what you missed and why. That is next year's practice list (Tip).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Only bench groups | Cold shot from sticks every range day |
| Ammo in the dry fire room | Out of the room, every time |
| New load a week before the hunt | Freeze your setup now |

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): Practise the shot you will take, not the shot you like.

```more the mistakes in full
- Shooting perfect bench groups and never one cold shot from sticks.
- Dry firing with a box of shells on the table.
- Changing load, scope or rings the week before the hunt.
```

## Key points

```diagram gr-rifle-parts
```

- Bolt: up, back, forward, down (95%).
- Safety ON locks trigger, sear and bolt on the Tikka (95%).
- Click, no bang: wait 1 minute (95%).

```more all key points
- The bolt action works in four moves: lift, back, forward, down. Lifting unlocks, back extracts, forward feeds, down locks and cocks (95%, Tikka manual).
- The barrel's rifling spins the bullet. Your 1:10 twist means one full turn every 10 inches (95%).
- The trigger lets the sear go, the firing pin hits the primer, the powder burns, the bullet leaves (85%).
- Tikka safety ON locks the bolt closed, the firing pin cocked, and the sear and trigger (95%, Tikka manual).
- A safety is a mechanical device. It is never a substitute for safe handling (95%, Tikka manual).
- Misfire: keep it pointed safe and wait at least one minute before unloading with great care (95%, Tikka manual).
- Action screws: 7 Nm (newton metres), 62 in lb, with a metal trigger guard. Max 5 Nm (44 in lb) with a plastic one (95%, Tikka manual).
- Changes in feel, sound or function: stop shooting, see a gunsmith (95%, Tikka manual).
```

## The bolt action in four moves

```anim ff-rm-bolt-cycle Up, back, forward, down. Then safety on.
```

- Up unlocks. Back pulls the case out. Forward feeds. Down locks (95%).
- Run the bolt hard and full length. Short strokes jam (Tip).

```more the cycle in full
1. **Lift the handle (up).** The locking lugs turn out of their seats in the receiver. Lifting also cocks the firing pin: the cocking piece moves back as you lift (95%, Tikka manual).
2. **Pull back.** The extractor claw drags the fired case out and the ejector flips it out the ejection port (85%).
3. **Push forward.** The bolt face strips the top round from the magazine and pushes it into the chamber (95%, Tikka manual).
4. **Turn down.** The lugs lock. The rifle is cocked and ready to fire once the safety comes off (95%, Tikka manual).
- Tikka: "This rifle can have a live round in the chamber and can fire even if the magazine is empty or has been removed" (95%, Tikka manual). Magazine out never means unloaded.
- Short stroking: pulling the bolt only part way back. The case does not clear and the next round jams on it (Tip).
- Practise the cycle with snap caps or dummy rounds, never live ammunition, until it is smooth without looking (Tip).
```

## Inside the bolt

```diagram ff-rm-bolt-parts
```

- Lugs lock. Pin strikes. Claw extracts (85%).
- Do not strip the bolt for routine cleaning (95%).

```more bolt parts in full
- **Locking lugs:** at the bolt head, they turn into recesses in the receiver and hold the pressure of the shot (85%).
- **Firing pin and spring:** inside the bolt. Cocked when you lift the handle (95%, Tikka manual).
- **Cocking piece and shroud:** at the back. The cocking indicator shows when the pin is cocked (95%, Tikka manual).
- **Extractor and ejector:** pull the case out and throw it clear (85%).
- Tikka: "Generally, disassembling the bolt is not necessary." Strip it only if oil has got inside, which matters most in cold or harsh weather (95%, Tikka manual).
- Grease points: Tikka says keep the locking lug surfaces, cocking cam and head of the cocking piece lightly greased (95%). See [gun care](#/s/firearm-care/0).
```

## The firing chain

```anim ff-rm-fire-chain Six links, a few thousandths of a second.
```

- Trigger, sear, pin, primer, powder, bullet (85%).
- Follow through: keep pressing until the recoil ends (85%).

```more the chain in full
1. You press the **trigger**.
2. The trigger releases the **sear**, the catch that holds the cocked firing pin (85%).
3. The **firing pin** springs forward (85%).
4. It dents the **primer**, a small charge in the case head that ignites when struck (85%, hunter education).
5. The primer flash lights the **powder**. It burns fast and turns to expanding gas (85%, hunter education).
6. The gas drives the **bullet** down the barrel and out the muzzle (85%).
- The whole chain is over in a few thousandths of a second (Tip: order of magnitude, not a measured number).
- Anything that moves the rifle during that chain moves the shot. That is why hunter education teaches a slow, steady squeeze and follow through (85%).
- More on each part of the round: [how a cartridge works](#/s/cartridge-anatomy/0).
```

## The barrel

```diagram ff-rm-barrel
```

- Chamber, throat, bore, muzzle. Each matters (85%).
- A dinged crown can open groups. Protect the muzzle (Tip).

```more barrel parts in full
- **Chamber:** the cut at the back that holds the cartridge. Shaped exactly for .308 Winchester. The calibre is marked on the side of the barrel (95%, Tikka manual).
- **Throat:** where the bullet leaves the case and meets the rifling (70%).
- **Bore:** the inside of the barrel, with rifling (85%).
- **Muzzle and crown:** the last edge the bullet touches. Damage here can spoil accuracy (70%).
- Arctic barrel: 508 mm (20 inch), stainless (95%, Sako spec page).
- Threaded muzzle: some Tikkas have one. Keep the thread protector on when no brake or suppressor is fitted (95%, Tikka manual). Suppressor and brake law is not covered here: VERIFY before fitting anything.
- Obstruction check before loading: bolt out, look through from the back. Any obstruction is a gunsmith job (95%, Tikka manual).
```

## Rifling: lands and grooves

```diagram ff-rm-rifling
```

- Grooves are cut. Lands are the ridges between (85%).
- Land to land across the bore is the calibre (85%).

```more rifling in full
- "The spiral channels cut into a bore are the grooves, and the metal ridges between them are the lands. Together, the grooves and lands make up the rifling" (85%, hunter education study guide).
- Calibre: the barrel diameter measured land to opposite land (85%, hunter education).
- The bullet is a touch bigger than land to land, so the lands bite into it and force it to turn (70%).
- Shotguns have no rifling and are called smoothbores (85%, hunter education).
- Copper from bullet jackets can build up in the rifling. Tikka recommends removing copper fouling in periodic maintenance (95%, Tikka manual).
```

## Twist rate: 1 in 10

```anim ff-rm-twist One turn every 10 inches. About 3,400 turns a second.
```

- 1:10 means one full turn in 10 inches (95%).
- Faster twist suits longer, heavier bullets (70%).

```more twist rate in full
- Twist rate is the barrel length the rifling needs for one full turn. Lower second number = faster spin (70%, MeatEater).
- Your Arctic in .308: 1:10 per Sako's spec page (95%).
  - Note: the scope session lists 1 in 11 (80%). Sako's own page says 1:10. Trust the maker, and check the barrel stamp. VERIFY on your rifle.
- Common .308 twists: 1:10 and 1:12 (70%, MeatEater).
- Spin rate math: 2,820 fps (feet per second) × 12 inches per foot ÷ 10 inches per turn = about 3,400 turns a second, or about 200,000 rpm (revolutions per minute) (95%, arithmetic).
- Too slow a twist for a long bullet: it wobbles, keyholes (hits sideways) and groups open (70%).
- Factory rifles use twists that stabilise the common bullets for the cartridge (70%). A 1:10 .308 handles 150 to 180 gr (grain) hunting bullets (65%).

| Twist | Spin | Best with |
|---|---|---|
| 1:12 | Slower | Light to middle .308 bullets (65%) |
| 1:10 (yours) | Middle | 150 to 180 gr hunting bullets (65%) |
| 1:8 | Faster | Long, heavy for calibre bullets (65%) |
```

## The trigger

```diagram ff-rm-trigger
```

- Factory set about 3 lb (13 to 15 N, newtons) on Tikka (95%).
- Adjust range 2.5 to 4 lb. Never below 2.5 lb (95%).

```more the trigger in full
- Tikka: the trigger is adjusted at the factory and needs no adjustment. Factory weight 13 to 15 N (about 3 lb) (95%, Tikka manual).
- Adjustable 11 to 18 N (2.5 to 4 lb) with a 2.5 mm Allen key through the magazine well (95%, Tikka manual).
- "For safety reason do NOT attempt to reduce trigger weight below 11 N (2,5 lbs)" (95%, Tikka manual).
- Sako lists a **two stage trigger** on the Arctic (95%, Sako spec page). Two stage: light take up, then a firm wall, then the break.
  - Most T3x models have a single stage trigger. Dry fire yours with a snap cap to learn which you have (Tip).
- Do not make any other trigger adjustment. Changes in feel: gunsmith (95%, Tikka manual).
- Single set trigger (optional on some Tikkas, not the Arctic per Sako): when set, about 2 to 2.5 N, and any shock may fire it (95%, Tikka manual).

> [!lean]
> Leave the trigger at the factory setting your first season. Learn it with dry fire instead of changing it.
```

## The safety: Tikka versus CZ

```diagram ff-rm-safety
```

- Tikka: red dot showing = ready to fire (95%).
- CZ 457: bolt opens with the safety on. Tikka: it does not (95%).

```more the safety in full
- **Tikka:** the safety catch is beside the bolt. Back is ON. ON locks the bolt closed, the firing pin cocked, and the sear and trigger (95%, Tikka manual).
- The safety can only go ON when the firing pin is cocked (95%, Tikka manual).
- Red dot visible = safety OFF, ready to fire. No red dot = safety ON (95%, Tikka manual).
- Some Tikkas have a three position safety with a bolt release button. Sako lists a two position safety on the Arctic (95%).
- So on the Arctic, to unload you push the safety OFF with the muzzle pointed safe, then lift the bolt (95%, Tikka manual unloading steps).
- **CZ 457:** safety back shows a white dot and locks the trigger. You can pull the bolt out "even with the safety engaged" (95%, CZ 457 manual).
- The manual safety "is merely a mechanical device and is in no way a substitute for the Basic Safety Rules" (95%, Tikka manual).

> [!warn]
> Two rifles, opposite habits. Say it out loud when you switch: "Tikka: safety off to unload. CZ: safety stays on."
```

## Load check, step by step

```steps Prove the Tikka unloaded in 4 steps
ff-rm-check-1 | Muzzle safe, finger off. Safety off to work the bolt.
ff-rm-check-2 | Magazine out first.
ff-rm-check-3 | Bolt out. Look through chamber and bore.
ff-rm-check-4 | Bolt in, close, decock pointing safe.
```

- This is the Tikka manual's load check (95%).
- Same idea as [[PROVE]] in the safety lesson.

```more the manual's steps in full
1. Finger away from the trigger, barrel in a safe direction (95%).
2. Disengage the safety: push it forward, red dot showing (95%).
3. Remove the magazine with the magazine release button (95%).
4. Remove the bolt: lift the handle, press the bolt stop and pull it fully out (95%).
5. Check the chamber is empty. If not, follow "Unloading the rifle" (95%).
6. Put the bolt back and close it. The firing pin is now cocked. With the barrel pointed safe, decock by pulling the trigger (95%).
- Source: Tikka T1x and T3x instruction manual, v5.3, "Load check" (95%).
- Hand the rifle to your brother only with the magazine out, the bolt open and the chamber checked (95%, Tikka manual).
```

## Magazine and feeding

```diagram ff-rm-magazine
```

- Round on the follower, press down, slide back under the lips (95%).
- Never force an extra round in (95%).

```more magazines in full
- Load: hold the magazine, place a round on the follower in front of the lips, press down and slide it fully under the lips. Repeat (95%, Tikka manual).
- "Do not try to force more cartridges than the maximum capacity of your magazine" (95%, Tikka manual).
- Unload a magazine: thumb presses the rim forward, index finger tips the round up and out (95%, Tikka manual).
- Clean it: follower and spring out, wash in warm soapy water or solvent, lightly oil, reassemble (95%, Tikka manual).
- Arctic magazine capacity: 10 rounds per one retailer listing (65%). VERIFY with your magazine. Legal limits on magazine capacity: not covered here, VERIFY.
- Loaded means a round in the chamber or in an attached magazine. See [Rule Book: definitions](#/s/rb-definitions/0) (99%).
```

## Stock, recoil lug and screws

```diagram ff-rm-bedding
```

- Loose action screws move your zero (70%).
- Tikka: 7 Nm metal guard, max 5 Nm plastic guard (95%).

```more the stock and screws in full
- The recoil lug (a metal block) sits in the stock and takes the rearward shove of each shot (95%, Tikka manual: "metallic recoil block").
- When refitting the action: recoil block in place, pull the barrelled action back to take up slack, then tighten (95%, Tikka manual).
- Torque: 7 Nm (62 in lb) with metal trigger guards, max 5 Nm (44 in lb) with plastic ones (95%, Tikka manual).
- Tikka asks you to check receiver screw tightness during routine cleaning (95%).
- Length of pull spacers fit between stock and recoil pad. Too long a screw or over tightening can ruin the stock (95%, Tikka manual).
- Tip: a paint pen line across each screw head shows if one has turned. Same trick as the scope rings.
```

## Sights on the Arctic

```diagram gr-tikka-clearance
```

- Rear drum sight steps from 100 m to 600 m (95%).
- Open sights come zeroed at 100 m from the factory (95%).

```more iron sights in full
- Tikka open sights are factory adjusted to zero at 100 m (95%, Tikka manual).
- Arctic rear sight: six range steps, 100 m to 600 m, by rotating the drum (95%, Tikka manual).
- Windage: loosen the screw on the side you want the bead to move from, then tighten the other side. One click moves impact about 7 mm at 100 m (95%, Tikka manual).
- "Do not open the screw on the center of the rear sight drum" or spring parts fly out (95%, Tikka manual).
- Your rifle has a Picatinny rail for the scope (95%, Sako spec). Fit and clearance: [choosing and mounting your scope](#/s/scope-guide/0).
- Tip: learn the iron sights too. A fogged or broken scope on day 3 of a trip is real.
```

## Misfire: click, no bang

```diagram ff-rm-misfire
```

- Wait at least 1 minute, muzzle safe (95%).
- Then unload with extreme care (95%).

```more the misfire procedure
- Tikka: "If the rifle doesn't fire after pulling the trigger, wait at least one minute and unload the rifle with extreme care" (95%, Tikka manual).
- Why wait: a slow ignition (hang fire) can fire late (70%).
- Keep the round apart from your good ammunition. Dispose of it as your range or ammunition seller advises (Tip).
- A misfire is one of the "stop shooting" signs. Have the rifle checked (95%, Tikka manual).
- Never look down the muzzle of a loaded or doubtful rifle (95%, basic safety rules).
```

## Stop shooting signs

```diagram ff-rm-stop
```

- Any change in trigger, safety, sound or cases: stop (95%).
- Unload. Take it to a gunsmith or the maker (95%).

```more the warning list in full
Tikka says stop shooting immediately and see an authorised gunsmith if (95%, Tikka manual):
- the trigger mechanism works differently
- the rifle misfires
- the safety does not operate
- any cartridge malfunction: spitting gas, a punctured primer, a bulged or ruptured case
- the shot sounds different
- If the rifle is damaged while shooting, do not take it apart. Send it with an explanation, the case, and sample cartridges. Never ship a loaded firearm (95%).
- Warranty: 2 years, 3 if you register the rifle on the Tikka website (95%).
```

## Grandpa's rule and common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Safety on means safe | Muzzle and finger first, always |
| Short stroking the bolt | Full stroke, every time |
| Magazine out means unloaded | Look in the chamber |

> [!rule]
> Grandpa's rule (wisdom): The safety is a part. Safe is a habit.

```more the mistakes in full
- Trusting the safety. Tikka says plainly the safety is no substitute for the basic rules.
- Short stroking the bolt under pressure, so the empty case jams the next round.
- Pulling the magazine and calling it unloaded with a round still in the chamber.
- Bonus: mixing up the Tikka and CZ safety habits on the same trip.
```

> [!field]
> At home, with snap caps: 20 bolt cycles from the shoulder, eyes on a target, not on the rifle. Then a load check out loud.

## Memory hooks

```diagram ff-rm-hooks
```

- **UBFD:** Up, Back, Forward, Down.
- **Red is ready:** red dot means it can fire.
- **One minute, one click:** wait after a misfire.

```more more memory tricks
- **"Lands stand, grooves move":** lands are the raised ridges that stand up; grooves are the cut channels.
- **1 in 10 = 1 turn per 10 inches.** Lower number, faster spin.
- **"Seven for steel":** 7 Nm for a metal trigger guard. Plastic: 5 max.
- **"Two and a half, no less":** never set a Tikka trigger under 2.5 lb.
- **SAFE habits beat safety parts:** muzzle, finger, chamber, in that order.
```

# Image prompt pack (Gemini or ChatGPT)

Owner rule 2026-10-13 (see CLAUDE.md): photos and diagram art come from Gemini or ChatGPT; we add every label, number and arrow on top in SVG.

## How to use
1. Paste the STYLE block, then one prompt, into ChatGPT or Gemini (owner's Pro accounts, signed in browser).
2. Ask for 2 versions. Pick the better one.
3. Save as PNG named exactly like the "File" line, and upload it to the Claude session.
4. Claude checks it against the fact check line, adds labels in SVG, and shows a before and after. Nothing goes live without the owner's "go".
5. Reject any image with text, letters or numbers in it, wrong anatomy, or the wrong animal.

## STYLE (paste first, every time)
> Realistic field guide illustration, like a wildlife identification book. Soft natural colours, clean light cream background (#FFFAF0), even daylight, no harsh shadows. Accurate anatomy and proportions for the species. Landscape 4:3, 1600 x 1200 px. Leave clear empty space around the subject for labels. Absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, arrows, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

## Prompts (worst and most used first)

### 1. Deer vitals
- File: ai-vitals-deer.png (replaces art in vitals-deer)
- Prompt: Mule deer buck standing broadside, facing left, full body, legs straight. Semi transparent body so the lungs (pink, large, filling the chest above and behind the front leg) and the heart (dark red, low in the chest, just behind the front leg, between the elbows) are visible. Liver behind the diaphragm, brown. Nothing else inside shown.
- Fact check: heart low between the front legs; lungs above and behind it; aim point is up the back of the front leg, one third up the chest (85%).

### 2. Moose vitals
- File: ai-vitals-moose.png (vitals-moose)
- Prompt: Bull moose, spike fork antlers, broadside facing left, full body. Semi transparent body showing large pink lungs above and behind the front leg and a dark red heart low in the chest behind the front leg. Shoulder hump visible above the chest.
- Fact check: the hump is not the top of the chest; one third up is measured from the chest bottom to the spine.

### 3. Elk vitals
- File: ai-vitals-elk.png (vitals-elk)
- Prompt: Bull elk (Rocky Mountain elk), 6 points per antler, broadside facing left. Dark brown neck mane, pale buff body, cream rump patch. Semi transparent chest showing pink lungs and a dark red heart low behind the front leg.
- Fact check: aim 1/3 to 1/2 up the chest, behind the front leg (lesson value).

### 4. Black bear vitals
- File: ai-vitals-bear.png (vitals-bear)
- Prompt: Adult black bear standing broadside on all fours, facing left, near front leg stepped forward. Thick black fur. Faint outline of the true body under the fur. Pink lungs and dark red heart shown through the chest, low and just behind the front leg. No shoulder hump; straight face profile.
- Fact check: hair adds 8 to 10 cm (70%); wait for the near leg forward (85%). Must NOT look like a grizzly (no hump, no dished face).

### 5. Orchard and buildings (top view)
- File: ai-orchard-buffer.png (hf-orchard-buffer)
- Prompt: Bird's eye view, slightly angled, of a South Okanagan farm: rows of fruit trees on the right, a farmhouse, a metal roofed packing shed and small worker housing on the left, a gravel road along the bottom, dry grass and a few pines. Two fruit pickers with ladders in the orchard. Summer to early fall colours.
- Fact check: we draw the 100 m (109 yd) no shooting rings on top (99%); orchard needs permission (95%).

### 6 to 13. Field dressing, 8 steps (one consistent series)
Add to the STYLE block: "Same deer, same angle, same lighting in every image of this series. Clean, not gory; no blood pools. Gloved hands only, no faces."
- 6, ai-sk-gut-1.png: Deer lying on its back on a slight slope, head uphill, propped with rocks on both sides, legs up. Top view.
- 7, ai-sk-gut-2.png: Rear end close up of the same deer: a small circular cut around the anus with a knife, gloved hands.
- 8, ai-sk-gut-3.png: Same deer, belly up. Gloved fingers lift the belly skin while a short knife cuts edge up from the pelvis toward the breastbone.
- 9, ai-sk-gut-4.png: Belly open, the paunch and intestines rolled out to one side, still attached, intact.
- 10, ai-sk-gut-5.png: Inside view of the chest: the thin diaphragm wall along the ribs, and the windpipe high in the neck, both about to be cut.
- 11, ai-sk-gut-6.png: All organs pulled out together beside the deer, rectum pulled through the pelvis from inside; pelvis and breastbone NOT sawn.
- 12, ai-sk-gut-7.png: Empty body cavity wiped dry and propped open with two clean sticks.
- 13, ai-sk-gut-8.png: The carcass lying on logs in the shade of a big tree, off the ground, air moving around it, no tarp on top.
- Fact check: tail and sex parts stay attached (evidence of sex, Rule Book); cavity propped open; never a tarp on top; no direct sun.

### 14. Quail cover (South Okanagan)
- File: ai-quail-cover.png (hf-quail-cover)
- Prompt: Bird's eye scene of a dry South Okanagan valley edge: a brushy creek with wild rose and berry thickets, orchard rows, a grain stubble field, a dirt farm road with dust bathing spots, a covey of California quail running along the brush edge.
- Fact check: California quail males have a forward drooping black head plume.

### 15. Moose antler classes
- File: ai-moose-antlers.png (moose-antlers)
- Prompt: Three bull moose heads side by side, front three quarter view: (1) young bull with simple spike fork antlers, (2) bull with small palmate antlers and 3 points on the brow palm, (3) mature bull with wide palms and many points. Plain background.
- Fact check: counting rules come from the Rule Book; we add tine numbers on top.

### 16. Elk antler legal vs not legal
- File: ai-elk-antlers.png (elk-antlers)
- Prompt: Two bull elk heads side by side, side view facing left: left has 6 points on the near antler, right has 5 points on the near antler. Identical lighting and size.
- Fact check: 6 point legal, 5 point not legal; brow and beam tip count (Rule Book).

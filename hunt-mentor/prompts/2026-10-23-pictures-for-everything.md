# Prompt: a picture and a how to for every feature and skill (2026-10-23)

## Who it is for
- A first time hunter (Mission, BC; black tailed deer near Harrison) who learns best from pictures, not text.

## Owner ask
"I want diagrams and how tos for every single one. Very picture based, user friendly, easy to understand and learn."

## Assumption
- "Every single one" means: every app feature (each tool and screen) and every hunting skill the app teaches. Today 317 of 319 lessons already have at least one picture, but many screens inside them are text only, and the app features have no picture how to at all.

## Rules
- hunt-mentor/CLAUDE.md applies. Diagram rules: SVG, 400 wide, 14 px text minimum, ids prefixed by file name, aria label, numbered steps, big labels, few words, real proportions, Style A (field guide) or B (infographic), new design colours (spruce #1F3D2B, lichen #EEF1EA, blaze #FF6A13 only for the key action or safety, text #17201A).
- No new facts: every word on a diagram comes from the lesson or feature it explains. Rules keep their certainty.
- Tap to learn: add data-tip hotspots where they help.
- Check each picture at 360 px (render to PNG and look at it).

## Parts
1. **Picture how to for every app feature** (about 30): Home study card and pace, daily cards, hunt plans and Harrison presets, hunt week, Hunt ready, guide, map layer sets, What am I looking at?, search, tap card, Can I hunt here now?, Where can I hunt?, spots and camp cards, Mark my truck, legal light and turnaround chips, Save this hunt area for no signal, Steep ground, Help and tour, Safety (trip plan, turnaround, emergency card, bears and cougars, conditions), trainers and Shoot or don't shoot, After the shot, Read aloud, Field Mode, journal, backup and restore, Add to Home Screen.
   - Each: 3 to 5 numbered steps, one SVG picture per step showing the real button shape and where it is on screen, one line per step.
   - Shown in the guide (#/guide) and from a small "How to" button inside each feature.
2. **Picture for every text only screen in the must know path** (Harrison deer plan: Rule Book Region 2, Your Harrison deer hunt, tagging, shot placement, blood trailing, field dressing, meat care, safety and lost, the deer Masterclass hunt and shot chapters): add a diagram, step strip or labelled photo to each screen that has none, most important first.
3. **The new Black tailed deer Masterclass** gets diagrams on most screens.

## Done means
- Every feature has a picture how to; every must know screen has a picture; tested at 360 px; merged and published; a short report with counts before and after.

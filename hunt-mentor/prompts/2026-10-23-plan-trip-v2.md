# Prompt: hunt plan and trip, built to get the outcome (2026-10-23, version 2)

## Who it is for
- A first time hunter from Mission, BC. Rifle (Tikka T3x Arctic .308, iron sights). Black tailed deer near Harrison (MUs 2-18, 2-19, 2-8), with a brother or solo, a November weekend.

## Owner ask (in their words)
"Improve the plan, the trip section. Add more details, ask more questions, give more information that's live, so I can see the possible outcome. Give the quantity and probability of getting an animal at the top spots. Whatever it takes to be successful and actually get one there."

## The honest base (official BC harvest statistics, resident hunters, 2020 to 2024, synopsis deer, already in data/harvest)
| MU | Hunters a year | Deer a year | Success a season | Days per deer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-18 | 201 to 315 | 27 to 69 | 10 to 27% | about 16 to 46 |
| 2-19 | 114 to 191 | 8 to 27 | 7 to 16% | about 18 to 48 |
| 2-8 | 92 to 198 | 8 to 42 | 9 to 21% | about 22 to 69 |
- These are season averages over all hunters, all skill levels. They are the only official odds. Everything finer (a spot, a day, a hunter) is an estimate and must say so.

## Rules
- hunt-mentor/CLAUDE.md applies in full: sources and certainty on every number, Tips labelled, VERIFY when unsure, plain words, no em dashes, no hyphens in prose, 14 px text, 44 px taps, offline first, remove nothing.
- **Never a fake number.** Every probability shows: the official base, each adjustment with its reason and source, the range (low, likely, high), and "estimate" when it is not official. If data is too thin (low confidence rows, small samples), say so and widen the range.
- **Live means fetched when online and saved for offline:** shown with "as of" time; a stale or missing feed shows the rule and the official link instead.
- Picture first: each section gets diagrams (odds bars, day timeline, wind and approach arrows, spot cards).
- Safety and law before success: no tactic that breaks a rule; legal light, road allowance, private land and no shooting areas checked on every recommended spot.

## What version 2 adds over version 1 (the "make it even better" pass)
- Odds shown as **days per deer and chance per day**, not only per season, because the owner hunts a weekend.
- A **levers** panel: what changes the odds and by roughly how much, each with a source or labelled Tip (more days, rut dates, quiet spots far from roads, still hunting skill, a practised shot, a partner, scouting trips).
- **Backup plans** for rain, wind, road closures, other hunters at the spot.
- **After the hunt debrief** feeds the next plan (what was seen where, at what time, weather), so the app learns from the owner's own data.
- **Readiness gates** before the hunt: sighted in, licence and tag, area saved for no signal, trip plan sent: the plan shows "Not ready" until done.

## Parts
1. **Smarter questions (plan wizard)**: add, each optional with a skip: hunting experience; fitness and pack weight comfort; hours you can hunt each day; travel time limit from Mission; truck type (2 wheel or 4 wheel drive, clearance); camping or day trips; partner's experience and whether they will also carry a tag; rifle and sights (prefilled from My guns), longest shot you have practised; budget for the trip; what success means to you (see a deer, a shot opportunity, meat in the freezer). Every answer changes the plan; show how.
2. **Odds engine** (data/harvest): per MU and season: official success per hunter, deer per year, hunter days, days per deer; turned into chance per day and chance over the planned days (with the method shown), plus a 5 year trend and data confidence. Per spot: an **estimate** adjusting the MU base for distance from roads (pressure), habitat score, rut timing on the dates, and the owner's answers, each adjustment shown with its reason and certainty. Expected numbers: deer you might see per day (Tip, from the lessons' density and sign guidance only where sourced, else VERIFY), shot chances, and a realistic outcome range.
3. **Live conditions for the hunt dates** (offline cached): hourly forecast at each top spot (Open-Meteo: wind speed and direction, gusts, rain, temperature, freezing level), so the plan picks the approach that keeps your scent away; legal light each day; moon; rut phase from the Masterclass; official road notices (Chilliwack district page) and fire bans; snow line; "as of" times.
4. **Day by day, hour by hour plan**: for each hunt day: leave Mission time, drive time, parking, first light set up spot chosen by wind and thermals, midday move (still hunt or scout), evening sit, legal light end, turnaround, back at the truck; for pair and solo; with backup spots; printed and shareable trip plan.
5. **Top spots board**: the top 5 spots for this plan with odds estimate, access, pressure, wind fit for the forecast, legal checks passed, pack out estimate, nearest camp, "why this spot", and one tap to map, save offline, or swap.
6. **Readiness and debrief**: the readiness gates on the plan; after the hunt a short debrief form (what, where, when, weather, shots) saved to the journal and used to adjust the next plan's spot ranking (shown as "from your own trips").

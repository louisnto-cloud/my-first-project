# Prompt: "My guns": how to use, care for, fix and upgrade every gun (2026-10-23)

## Who it is for
- A first time hunter in Mission, BC. Learns best from pictures. Hunting black tailed deer near Harrison with a rifle; birds later.

## The owner's guns (confirmed 2026-10-23)
1. **Tikka T3x Arctic, .308 Winchester.** Iron sights only today, about 18 in barrel, "Canada version" (from PLAN.md). Budget noted earlier: about $10,000 total for gear.
2. **CZ 457, .22.** Variant and exact chambering (likely .22 LR): VERIFY with the owner; build for the CZ 457 family and flag variant differences.
3. **SKS, Chinese** (Type 56 family, 7.62x39 mm). Surplus rifle: condition unknown.
4. **No shotgun yet.** Teach shotguns fully and give a buying guide.

## Owner ask
Teach how to use each gun, strategies and safety; how to maintain, clean, take apart and put back together; what to update; how to make each better; guns to add. Great detail, lots of diagrams.

## Rules
- hunt-mentor/CLAUDE.md applies in full. Every fact sourced (maker manuals first: Tikka T3x manual v5.3 already cited in the app, CZ 457 manual, SKS military/armourer manuals and reputable references; RCMP Canadian Firearms Program; Firearms Act; storage, display and transport regulations; Criminal Code s. 84 and SOR/98-462 for magazines; BC synopsis for hunting rules) with certainty %; Tips labelled; VERIFY when unsure.
- Legal first: anything that could make a gun prohibited or illegal (magazine capacity, barrel length, stocks, alterations, the SKS 5 round centrefire semi auto limit, pinned magazines, suppressors, bayonets on hunting, transport) is stated with the law and certainty, and "ask a qualified gunsmith" where it applies. Never tell the owner to alter a gun in a way that changes its legal class.
- Safety first: every handling step starts with "point in a safe direction, finger off the trigger, check it is unloaded (ACTS and PROVE)".
- Picture first: every step of handling, loading, unloading, field stripping and reassembly gets a numbered SVG diagram of THAT gun's real parts (labelled, to scale where possible, design colours: spruce #1F3D2B, lichen #EEF1EA, blaze #FF6A13 only for safety or the key action, text #17201A; 400 wide; 14 px text min; ids prefixed; aria label; data-tip hotspots). Use Creative Commons photos (with credit) where they help.
- Gear prices: entry, mid and premium tiers, Canadian prices, "price checked" date (CLAUDE.md rule).
- Builds on the existing lessons (phase2 24-rifle-mechanics, 25-cartridge-anatomy, 30-shotgun-mastery, 31-rimfire-22, 32-firearm-care, 33-optics-mastery); link to them, do not duplicate.

## Parts
1. **Tikka T3x Arctic .308 Masterclass:** parts and controls; safety and bolt release; loading and unloading the detachable magazine; iron sights and zeroing; choosing and mounting a scope (rings, bases, eye relief, torque, lapping not needed), sighting in step by step; field positions and practice drills; ammunition for black tailed deer; cleaning routine, bore and action; bolt disassembly and reassembly; trigger (factory adjustment only, per manual); cold and wet weather care (Arctic in Harrison rain); upgrades worth it and not worth it; a range day plan.
2. **CZ 457 (.22) course:** controls; safety; magazines; loading and unloading; sighting in; practice games that make you a better deer shot; small game use and legal rules; cleaning (rimfire specifics); bolt and action field strip and reassembly; barrel swap system (what it is, the law, VERIFY the owner's variant); upgrades (scope, sling, suppressor is illegal in Canada).
3. **SKS (Chinese) course:** first, a safety inspection list for a surplus rifle before firing (firing pin free movement and slam fire risk, cosmoline removal, bore and chrome lining, headspace check by a gunsmith, bayonet, magazine pinned to 5 for hunting and the law); controls; loading with stripper clips and singly; unloading the fixed magazine safely; sights and zero; corrosive surplus ammunition and cleaning after it; full field strip (top cover, recoil spring, bolt carrier, bolt, gas piston, gas tube) and reassembly with pictures; is it a good deer rifle (7.62x39 at short range, Tip, sources); legal hunting use in BC; upgrades that are legal and those that are not.
4. **Shotguns, full course plus buying guide:** actions (pump, semi auto, over under), gauges, chokes, shot sizes and steel for waterfowl (federal rule), patterning, swing and lead, safety in a line of hunters and from a boat, grouse and duck strategies, cleaning and field strip by action type, buying guide for this owner (entry, mid, premium, Canadian prices, what to look for, try before buy).
5. **"My guns" hub** (new add on, route #/guns): a card per gun (photo or drawing, calibre, sights), quick links to its course chapters, a cleaning and maintenance log with reminders (rounds fired, last cleaned, next check), a range day checklist, storage and transport rules card, and "guns you might add later" (only as information with pros and cons, for example a shotgun for birds), with prices.

## Done means

- A report with sources and certainty, VERIFY items, and any questions for the owner (for example the CZ 457 variant).

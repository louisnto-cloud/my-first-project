## The one-line call

Grow Vietnam on a narrow path: one committed distributor structure with **Organika controlling registrations and trademarks**, official channels only, and a calendar built around Tet (first full Tet play is Tet 2028, 26 Jan 2028). **Lead with Enhanced Collagen and magnesium in a Vietnam pack; greens follow.** This changed on 24 Oct 2026, when a red team pass priced every SKU from real Canadian shelf prices: collagen lands below its Vietnamese band, the only line with room for full promo headroom; magnesium and greens in Canadian packs land at or above theirs. Full reasoning in the primer's READ THIS FIRST section.

## The four things to do first

1. **Settle the existing partner plan.** Organika already sells through Kat Vietnam Trading (organikacanada.vn). Confirm the agreement status and terms, the registration holder and the trademark position, and agree targets and official stores, before opening any other conversation.
2. **Secure the IP.** Registrations are not transferable; trademarks are first to file. File or reclaim before negotiating.
3. **Get real Vietnam COGS per SKU.** The SKU order, every target price and the max FOB depend on them (internal margin model).
4. **Fix your own grey market.** Hand carry sellers already ship Organika Enhanced Collagen from Canada. Plan Vietnam specific packs and a controlled price ladder from day one.

## The one-line call

Enter, on a narrow path: one committed distributor structure with **Organika controlling registrations and trademarks**, official channels only, **magnesium and greens ahead of collagen**, and a calendar built around Tet (6 Feb 2027; first full Tet play Tet 2028). Full reasoning in the primer's READ THIS FIRST section.

## The three things to do first

1. **Audit the incumbent.** An exclusive distributor already exists — Kat Vietnam Trading (organikacanada.vn). Establish its performance and whether it holds the registrations and the Organika trademark before opening any other conversation.
2. **Secure the IP.** Registrations are not transferable; trademarks are first to file. File or reclaim before negotiating.
3. **Fix your own grey market.** Hand carry sellers already ship Organika Enhanced Collagen from Canada. Plan Vietnam specific packs and a controlled price ladder from day one.

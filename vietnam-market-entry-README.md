# Vietnam Market Entry: Project Index

Research, planning, pitch and decision package on growing Organika in Vietnam's dietary supplement market. Prepared for **Louis To, Senior Sales Manager, Emerging Channels, Organika Health Products**.

**As of:** body research 17–18 July 2026; refreshed 23–24 Oct and 30 Oct 2026. The 30 Oct pass corrected Shopee's May 2026 fee wave (Mall take now ~25–27% before ads) and updated Long Chau to ~2,639 stores (Jun 2026). Refresh FX, prices and store counts before a live negotiation.

## The call

Grow Vietnam on a narrow path: one committed distributor structure, **Organika holding registrations and trademarks**, official channels only, and a calendar built around Tet 2028 (26 Jan 2028).

**Lead with Enhanced Collagen and magnesium in a Vietnam pack; greens follow.** Priced from real 2026 Canadian shelf prices, collagen models ~19% below its Vietnamese band, the only line with room for full promo headroom. Magnesium lands at its band ceiling; greens well above. Full reasoning: the primer's READ THIS FIRST page.

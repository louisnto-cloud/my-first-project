# CHAPTER 1: HOW THIS MARKET ACTUALLY WORKS

> **What you need to know**
> The market is worth about USD 0.9 billion a year under the analyst definition and USD 2.4–2.9 billion under the widest Vietnamese industry definition; the gap is definitional, not a dispute about growth. Growth is 8–15% a year depending on scope, the fastest in Southeast Asia. A foreign product legally reaches consumers only through a Vietnamese registration holder, which is why the distributor relationship is also a regulatory relationship. A large grey market (hand-carried goods) and a counterfeit industry exposed in 2025 shape every buying decision. Your products are legally "TPBVSK" (health protection food), the most regulated food class in Vietnam.

| 6. Consumer | — | Pays shelf price ≈ **2.0–2.5× landed cost** (computed from steps 3+5 — LOW, model inference, not a sourced multiple) | — | |

- **Where power sits:** (1) the registration holder — the registration is issued to the local filer and is **not transferable**; switching distributors means re-registering every SKU from scratch (Medibase 2026; Global Expansion Vietnam 2026 — HIGH that practitioner commentary consistently warns of this); (2) organized retail — modern trade + ecommerce claimed to be >55% of supplement retail (Asia Pro Distribution, 2026 — LOW-MEDIUM, single source); (3) platform ecosystems and KOL sellers (Metric 2025 — HIGH for the online shift).
- Foreign-invested enterprises may hold import/distribution rights for foods (unlike pharmaceuticals, where FIEs cannot distribute), but observed practice for supplements is still distributor-led entry (trade.gov Country Commercial Guide, ~2024 — MEDIUM; observed brand cases Ch4 — HIGH).
- Vietnam's trademark system is strictly **first-to-file**; law firms document local distributors registering foreign principals' marks as negotiating pressure (Harris Sliwoski 2023–25; KENFOX — HIGH). File Vietnamese trademarks before the first distributor meeting.

## 1.4 The three supply channels

**1. Official imports (chính ngạch = official-channel imports; chính hãng = genuine/official goods)**
- Registered with the VFA, Vietnamese supplementary label (nhãn phụ), duty and VAT paid, sold in chains/malls.
- The 2025 crackdown is pushing volume here: official "Mall" stores were only **2.12% of active shops on Shopee + TikTok Shop but took 32.6% of 2025 GMV** (Metric, 2026 — HIGH).

**2. Grey market (hàng xách tay = "hand-carried goods", plus unofficial parallel imports)**
- Mechanics: goods bought abroad (or claimed to be) resold without official import — avoiding duty, VAT, and registration cost — undercutting official stock; consumer folklore trusts goods "carried by flight attendants or relatives" as more authentic than shelf stock (VTV, 2023 — HIGH).
- Physical hubs exist: the Nguyễn Sơn street cluster (Long Biên, Hanoi) is known as the capital of hand-carried Korean/Japanese/European cosmetics and functional foods (VTV, 2023 — HIGH).
- Legal status: selling hand-carried goods without import papers is punishable as dealing in smuggled goods, fines to VND 100M (individuals)/200M (organizations) under Decree 98/2020 (statute — MEDIUM, not re-verified this session).
- **Share: no analyst or government body publishes a grey-market percentage — no reliable data exists.** Proxy: as of Q1 2025, only **22.6% of supplement ecommerce revenue on Shopee and 32.2% on TikTok Shop came from official stores** — i.e., >70% of online purchases were from unverified sellers (Metric via Dân Trí, May 2025 — HIGH). That unverified 70% mixes genuine grey goods and fakes.
- Why it persists: 15% MFN duty + VAT + registration cost gap (see Ch5.6), plus the origin-trust premium on "airport-bought" goods.

**3. Counterfeits**
- 2025 was the watershed: ~100 tons seized in the April 2025 MegaPhaco/MediUSA/MediPhar case alone; 88 products confirmed fake; market value >VND 539B; actives at <30% of declared content; Chinese raw materials labeled as US/EU imports (CAND, Tuổi Trẻ, VOV, 2025 — HIGH). Full case files in Chapter 4.
- MOH post-market review identified **67 counterfeit health protection foods and 675 substandard products**; ~750 SKUs recalled in the Herbitech case (vietnam.vn 2025 — MEDIUM; VnExpress 2025 — HIGH).
- Enforcement volume: >34,000 violations handled Jan–Apr 2025 (8,200+ smuggling, 25,100+ commercial/tax fraud, 1,100+ counterfeit/IP cases) (Vietnamnet, 2025 — MEDIUM-HIGH).
- **Counterfeit share of sales: no reliable quantified estimate exists** (LOW for any % you see quoted). The counterfeiters' favorite camouflage was fake "hàng xách tay US/EU goods" — so the crackdown hits the grey channel too (VTV 2023; case files 2025 — HIGH).

## 1.5 TPBVSK: the classification that decides your regulatory life

- **Thực phẩm chức năng (TPCN)** = "functional food," the umbrella term from the Law on Food Safety 2010, Art. 2.23 (HIGH, statute).
- **Thực phẩm bảo vệ sức khỏe (TPBVSK)** = "health protection food": products in **dosed formats** (capsules, tablets, pills, powders, liquids, small-unit doses) containing vitamins, minerals, amino acids, enzymes, probiotics, botanicals, used to maintain/enhance body functions and reduce disease risk (Decree 15/2018 definition — HIGH). **Every Organika product — collagen powder, greens, probiotics, magnesium, fibre, vitamins — is TPBVSK.**
- **Thực phẩm bổ sung** = "supplemented food": ordinary food fortified with micronutrients (fortified milk, biscuits, drinks) (HIGH).
- Why it matters — the routes diverge (operative regime, Decree 15/2018):

| | TPBVSK (you) | Supplemented food |
|---|---|---|
| Route | **Đăng ký bản công bố sản phẩm** — registration with pre-market review by the Vietnam Food Administration (VFA / Cục An toàn thực phẩm, Ministry of Health) | **Tự công bố** — self-declaration to local authority, market immediately |
| Statutory review | 21 working days (2–4+ months in practice — LOW, anecdote) | None |
| Foreign factory GMP evidence | Mandatory since 1 July 2019 | Not required |
| Advertising | Content pre-approval required + mandatory disclaimer | Lighter |
| Import inspection | Currently **exempt** from state inspection when registered (Decree 15/2018 Art. 13) | Tiered inspection |

- The self-declaration loophole for supplemented foods is what the 2025 counterfeit networks exploited, and closing it is the core of the pending reform (Vietnam Law Magazine 2025; TECOVA 2026 — HIGH). Sources: Decree 15/2018 text (FAO/faolex mirror); Medibase 2026; Long Châu explainer 2023–25 — HIGH.

## 2.6 Independent pharmacies: ~50,000 doors, still the volume channel

- Count: ~50,000 retail pharmacies (VDSC, Oct 2024 — MEDIUM); older figures 57,000 (Dream Incubator: "98.5% independent and small") and 62,500 (2019). Chains ≈ 4,500–5,000 outlets total in 2026 — under 10% of doors.
- Value share: independents hold roughly **75–85% of pharmacy retail value**, shrinking 2–4 points/yr (Ken Research/Chameleon estimates — MEDIUM; my synthesis of conflicting figures).
- How they buy:
  - **Wholesale hubs**: Hapulico / Hapu Medicenter (1 Nguyễn Huy Tưởng, Thanh Xuân, Hanoi) — 20,000 m², 5 floors, 200+ stalls of drugs, TPCN, cosmetics; the North's largest pharma wholesale market. Southern equivalent: Tô Hiến Thành market, District 10, HCMC (vnras.com; pharma360.vn, 2023–24, translated — MEDIUM).
  - **Distributor sales reps** — the dominant contact channel. Vietnam needs ~55 reps per USD 10M of sales vs 3 in the US (Dream Incubator, 2022 — MEDIUM, dated). Credit extended only after a purchase track record; general trade is "very credit-dependent" with long payment terms and bad-debt risk (FMCG trade sources — MEDIUM).
- Why they still matter: proximity, personal pharmacist relationships, price flexibility, willingness to sell Rx meds OTC. **The pharmacist behind the counter is the de-facto prescriber for supplements** — recommendation drives conversion and repeat (Ken Research 2025; Chameleon Pharma — MEDIUM-HIGH).
- 2025–26 squeeze: mandatory e-invoicing, tax tightening and the counterfeit crackdown are closing marginal independents and shifting share to chains (vietdata 2025 — MEDIUM).
- For Organika: unreachable without a national distributor rep force; hard to hold premium price integrity there. Phase-2 channel.

# CHAPTER 3: THE BRANDS, PROFILED

> **What you need to know**
> The brands an insider expects you to know split into four blocks: Australian/Western pharmacy brands run by exclusive Vietnamese distributors (Blackmores/Mesa, Doppelherz/Mastertran, Swisse/Grove), Japanese and Korean beauty-format brands (DHC, Orihiro, 82X, Lemona, KGC), a US value tier living mostly in the grey market (Kirkland, Nature Made, Puritan's Pride), and domestic manufacturers spanning credible listed pharma (Traphaco, DHG) to ad-driven contract-manufactured labels damaged by the 2025 scandals. # CHAPTER 5: THE RULES OF THE GAME (THE REAL 2026 REGIME)

> **What you need to know**

Your products (TPBVSK) already require pre-market registration with the VFA, held by your Vietnamese importer, and that registration is not transferable — the single biggest lock-in risk in your distributor contract. From 1 July 2026 supplement advertising approval moved to provincial authorities, every ad needs the exact not-a-medicine disclaimer, and KOLs carry personal statutory liability. Duty on Canadian supplements should be at or near zero under CPTPP but the current-year rate was not verifiable this session — confirm before quoting landed costs. ## 5.4 Advertising rules now in force

- **Amended Law on Advertising** (passed June 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026): statutory duties for "advertisement conveyors" (KOLs/KOCs/celebrities) — must have used or understood the product, verify the advertiser's legal documents, disclose paid content, bear joint administrative/civil/criminal liability; 24-hour takedown for violating online ads; organizations face criminal exposure from 1 Jan 2026 (Lexology 2025; Vietnam Business Law Oct 2025; VietnamNet 2025; Tilleke — HIGH).
- **Decree 342/2025/NĐ-CP (effective 15 Feb 2026)**: supplement ad content must state functions, applicable warnings, and the exact mandatory disclaimer: **"Thực phẩm này không phải là thuốc và không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh"** ("This food is not a medicine and cannot substitute for medicines to treat disease"). TV/radio spots under 15 seconds may display rather than voice it (thuvienphapluat; LuatVietnam; Tuổi Trẻ/PLO 2026 — HIGH).
- **Doctors/medical staff in ads: prohibited** — no images, names, uniforms, or letters of medical facilities, doctors, pharmacists, or medical staff in food advertising; VFA repeatedly warns against products advertised with doctors' images (Lexology; Vietnam Law Magazine; VFA — HIGH). Celebrities are not banned but carry conveyor liability (MEDIUM).
- **Provincial approval from 1 July 2026** (Resolution 21/2026/NQ-CP, 29 Apr 2026): TPBVSK ad-content confirmation moved entirely from VFA to provincial People's Committees; VFA ran nationwide training 10 June 2026; 16 of 19 MOH food-safety procedures decentralized (Báo Pháp Luật; VTV May 2026; VietnamPlus; Sức khỏe & Đời sống — HIGH). Execution risk: provincial consistency is untested (LOW — too early for evidence).
- Sanctions base: Decree 115/2018 (food safety; fines doubled for organizations) and Decree 38/2021 (advertising) — amounts and any 2025–26 replacement decree **UNVERIFIED this session**; criminal exposure from 1 Jan 2026 verified (HIGH).

# CHAPTER 8: THE BUYER'S LENS

> **What you need to know**
> Chain trade terms are genuinely unpublished — nobody's blog has Long Chau's margin sheet, and anyone who claims otherwise is guessing. What is documented: retailers are risk-averse and ask for listing fees; supermarket-type commissions run 15–30% plus extra fees for foreign brands; distributors expect 35–60% margin on landed cost and full control of compliance; initial import deals run on advance payment or 60–120-day letters of credit; and registration-before-listing is a hard gate. The most dangerous clause in your first contract is who holds the product registration. This chapter separates sourced fact from labeled inference — treat inferences as interview questions, not answers.

## 8.1 Retail category buyers (Long Chau, Pharmacity, platforms)

**Sourced:**
- Buyers screen hard for complete, legal import/registration paperwork — the tax/anti-counterfeit wave made documentation a listing precondition (Vietdata 2025 — HIGH). Long Chau additionally demands origin/traceability dossiers verifiable through its NIFC testing partnership (Tuổi Trẻ Apr 2026 — HIGH).
- "Vietnamese retailers are very risk-averse and may ask for a listing fee before they put a product on the shelves"; the fee is volume-independent and on top of margin; negotiating advertising/sampling in lieu of cash listing fees is the recommended counter (Exporteers, Vietnam-focused export consultancy — MEDIUM).
- Supermarket-type commission 15–30%, plus extra fees and taxes for foreign brands (Exporteers — MEDIUM; supermarket channel — pharmacy-chain schedules unpublished).
- Payment: initial deals — payment in advance or irrevocable confirmed L/C; "L/Cs up to 60, 90, or 120 days are most common in Vietnam"; open credit only after track record (US Country Commercial Guide ~2024 — HIGH).
- Modern-trade payment terms and listing/promo fees are described as "very severe on manufacturer margins" (FMCG trade commentary — LOW as Vietnam-specific).
- Long Chau/Pharmacity specific listing fees, margin thresholds, category review calendars: **no reliable public data exists.** Obtain via the Canadian Trade Commissioner Service (TCS) HCMC life-sciences desk and distributor interviews.

**Labeled inference (consistent with the sourced facts; verify in diligence):**
- What they weigh, in probable order: completed VFA registration (hard gate); margin at or above house norms; proof of demand (Metric/Shopee data, existing Mall store sales); committed marketing investment (launch A&P, pharmacist training, sampling); supply reliability (stock depth, lead times — you are shipping trans-Pacific); exclusivity or first-launch rights. (LOW-MEDIUM — inference.)
- Fast "no" triggers: no registration filed; price above the imported-premium ceiling with no headroom; single-container supply plans; no promo budget; asking the chain to build the brand. (LOW — inference.)
- Category review timing: no published calendar. Tet structurally concentrates the year (Decree 81's 30-days-before-Tet deep-promo window — HIGH), and Tet production planning starts early (Cosmo/VALO — MEDIUM thin); the widely-advised Sept–Nov sell-in window for Tet is **UNVERIFIED — general practice knowledge, LOW-MEDIUM.**

## 8.2 Import distributors

**Sourced:**
- The archetype deal (Mastertran/Doppelherz): one exclusive national distributor, decade-plus horizon, distributor runs registration, labeling, customs, reps, trade marketing; structured like a quasi-JV partnership since 2012 (GBA/EuroCham profiles — HIGH). - Unless a foreign company holds an investment license permitting direct distribution, it must appoint an authorized agent/distributor (trade.gov CCG — HIGH).
- The registration holder is the Vietnamese declarant; practitioner commentary warns explicitly: appoint an independent Responsible Company rather than the distributor "so you keep ownership of your registrations… and can switch commercial partners without regulatory disruption" (Global Expansion Vietnam — HIGH that this is practitioner consensus).
- Named distributor universe beyond the verified ones (Mesa, Mastertran, Belie, Grove, Master Bee, Maple Care, VE Pharma, JM Group, OQR, LyChee, Jin Healthy Life): Zuellig Pharma, DKSH, Mega Lifesciences all operate Vietnam healthcare distribution (**UNVERIFIED for supplements this session — LOW; verify roles before naming them in a pitch**). **What makes a foreign brand attractive vs a burden (inference from case evidence — MEDIUM):**
- Attractive: origin credential they can advertise ("#1 in Canada" equivalent — note Organika can't claim that; find the honest credential); GMP/CFS dossier ready; a real marketing budget; pack sizes and price ladder designed for VND price points; patience for a 6–12-month registration + listing runway; Tet gift SKU.

## 8.4 Pricing structure: the stack and the trap

- Sourced stack: distributor 35–60% on landed (MEDIUM) → retail 15–30%+ commission-equivalent (MEDIUM) → legal promo depth to 50–100% (HIGH) → platform take 7–22% online (MEDIUM-HIGH). Sub-distributor layer for independents: few points, unpublished (UNVERIFIED).
- Where list price sits: real SKUs show 20–45% gaps between official list and street/promo price (Adiva, LiveSpo, DHC — Ch6.2, MEDIUM). Everyday-promo price is the real consumer price; list exists to fund the gap.
- **The trap (synthesis — MEDIUM):** launching at a shelf price computed from cost-plus with thin margins leaves nothing for the promo cadence the market expects (Tet window, 11.11, livestream pushes, GWP). Brands then either break price integrity (killing pharmacy relationships) or go dark on promos (killing velocity). Build the ladder backward: target everyday-promo consumer price → add 25–35% headroom to set list → check the stack still pays everyone → only then quote your distributor transfer price.

## 8.5 The first meeting: what to bring, what they'll ask, what suppliers get wrong

**Documented guidance:**
- TCS has life-sciences/consumer-products trade commissioners in HCMC who identify and validate distributors and advise on regulation/packaging; they advise arriving with a market-entry plan covering positioning, pricing strategy, distribution, promotion, logistics, financing (TCS; AAFC exporting-to-Vietnam guide, 2021–24 — HIGH that this guidance exists).
- Dossier readiness is the hard gate: CFS (consularized), GMP, CoA/lab tests, label translations, claim evidence (Ch5 — HIGH).

**Labeled inference (consistent with sourced facts — LOW-MEDIUM):**
- Bring: (1) registration-ready dossier index per SKU; (2) landed-cost and margin-ladder sheet in VND at real FX; (3) launch A&P commitment with a number attached; (4) authenticity plan (QR, Vietnam-specific label/pack); (5) samples and a Tet gift-box concept; (6) proof of demand elsewhere (Canadian/US velocity data, any existing VN sales via Kat Vietnam); (7) your Vietnamese trademark filing receipts.
- They will ask: Who holds the registration? What exclusivity and for how long? What's my margin after your promo asks? What's the marketing budget and who spends it? MOQ and lead times? What happens to grey-market Organika from Canada/iHerb? Why you versus Webber Naturals?
- What foreign suppliers typically get wrong: quoting global list prices with no VND ladder; assuming registration is the distributor's problem alone; skipping trademark filing; treating the first meeting as a close instead of the start of a relationship sequence; no Tet plan; underestimating that the distributor is evaluating a 10-year commitment (the Mastertran precedent), not a PO.

> This chapter is the least evidenced in the document: the research session could source the legal-institutional facts (Tet's statutory promo season, contract/IP realities, the north-south commercial split's existence) but most etiquette detail is practitioner knowledge, marked UNVERIFIED below rather than dressed up as research. Treat the marked items as high-probability defaults to confirm with the TCS HCMC desk and CanCham Vietnam before your first trip.

## 9.1 Relationship before business

- Vietnam-specific sourced anchor: the Mastertran/Doppelherz relationship is publicly framed as a partnership built over 12+ years with joint community programs — the archetype your counterparties know (GBA/EuroCham profiles — HIGH). Trade-guide titles on Vietnamese supplier negotiation exist (Cosmo Sourcing; ARC Group) but contents were unfetchable — cite-and-read later.
- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge, VIETNAM-SPECIFIC, LOW-MEDIUM): expect 2–4 meetings over weeks-to-months before substantive terms; early meetings assess you, not the product; consistency of the same faces on your side matters — swapping your negotiator resets the clock.

## 9.2 Hierarchy, age, title

- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge; partly GENERIC-ASIA, LOW-MEDIUM): the most senior person speaks least and decides; titles matter (Tổng Giám đốc = general director; Phó = deputy); address counterparts by title + given name (Mr. Đức, not Mr. Đoàn); in family-owned distributors the founder or founder's family decides regardless of the org chart; the person asking detailed questions is usually the evaluator, not the decider.

## 9.3 Meeting etiquette

- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge; card exchange and seating are GENERIC-ASIA; specifics below are VIETNAM-SPECIFIC, LOW-MEDIUM): two-handed business-card exchange, read before pocketing; guests seated facing the door; small talk on family, food, Vietnam impressions is expected and is part of the evaluation; gifts from Canada — maple syrup, ice wine, branded premium samples — are safe; wrap in red/gold, never black or white (funeral colors); avoid clocks and knives as gifts; present and receive with both hands.

## 9.4 Meals, drinking, and how deals progress

- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge, VIETNAM-SPECIFIC, LOW-MEDIUM): the dinner after the second or third meeting is where the relationship is actually decided; beer culture is real — "một, hai, ba, dô!" (one-two-three, cheers!) toasts, and in the North rice-wine toasts person-by-person; you may decline alcohol on health grounds if you do it warmly and toast with something; karaoke invitations signal progress; the most senior host pays; reciprocate on the next round.
- Sourced adjacent fact: HCP entertainment in pharma is regulated/ethics-coded (EuroCham Pharma Group Code — HIGH) — keep distributor entertainment normal-commercial.

## 9.5 Indirect communication and face

- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge; the mechanism is GENERIC-ASIA, phrasing VIETNAM-SPECIFIC, LOW-MEDIUM): "we will consider it," "it may be difficult," a smile plus a topic change, or endless requests for more documents usually mean no; a direct public "no" from you causes loss of face — disagree by asking questions ("how would this work if…?") or deferring to constraints ("our GMP rules require…"); praise publicly, push back privately; written follow-ups let counterparts retreat without face loss.

## 9.6 Negotiation style

- Sourced anchors: initial-deal payment norms (advance or 60–120-day L/C — trade.gov, HIGH); trademark squatting by local partners is documented — contracts alone don't protect the brand (Harris Sliwoski — HIGH); Decree 81 institutionalizes deep discounting seasons, so price negotiation presumes promo funding (HIGH).
- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge, LOW-MEDIUM): expect patient, price-focused bargaining with 15–30% built-in haggling room; first offers are openers; contracts are treated as the start of the relationship, renegotiable when conditions change — the relationship, not the paper, is the enforcement mechanism (GENERIC-ASIA pattern, true in Vietnam); put registration ownership, dossier handover, non-compete, and termination mechanics in writing anyway and register the trademark first.

## 9.7 North vs South

- Sourced anchor: the market itself splits — chains are southern-born (Pharmacity HCMC, Trung Son Mekong Delta) or nationally run from both (Long Chau); Hanoi hosts the pharma wholesale hub Hapulico, HCMC hosts Tô Hiến Thành (Ch2 — MEDIUM); distributor HQs split (Mastertran Hanoi; OQR/KQ HCMC).
- UNVERIFIED (general knowledge, VIETNAM-SPECIFIC, LOW-MEDIUM): Hanoi business culture is more formal, protocol- and relationship-heavy, closer to government, slower to decide; HCMC is faster, more transactional, more Western-commercial; plan longer cycles and more banquets in the North; many national distributors run dual offices — ask where the decision sits.

## 9.8 The Tet calendar and the business year

| Period | What happens | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sept–Nov | Tet sell-in: chains and distributors commit Tet assortments and gift boxes; production planning starts early | Cosmo/VALO — MEDIUM thin; sell-in window itself UNVERIFIED general practice |
| 30 days before Tet (late Dec–Jan/Feb, lunar) | Statutory concentrated-promotion window — discounts to 100% legal; gifting peaks; health gift sets displace alcohol/confectionery | Decree 81/2018 — HIGH; VnBusiness 2025–26 — MEDIUM |
| ~2 weeks around Tet | National shutdown; decisions freeze from ~2–3 weeks before; logistics jam (the Jan 2026 border crisis hit exactly this window) | Border-crisis reporting 2026 — HIGH for the jam; freeze duration UNVERIFIED general knowledge |
| Feb–Mar (post-Tet) | Slow restart; Q1 results carry the Tet quarter (Sanvinest: Q1 = 26% of annual plan) | DNSE 2026 — MEDIUM-HIGH |
| Apr–Aug | The real negotiation and registration window for a next-Tet launch | Derived — plan 12+ months registration-to-shelf |

## 9.9 Five common Western-supplier mistakes (each with its real anchor)

1. **Pricing from global lists with no promo headroom** — the market's real price is everyday-promo (20–45% below list, Ch6.2); anchor: Pharmacity's own above-market pricing failure (Dân Trí 2024 — HIGH).
2. **Letting the distributor own the registration and the trademark** — non-transferable registrations + first-to-file trademarks = hostage risk; anchor: documented distributor mark-squatting cases (Harris Sliwoski/KENFOX — HIGH).
3. **Treating ecommerce as optional or delegating it to grey sellers** — anchor: Lazada-era brands missed the livestream shift and lost the channel (vietnam.vn 2026 — MEDIUM); non-mall sellers were purged in 2025 (HIGH).
4. **Making claims marketing wrote, not the dossier** — anchor: VND 560M of VFA fines Jul–Oct 2025; celebrity conveyor liability since 1 Jan 2026 (HIGH).
5. **Skipping the Tet build** — anchor: Sanvinest stakes VND 390B of inventory on the season and books a quarter of its year in Q1 (MEDIUM-HIGH); a brand without a gift SKU and Sept–Nov sell-in misses the deepest legal promo window of the year (Decree 81 — HIGH).

# CHAPTER 10: CHEAT SHEET, MEETING CHECKLIST, GLOSSARY

> **What you need to know**
> Three standalone tools: a two-page cheat sheet you can reread in a taxi, a one-page meeting checklist, and a glossary of every Vietnamese term, chain, and regulation used in this document. Everything here is sourced in the chapters above; confidence ratings carry over.

## 10.1 The market in 10 bullets

1. Analyst-consensus market: **USD ~0.9B (2024), ~11%/yr → ~1.7B by 2030** (GVR + Ken agree within 2% — HIGH); the "USD 2.4–2.9B" numbers you'll hear = the widest functional-food definition (MEDIUM).
2. Fastest-growing supplement market in SEA; #2 destination for Australian supplement exports (**AUD 86M, 2024** — CMA, HIGH).
3. Pharmacy is the trust channel: **59% of purchases** (Rakuten 2022 — HIGH, dated); the pharmacist is the de-facto prescriber.
4. **Long Chau won**: 2,517 stores (Mar 2026), VND 34,501B revenue 2025 (+36%), profitable, Creador valued it at USD 1.5B (HIGH).
5. Ecommerce = Shopee (>56%) + TikTok Shop (>41%) duopoly; **TikTok Shop is already #1 for supplements** (Metric — HIGH/MEDIUM).
6. The 2025 counterfeit crisis (100-ton MegaPhaco case, Medistar VND 1.8T, Kera convictions, ex-VFA chief 20 years) cut online category GMV 22% in USD and pushed demand into official channels: **Mall stores = 2.12% of shops, 32.6% of GMV** (HIGH).
7. Your products are **TPBVSK** — pre-market VFA registration, held by a Vietnamese entity, **not transferable** (HIGH/MEDIUM).
8. The operative law is **Decree 15/2018** — Decree 46/2026 is suspended until the new Food Safety Law (~Mar 2027) (HIGH).
9. Ads: provincial approval since 1 Jul 2026 + exact disclaimer + no doctors/medical imagery + KOL personal liability (HIGH).
10. Canada = trusted category, **zero flagship brand** — the "Canadian Doppelherz" seat is open; Organika already has a small distributor (Kat Vietnam) and no Mall store (MEDIUM-HIGH).

## 10.2 The 5 channels that matter (in order)

1. **Long Chau** — the must-win door; enter via an exclusive distributor + chain-level agreement (Blackmores/Vitabiotics pattern).
2. **Shopee Mall** — authenticity shelf + demand proof; ~21–22% all-in take.
3. **TikTok Shop Mall** — #1 supplement platform; restricted-category approval; livestream engine.
4. **Pharmacity** — premium-wellness second chain; 7,000-SKU discipline.
5. **Independent pharmacies via distributor reps** — phase 2 volume; Hapulico/Tô Hiến Thành hubs.

## 10.3 The 10 brands to know cold

Blackmores (Mesa; chính hãng playbook) · Doppelherz (Mastertran; pharmacist playbook — the benchmark) · DHC (Belie; #1 by units online) · Swisse (Grove; H&H-owned) · Orihiro (jelly formats) · KGC Cheong Kwan Jang (Tet gifting) · Nature's Way AU (kids' niche #1) · Traphaco (domestic herbal #1) · DHG/NattoEnzym (domestic pharma credibility) · Webber Naturals (the only structured Canadian play).

## 10.4 Three winning plays / three losing plays

- **Win:** origin credential + one committed exclusive distributor + official-channel saturation (Doppelherz, DHC, Blackmores).
- **Win:** authenticity infrastructure — QR, GS1, named-importer sub-labels, Mall stores (Blackmores 2024; Mall = 32.6% of GMV).
- **Win:** Tet gift SKU with Sept–Nov sell-in (KGC; Sanvinest VND 390B Tet inventory, Q1 = 26% of year).
- **Lose:** claims beyond the dossier (Kera: 2-year prison sentences; VFA fines).
- **Lose:** letting the distributor own registration + trademark (non-transferable; first-to-file squatting documented).
- **Lose:** grey-market/non-mall ecommerce strategy (13,700+ shops purged; category −22% USD GMV 2025).

## 10.5 Three regulatory facts that change everything

1. Decree 46/2026 is **suspended**; register now under Decree 15/2018; re-comply ~2027 under the new law.
2. The registration belongs to the Vietnamese filer and **cannot be transferred** — decide who files before you sign anything.
3. Since 1 Jul 2026, ad approval is **provincial**, the disclaimer is mandatory verbatim, doctors/medical imagery are banned, and your KOLs are personally liable.

## 10.6 One-page buyer/distributor meeting checklist

**Bring:**
- [ ] Dossier index per SKU: consularized CFS, GMP cert, ISO 17025 lab tests (<12 months), claim evidence, draft Vietnamese label
- [ ] VND price ladder: FOB → landed → distributor → retail → everyday-promo, at stated FX, with 25–35% promo headroom shown
- [ ] Launch A&P commitment (number, split: pharmacist training / KOC budget / platform vouchers)
- [ ] Authenticity plan: QR/trace, Vietnam-specific pack, Mall-store plan
- [ ] Samples + Tet gift-box concept + Canadian credential proof (Health Canada licenses, NPNs, velocity data)
- [ ] Vietnamese trademark filing receipts (file BEFORE this meeting)
- [ ] Status of the existing Kat Vietnam relationship, clarified internally

**They will ask:**
- [ ] Who holds the registration? (Have your answer: independent agent or own entity preferred; cooperation clause if distributor)
- [ ] Exclusivity: scope, duration, performance conditions?
- [ ] My margin after your promo asks? MOQ, lead time, stock depth?
- [ ] What stops Canadian/iHerb Organika undercutting me? (Pack differentiation answer)
- [ ] Why you vs Webber Naturals/Blackmores? What's the marketing money?

**Never:**
- [ ] Never quote a price with no promo headroom · never promise claims beyond the dossier · never let registration/trademark questions slide to "later" · never disparage a counterpart's counteroffer bluntly in front of their team · never schedule decisions in the 3 weeks before Tet

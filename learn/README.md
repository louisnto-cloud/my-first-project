# Learnverse 🌈 — a talking learning playground (Kindergarten → Grade 7)

A web app for a child who is learning to read. Everything is spoken out loud
by a friendly fox guide — the child never needs to read an instruction. It is
a free-roam playground: she taps big picture buttons, and every tap reacts
instantly with sounds, sparkles, and stars. There are no fail states, no
timers, and no way to lose.

## How to open it on the iPad

1. Open **Safari** on the iPad.
2. Type this address in the bar at the top and tap Go:
   **https://louisnto-cloud.github.io/my-first-project/learn/**
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the
   top-right of Safari).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
5. A **Learnverse** icon (purple with a star) appears on the home screen.
   Open the app from that icon — it fills the whole screen like a real app.
6. The app starts with a big rocket 🚀. The child taps it once and the fox
   starts talking. (That first tap is required by Apple to allow sound.)

**Volume:** make sure the iPad is not muted and the volume is up — the whole
app is voice-driven.

## What's inside

| Island | Skill | Rough level |
|---|---|---|
| 🔤 Letter Land | Meet each letter + its picture word | Kindergarten |
| ✏️ Tracing Trail | Trace letters with a finger | Kindergarten |
| 🔎 Finding Forest | Hear a letter, find it | Kindergarten |
| 🧱 Word Builder | Build simple words letter by letter | Grade 1 |
| 🖼️ Picture Pond | Hear a word, tap its picture | K–Grade 1 |
| ⚡ Speedy Words | Sight words (the, and, said…) | Grades 1–3 |
| 📖 Story Sea | Read-along stories + a comprehension question | Grades 1–4 |
| 🐝 Spelling Bee | Hear a word, spell it with letter tiles | Grades 2–7 |
| 🌋 Word Volcano | Hear a definition, pick the word | Grades 3–7 |

Islands open up gently as stars are collected, so the harder material arrives
in the right order — but nothing is ever locked behind a "wrong answer."
Difficulty inside each island rises automatically as she masters items.

## The hidden parent screen

Press and **hold the little gray gear ⚙️ in the bottom-right corner for
3 full seconds**. A child tapping it briefly gets nothing. Inside you'll see:
stars, total play time, when the app was last used, and every skill she has
touched marked **new / learning / mastered**.

## The voice

The app uses the high-quality voice built into the iPad (Samantha). All words,
letters, spelling words, and definitions are spoken by it. No accounts, no
setup, works offline once loaded.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device itself (in the browser's storage), so use the
same home-screen icon each time. Deleting the icon or clearing Safari website
data resets progress.

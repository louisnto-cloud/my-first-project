How to use this

  1. Pick a scenario at the top (Low, Base, High, Stretch). Every view reflects it.
  2. Go to Assumptions — the only place you type. Change any yellow cell.
  3. Read the results: Overview for headlines, P&L for the full picture, Monthly for phasing.
  4. Use Pricing Lab to see the best wholesale price, and Targets to work backwards from a goal.
  5. Click Save to keep your numbers in this browser; Export CSV to take them elsewhere.

# Organika RTD Forecast

Open the file. Look at **Home**. Edit on **Control Panel**. That's it.

---

## In 60 seconds

The file has 23 tabs. You only need two.

- **Home** - the numbers and the story.
- **Control Panel** - the five inputs that matter.

Everything else is the engine. It hums in the background.

---

## Open it

**On your computer.** Double-click the file. Excel opens it.

**On Chrome.** Drag the file into <https://drive.google.com>. Right-click it. Open with Google Sheets. Save as Google Sheets.

**On your phone.** Don't. The grid is too wide.

---

## Read it

1. Open **Home**.
2. Look at the four big numbers. Revenue. Gross profit. EBITDA. Cases to produce.
3. Read the story below them.
4. Scan the eight smaller stats.

That's the briefing.

---

## Edit it

1. Open **Control Panel**.
2. There are five knobs. Change any one. Watch Home update.
3. Done.

For deeper edits, the Control Panel points to the right tab:
- More doors -> **Doors**
- Different velocity -> **Velocity**
- Pricing -> **Pricing**
- Marketing -> **Marketing and Sampling**
- Trade spend -> **Trade Spend**
- OPEX -> **OPEX**

## When numbers look wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| `#####` | Make the column wider. |
| `#VALUE!` | A referenced cell got text instead of a number. Open the cell the formula points at. |
| Numbers didn't update | Press F9 (Windows) or Cmd-Equals (Mac). |
| Red on Class Mix | The row doesn't add to 100%. Re-enter as decimals (0.25, not 25%). |
| Green on Class Mix | Good. |

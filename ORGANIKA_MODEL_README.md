# Organika · Sparkling Daily — One-Year Operating Model

An interactive, single-file financial model for the Sparkling Daily line
(3 flavours × Singles + 4-Pack = 6 SKUs). Open
[`organika_sparkling_daily_model.html`](./organika_sparkling_daily_model.html)
in any browser — no install, works offline, and everything recalculates live.
A matching Excel workbook is generated by [`build_model.py`](./build_model.py).

> Figures are illustrative placeholders. Put your real numbers in on the
> **Assumptions** tab — every view updates instantly.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | An auto-written headline for the selected scenario, six KPI cards (each with its delta vs Base), and an EBITDA-by-scenario chart + full scenario comparison (with a Δ-vs-Base column). |
| **P&L** | Full profit & loss by SKU to net income, a colour-coded **money waterfall** (Net Sales → … → Net Income), and **contribution by SKU** (flavour-coloured). |
| **Pricing Lab** | How wholesale case price (CP) drives **Gross Profit %** or **Annual Contribution** — each row heat-shaded, best price in range ★-marked. |
| **Sensitivity** | An EBITDA heatmap across price × volume, a **break-even curve** (scenarios plotted against the loss line), and a **tornado** ranking the biggest EBITDA levers (±10%). |
| **Monthly** | The annual result phased across 12 months by an editable seasonality curve; fixed costs land evenly so low-volume months show as EBITDA losses. |
| **Targets** | Goal-seek: type a target EBITDA or Net Income and it solves the volume needed to reach it (or flags it as unreachable on volume alone). |
| **Assumptions** | The only place you type. Per-SKU economics, company overheads, and the four scenario levers. Hover any **?** for what a field means. |

## Scenarios & levers

Four scenarios — **Low · Base · High · Stretch** — are the same model flexed by
three levers each: a **volume ×**, a **price ×**, and a **trade-discount Δ**.
Optional **price elasticity** (Assumptions) makes volume respond to price changes.
Pick a scenario from the pills at the top; every view follows it.

## Handy features

- **Dark mode** — follows your system theme.
- **Your edits are saved** in the browser (localStorage); **Reset** restores defaults.
- **🔗 Share** — copies a link that encodes the entire model, so a colleague opens
  the exact same numbers (no backend).
- **↓ CSV** — exports scenario summary + the selected P&L by SKU + monthly phasing.
- **⎙ Print** — clean print / save-as-PDF.
- **Keyboard** — Arrow keys / Home / End move between tabs; everything is labelled
  for screen readers.

## The model, briefly

Per SKU: `cases = volume × vol× × price×^elasticity`; `net = cases × CP × price× ×
(1 − discount)`; `gross profit = net − COGS`; `contribution = gross profit − A&P`.
Company: `EBITDA = Σ contribution − fixed overhead − logistics(% of net)`; then
`− D&A − interest − tax = net income`. Break-even and the price×volume sensitivity
use a closed form of the same equations, so the charts always tie to the P&L.

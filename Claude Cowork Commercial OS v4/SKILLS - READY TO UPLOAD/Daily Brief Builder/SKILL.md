# Daily Brief Builder

> Authored to the Claude Cowork Commercial OS v4 spec. The Operator Guide names this skill but does not include its body; this is written to the system's conventions. Tune to taste.

## What this skill does

Produce the morning Daily Commercial Brief, and focused single-meeting prep when asked. Cuts prep time and forces the day into priorities instead of noise.

## When to use

- The scheduled Daily Commercial Brief.
- Ad-hoc "prep me for today" or "prep me for this meeting" requests.

## When NOT to use

- Weekly movement and story → use **Deck Story Builder** / the Friday Account Pulse.
- Turning a transcript into structured items → use **Commercial Meeting Extractor**.

## How to build the brief

1. **Start from the File Index** — use current approved files first.
2. **Review:** calendar for the next 24 hours; email/chat tied to those meetings; open P0/P1 actions; overdue actions; actions due in the next 7 days; open customer asks; live risks; latest meeting summaries; deck/files relevant to today.
3. **For each meeting in the next 24 hours**, give: objective; why it matters; what changed since last touchpoint; open commitments/risks; the 3 points to land; the 3 questions to ask; red flags to avoid; the file/slide/workbook to open first.
4. **Prioritize ruthlessly** — clarity over coverage.

## Output

1. Today at a glance
2. Meeting-by-meeting prep
3. Open commitments due in the next 7 days
4. Risks and opportunities
5. Recommended talking points
6. Files to review
7. A 5-sentence executive brief

## Rules

- Keep it readable in under 5 minutes.
- Do not send messages.
- Do not modify source-of-truth files.
- Do not invent commitments, owners, or due dates.

## Done when

You can read the brief in under five minutes and walk into the day knowing what matters, what changed, what is due, what is at risk, and what to open first.

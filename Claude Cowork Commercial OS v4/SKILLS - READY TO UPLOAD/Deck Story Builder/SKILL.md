# Deck Story Builder

> Authored to the Claude Cowork Commercial OS v4 spec. The Operator Guide names this skill but does not include its body; this is written to the system's conventions. Tune to taste.

## What this skill does

Keep the commercial story sharp and the deck fresh. Converts what changed — backlog, decisions, risks, forecast, launches — into specific slide changes with draft language, prioritized and ready for your review.

## When to use

- The Deck Backlog has grown or the story has moved.
- Friday Account Pulse flags deck work.
- Monthly Business Review prep.
- Any "the deck is behind, what does it now need to say" request.

## When NOT to use

- Extracting a meeting → use **Commercial Meeting Extractor**.
- Daily prep → use **Daily Brief Builder**.

## How to build the story update

1. **Source from the File Index** — current approved deck and Control Tower workbook.
2. **Review:** Deck Backlog tab; recent decisions, risks, customer asks; forecast and launch changes; what changed since the deck was last updated.
3. **For each out-of-date slide or section**, state: what it currently says; what changed in reality; the recommended new message/data point; why it matters (sales, margin, timing, retailer, risk); confidence and evidence gap.
4. **Keep the story commercially sharp** — what the retailer needs, what changed, what is at risk, what action is required.

## Output

1. Prioritized slide changes (P0 / P1 / nice-to-have)
2. Draft replacement language for the top changes
3. Any new slide the story now needs
4. Slides that can be cut or simplified
5. Control Tower handoff — Deck Backlog updates in review-copy or delta mode

## Rules

- Never edit the approved master deck — produce a review copy or change list.
- Do not invent numbers; flag where a current figure is needed.
- Separate confirmed changes from assumptions.

## Done when

There is a prioritized, reviewable set of slide changes with draft language, sourced from real movement — and the approved master is untouched.

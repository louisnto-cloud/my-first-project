# Friday Account Pulse

- **Suggested cadence:** Friday afternoon
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Daily Brief Builder, Open Loop Manager, Deck Story Builder
- **Why this exists:** It gives you the weekly commercial truth, not just the weekly activity.

## Paste into a scheduled task:

Build the Friday pulse for this project.

Review the last 7 days of:

- meetings
- meeting summaries
- open-loop changes
- email context
- deck backlog changes
- risk changes
- customer asks
- major workbook changes

Tell me:

1. What materially moved this week
2. What slipped or stalled
3. What changed in the commercial story
4. What needs action next week
5. What needs leadership visibility
6. What the deck or business review now needs to reflect

Then produce:

- a one-page weekly pulse
- a short action list for next week
- the top 3 risks
- the top 3 opportunities
- the top 3 files or slides that now need attention

Important:

- do not just restate meetings
- focus on movement, consequence, and action
- do not edit master files unless explicitly asked

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Friday Pulse`

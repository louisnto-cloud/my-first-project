# Daily Commercial Brief

- **Suggested cadence:** Weekdays, early morning
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Daily Brief Builder, Open Loop Manager
- **Why this exists:** It cuts prep time and forces the day into priorities instead of noise.

## Paste into a scheduled task:

Prepare my commercial brief for today.
Work inside this project and use the current approved files from the File Index first.

Review:

- calendar events for today and the next 24 hours
- recent email or chat context tied to those meetings
- open P0 and P1 actions
- overdue actions
- actions due in the next 7 days
- open customer asks
- live risks
- the latest meeting summaries
- the current deck or files relevant to today's meetings

For each meeting in the next 24 hours, tell me:

- meeting objective
- why it matters
- what changed since the last touchpoint
- open commitments or risks tied to this meeting
- the 3 points I should land
- the 3 questions I should ask
- any red flags or topics to avoid unless needed
- which file, slide, or workbook I should open before the meeting

Then give me:

1. Today at a glance
2. Meeting-by-meeting prep
3. Open commitments due in the next 7 days
4. Risks and opportunities
5. Recommended talking points
6. Files to review
7. A 5-sentence executive brief

Important:

- keep this short enough to read in under 5 minutes
- prioritize clarity over coverage
- do not send messages
- do not modify source-of-truth files

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Daily Brief`

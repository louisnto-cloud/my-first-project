# Launch and Forecast Check

- **Suggested cadence:** Weekly or bi-weekly (Full Mode only)
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Open Loop Manager, Deck Story Builder
- **Why this exists:** Launch timing and forecast accuracy are two of the highest-consequence commercial variables. This keeps them honest between formal reviews.

> Full-Mode / optional. Listed under Full Mode in the guide ("launch and forecast checks"). Authored to the system's spec. Forecast and launch-timing changes stay under human review — this check surfaces and drafts, it does not commit.

## Paste into a scheduled task:

Run the launch and forecast check for this project.
Use the current approved forecast, launch, and pricing files, and the Control Tower, from the File Index.

For launches:

1. List each active or upcoming launch and its status against plan
2. Flag any timing risk (item setup, artwork, claims, supply, retailer window)
3. Tie each risk to an owner and next step, and to a Risk Register entry
4. Call out anything that has slipped since the last check

For the forecast:

1. Note where the forecast has moved and why (commitments, promo, distribution, seasonality)
2. Flag gaps between what was committed in meetings and what the forecast reflects
3. Identify assumptions that now look stale or unsupported
4. Surface the 3 forecast risks with the most upside or downside

Then give me:

- a one-page launch + forecast status
- the timing risks ranked by consequence
- the forecast changes worth escalating
- what should be logged in the Control Tower or reflected in the deck

Important:

- do NOT change the forecast, launch timing, or any master file — surface and draft only
- distinguish confirmed facts, inferred points, and assumptions
- do not invent numbers; flag where a current figure is needed

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Launch and Forecast Check`

# Monthly Business Review Prep

- **Suggested cadence:** Monthly, a few days before the business review
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Deck Story Builder, Open Loop Manager, Daily Brief Builder
- **Why this exists:** It forces the system to connect a month of activity into an executive-ready narrative instead of a pile of weekly updates.

> Full-Mode / optional. Referenced in "How to Use This Every Week" (Monthly). Authored to the system's spec; the guide names it but does not include a body.

## Paste into a scheduled task:

Prepare the monthly business review for this project.
Use the current approved deck and Control Tower workbook from the File Index, and the last ~30 days of meetings, summaries, and briefs.

Connect the month across these threads and tell the story, not the log:

- actions — what got done, what is still open, what slipped
- decisions — what was decided and what it changed
- risks — what emerged, what was mitigated, what is still live
- retailer / customer asks — what they wanted and where we landed
- launches — status against plan
- forecast shifts — what moved and why
- deck updates — what the story now needs to say

Then produce:

1. The one-paragraph headline: where this account/function stands this month
2. What materially changed in the last 30 days
3. Wins worth surfacing to leadership
4. The live risks and what we are doing about them
5. The decisions needed from this review
6. A prioritized slide-change list for the review deck (P0 / P1)
7. The 3 numbers or proof points the deck must carry
8. Next month's top priorities

Important:

- executive-ready and concise — movement and consequence, not restatement
- distinguish confirmed facts, inferred points, and assumptions
- do not invent numbers; flag where a current figure is needed
- do not edit the approved master deck — produce a review copy or change list

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Monthly Business Review Prep`
and if a review deck copy is built:
`REVIEW - YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - MBR Deck`

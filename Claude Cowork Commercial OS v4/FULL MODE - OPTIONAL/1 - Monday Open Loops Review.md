# Monday Open Loops Review

- **Suggested cadence:** Monday morning (only when your workload is heavy)
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Open Loop Manager
- **Why this exists:** When too many actions are drifting, too many items lack owners, or too many "waiting" items never come back, this resets the accountability layer for the week.

> Full-Mode / optional. Referenced in "How to Use This Every Week" (Mondays). Authored to the system's spec; the guide names it but does not include a body. Only turn this on after the core three scheduled tasks are stable.

## Paste into a scheduled task:

Run the open-loops review for this project.
Work from the current approved Control Tower workbook in the File Index.

Sweep every open item across the Action Tracker, Customer Asks, Decision Log (Revisit), Risk Register, and Deck Backlog, and check:

- is the owner clear?
- is the due date clear?
- is the status accurate against the latest evidence?
- is it overdue, blocked, or repeatedly slipping?
- is it a "waiting" item that has gone quiet and needs a nudge?
- is it a duplicate of a materially-same item?

Then give me, for the week ahead:

1. Open-loops health summary — counts: open, overdue, blocked, no owner, no due date, waiting > 7 days
2. The items that need my decision this week
3. The "waiting" items that have stalled and who I should chase
4. Duplicates to merge and items that look closable (with the evidence gap noted)
5. The 5 loops most likely to hurt if they slip

Important:

- update the workbook in review-copy mode, or create a delta file — do not overwrite the master
- do not invent owners, due dates, or status changes
- never mark anything Done without evidence

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Open Loops Review`
and if needed:
`DELTA - YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Control Tower`

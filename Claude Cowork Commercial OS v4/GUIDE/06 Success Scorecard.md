# Success Scorecard

Do not measure success by how impressive the prompts sound.
Measure the operating outcomes.

## The five measures that matter

### 1. Missed follow-ups
**Target direction: down**
If the system is working, fewer commitments should disappear after meetings.

### 2. Actions with owner and due date
**Target direction: up**
A good system forces clarity.
An unclear tracker usually means the meeting extraction is still too soft.

### 3. Prep time before meetings
**Target direction: down**
The Daily Commercial Brief should reduce the time it takes to get ready.

### 4. Time from meeting to tracker update
**Target direction: down**
If actions, risks, and asks are still sitting in raw notes two days later, the loop is broken.

### 5. Deck freshness lag
**Target direction: down**
When the commercial story changes, the deck backlog should reflect it quickly.

## The best weekly check

Every Friday, ask:

> Did the system help me make a better decision faster this week?

If the answer is no, reduce complexity and fix the part that failed.

# What to Automate vs What to Review

A system becomes trustworthy when you are clear about this line.

## Safe to automate first

These are good first-wave automations:

- daily briefs
- meeting synthesis
- tracker delta files
- decision memos
- weekly account pulses
- retailer and competitor watch digests
- deck update recommendations
- internal draft follow-ups
- hygiene audits

These are mostly summarization, extraction, and prioritization tasks.
They are reversible and easy to review.

## Keep under human review

These should stay under review:

- anything customer-facing before sending
- forecast changes
- launch timing changes
- final deck edits to the approved master file
- file deletes, moves, or renames of source-of-truth files
- any work involving sensitive or regulated material
- anything with financial or legal consequence

## The rule of thumb

> Automate preparation.
> Review commitment.
> Approve consequence.

That is the line.

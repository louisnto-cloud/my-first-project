# Meeting Prep Note

- **Best used when:** you have a specific meeting coming up and want to walk in ready
- **Best used in:** Cowork project
- **Recommended skills:** Daily Brief Builder, Open Loop Manager
- **Why this exists:** It produces focused prep for one meeting, deeper than the daily brief line item.

> Authored to the system's spec. The Operator Guide names this prompt but does not include its body; this version follows the guide's conventions. Tune to taste.

## Paste into Claude:

Prepare me for this meeting: [Meeting name / attendees / time].
Work inside this project and use the current approved files from the File Index first.

Pull the relevant context:

- the last meeting summary with this stakeholder or on this topic
- open actions, customer asks, decisions, and risks tied to them
- recent email or chat context leading into the meeting
- the current approved deck or workbook relevant to the agenda
- anything that changed since the last touchpoint

Then give me:

1. Meeting objective and why it matters now
2. What changed since the last touchpoint
3. Open commitments and risks tied to this relationship
4. The 3 points I should land
5. The 3 questions I should ask
6. Likely objections or asks, and a grounded response to each
7. Red flags or topics to avoid unless raised
8. The exact file, slide, or workbook to open before I walk in

Important:

- keep it to one page, readable in under 3 minutes
- separate confirmed facts from assumptions
- do not invent commitments, owners, or due dates
- do not send messages or modify source-of-truth files

Save as:
`YYYY-MM-DD - [Project] - Meeting Prep - [Meeting Name]`

The ten rules

  1. Settle the existing partner plan first. Organika already sells through Kat Vietnam Trading (organikacanada.vn, ~10 SKUs, no Shopee Mall store). Agree targets, registration holder and official stores before any other distributor talk.
  2. Know who holds the registrations and the trademark today. Agree handover terms in writing.
  3. Treat the grey market as a competitor. Vietnam packs and a price ladder from day one.
  4. Copy the proven pattern. One national distributor, origin credential, chain listing, Mall stores, QR authenticity (Doppelherz, Blackmores, Swisse).
  5. Lead with what is open and priced to win. Rerun the order when real COGS arrive.
  6. Price backward from the promo price with 25–35% headroom. Never show FOB to a distributor.
  7. Confirm the registration window before committing dates. Get the consultant's answer on intake and transition in writing.
  8. Write every claim from the dossier. Provincial ad approval since 1 Jul 2026, verbatim disclaimer, no doctors, personal KOL liability.
  9. Online proof first, then Long Chau. Shopee Mall takes ~25–27% before ads (May 2026); chains ask for velocity data.
  10. Run the calendar off Tet 2028 (26 Jan 2028), sold in Sep–Nov 2027.

5 · Regulation (the real 2026 regime)

What you need to know. The operative regime is Decree 15/2018. Decree 46/2026 was suspended twice and stays suspended until the amended Law on Food Safety takes effect (~Mar 2027). Your products (TPBVSK) need pre market VFA registration held by your Vietnamese importer — and it is not transferable. From 1 Jul 2026 ad approval is provincial, the not a medicine disclaimer is mandatory, and KOLs carry personal liability.

8 · The buyer drill

What you need to know. Chain trade terms are unpublished. What's documented: retailers ask listing fees, distributors expect 35–60% on landed and full compliance control, initial deals run on 60–120-day L/Cs, and registration before listing is a hard gate. The most dangerous clause in your first contract is who holds the registration.

9 · Culture (Vietnam specific)

  • Relationship before business: 2–4 meetings over weeks to months; the dinner decides more than the meeting. practitioner
  • The senior person speaks least and decides; in family owned distributors the founder decides regardless of the org chart.
  • Indirect no: "we'll consider it," endless document requests, a smile + topic change. Disagree by asking questions, never bluntly in front of their team.
  • North (Hanoi) formal, protocol/government-oriented, slower; South (HCMC) faster, commercial. Many distributors run dual offices — ask where the decision sits.
  • Tet 2027 = 6 Feb; official holiday ~3–11 Feb; decisions freeze ~3 weeks before. Never schedule a decision into pre-Tet. HIGH

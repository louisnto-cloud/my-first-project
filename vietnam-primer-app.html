The call: enter, on a narrow path

One committed distributor structure with Organika controlling registrations and trademarks, official channels only, magnesium and greens ahead of collagen, calendar built around Tet.

Market USD ~0.9B growing ~11%/yr HIGH. The 2025 counterfeit purge is handing share to provably authentic imports HIGH. No Canadian brand holds the flagship seat MED.

  1. Resolve Kat Vietnam Trading first. An exclusive distributor already exists (organikacanada.vn, ~10 SKUs, no Shopee Mall store). Audit, then upgrade / restructure / exit before opening any other conversation.
  2. Establish who holds registrations and the Organika trademark today. Registrations are not transferable; trademarks are first to file; distributor squatting is documented.
  3. Treat your own grey market as a live competitor. Hand carry sellers already ship Enhanced Collagen from Canada. Vietnam specific packs + controlled price ladder from day one.
  4. Copy the proven pattern. Exclusive distributor + origin credential + chain listing + Mall stores + QR authenticity (Doppelherz, Blackmores, Swisse).
  5. Lead with the open shelves. Magnesium and greens are the lead SKUs; collagen is the credential flag, not the volume driver.
  6. Price backward from the everyday-promo price with 25–35% headroom. Real list-to-street gaps run 20–45%.
  7. Register now under Decree 15/2018; budget a 2027 re-compliance round. Decree 46/2026 is suspended; new law ~Mar 2027.
  8. Write every claim from the dossier. Provincial ad approval since 1 Jul 2026, verbatim disclaimer, no doctors, personal KOL liability.
  9. Sequence channels: Shopee + TikTok Mall for demand proof, then Long Chau.
  10. Run the calendar off Tet (6 Feb 2027). First full Tet play is Tet 2028, sold in Sep–Nov 2027.

5 · Regulation (the real 2026 regime)

What you need to know. The operative regime is Decree 15/2018. Decree 46/2026 was suspended twice and stays suspended until the amended Law on Food Safety takes effect (~Mar 2027). Your products (TPBVSK) need pre-market VFA registration held by your Vietnamese importer — and it is not transferable. From 1 Jul 2026 ad approval is provincial, the not-a-medicine disclaimer is mandatory, and KOLs carry personal liability.

Model. Distributor 35–60% and retail 15–30%+ are sourced ranges (Asia Pro 2026, LOW-MEDIUM); confirm against Kat Vietnam's real landed costs. VAT recoverable for a registered importer — shown as a shelf-price component, not a margin cost.

7 · Consumer

What you need to know. 60–70% of urban adults have used supplements, ~half of users daily. Buyers skew female, 25–45, urban. Immunity is the #1 stated reason (74%). Pharmacies still take 59% of purchases. The 2025 scandals made authenticity the #1 stated demand. Canada is trusted as a category but near-zero top-of-mind.

8 · The buyer drill

What you need to know. Chain trade terms are unpublished. What's documented: retailers ask listing fees, distributors expect 35–60% on landed and full compliance control, initial deals run on 60–120-day L/Cs, and registration-before-listing is a hard gate. The most dangerous clause in your first contract is who holds the registration.

9 · Culture (Vietnam-specific)

  • Relationship before business: 2–4 meetings over weeks-to-months; the dinner decides more than the meeting. practitioner
  • The senior person speaks least and decides; in family-owned distributors the founder decides regardless of the org chart.
  • Indirect no: "we'll consider it," endless document requests, a smile + topic change. Disagree by asking questions, never bluntly in front of their team.
  • North (Hanoi) formal, protocol/government-oriented, slower; South (HCMC) faster, commercial. Many distributors run dual offices — ask where the decision sits.
  • Tet 2027 = 6 Feb; official holiday ~3–11 Feb; decisions freeze ~3 weeks before. Never schedule a decision into pre-Tet. HIGH

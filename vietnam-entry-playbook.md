**How to choose (decision rule):**
- If the existing partner holds the registrations and trademark and the plan is agreed: renew on the new plan (official stores, a rep supported chain push, IP handover terms). Cheaper and faster than switching; fresh registration is months per SKU (Ch5.3).
- If the plan cannot be agreed: line up an alternative before acting, file or reclaim trademarks first, and prefer a distributor with pharmacy depth (Mastertran class) over a beauty tilted one. Handle this privately and in person; in Vietnam, word travels and face matters (Ch9).
- Pair any pharmacy distributor with PharmaDi style B2B reach + Mall stores for demand data. Do not hand a multinational the line unless they commit a named brand manager and a marketing budget — big partner small brand neglect is the classic trap.

### D2. One page leave behind (the "why Organika" sheet)

**Organika Health Products — Vietnam partnership, at a glance**

- **Who we are.** Canadian natural health brand since 1990. Collagen, magnesium, greens, probiotics, fibre, vitamins. Made under GMP; Health Canada licensed.
- **Why Canada, honestly.** Not a famous origin in Vietnam yet, and we are not pretending otherwise. This is a quality and category play: Canada ranked #1 on the 2022 Global Food Security Index quality and safety sub index, and Jamieson, Webber Naturals and Natural Factors are already familiar names.
- **Where we play.** Lead with Enhanced Collagen, priced into the premium band below Japanese imports and offered as a Tet gift format, and chelated sleep and stress magnesium in a Vietnam pack, a niche no official brand owns. Greens follow in a smaller format.
- **What we bring the partner.** Registration support from GMP certified Canadian sites (free sale certificates, lab tests, claim evidence) to build each dossier; a launch marketing budget; Vietnam specific packs so our own grey market does not undercut the shelf; and a price ladder built with promo headroom so Tet and double day campaigns are fundable.
- **What we ask.** Pharmacy chain placement (Long Chau first), official Mall stores on Shopee and TikTok Shop, and exclusivity earned against volume milestones. We hold the registrations and trademarks so both sides can invest without hostage risk.
- **The market in one line.** USD ~0.9B, growing ~11% a year, the fastest in Southeast Asia, with the 2025 counterfeit purge moving consumers decisively toward provably genuine imports.
